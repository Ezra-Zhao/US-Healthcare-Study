## 一、先定性：网络、目录、等待是三格，幽灵网络坏在第二格

### 中文
看病找不到医生，法律上要先分清坏在哪一格。第一格是网络充足（network adequacy）：计划签下的医生与机构，在数量、专科构成与地理距离上够不够参保人用。第二格是目录准确（directory accuracy）：计划公布的医生目录里，人是不是真的在网内、真的在这个地址执业、真的收新病人。第三格是及时可及（timely access）：约不约得到、要等多久，本仓在专科预约流程篇里已按加州标准展开，本篇只作衔接。幽灵网络（ghost network）坏在第二格：目录里列着一长串名字，逐个打过去却是已退休、已搬走、电话打不通、根本不收这个计划、或者不收新病人——纸面网络很充足，真实网络是空的。分清三格的意义在于抓手不同：第一格的义务主体是计划对监管者的备案与测评，第二格有专门的核验与纠错时限和患者信赖保护，第三格走申诉与及时可及标准。用错格，投诉就打不到点上。

### English
When a patient cannot find a doctor, the law first asks which of three boxes is broken. Box one is network adequacy: whether the plan's contracted physicians and facilities are enough, in number, specialty mix, and geographic reach, for the people enrolled. Box two is directory accuracy: whether the people in the plan's published provider directory are actually in network, actually practicing at that address, and actually accepting new patients. Box three is timely access: whether an appointment can be had and how long the wait is — a box this repository already develops under California standards in its specialist-appointment article, so here it is only a pointer. A ghost network is a failure of box two: the directory lists name after name, and calling down the list reaches people who have retired, moved, cannot be reached, do not take the plan at all, or are closed to new patients — the paper network looks ample and the real network is empty. Keeping the boxes separate matters because the levers differ: box one runs through plan filings and regulator measurement, box two carries its own verification and correction clocks plus reliance protection for patients, and box three runs through grievances and timely-access standards. Complaining into the wrong box misses the point of failure.

## 二、联邦充足标准：交易所计划、Medicare Advantage 与 Medicaid 三套尺

### 中文
联邦层没有一把统一的尺，三个市场三套口径。第一套是交易所合格健康计划（QHP）：《平价医疗法案》第 1311 条（42 U.S.C. § 18031(c)(1)(B)）要求计划保证参保人能获得足够选择，实施细则在 45 C.F.R. § 156.230——计划须维持一张满足可及标准的网络，量化口径近年以时间与距离标准为骨架由联邦与各州分工把关，基本社区提供方（essential community providers）另有第 156.235 条一带的纳入要求。第二套是 Medicare Advantage：42 C.F.R. § 422.116 是核心条文，按专科与县类型定下时间与距离上限，并以受益人覆盖面测评——核心测法是每个县内至少百分之九十的受益人住在标准之内，算例先复算两遍：某县 1,000 名受益人，1,000 乘以百分之九十等于 900，至少 900 人达标才算过这一格；同条的 (d) 款一带近年又叠上预约等待时间标准（行为健康与初级保健等类别各有工作日上限口径，以当期原文为准）。第三套是 Medicaid 管理式照护：42 C.F.R. § 438.68 要求各州自定时间与距离标准并测评计划，目录义务在第 438.10(h) 条一带。三套尺共同点是测评对象都是计划：不合格的后果落在计划的认证、参展与整改上，不直接变成患者个人的赔付请求权——患者要把网络问题转成个人抓手，走的是下一节的目录与申诉通道。

### English
The federal layer has no single ruler; three markets run three versions. The first is the marketplace qualified health plan (QHP): section 1311 of the Affordable Care Act (42 U.S.C. section 18031(c)(1)(B)) requires plans to ensure sufficient choice of providers, with the operative rule at 45 C.F.R. section 156.230 — a plan must maintain a network meeting access standards, measured in recent years on a time-and-distance skeleton shared between federal and state oversight, while essential community providers carry their own inclusion requirements around section 156.235. The second is Medicare Advantage: 42 C.F.R. section 422.116 is the core provision, setting maximum time and distance by specialty and county type and testing the plan against beneficiary coverage — the core test is that at least 90 percent of beneficiaries in each county live inside the standard, and the arithmetic, checked twice, is that in a county of 1,000 beneficiaries, 1,000 times 90 percent equals 900, so at least 900 people must be inside the standard for that cell to pass; paragraph (d) of the same section has in recent years added appointment wait-time standards, with business-day ceilings by category such as behavioral health and primary care, all as of the current text. The third is Medicaid managed care: 42 C.F.R. section 438.68 requires states to set their own time-and-distance standards and test plans against them, with directory duties around section 438.10(h). The common thread is that all three rulers measure the plan: failure lands on the plan's certification, participation, and corrective action, and does not directly become an individual payment claim — a patient turns a network problem into a personal lever through the directory and grievance channels of the next section.

### Español (初译待校)
La capa federal no tiene una regla única; tres mercados corren tres versiones. La primera es el plan calificado del mercado (QHP): el artículo 1311 de la ACA (42 U.S.C. § 18031(c)(1)(B)) exige que el plan garantice elección suficiente de proveedores, con la regla operativa en 45 C.F.R. § 156.230: el plan debe mantener una red que cumpla estándares de acceso, medidos en años recientes sobre un esqueleto de tiempo y distancia compartido entre supervisión federal y estatal, mientras los proveedores comunitarios esenciales llevan sus propios requisitos de inclusión en torno al § 156.235. La segunda es Medicare Advantage: 42 C.F.R. § 422.116 es la disposición central, fija tiempo y distancia máximos por especialidad y tipo de condado y evalúa al plan contra la cobertura de beneficiarios; la prueba central es que al menos el 90 por ciento de los beneficiarios de cada condado viva dentro del estándar, y la aritmética, verificada dos veces, es que en un condado de 1,000 beneficiarios, 1,000 por 90 por ciento igual a 900, así que al menos 900 personas deben estar dentro del estándar para que esa casilla pase; el apartado (d) de la misma sección ha añadido en años recientes estándares de espera para citas, con techos en días laborables por categoría como salud conductual y atención primaria, todo según el texto vigente. El hilo común es que las tres reglas miden al plan: el incumplimiento cae sobre la certificación, la participación y la acción correctiva del plan, y no se convierte directamente en un reclamo de pago individual; el paciente convierte un problema de red en palanca personal por los canales de directorio y queja de la sección siguiente. ## 三、目录核验与信赖保护：《无意外账单法》的四件套

### 中文
对付幽灵网络，联邦层最有牙齿的是《无意外账单法》（No Surprises Act）加进《公共健康服务法》的目录条款，条文在 42 U.S.C. § 300gg-115 一带，给团体与个人健康计划压上四件套义务，逐项数得 4：一是核验——至少每 90 天核验一次目录信息的准确性，算例 90 乘以 4 等于 360，约合一年至少四轮；二是更新——收到需要更新的信息后 2 个工作日内改目录；三是回应——参保人问某位医生是否在网内，计划须在 1 个工作日内书面答复，并把答复留存 2 年；四是除名——对核验时不回应的医生，计划须从目录里移除或按规则处理，不能任由错误条目继续挂着。压轴的是信赖保护：参保人是照着错误目录去就医的——目录说在网内、实际不在——由此接受的服务，自付按网内标准算，并计入网内的免赔额与年度自付上限，不能因为目录是计划自己写错的，就把网外账单压回患者头上。这条保护的前提是留痕：就医前目录长什么样、什么时候查的、计划怎么答复的，都要有据可查，所以截图带日期、答复留书面，是本格最实用的动作。

### English
Against ghost networks, the sharpest federal teeth are the directory provisions the No Surprises Act wrote into the Public Health Service Act, around 42 U.S.C. section 300gg-115, which lay a four-part duty on group and individual health plans, counted one by one. One, verification: verify the accuracy of directory information not less frequently than once every 90 days — 90 times 4 equals 360, roughly four rounds a year. Two, updating: update the directory within 2 business days of receiving information that requires an update. Three, response: when an enrollee asks whether a provider is in network, the plan must answer in writing within 1 business day and retain the response for 2 years. Four, removal: a provider who does not respond to verification must be removed from the directory or handled under the rule, rather than left hanging as a wrong entry. The capstone is reliance protection: where an enrollee got care in reliance on a wrong directory — listed as in network, actually out of network — cost sharing for that care is capped at the in-network level and counts toward the in-network deductible and out-of-pocket maximum, because the directory error was the plan's own writing and may not be pushed back onto the patient as an out-of-network bill. The protection runs on evidence: what the directory showed before the visit, when it was checked, and what the plan answered must all be provable, so dated screenshots and written answers are the most practical moves in this box.

## 五、患者动作：先留痕、再纠错、后申诉的五步

### 中文
把法条落到个人动作上，按五步走。第一步，选医生时先核验：别只看目录页面，打诊所电话确认三件事——是否收这个具体计划、是否收新病人、最早能约到哪天，记下日期、接电话人的名字与答复。第二步，对关键医生向计划书面发问：用计划的官方渠道问其是否在网内，计划按联邦口径应在 1 个工作日内书面答复，把问答连同带日期的目录截图一起存档——这是日后信赖保护的证据底子。第三步，发现目录错误立刻报纠错：告诉计划哪条错了（人已离职、地址不对、不收新病人），计划收到更新信息后有 2 个工作日的更新义务；同时自己换一位已核验在网的医生继续就医，别在错误条目上耗时间。第四步，网络实在约不到人时走申诉：向计划提交 grievance，写清已打过的电话、等待天数与病情需要，请求安排网外医生按网内自付、或给衔接照护；计划拒绝的，再向 DMHC 求助中心（或 CDI 管的产品向 CDI）投诉，把申诉记录一并附上。第五步，已按错误目录就医并收到网外账单的，先别全额付：援引信赖保护说明就医时目录显示在网内、附上截图与计划答复，要求按网内标准重算并计入网内免赔与自付上限，同时对账单走本仓账单争议篇的对账顺序。五步的共同点是全部留痕：幽灵网络案件输赢多半不在法条，而在谁拿得出当时的目录长什么样。

### English
Turning the law into personal moves takes five steps. Step one, verify before choosing: do not stop at the directory page — call the clinic and confirm three things, whether it takes this specific plan, whether it is accepting new patients, and the earliest available appointment, noting the date, the name of whoever answered, and the answer. Step two, ask the plan in writing about key providers: use the plan's official channel to ask whether the provider is in network; under the federal rule the plan should answer in writing within 1 business day, and the exchange plus a dated directory screenshot goes into the file — this is the evidence base for later reliance protection. Step three, report directory errors the moment they surface: tell the plan which entry is wrong (provider gone, address wrong, closed to new patients), since the plan owes an update within 2 business days of receiving updating information, and meanwhile switch to a verified in-network provider and keep getting care rather than burning time on a bad entry. Step four, when the network genuinely has nobody available, grieve: file a grievance with the plan listing the calls made, the days waited, and the clinical need, asking for an out-of-network arrangement at in-network cost sharing or for continuity of care; if the plan refuses, complain to the DMHC Help Center (or to the CDI for CDI-regulated products) with the grievance record attached. Step five, if care was already received on a wrong directory and an out-of-network bill has arrived, do not pay it in full first: invoke reliance protection, explain that the directory showed in-network at the time of care, attach the screenshot and the plan's answer, and ask for recalculation at in-network terms counting toward the in-network deductible and out-of-pocket maximum, while running the bill through this repository's billing-dispute sequence. All five steps share one trait — everything is documented: ghost-network cases are usually won or lost not on the statute but on who can produce what the directory looked like at the time.

Bước hai, hỏi chương trình bằng văn bản về các nhà cung cấp then chốt: dùng kênh chính thức của chương trình để hỏi nhà cung cấp có trong mạng lưới không; theo quy tắc liên bang chương trình phải trả lời bằng văn bản trong 1 ngày làm việc, và trao đổi đó cộng ảnh chụp danh mục có ngày tháng đi vào hồ sơ — đây là nền bằng chứng cho bảo vệ tin cậy về sau. Bước ba, báo lỗi danh mục ngay khi phát hiện: nói với chương trình mục nào sai (nhà cung cấp đã đi, địa chỉ sai, đóng cửa với bệnh nhân mới), vì chương trình nợ việc cập nhật trong 2 ngày làm việc kể từ khi nhận thông tin cập nhật, và trong lúc đó hãy chuyển sang một nhà cung cấp trong mạng lưới đã xác minh và tiếp tục chăm sóc thay vì đốt thời gian vào một mục sai. Bước bốn, khi mạng lưới thật sự không có ai trống, hãy khiếu nại: nộp grievance lên chương trình liệt kê các cuộc gọi đã thực hiện, số ngày đã chờ và nhu cầu lâm sàng, yêu cầu sắp xếp ngoài mạng lưới với chia sẻ chi phí trong mạng lưới hoặc yêu cầu liên tục chăm sóc; nếu chương trình từ chối, khiếu nại lên Trung tâm Trợ giúp của DMHC (hoặc CDI đối với sản phẩm do CDI quản lý) kèm hồ sơ khiếu nại. Bước năm, nếu đã nhận chăm sóc theo danh mục sai và hóa đơn ngoài mạng lưới đã đến, đừng trả toàn bộ trước: viện dẫn bảo vệ tin cậy, giải thích rằng danh mục cho thấy trong mạng lưới tại thời điểm chăm sóc, đính kèm ảnh chụp và câu trả lời của chương trình, và yêu cầu tính lại theo điều kiện trong mạng lưới, tính vào khấu trừ trong mạng lưới và trần tự trả, đồng thời chạy hóa đơn qua trình tự tranh chấp hóa đơn của kho này. Cả năm bước có một điểm chung: mọi thứ đều được ghi lại — các vụ mạng ma thường thắng hay thua không ở điều luật mà ở ai xuất trình được danh mục trông thế nào vào thời điểm đó. (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)
कानून को व्यक्तिगत कदमों में बदलना पाँच चरणों का है। चरण एक, चुनने से पहले सत्यापित करें: directory page पर न रुकें — clinic को call करके तीन बातें पुष्ट करें, क्या वह यह विशिष्ट plan लेता है, क्या वह नए मरीज़ ले रहा है, और सबसे पहला उपलब्ध appointment कब है, तारीख, उत्तर देने वाले का नाम और उत्तर नोट करते हुए। चरण दो, key providers के बारे में plan से लिखित में पूछें: plan के official channel से पूछें कि provider in-network है या नहीं; federal rule के तहत plan को 1 business day के भीतर लिखित उत्तर देना चाहिए, और वह आदान-प्रदान तथा दिनांक वाली directory screenshot file में जाती है — यह बाद की reliance protection का साक्ष्य आधार है। चरण तीन, directory की त्रुटियाँ सामने आते ही report करें: plan को बताएँ कि कौन सी entry ग़लत है (provider चला गया, पता ग़लत, नए मरीज़ों के लिए बंद), क्योंकि update वाली जानकारी मिलने के 2 business days के भीतर plan को update करना होता है, और इस बीच किसी verified in-network provider पर जाकर care जारी रखें, ख़राब entry पर समय न जलाएँ। चरण चार, जब network में सचमुच कोई उपलब्ध न हो, तो grievance करें: plan के पास grievance दाख़िल करें जिसमें की गई calls, प्रतीक्षा के दिन और clinical need लिखे हों, out-of-network arrangement in-network cost sharing पर या continuity of care माँगते हुए; plan मना करे तो grievance record संलग्न करके DMHC Help Center (या CDI-regulated products के लिए CDI) में शिकायत करें। चरण पाँच, यदि ग़लत directory पर care पहले ही ले लिया गया है और out-of-network bill आ गया है, तो पहले पूरा भुगतान न करें: reliance protection का हवाला दें, समझाएँ कि care के समय directory in-network दिखा रहा था, screenshot और plan का उत्तर संलग्न करें, और in-network शर्तों पर पुनर्गणना की माँग करें जो in-network deductible और out-of-pocket maximum में गिनी जाए, साथ ही bill को इस repository की billing-dispute sequence से गुज़ारें। पाँचों चरणों में एक बात साझा है: सब कुछ documented है — ghost-network मामले आमतौर पर statute पर नहीं, इस पर जीते या हारे जाते हैं कि उस समय directory कैसा दिखता था, यह कौन प्रस्तुत कर सकता है। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)
