# 选择性病原体与毒素怎么管：42 CFR Part 73 联邦登记、安全计划与加州规则

> 个人学习笔记，不构成医疗或法律建议。条文以 42 CFR Part 73 现行文本与 Federal Select Agent Program（FSAP）官方指引为准。全文不写药物剂量。

## 一、这套规则管什么：名单制，三部法规一个计划

### 中文
美国对最危险的一批病原体和毒素，不走普通实验室那套宽松管理，而是名单制：先列名单，再围着名单建登记、安全与报告义务。法律授权是《公共卫生服务法》第 351A 条，即 42 U.S.C. §262a。卫生与公众服务部（HHS）这一块的实施条例是 42 CFR Part 73：第 73.3 条列 HHS 选择性病原体与毒素（HHS select agents and toxins）名单，第 73.4 条管同时落在 HHS 与农业部名单上的重叠（overlap）病原体与毒素，名单里风险最高的一档另标 Tier 1，要求更严。农业部那侧另有两部平行法规：动植物卫生检验局（APHIS）管动物与重叠病原体的 9 CFR Part 121、管植物病原体的 7 CFR Part 331。三部法规合起来是 3 个 CFR 分部，由联邦选择性病原体计划（Federal Select Agent Program，FSAP）统一运行：HHS 侧由疾病控制与预防中心（CDC）的监管科学与合规部门管，农业部侧由 APHIS 管。一般医院检验科平时不碰这套登记，但一旦在标本里鉴定出名单上的病原体，报告义务立刻落到自己头上，见第三节末尾。

### English
For the most dangerous set of pathogens and toxins, the United States does not use ordinary laboratory oversight; it uses a list-based regime: publish the list first, then build registration, security, and reporting duties around it. The statutory authority is section 351A of the Public Health Service Act, 42 U.S.C. §262a. The HHS implementing regulation is 42 CFR Part 73: §73.3 lists the HHS select agents and toxins, §73.4 covers overlap agents and toxins appearing on both the HHS and USDA lists, and the highest-risk subset on the list is designated Tier 1 and held to stricter requirements. USDA runs two parallel regulations: 9 CFR Part 121 through APHIS for animal and overlap agents, and 7 CFR Part 331 for plant pathogens. Together that is three CFR parts, run as one Federal Select Agent Program (FSAP): CDC's regulatory science and compliance division on the HHS side and APHIS on the USDA side. A routine hospital laboratory normally never touches this registration system — but the moment it identifies a listed agent in a specimen, reporting duties land on it immediately, as the end of section three explains.

### Español
Para el conjunto más peligroso de patógenos y toxinas, Estados Unidos no usa la supervisión ordinaria de laboratorio, sino un régimen basado en listas: primero se publica la lista y luego se construyen sobre ella las obligaciones de registro, seguridad y notificación. La autoridad legal es la sección 351A de la Public Health Service Act, 42 U.S.C. §262a. El reglamento de implementación del HHS es 42 CFR Part 73: la sección 73.3 enumera los agentes y toxinas selectos del HHS (HHS select agents and toxins), la 73.4 cubre los agentes de solapamiento (overlap) que figuran en las listas del HHS y del USDA, y el subconjunto de mayor riesgo se designa Tier 1, con requisitos más estrictos. El USDA corre dos reglamentos paralelos: 9 CFR Part 121, vía APHIS, para agentes animales y de solapamiento, y 7 CFR Part 331 para patógenos de plantas. En total son tres partes del CFR, operadas como un solo programa, el Federal Select Agent Program (FSAP): la división de ciencia regulatoria y cumplimiento del CDC por el lado del HHS y APHIS por el del USDA. Un laboratorio hospitalario común normalmente no toca este sistema de registro, pero en el momento en que identifica en una muestra un agente de la lista, las obligaciones de notificación caen sobre él de inmediato, como explica el final de la tercera sección.

## 二、先登记才能碰：注册证、责任官员与安全风险评估

### 中文
这套体系的第一原则是先登记、后持有。42 CFR §73.7 写明：除豁免情形外，任何个人或实体没有 HHS 部长签发的注册证（certificate of registration），不得持有、使用或转移 HHS 选择性病原体与毒素；重叠病原体还要连农业部一侧的登记一并满足。申请走 APHIS／CDC Form 1，只含 HHS 病原体的交 CDC，含重叠或农业部病原体的交 CDC 或 APHIS 一家、不能两家重复交。注册证最长有效 3 年，换算就是 36 个月，到期前续证的账要提前排。登记不是一张纸：实体须指定一名责任官员（Responsible Official，§73.9）对合规负总责，通常再配候补责任官员；实体本身、责任官员与每一个能接触病原体的人，都要先过司法部长一侧的安全风险评估（security risk assessment，§73.10，含指纹核查），评估不过，接触资格就没有。实体还要分别落三份计划并真正执行：安保计划（§73.11，防未授权接触、盗窃与丢失）、生物安全计划（§73.12，按病原体风险与实验操作定防护）、事件响应计划（出事、库存对不上、自然灾害时怎么办），再加人员培训与完整记录。检查可以是发证前置条件：注册证的签发可以以现场检查或补交计划文件为条件，登记后同样有例行与突击检查。登记被拒、被吊销或被暂停的处置在 §73.8，申诉与行政复核在 §73.20。

### English
The system's first principle is registration before possession. Under 42 CFR §73.7, unless an exemption applies, no individual or entity may possess, use, or transfer an HHS select agent or toxin without a certificate of registration issued by the HHS Secretary; overlap agents must additionally satisfy the USDA side. The application runs on APHIS/CDC Form 1 — filed with CDC when the registration covers only HHS agents, and with either CDC or APHIS, but not both, when it covers overlap or USDA agents. A certificate is valid for a maximum of three years — 36 months — so the renewal calendar has to be planned ahead. Registration is more than paper: the entity must designate a Responsible Official (§73.9) accountable for compliance, usually with an Alternate Responsible Official; the entity itself, the Responsible Official, and every individual who will access the agents must first pass a security risk assessment (§73.10, including fingerprint checks) run through the Attorney General, and access does not exist until that assessment clears. The entity must also actually implement three plans: a security plan (§73.11, against unauthorized access, theft, and loss), a biosafety plan (§73.12, matched to the agents and procedures), and an incident response plan covering theft, loss, release, inventory discrepancies, and natural disasters — plus training and complete records. Issuance may be conditioned on an inspection or on submitting the plans, and inspections continue after registration, both routine and unannounced. Denial, revocation, or suspension of registration sits in §73.8, and administrative review in §73.20.

### Español
El primer principio del sistema es registrarse antes de poseer. Según 42 CFR §73.7, salvo exención, ninguna persona o entidad puede poseer, usar o transferir un agente o toxina selecto del HHS sin un certificado de registro (certificate of registration) emitido por el Secretario del HHS; los agentes de solapamiento deben satisfacer además el lado del USDA. La solicitud corre con el APHIS/CDC Form 1: se presenta ante el CDC cuando el registro cubre solo agentes del HHS, y ante el CDC o APHIS —pero no ante ambos— cuando cubre agentes de solapamiento o del USDA. El certificado vale como máximo tres años, es decir, 36 meses, así que la renovación se planifica con antelación. El registro es más que papel: la entidad debe designar un funcionario responsable (Responsible Official, §73.9) que responde por el cumplimiento, normalmente con un suplente; la propia entidad, el funcionario responsable y cada persona que tendrá acceso a los agentes deben pasar antes una evaluación de riesgo de seguridad (security risk assessment, §73.10, con verificación de huellas) tramitada por el Fiscal General, y sin esa aprobación no hay acceso. La entidad debe además implementar de verdad tres planes: un plan de seguridad (security plan, §73.11, contra acceso no autorizado, robo y pérdida), un plan de bioseguridad (§73.12, ajustado a los agentes y procedimientos) y un plan de respuesta a incidentes que cubra robo, pérdida, liberación, discrepancias de inventario y desastres naturales, más capacitación y registros completos. La emisión puede condicionarse a una inspección o a la entrega de los planes, y las inspecciones continúan tras el registro, rutinarias y sin aviso. La denegación, revocación o suspensión del registro está en §73.8 y la revisión administrativa en §73.20.

### 한국어 (초역 · 初译待校)
이 체제의 첫 원칙은 보유 전에 등록하는 것입니다. 42 CFR §73.7에 따르면, 면제 경우가 아니면 어떤 개인이나 기관도 HHS 장관이 발급한 등록증(certificate of registration) 없이는 HHS 선택 병원체와 독소를 보유·사용·이전할 수 없습니다. 중복 병원체는 농무부 쪽 등록까지 함께 충족해야 합니다. 신청은 APHIS/CDC Form 1로 하며, HHS 병원체만 포함하면 CDC에, 중복이나 농무부 병원체를 포함하면 CDC나 APHIS 중 한 곳에만 내야 합니다(양쪽 중복 제출 금지). 등록증 유효기간은 최장 3년, 즉 36개월이므로 갱신 일정을 미리 잡아야 합니다. 등록은 서류 한 장이 아닙니다. 기관은 준수를 총괄하는 책임 담당자(Responsible Official, §73.9)를 지정해야 하고 보통 대리 담당자도 둡니다. 기관 자체, 책임 담당자, 병원체에 접근할 모든 개인은 먼저 법무장관 측이 주관하는 보안 위험 평가(security risk assessment, §73.10, 지문 확인 포함)를 통과해야 하며, 평가를 통과하기 전에는 접근 자격이 생기지 않습니다. 기관은 세 가지 계획도 실제로 이행해야 합니다. 무단 접근·도난·분실을 막는 보안 계획(§73.11), 병원체와 실험 절차에 맞춘 생물안전 계획(§73.12), 도난·분실·유출·재고 불일치·자연재해에 대응하는 사건 대응 계획이 그것이며, 교육과 완전한 기록이 따라붙습니다. 등록증 발급은 현장 검사나 계획 파일 제출을 조건으로 할 수 있고, 등록 뒤에도 정기 검사와 불시 검사가 이어집니다. 등록 거부·취소·정지는 §73.8에, 행정 재심은 §73.20에 있습니다. (초역·待校)

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)
Nguyên tắc đầu tiên của hệ thống này là đăng ký trước khi nắm giữ. Theo 42 CFR §73.7, trừ trường hợp được miễn, không cá nhân hay tổ chức nào được nắm giữ, sử dụng hay chuyển giao tác nhân hoặc độc tố chọn lọc của HHS nếu không có giấy chứng nhận đăng ký (certificate of registration) do Bộ trưởng HHS cấp; tác nhân trùng lặp còn phải thỏa mãn cả phía USDA. Đơn đăng ký dùng APHIS/CDC Form 1: nộp cho CDC khi đăng ký chỉ gồm tác nhân HHS, và nộp cho CDC hoặc APHIS — không nộp cả hai — khi gồm tác nhân trùng lặp hoặc của USDA. Giấy chứng nhận có hiệu lực tối đa ba năm, tức 36 tháng, nên lịch gia hạn phải được tính trước. Đăng ký không chỉ là một tờ giấy: tổ chức phải chỉ định một cán bộ chịu trách nhiệm (Responsible Official, §73.9) chịu trách nhiệm tổng thể về tuân thủ, thường kèm một cán bộ dự khuyết; bản thân tổ chức, cán bộ chịu trách nhiệm và mọi cá nhân sẽ tiếp cận tác nhân đều phải vượt qua thẩm định rủi ro an ninh (security risk assessment, §73.10, gồm kiểm tra vân tay) do phía Bộ trưởng Tư pháp thực hiện, và chưa qua thẩm định thì chưa có quyền tiếp cận. Tổ chức còn phải thực thi thật ba kế hoạch: kế hoạch an ninh (§73.11, chống tiếp cận trái phép, trộm cắp và thất lạc), kế hoạch an toàn sinh học (§73.12, theo rủi ro của tác nhân và thao tác thí nghiệm), và kế hoạch ứng phó sự cố bao quát trộm cắp, thất lạc, phát tán, chênh lệch tồn kho và thiên tai, cộng với đào tạo và hồ sơ đầy đủ. Việc cấp chứng nhận có thể đặt điều kiện kiểm tra hiện trường hoặc nộp bổ sung các kế hoạch, và kiểm tra vẫn tiếp tục sau đăng ký, cả định kỳ lẫn đột xuất. Việc từ chối, thu hồi hoặc đình chỉ đăng ký nằm ở §73.8, và xét lại hành chính ở §73.20. (bản dịch sơ bộ·待校)

## 三、转移要事先批，受限实验另有闸，普通检验科靠豁免衔接

### 中文
登记之后也不是想送谁就送谁。§73.16 管转移：选择性病原体与毒素原则上只能转给同样持有覆盖该病原体的注册证、或符合豁免条件的接收方，且转移前要经 CDC 或 APHIS 授权，走 APHIS／CDC Form 2；授权只有 30 个日历日有效，过期要重办。同一注册证覆盖范围内的内部流转不算这条所说的转移。§73.13 另设受限实验（restricted experiments）的闸：涉及增强毒力、改变宿主范围等特定高风险实验，未经 HHS 批准不得开展，这一条是名单管理之外对实验本身的第二道闸。豁免分两处写：§73.5 是 HHS 病原体的豁免，§73.6 是重叠病原体的豁免，其中与普通人最相关的是临床与诊断实验室：检验科在诊断、验证或能力测试中鉴定出名单病原体，本身不因此被要求先办注册证，但豁免有条件——要把病原体妥善封存防盗失与释放、按规定用 APHIS／CDC Form 4 报告鉴定结果与最终去向（销毁或转移给有资格的一方），Tier 1 病原体的鉴定还要立即通知。把时间账摆在一起看更清楚：转移授权 30 天、盗失报告的正式表 7 天（见第四节），30 减 7 等于 23 天，两条时钟完全不是一回事，办转移的人别拿报告期限去套授权期限。

### English
Registration does not mean material can be sent to anyone. Section 73.16 governs transfers: a select agent or toxin may in principle go only to a recipient holding a registration covering that agent, or meeting an exemption, and the transfer must be authorized by CDC or APHIS beforehand on APHIS/CDC Form 2; an authorization is valid for only 30 calendar days, after which it must be obtained again. Movement within the same certificate of registration is not a transfer under this section. Section 73.13 adds a separate gate for restricted experiments: specified high-risk work, such as experiments that enhance virulence or alter host range, may not proceed without HHS approval — a second gate aimed at the experiment itself, beyond list management. Exemptions are written in two places, §73.5 for HHS agents and §73.6 for overlap agents, and the one that matters most to ordinary readers is the clinical and diagnostic laboratory: a laboratory that identifies a listed agent during diagnosis, verification, or proficiency testing is not thereby required to register first, but the exemption is conditional — the material must be secured against theft, loss, and release, the identification and its final disposition (destruction or transfer to an eligible party) must be reported on APHIS/CDC Form 4, and identification of a Tier 1 agent requires immediate notification. Putting the clocks side by side helps: a transfer authorization runs 30 days while the formal theft/loss report runs 7 days (section four); 30 minus 7 equals 23 days, and the two clocks are entirely different things — never plan a transfer around the reporting deadline.

### Español
Estar registrado no significa poder enviar el material a cualquiera. La §73.16 regula las transferencias: un agente o toxina selecto, en principio, solo puede ir a un destinatario con registro que cubra ese agente o que cumpla una exención, y la transferencia debe autorizarla antes el CDC o APHIS con el APHIS/CDC Form 2; la autorización vale solo 30 días naturales y, vencida, hay que obtenerla de nuevo. Los movimientos dentro del mismo certificado de registro no cuentan como transferencia en esta sección. Las exenciones están en dos lugares: §73.5 para agentes del HHS y §73.6 para los de solapamiento, y la más relevante para el lector común es la del laboratorio clínico y de diagnóstico: el laboratorio que identifica un agente de la lista durante diagnóstico, verificación o pruebas de competencia no queda por ello obligado a registrarse antes, pero la exención es condicional — hay que asegurar el material contra robo, pérdida y liberación, notificar la identificación y su destino final (destrucción o transferencia a una parte elegible) con el APHIS/CDC Form 4, y la identificación de un agente Tier 1 exige notificación inmediata. Conviene juntar los relojes: la autorización de transferencia corre 30 días y el informe formal de robo o pérdida, 7 días (sección cuatro); 30 menos 7 igual a 23 días, y son relojes completamente distintos: no planifique una transferencia con el plazo del informe.

Việc di chuyển nội bộ trong cùng một giấy chứng nhận đăng ký không tính là chuyển giao theo mục này. ## 四、出事怎么报、罚到什么程度：立即通知加七天正式表

### 中文
这套体系对出事的报告要求只有两个词：立即、七天。§73.19 规定，发现选择性病原体或毒素被盗（未经授权被取走）或丢失（账上有、实物对不上），必须立即通知 CDC 或 APHIS 以及相应的联邦、州或地方执法机构，先用电话、传真或邮件报，信息要含病原体名称与特征、数量估计、发生时间地点与已报告的执法机构名单；随后 7 个日历日内提交填妥的 APHIS／CDC Form 3。哪怕后来找回来了、责任人也查清了，报告义务不消。释放（release）——造成职业暴露、或病原体跑到生物 containment 一级屏障之外——同样是立即通知加 7 天内 Form 3，而且这一条连未注册的实体也管：普通检验科发现自己这里发生了名单病原体的释放，照样要报。罚则分层：§73.21 是民事罚款（civil money penalties），§73.8 是登记层面的拒绝、吊销与暂停，刑事一侧另有 18 U.S.C. §175b 对受限人员持有选择性病原体的禁令与刑责。实务上最先踩雷的往往不是故意违规，而是库存对不上又拖着没报：这套规则把迟报本身当问题，发现对不上账的那一刻，时钟就已经开始走。

### English
For incidents, the regime runs on two words: immediately, and seven days. Section 73.19 requires that discovery of a theft (an unauthorized taking) or loss (inventory that cannot be accounted for) of a select agent or toxin be reported immediately to CDC or APHIS and to the appropriate federal, state, or local law enforcement agencies — first by telephone, facsimile, or email, with the agent's name and characterization, an estimate of quantity, the time and place, and the law-enforcement agencies notified — followed by a completed APHIS/CDC Form 3 within seven calendar days. Recovery of the material, or identifying who was responsible, does not erase the duty to report. A release — an occupational exposure, or an agent escaping outside the primary biocontainment barriers — carries the same immediate-plus-seven-days structure, and it binds even unregistered entities: an ordinary clinical laboratory that discovers a release of a listed agent on its own premises must report it all the same. Penalties layer up: §73.21 provides civil money penalties, §73.8 handles denial, revocation, and suspension on the registration side, and the criminal side includes 18 U.S.C. §175b, which bars restricted persons from possessing select agents. In practice the most common tripwire is not deliberate violation but an inventory discrepancy left unreported: this regime treats late reporting itself as a violation, and the clock starts the moment the discrepancy is discovered.

### Español
Para los incidentes, el régimen corre con dos palabras: de inmediato y siete días. La §73.19 exige que el descubrimiento de un robo (sustracción no autorizada) o una pérdida (inventario que no se puede contabilizar) de un agente o toxina selecto se notifique de inmediato al CDC o a APHIS y a las agencias policiales federales, estatales o locales correspondientes —primero por teléfono, fax o correo electrónico, con el nombre y la caracterización del agente, una estimación de la cantidad, el momento y el lugar, y la lista de agencias avisadas— seguido del APHIS/CDC Form 3 completo dentro de siete días naturales. Recuperar el material o identificar al responsable no borra el deber de notificar. Una liberación (release) —una exposición ocupacional o la salida del agente fuera de las barreras primarias de biocontención— sigue la misma estructura de inmediatez más siete días, y obliga incluso a entidades no registradas: un laboratorio clínico común que descubre una liberación de un agente de la lista en sus instalaciones debe notificarla igualmente. Las sanciones se escalonan: §73.21 prevé multas civiles (civil money penalties), §73.8 maneja la denegación, revocación y suspensión del registro, y el lado penal incluye 18 U.S.C. §175b, que prohíbe a las personas restringidas poseer agentes selectos. ### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)
कैलिफ़ोर्निया को अलग से दर्ज करें। पहला, कैलिफ़ोर्निया में चुनिंदा कारकों का कोई समानांतर राज्य पंजीकरण नहीं है जो संघीय पंजीकरण का स्थान ले सके: कैलिफ़ोर्निया में सूचीबद्ध कारक रखने या उपयोग करने वाली संस्था 42 CFR Part 73 के अनुसार FSAP में ही पंजीकृत होती है, सुरक्षा जोखिम आकलन पास करती है और तीनों योजनाएँ बनाए रखती है; राज्य कानून इसके बदले कोई दूसरा प्रमाणपत्र नहीं देता। दूसरा, कैलिफ़ोर्निया की संक्रामक-रोग रिपोर्टिंग लाइन एक अलग, स्वतंत्र दायित्व है: जब नैदानिक प्रयोगशालाएँ और स्वास्थ्य सुविधाएँ रिपोर्ट योग्य रोगों और रोगाणुओं की पहचान करती हैं, तो वे California Code of Regulations, title 17, §2500 और Health and Safety Code §120130 के आसपास की रिपोर्टिंग शर्तों के अनुसार स्थानीय स्वास्थ्य अधिकारी और California Department of Public Health (CDPH) को रिपोर्ट करती हैं; यह लाइन रोग निगरानी के लिए है, जो FSAP की धारण-सुरक्षा से अलग बात है, और एक ओर की रिपोर्ट दूसरी ओर को रद्द नहीं करती। तीसरा, कैलिफ़ोर्निया की नैदानिक प्रयोगशालाएँ CDPH के Laboratory Field Services पक्ष द्वारा संचालित राज्य प्रयोगशाला लाइसेंस और कार्मिक नियमों के अधीन भी हैं, इसलिए जब कोई प्रयोगशाला नमूने में सूचीबद्ध कारक की पहचान करती है, तो खंड तीन की संघीय छूट और Form 4 रिपोर्टिंग राज्य की रिपोर्ट-योग्य रोग लाइन के समानांतर चलती है। चौथा, नियोक्ता-पक्ष की प्रयोगशाला सुरक्षा पर Cal/OSHA मानक और संस्था की अपनी Institutional Biosafety Committee (IBC) समीक्षा और जुड़ती है: संघीय पंजीकरण, राज्य रिपोर्टिंग और संस्थागत समीक्षा प्रत्येक अलग खंड संभालता है, और कोई भी दूसरे की ओर से हस्ताक्षर नहीं कर सकता। पंजीकरण और अनुपालन के निर्णय FSAP, CDC और CDPH के वर्तमान आधिकारिक पृष्ठों से लेने चाहिए, द्वितीय-हस्त सारांश तालिकाओं से नहीं। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद·待校)
