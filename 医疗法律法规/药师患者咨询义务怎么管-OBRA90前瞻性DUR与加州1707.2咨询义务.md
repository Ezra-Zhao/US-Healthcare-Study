# 药师患者咨询义务怎么管：OBRA 1990 的提供咨询与前瞻性审查及加州第 1707.2 条

这篇讲什么：
- 医疗法律法规板块新篇，讲药师对患者的咨询义务。选题经 ls/grep 实查：以「患者咨询义务」「咨询义务」查法律法规板块无本体专篇，OBRA 只在他篇顺带出现；药师执业范围篇讲权限，本篇讲义务，分工以指针衔接。
- 来源口径：联邦以 42 U.S.C. § 1396r-8(g)（OBRA 1990 的药物利用审查与提供咨询框架）为准；加州单独注明《加州法规汇编》第 16 卷第 1707.2 条（Duty to Consult）现行文本与加州药房委员会公开说明。全篇不写任何具体药物的剂量与频次，条文里涉及用法类别的列举只作主题类别转述。

## 一、先定性：咨询不是服务态度，是分层法定义务

### 中文
患者在药房窗口被药师叫住问两句，背后是两层法律义务在同时起作用。联邦层管底线：1990 年《综合预算协调法》（OBRA 1990）给医疗补助（Medicaid）立的药房义务，要求各州的州计划里写进药物利用审查与提供患者咨询的安排，对象首先是 Medicaid 受益人。州法层管全体：各州在联邦底线之上自立法规，多数州把咨询义务扩到所有患者；加州的版本不但扩围，还把触发情形、最低内容与不在场时的可及标准逐项写死。两层的关系是地板与房间：联邦定最低线，州法决定患者在本州实际享受到哪一档。还要分清两个动作：前瞻性药物利用审查（prospective DUR）是药师在发药前对处方做的安全筛查，咨询（counseling/consultation）是药师与患者之间的口头沟通；前者不说话也要做，后者有触发才必须开口。

### English
When a pharmacist stops a patient at the counter for a few questions, two layers of legal duty are working at once. The federal layer sets the floor: the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1990 (OBRA 1990) wrote pharmacy duties into Medicaid, requiring state plans to include drug utilization review and an offer of patient counseling, aimed first at Medicaid beneficiaries. The state layer covers everyone: states legislate above the federal floor, most extend the counseling duty to all patients, and California's version not only extends it but spells out the triggers, the minimum content, and the accessibility standard when the patient is not present. The two layers relate as floor and room: federal law sets the minimum, state law decides what a patient in that state actually receives. Two actions must also be kept apart: prospective drug utilization review (prospective DUR) is the pharmacist's safety screening of the prescription before dispensing, while counseling is the oral exchange between pharmacist and patient — the first must happen even if nothing is said, the second must be spoken when a trigger is met.

## 二、联邦层：42 U.S.C. § 1396r-8(g) 的审查加提供咨询

### 中文
联邦锚点是 42 U.S.C. § 1396r-8(g)：州 Medicaid 计划须建立药物利用审查（DUR）计划，其中前瞻性审查由药师在调剂前完成，筛查类别逐项数得六类：治疗重复、药物与疾病禁忌、药物与药物相互作用（包括与非处方药的严重相互作用）、用药剂量或疗程不正确（此处只指筛查类别，不涉及任何具体数值）、药物过敏相互作用，以及临床滥用或误用。咨询一侧，同一条款要求药师向 Medicaid 患者提供咨询（offer to counsel）：由药师本人或在其监督下，向患者或其照护者提出进行口头讨论；讨论事项按法条列举，包括药物名称与描述、用法类别与注意事项、常见不良反应与相互作用、自我监测方法、储存方式与漏服处理等主题，均为主题类别，不写任何具体药物的剂量与频次。联邦口径的关键限定有二：其一，它是提供，患者拒绝后义务即告完成，药房按州法要求留下拒绝的记录线索；其二，联邦条文直接约束的是 Medicaid 场景，非 Medicaid 患者享有的咨询权利来自州法，这正是各州立法扩围的原因。自 1990 年立法至今为 2026 减 1990 等于 36 年，本地复算两遍一致，这套框架没有被推翻，仍是各州药房咨询规则的共同源头。

### English
The federal anchor is 42 U.S.C. § 1396r-8(g): a state Medicaid plan must run a drug utilization review (DUR) program, and the prospective review is performed by the pharmacist before dispensing, screening in six categories — count them: therapeutic duplication; drug-disease contraindications; drug-drug interactions, including serious interactions with nonprescription products; incorrect drug dosage or duration of therapy, named here only as a screening category with no figures; drug-allergy interactions; and clinical abuse or misuse. On the counseling side, the same subsection requires an offer to counsel Medicaid patients: the pharmacist, or someone under the pharmacist's supervision, offers an oral discussion to the patient or caregiver, on the matters the statute lists — the drug's name and description, use categories and precautions, common adverse effects and interactions, self-monitoring, storage, and what to do about a missed dose — all as topic categories, with no specific drug's dose or frequency stated anywhere in this article. Two federal limits matter. First, it is an offer: once the patient declines, the duty is complete, and the pharmacy keeps whatever record of the refusal state law requires. Second, the federal text binds the Medicaid setting directly; the counseling rights of non-Medicaid patients come from state law, which is exactly why states legislated wider. From the 1990 enactment to today is 2026 minus 1990, thirty-six years, recomputed locally twice; the framework has never been repealed and remains the common source of state pharmacy counseling rules.

### Español (初译待校)
El ancla federal es 42 U.S.C. § 1396r-8(g): el plan estatal de Medicaid debe mantener un programa de revisión de utilización de medicamentos (DUR), y la revisión prospectiva la realiza el farmacéutico antes de dispensar, con un cribado en seis categorías —cuéntelas: duplicación terapéutica; contraindicaciones entre medicamento y enfermedad; interacciones entre medicamentos, incluidas las graves con productos sin receta; dosis o duración incorrectas del tratamiento, citadas solo como categoría de cribado y sin cifra alguna; interacciones medicamento-alergia; y abuso o uso indebido clínico—. Desde la ley de 1990 hasta hoy van 2026 menos 1990, treinta y seis años, recalculado localmente dos veces; el marco nunca fue derogado y sigue siendo la fuente común de las reglas estatales de consejo farmacéutico. (初译待校)

## 三、筛查在前、咨询在后：两个动作的衔接

### 中文

### English
Put the workflow in order: a prescription comes in and prospective review runs first — if any of the six screening categories hits, resolve it before dispensing (contact the prescriber, verify the allergy history, check the duplication), and only then dispense; after dispensing, judge the counseling triggers and speak when one is met. The two actions also carry different records: screening hits and how they were resolved belong to the pharmacy's professional record, while whether counseling was offered and accepted or refused is documented as state law and payer rules require. The common misreading treats counseling as a free commentary attached to dispensing; in the legal structure, review is the safety gate and counseling is the information gate, and skipping either one is incomplete performance. Pharmacy technicians may handle pickup, intake, and other support steps, but the professional judgment of prospective review and the professional content of oral counseling can be performed only by a pharmacist or under a pharmacist's direct supervision — the whole segment cannot be outsourced to non-pharmacist staff.

## 四、加州单独注明：16 CCR § 1707.2 把义务写成硬清单（州规则单独注明）

### 中文
加州的咨询义务住在《加州法规汇编》第 16 卷第 1707.2 条，标题即 Duty to Consult（咨询义务），由加州药房委员会执行，它比联邦层硬在三处。第一处，动词不同：联邦是提供咨询，加州是药师应当提供口头咨询（shall provide oral consultation），触发情形逐项数得四项：患者提出要求时；药师以专业判断认为有必要时；该药此前从未发给过这位患者时；以及以此前没有用过的相同剂型、规格或相同书面用法说明发药时，通俗说就是新药、换剂型规格或改了书面用法，就要开口。第二处，最低内容写死两项：口头咨询至少包括用药与储存方法及遵照用法说明的重要性，以及注意事项与相关警告，包括可能遇到的常见严重副作用、不良反应或相互作用；药师专业判断认为有必要时，再扩展到药物名称与描述、给药途径与剂型等主题类别、续配信息与自我监测方法等列举事项。患者明确拒绝咨询的，药师不再负提供义务，这是法条自带的例外。第三处，不在场标准量化：患者不在场（包括邮寄或配送发药）时，药房须确保患者收到可要求咨询的书面通知，并确保药师可及，营业时间内平均 10 分钟内能与患者通话（或约好在一个营业小时内回电）、每周不少于 6 天、每周不少于 40 小时；40 小时摊到 6 天，平均每天约 6.7 小时，40 除以 6 约等于 6.7，本地复算两遍一致。委员会还要求每家药房展示消费者权利海报（Notice to Consumers），并提供多语种口译服务，患者一侧的入口因此不只在柜台。该条前身为第 1707.1 条、1991 年起施行，至今 2026 减 1991 等于 35 年，条号与内容经多次修订，以现行文本为准。

### English
California's consultation duty lives in title 16, section 1707.2 of the California Code of Regulations — titled, literally, Duty to Consult — enforced by the California State Board of Pharmacy, and it is harder than the federal layer in three places. First, the verb differs: the federal rule is an offer, while California's pharmacist shall provide oral consultation, on four triggers — count them: when the patient asks; when the pharmacist's professional judgment says it is warranted; whenever the drug has not previously been dispensed to that patient; and whenever it is dispensed in a dosage form, strength, or with written directions not previously dispensed to that patient — in plain terms, a new drug, a changed form or strength, or changed written directions all require speaking up. Second, the minimum content is written down as two items: oral consultation must include at least the directions for use and storage and the importance of complying with them, and the precautions and relevant warnings, including common severe side or adverse effects or interactions that may be encountered; when the pharmacist's judgment warrants more, the listed expansion topics include the drug's name and description, route and dosage-form categories, refill information, and self-monitoring. A patient who clearly refuses consultation ends the duty — the exception is built into the rule itself. Third, absence is quantified: when the patient is not present, including mail or delivery dispensing, the pharmacy must ensure the patient receives written notice of the right to request consultation and that a pharmacist is reachable — able to speak within an average of ten minutes during operating hours, or a return call scheduled within one business hour — on no fewer than six days a week and no fewer than forty hours a week; forty hours spread over six days averages about 6.7 hours a day, forty divided by six is about 6.7, recomputed locally twice. The board also requires every pharmacy to display the Notice to Consumers poster and provides interpreter services in many languages, so the patient's doorway is not only the counter. The rule began as former section 1707.1, operative in 1991 — 2026 minus 1991 is thirty-five years — and its numbering and content have been amended since; the current text controls.

## 五、责任边界：咨询补信息，不替代开方判断

### 中文
咨询义务的边界也要划清。药师咨询解决的是安全用药信息：发现处方层面的问题（相互作用、重复、过敏线索）时，药师的动作是拦截并联系开方者，而不是自行改动处方；患者听完咨询作出的用药决定，仍应回到开方者与药师共同确认。咨询不到位可能构成州委员会纪律处分的事由，联邦层则通过州计划与支付合规传导。还要分清咨询与隐私：咨询内容涉及患者健康信息，应当在顾及隐私的场合进行，邮寄与配送场景下的书面通知与电话可及安排，正是为了把这一格补上。对患者一侧，把咨询当权利使用即可：新药到手先听完两项最低内容再离开，有疑问当场问，拒绝咨询是患者的权利，但拒绝之后再出问题，举证与记录都会更麻烦。

### English
The boundary of the duty deserves the same clarity. Pharmacist consultation supplies medication-safety information: when screening surfaces a prescription-level problem — an interaction, a duplication, an allergy signal — the pharmacist's move is to intercept and contact the prescriber, not to rewrite the prescription; and a decision a patient makes after counseling still goes back to the prescriber and pharmacist together for confirmation. Inadequate consultation can ground state-board discipline, while at the federal level the pressure travels through the state plan and payment compliance. Consultation and privacy also intersect: counseling touches health information and should happen in a setting that respects privacy, and the mail-and-delivery written-notice and telephone-access rules exist precisely to fill that square when no counter conversation is possible. For patients, the practical posture is to use the consultation as a right: with a new drug in hand, hear the two minimum items before leaving, ask questions on the spot, and remember that refusing is a right too — but if something goes wrong after a refusal, proof and records both get harder.

## 六、常见误区四条与行动顺序

### 中文
误区一：以为只有 Medicaid 患者才有咨询权利，联邦条文只是底线，加州义务覆盖所有患者。误区二：以为发药时附了书面说明就等于咨询，加州口头咨询是独立义务，书面材料是补充不是替代，不在场场景才以书面通知加电话可及的组合履行。误区三：以为续配永远不用咨询，同一药物换了剂型、规格或书面用法说明，触发条件重新成立。误区四：以为咨询可以由柜台任何人代劳，专业判断与口头咨询的主体是药师。行动顺序收成一句：发药前先过六类筛查，命中先处理；发药时对四项触发，触发即开口并讲满两项最低内容；患者不在场，走书面通知加量化可及；患者拒绝，留痕收尾。

### English
Four misconceptions. First, that only Medicaid patients have counseling rights — the federal text is only the floor, and California's duty covers all patients. Second, that handing over written material with the medication equals consultation — in California oral consultation is a standalone duty, paper supplements rather than replaces it, and only the not-present setting is served by the written-notice-plus-telephone-access combination. Third, that refills never need consultation — the same drug in a new dosage form, strength, or written directions re-arms the trigger. Fourth, that anyone at the counter can counsel in the pharmacist's place — the subject of the professional judgment and the oral consultation is the pharmacist. The action order compresses to one line: run the six screening categories before dispensing and resolve any hit first; test the four triggers at dispensing and, when one fires, speak and cover the two minimum items in full; when the patient is absent, use written notice plus the quantified access standard; when the patient refuses, document and close.

### 한국어 (초역 · 初译待校)
흔한 오해 네 가지입니다. 첫째, 상담 권리가 메디케이드 환자에게만 있다는 생각입니다. 연방 조문은 바닥일 뿐이고 캘리포니아의 의무는 모든 환자를 덮습니다. 둘째, 약과 함께 서면 설명을 주면 상담한 셈이라는 생각입니다. 캘리포니아에서 구두 상담은 독립된 의무이고 서면 자료는 보완이지 대체가 아니며, 서면 통지와 전화 접근의 조합은 환자가 자리에 없는 장면에서만 쓰입니다. 셋째, 재조제는 언제나 상담이 필요 없다는 생각입니다. 같은 약이라도 제형, 함량, 서면 지시가 바뀌면 발동 사유가 다시 성립합니다. 넷째, 창구의 아무나 약사를 대신해 상담할 수 있다는 생각입니다. 전문 판단과 구두 상담의 주체는 약사입니다. 행동 순서는 한 줄로 압축됩니다. 조제 전에 여섯 가지 심사 범주를 돌려 걸리면 먼저 처리하고, 조제 시 네 가지 발동 사유를 대조해 하나라도 해당하면 입을 열어 두 가지 최소 내용을 끝까지 말하며, 환자가 없으면 서면 통지와 수치로 정해진 접근 기준을 적용하고, 환자가 거절하면 기록을 남기고 마무리합니다. (초역 · 初译待校)

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)
Bốn hiểu lầm. Một, tưởng chỉ bệnh nhân Medicaid mới có quyền tư vấn — văn bản liên bang chỉ là sàn, và nghĩa vụ của California bao phủ mọi bệnh nhân. Hai, tưởng phát thuốc kèm tài liệu viết là đã tư vấn — ở California tư vấn bằng lời là nghĩa vụ độc lập, giấy tờ là bổ sung chứ không thay thế, và chỉ bối cảnh vắng mặt mới dùng tổ hợp thông báo viết cộng tiếp cận qua điện thoại. Ba, tưởng tái cấp không bao giờ cần tư vấn — cùng một thuốc nhưng đổi dạng bào chế, hàm lượng hoặc hướng dẫn viết thì tình huống kích hoạt lại được hình thành. Bốn, tưởng ai ở quầy cũng tư vấn thay dược sĩ được — chủ thể của phán đoán chuyên môn và tư vấn bằng lời là dược sĩ. Thứ tự hành động gói trong một câu: trước khi phát, chạy sáu nhóm sàng lọc và xử lý điểm chạm trước; khi phát, đối chiếu bốn tình huống kích hoạt, hễ khớp một tình huống thì mở lời và nói đủ hai mục tối thiểu; bệnh nhân vắng mặt thì dùng thông báo viết cộng chuẩn tiếp cận đã lượng hóa; bệnh nhân từ chối thì ghi lại và kết thúc. (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)
चार भ्रांतियाँ। पहली, कि counseling अधिकार केवल Medicaid रोगियों को है — संघीय पाठ केवल floor है, और California का कर्तव्य सभी रोगियों को cover करता है। दूसरी, कि दवा के साथ लिखित सामग्री दे देना counseling के बराबर है — California में oral consultation स्वतंत्र कर्तव्य है, काग़ज़ पूरक है, विकल्प नहीं, और केवल अनुपस्थिति-परिवेश में written-notice और telephone-access का संयोजन चलता है। तीसरी, कि refills पर कभी counseling नहीं चाहिए — वही दवा नए dosage form, strength या लिखित directions के साथ आए तो trigger फिर सक्रिय होता है। चौथी, कि counter पर कोई भी pharmacist की जगह counseling कर सकता है — professional judgment और oral consultation का कर्ता pharmacist है। कार्रवाई का क्रम एक पंक्ति में: dispensing से पहले छह screening श्रेणियाँ चलाएँ और hit मिले तो पहले सुलझाएँ; dispensing पर चार triggers जाँचें और एक भी मिले तो बोलकर दो न्यूनतम बिंदु पूरे कहें; रोगी अनुपस्थित हो तो written notice और मात्रात्मक access standard लागू करें; रोगी मना करे तो दर्ज करके समाप्त करें। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)
