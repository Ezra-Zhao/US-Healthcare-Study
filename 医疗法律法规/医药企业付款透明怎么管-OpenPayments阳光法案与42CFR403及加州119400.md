# 医药企业付款透明怎么管：Open Payments 阳光法案、42 C.F.R. Part 403 与加州第 119400 条

> 医疗法律法规板块新篇（四板块轮转：上一轮病例分析＋常用就医流程，本轮回执照备考＋医疗法律法规各 1 篇）。选题经 ls/grep 实查：「Open Payments」「阳光法案」「Sunshine」grep 全仓均零命中、无本体专篇，与转诊利益冲突篇（反回扣法与 Stark 法管付款本身合不合法）、医疗保健欺诈篇（虚假申报法管骗保执法）、医疗广告篇（FTC 管宣称真不真）以指针分工——本篇只管一件事：医药企业的钱流向医务人员时，要怎样被记录、核对与公开。全篇不写任何药物剂量与频次。个人学习笔记，不构成法律或医疗建议。

## 一、先定性：阳光法案只晒不判，上了公开库不等于违法、没上报更不等于清白

### 中文
先把这部法的性子说清：它是一部透明法，不是禁付法。出处是《平价医疗法案》第 6002 条，即《医师付款阳光法案》（Physician Payments Sunshine Act），成文在《美国法典》第 42 编第 1320a-7h 条，执行规则在《联邦法规法典》第 42 编 Part 403 的 Subpart I（定义在第 403.902 条、报告义务在第 403.904 条一带），执行机关是联邦医疗保险与医疗补助服务中心（CMS），项目名叫 Open Payments（开放付款）。它的逻辑只有一条：药械企业给医务人员的钱与价值转移，金额过了线就要逐笔报给 CMS，再由 CMS 放进任何人都能按姓名检索的公开数据库。它不判断某一笔付款合不合法——一笔咨询费上了库，可能完全正当；一笔没上报的付款，也可能正踩在反回扣法的线上。合不合法归反回扣法与 Stark 法那两篇管，骗保执法归虚假申报法管，本篇只管晒这一层：谁要报、报给谁看、报什么、哪天报、报错了谁能改。读者用公开库时的第一条纪律也在这里：看到某位医生名下有企业付款，先看付款性质与背景再说话，把「被公开」读成「有问题」是这部法最常见的误读。来源：42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7h 与 42 C.F.R. Part 403 Subpart I 原文、CMS 的 Open Payments 项目公开说明。

### English
Start with the statute's character: this is a transparency law, not a payment ban. Its source is section 6002 of the Affordable Care Act, the Physician Payments Sunshine Act, codified at 42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7h, with implementing rules at 42 C.F.R. Part 403, Subpart I (definitions at § 403.902 and reporting duties around § 403.904), administered by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) as the Open Payments program. Its logic has a single strand: money and transfers of value from drug and device companies to health care professionals must, above set thresholds, be reported item by item to CMS, which then places them in a public database anyone can search by name. It does not decide whether any given payment was lawful — a consulting fee that appears in the database may be entirely proper, and an unreported payment may sit squarely on an Anti-Kickback line. Lawfulness belongs to the Anti-Kickback Statute and the Stark Law covered elsewhere in this repository, and fraud enforcement to the False Claims Act; this article covers only the sunlight layer: who reports, who gets reported, what is reported, on what calendar, and who may correct the record. The first discipline for readers of the public database lives here too: on seeing a company payment under a clinician's name, read the nature of the payment and its context before drawing conclusions, because reading "published" as "problematic" is this statute's most common misreading. Sources: the text of 42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7h and 42 C.F.R. Part 403, Subpart I, and CMS's published Open Payments program descriptions.

### Español (译稿)
Conviene empezar por el carácter de la norma: es una ley de transparencia, no una prohibición de pagar. Su origen es el artículo 6002 de la Affordable Care Act, la Physician Payments Sunshine Act, codificada en 42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7h, con reglas de desarrollo en 42 C.F.R. Part 403, Subpart I (definiciones en el § 403.902 y deberes de reporte en torno al § 403.904), gestionada por los Centros de Servicios de Medicare y Medicaid (CMS) como el programa Open Payments. Su lógica tiene un solo hilo: el dinero y las transferencias de valor de empresas de medicamentos y dispositivos a profesionales sanitarios deben, por encima de ciertos umbrales, reportarse partida por partida a CMS, que los coloca en una base de datos pública consultable por nombre. La ley no decide si un pago concreto fue lícito: unos honorarios de consultoría que aparecen en la base pueden ser plenamente correctos, y un pago no reportado puede estar justo sobre la línea de la ley antisobornos. La licitud corresponde a la Anti-Kickback Statute y a la Stark Law, tratadas en otras piezas de este repositorio, y la persecución del fraude a la False Claims Act; este artículo cubre solo la capa de luz solar: quién reporta, sobre quién se reporta, qué se reporta, con qué calendario y quién puede corregir el registro. La primera disciplina de quien consulta la base pública también está aquí: al ver un pago empresarial junto al nombre de un médico, hay que leer la naturaleza del pago y su contexto antes de concluir nada, porque leer «publicado» como «problemático» es el malentendido más frecuente de esta ley. Fuentes: el texto de 42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7h y de 42 C.F.R. Part 403, Subpart I, y las descripciones públicas del programa Open Payments de CMS. (初译待校)

Nguồn của nó là mục 6002 của Affordable Care Act, tức Physician Payments Sunshine Act, được hệ thống hóa tại 42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7h, với quy tắc thi hành tại 42 C.F.R. ## 二、谁报告、报给谁看：报的一头是企业，晒的一头起初只有医师与教学医院、2021 年起多了五类非医师执业者

### 中文
报告义务落在企业一头，不在医务人员一头。报的人叫 applicable manufacturer（适用制造商）：在美国经营、生产受覆盖药品、器械、生物制品或医疗用品的企业；另一类报送主体是 applicable group purchasing organization（适用团体采购组织），它报的是医师在企业里的所有权与投资权益。被晒的一头叫 covered recipient（受覆盖受款人），分三格逐项数：第一格是医师，法定口径含内科与整骨科医师、牙科、足病、验光与脊椎按摩这几类持照医业人员；第二格是教学医院，以 CMS 每年公布的名单为准；第三格是 2021 数据年起新增的五类非医师执业者——医师助理（PA）、执业护士（NP）、临床护士专家（CNS）、注册麻醉护士（CRNA）与认证助产护士（CNM），逐项数得 5，这是 2018 年《SUPPORT 法案》扩围、CMS 自 2021 年数据起执行的结果，在那之前 PA 与 NP 收到的付款不在这个库里，查旧年份数据先看清这一格的时间界。两个排除要记：企业自己的真实雇员（bona fide employee）以雇员身份收到的钱不在报告范围；受款人已经去世等特殊情形另有规则细目，以当期规则为准。来源：42 C.F.R. § 403.902 的定义条与 CMS 关于受覆盖受款人扩围的公开说明。

### English
The reporting duty sits on the company side, not on the clinician side. The reporters are applicable manufacturers — entities operating in the United States that produce a covered drug, device, biological, or medical supply — and, for a second stream, applicable group purchasing organizations, which report physician ownership and investment interests in the companies. The reported side, covered recipients, falls into three boxes counted one by one. Box one is physicians, a statutory term spanning doctors of medicine and osteopathy, dentistry, podiatric medicine, optometry, and chiropractic. Box two is teaching hospitals, as listed on the list CMS publishes each year. Box three, added from data year 2021, is five types of non-physician practitioners — physician assistants (PA), nurse practitioners (NP), clinical nurse specialists (CNS), certified registered nurse anesthetists (CRNA), and certified nurse-midwives (CNM), counted at five — the result of the 2018 SUPPORT Act expansion that CMS implemented starting with 2021 data; before that, payments to PAs and NPs were not in this database at all, so anyone reading older years must respect this box's time boundary. Two exclusions are worth fixing: payments to a company's own bona fide employees in their capacity as employees fall outside reporting, and special situations such as a deceased recipient follow their own detailed rules in the current text. Sources: the definitions at 42 C.F.R. § 403.902 and CMS's published statements on the covered-recipient expansion.

### Español (译稿)
El deber de reportar recae en el lado empresarial, no en el sanitario. Quienes reportan son los applicable manufacturers, entidades que operan en Estados Unidos y producen un medicamento, dispositivo, producto biológico o suministro médico cubierto; y, en una segunda corriente, las applicable group purchasing organizations, que reportan la propiedad y los intereses de inversión de los médicos en las empresas. El lado reportado, los covered recipients, se divide en tres casillas contadas una por una. La primera es la de los médicos, término legal que abarca medicina y osteopatía, odontología, podología, optometría y quiropráctica. La tercera, añadida desde el año de datos 2021, reúne cinco tipos de profesionales no médicos: asistentes médicos (PA), enfermeras practicantes (NP), enfermeras clínicas especialistas (CNS), enfermeras anestesistas certificadas (CRNA) y enfermeras parteras certificadas (CNM), contados en cinco; es el resultado de la ampliación de la SUPPORT Act de 2018 que CMS aplicó desde los datos de 2021, y antes de eso los pagos a PA y NP no estaban en esta base, así que quien lea años antiguos debe respetar el límite temporal de esta casilla. Dos exclusiones conviene fijar: los pagos a los empleados genuinos (bona fide employees) de la propia empresa en su condición de empleados quedan fuera del reporte, y situaciones especiales como un receptor fallecido siguen sus propias reglas detalladas del texto vigente. Fuentes: las definiciones de 42 C.F.R. § 403.902 y las explicaciones públicas de CMS sobre la ampliación de receptores cubiertos. (初译待校)

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)
Nghĩa vụ báo cáo nằm ở phía công ty, không ở phía nhân viên y tế. Phía được báo cáo, covered recipient, chia thành ba ô đếm từng ô. Ô một là bác sĩ, thuật ngữ luật định bao gồm bác sĩ y khoa và nắn xương, nha khoa, chuyên khoa bàn chân, đo thị lực và nắn chỉnh cột sống. Ô hai là bệnh viện giảng dạy, theo danh sách CMS công bố hằng năm. Ô ba, được thêm từ năm dữ liệu 2021, là năm loại nhân viên hành nghề không phải bác sĩ — trợ lý bác sĩ (PA), điều dưỡng hành nghề (NP), điều dưỡng chuyên khoa lâm sàng (CNS), điều dưỡng gây mê đăng ký chứng nhận (CRNA) và điều dưỡng hộ sinh chứng nhận (CNM), đếm được 5 — kết quả của việc mở rộng theo SUPPORT Act 2018 mà CMS thực thi từ dữ liệu 2021; trước đó, các khoản chi cho PA và NP hoàn toàn không có trong cơ sở dữ liệu này, nên ai đọc dữ liệu năm cũ phải tôn trọng ranh giới thời gian của ô này. Hai loại trừ cần ghi nhớ: khoản chi cho nhân viên thật sự (bona fide employee) của chính công ty trong tư cách nhân viên nằm ngoài phạm vi báo cáo, và các tình huống đặc biệt như người nhận đã qua đời tuân theo quy tắc chi tiết riêng trong văn bản hiện hành. Nguồn: định nghĩa tại 42 C.F.R. § 403.902 và giải thích công khai của CMS về việc mở rộng đối tượng được bao phủ. (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)
रिपोर्टिंग का कर्तव्य कंपनी पक्ष पर है, चिकित्सक पक्ष पर नहीं। रिपोर्ट करने वाले applicable manufacturer हैं — अमेरिका में कार्यरत संस्थाएँ जो शामिल दवा, उपकरण, जैविक उत्पाद या चिकित्सा आपूर्ति बनाती हैं — और दूसरी धारा में applicable group purchasing organization, जो कंपनियों में चिकित्सकों के स्वामित्व और निवेश हित रिपोर्ट करती हैं। रिपोर्ट होने वाला पक्ष, covered recipient, तीन खानों में बँटा है, एक-एक गिनें। खाना एक चिकित्सक है, एक वैधानिक शब्द जिसमें चिकित्सा और अस्थि-चिकित्सा के डॉक्टर, दंत, पाद-चिकित्सा, दृष्टि-मापन और काइरोप्रैक्टिक शामिल हैं। खाना दो शिक्षण अस्पताल है, CMS द्वारा हर वर्ष प्रकाशित सूची के अनुसार। खाना तीन, डेटा वर्ष 2021 से जुड़ा, पाँच प्रकार के गैर-चिकित्सक व्यवसायी हैं — physician assistant (PA), nurse practitioner (NP), clinical nurse specialist (CNS), certified registered nurse anesthetist (CRNA) और certified nurse-midwife (CNM), गिनने पर 5 — यह 2018 के SUPPORT Act विस्तार का परिणाम है जिसे CMS ने 2021 डेटा से लागू किया; उससे पहले PA और NP को मिले भुगतान इस डेटाबेस में थे ही नहीं, इसलिए पुराने वर्षों के आँकड़े पढ़ते समय इस खाने की समय-सीमा का सम्मान करें। दो अपवर्जन याद रखें: कंपनी के अपने वास्तविक कर्मचारियों (bona fide employee) को कर्मचारी के रूप में मिले भुगतान रिपोर्टिंग से बाहर हैं, और मृत प्राप्तकर्ता जैसी विशेष स्थितियाँ वर्तमान पाठ के अपने विस्तृत नियमों का पालन करती हैं। स्रोत: 42 C.F.R. § 403.902 की परिभाषाएँ और CMS के covered-recipient विस्तार पर प्रकाशित कथन। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)

## 三、报什么：三桶数据、付款性质要标清，还有一串法定排除

### 中文
报的内容分三桶，逐项数得 3。第一桶是一般付款（general payments）：咨询费、讲课费、餐饮、差旅、教育物品、礼品、补助等给受覆盖受款人的付款与价值转移，每笔要标付款性质类别、金额、日期与关联产品。第二桶是研究付款（research payments）：研究协议项下的付款单独成桶公开，因为同样一笔钱进了研究合同与进了个人口袋，读法完全不同。第三桶不是付款而是权益：医师及其直系亲属在报告企业里的所有权与投资权益，由制造商与团体采购组织报送。每笔记录还要带受款人的身份坐标（姓名、执业地址、NPI 与州执照信息一带），方便公众把记录对到具体的人。排除清单同样要读：给患者用的药品样品不报、给患者的教育材料不报、短期器械借用评估等若干法定情形不报，细目在第 403.904 条的排除条款里，判断某一笔该不该报先查排除再查门槛。还有一条读库纪律与三桶对应：研究桶里的钱先按研究读、一般桶里的大额咨询与讲课先按市场对价读、权益桶先按利益冲突的浓度读，三桶混读是公开数据最常见的第二种误读。来源：42 C.F.R. §§ 403.904、403.905、403.906 的报告结构与 CMS 的数据分类公开说明。

### English
What gets reported falls into three buckets, counted at three. Bucket one is general payments: consulting fees, speaking fees, meals, travel, educational items, gifts, grants, and other payments or transfers of value to covered recipients, each tagged with its nature-of-payment category, amount, date, and related product. Bucket two is research payments: payments under research agreements are published as their own bucket, because the same sum reads completely differently inside a research contract than inside a personal pocket. Bucket three is not payments at all but interests: ownership and investment interests held by physicians and their immediate family members in reporting companies, submitted by manufacturers and group purchasing organizations. Each record also carries the recipient's identity coordinates — name, practice address, NPI, and state licensure information — so the public can tie a record to a specific person. The exclusion list deserves equal reading: drug samples intended for patients are not reported, educational materials for patients are not reported, and several other statutory situations such as short-term device loans for evaluation are excluded, with details in the exclusion provisions of § 403.904; deciding whether a given transfer is reportable means checking exclusions before thresholds. A reading discipline matches the three buckets: read research-bucket money as research first, read large consulting and speaking entries in the general bucket as market-rate services first, and read the interests bucket as conflict-of-interest density first — mixing the three is the second most common misreading of the public data. Sources: the reporting structure of 42 C.F.R. §§ 403.904, 403.905, and 403.906 and CMS's published data-category descriptions.

### Español (译稿)
Lo que se reporta se divide en tres cubos, contados en tres. El primer cubo es el de pagos generales (general payments): honorarios de consultoría y de conferencias, comidas, viajes, artículos educativos, regalos, subvenciones y demás pagos o transferencias de valor a receptores cubiertos, cada uno con su categoría de naturaleza de pago, importe, fecha y producto relacionado. El segundo cubo es el de pagos de investigación (research payments): los pagos bajo acuerdos de investigación se publican en cubo propio, porque la misma suma se lee de forma completamente distinta dentro de un contrato de investigación que dentro de un bolsillo personal. El tercer cubo no son pagos sino intereses: la propiedad y los intereses de inversión de los médicos y sus familiares directos en las empresas que reportan, enviados por fabricantes y organizaciones de compra en grupo. Cada registro lleva además las coordenadas de identidad del receptor — nombre, dirección de ejercicio, NPI e información de licencia estatal — para que el público pueda atribuir el registro a una persona concreta. La lista de exclusiones merece la misma lectura: no se reportan las muestras de medicamentos destinadas a pacientes, no se reportan los materiales educativos para pacientes, y quedan excluidas varias situaciones legales más, como los préstamos breves de dispositivos para evaluación, con el detalle en las cláusulas de exclusión del § 403.904; para decidir si una transferencia concreta es reportable hay que mirar primero las exclusiones y después los umbrales. Una disciplina de lectura acompaña a los tres cubos: el dinero del cubo de investigación se lee primero como investigación, los importes grandes de consultoría y conferencias del cubo general se leen primero como servicios a precio de mercado, y el cubo de intereses se lee primero como densidad de conflicto de intereses; mezclar los tres es el segundo malentendido más frecuente de los datos públicos. Fuentes: la estructura de reporte de 42 C.F.R. §§ 403.904, 403.905 y 403.906 y las descripciones públicas de categorías de datos de CMS. (初译待校)

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)
Nội dung báo cáo chia thành ba thùng, đếm được 3. Thùng một là chi trả chung (general payments): phí tư vấn, phí thuyết trình, bữa ăn, đi lại, vật phẩm giáo dục, quà tặng, tài trợ và các khoản chi hay chuyển giao giá trị khác cho đối tượng được bao phủ, mỗi khoản gắn nhãn loại bản chất chi trả, số tiền, ngày và sản phẩm liên quan. Thùng hai là chi trả nghiên cứu (research payments): các khoản theo thỏa thuận nghiên cứu được công khai thành thùng riêng, vì cùng một khoản tiền nằm trong hợp đồng nghiên cứu đọc hoàn toàn khác khi nằm trong túi cá nhân. Thùng ba không phải chi trả mà là lợi ích: quyền sở hữu và lợi ích đầu tư của bác sĩ và người thân trực hệ của họ trong các công ty báo cáo, do nhà sản xuất và tổ chức mua theo nhóm nộp. Mỗi bản ghi còn mang tọa độ danh tính của người nhận — tên, địa chỉ hành nghề, NPI và thông tin giấy phép bang — để công chúng nối bản ghi với một người cụ thể. Danh sách loại trừ cũng đáng đọc ngang: mẫu thuốc dành cho bệnh nhân không phải báo cáo, tài liệu giáo dục cho bệnh nhân không phải báo cáo, và vài tình huống luật định khác như cho mượn thiết bị ngắn hạn để đánh giá cũng bị loại trừ, chi tiết tại các điều khoản loại trừ của § 403.904; khi xét một khoản chuyển giao có phải báo cáo hay không, hãy kiểm tra loại trừ trước ngưỡng. Một kỷ luật đọc đi kèm ba thùng: tiền trong thùng nghiên cứu đọc trước hết như nghiên cứu, các khoản tư vấn và thuyết trình lớn trong thùng chung đọc trước hết như dịch vụ theo giá thị trường, và thùng lợi ích đọc trước hết như mật độ xung đột lợi ích — trộn ba thùng là cách hiểu sai phổ biến thứ hai của dữ liệu công khai. Nguồn: cấu trúc báo cáo của 42 C.F.R. §§ 403.904, 403.905 và 403.906 cùng mô tả phân loại dữ liệu công khai của CMS. (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)
रिपोर्ट होने वाली सामग्री तीन बाल्टियों में बँटी है, गिनने पर 3। बाल्टी एक सामान्य भुगतान (general payments) है: परामर्श शुल्क, व्याख्यान शुल्क, भोजन, यात्रा, शैक्षिक वस्तुएँ, उपहार, अनुदान और covered recipient को अन्य भुगतान या मूल्य-हस्तांतरण, हर मद के साथ भुगतान-प्रकृति श्रेणी, राशि, तिथि और संबंधित उत्पाद। बाल्टी दो अनुसंधान भुगतान (research payments) है: अनुसंधान समझौतों के अंतर्गत भुगतान अपनी अलग बाल्टी में प्रकाशित होते हैं, क्योंकि वही राशि अनुसंधान अनुबंध के भीतर और व्यक्तिगत जेब के भीतर बिल्कुल अलग पढ़ी जाती है। बाल्टी तीन भुगतान नहीं, हित है: रिपोर्टिंग कंपनियों में चिकित्सकों और उनके निकट परिवार के स्वामित्व और निवेश हित, जो निर्माता और group purchasing organization भेजते हैं। हर रिकॉर्ड के साथ प्राप्तकर्ता के पहचान निर्देशांक भी रहते हैं — नाम, अभ्यास पता, NPI और राज्य लाइसेंस जानकारी — ताकि जनता रिकॉर्ड को किसी विशिष्ट व्यक्ति से जोड़ सके। अपवर्जन सूची भी उतनी ही ध्यान से पढ़ें: रोगियों के लिए दवा नमूने रिपोर्ट नहीं होते, रोगियों के लिए शैक्षिक सामग्री रिपोर्ट नहीं होती, और मूल्यांकन हेतु अल्पकालिक उपकरण ऋण जैसी कई अन्य वैधानिक स्थितियाँ भी बाहर हैं, विवरण § 403.904 के अपवर्जन प्रावधानों में है; किसी हस्तांतरण के रिपोर्ट योग्य होने का निर्णय करते समय सीमा से पहले अपवर्जन जाँचें। तीनों बाल्टियों के साथ एक पठन अनुशासन जुड़ा है: अनुसंधान बाल्टी के धन को पहले अनुसंधान के रूप में पढ़ें, सामान्य बाल्टी की बड़ी परामर्श और व्याख्यान प्रविष्टियों को पहले बाज़ार-दर सेवाओं के रूप में पढ़ें, और हित बाल्टी को पहले हित-संघर्ष के घनत्व के रूप में पढ़ें — तीनों को मिलाकर पढ़ना सार्वजनिक आँकड़ों की दूसरी सबसे आम गलतफहमी है। स्रोत: 42 C.F.R. §§ 403.904, 403.905 और 403.906 की रिपोर्टिंग संरचना और CMS के प्रकाशित डेटा-श्रेणी विवरण। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)

## 四、小额线与一笔算例：单笔线与全年合计线是一对，过了合计线连小笔也要全报

### 中文
不是每一分钱都报，法里有一对小额线，且每年按消费者价格指数调整、数字以 CMS 当期公布为准。结构是这样：单笔付款低于当期单笔线的，原则上不用逐笔报；但同一年内同一企业给同一受款人的小笔累加过了当期合计线，先前那些小笔就全部变成要报，规则在第 403.904 条里。用 CMS 公布的 2026 程序年数字把结构算清：单笔线为 13.82 美元，合计线为 138.13 美元，两者相除，138.13 除以 13.82 约等于 10.0，即合计线约是单笔线的十倍。算例两笔，本地复算两遍一致：某企业一年内请同一位医生吃工作餐 12 次、每次折 12 美元，12 乘以 12 等于 144 美元，大于合计线 138.13 美元、超出 144 减 138.13 等于 5.87 美元，于是 12 笔全部要报；若只吃 10 次，10 乘以 12 等于 120 美元，小于 138.13 美元，则这串小笔整体留在报告之外。实务提醒：小额线只解决报不报，不解决该不该收；餐饮本身在机构合规与雇主政策里另有规矩，阳光法案的小额线不是请客通行证。来源：42 C.F.R. § 403.904 的小额排除结构与 CMS 公布的 2026 程序年门槛数字。

### English
Not every cent is reported: the statute carries a pair of small-payment thresholds, adjusted each year with the consumer price index, with figures governed by what CMS publishes for the current program year. The structure runs like this: an individual payment below the current single-payment threshold need not be reported on its own, but if the small payments from one company to one recipient within a year add up past the current aggregate threshold, all of those earlier small payments become reportable, under § 403.904. Using the CMS-published figures for program year 2026 makes the structure concrete: the single threshold is $13.82 and the aggregate threshold is $138.13, and dividing them, 138.13 divided by 13.82 is about 10.0, so the aggregate line sits at roughly ten times the single line. Two worked cases, recomputed twice locally with matching results: a company hosts the same physician at 12 working meals in a year valued at $12 each, and 12 times 12 equals 144 dollars, which exceeds the aggregate threshold of 138.13 dollars by 144 minus 138.13 equals 5.87 dollars, so all 12 meals become reportable; at only 10 meals, 10 times 12 equals 120 dollars, below 138.13 dollars, and the whole string of small payments stays outside reporting. A practical caution: the thresholds answer only whether something is reported, never whether it should have been accepted — meals carry their own rules in institutional compliance and employer policy, and the Sunshine thresholds are not a hospitality pass. Sources: the small-payment exclusion structure of 42 C.F.R. § 403.904 and the CMS-published program year 2026 threshold figures.

## 五、年度日历与争议：三月报、四到五月给受款人核对、六月底公开，CMS 不当裁判

### 中文
这条线的时间感全在日历上，一年一圈，逐段数清。第一段，数据年：每年 1 月 1 日到 12 月 31 日，企业逐笔留痕。第二段，报送与 attest（申报确认）：次年 2 月 1 日到 3 月 31 日之间把上一数据年的记录报进 Open Payments 系统并完成真实性确认，报而不确认不算报完。第三段，核对与争议：4 月 1 日到 5 月 15 日是受覆盖受款人的 review and dispute（核对与争议）窗——4 月 30 天加 5 月 15 天，30 加 15 等于 45 天，本地复算两遍一致；受款人注册进系统、把挂在自己名下的记录逐笔看、错了就发起争议，CMS 在这一段只提供系统、不裁决对错，争议由受款人与报送企业直接协商。第四段，修正：5 月 16 日到 5 月 30 日给企业 15 天改争议记录。第五段，公开：6 月 30 日前 CMS 发布该数据年全量数据；没能在修正窗内解决的争议照样公开、只是记录上标 disputed（有争议），公开后再发起的更正走次年刷新。罚则两档，数字以法条基数加逐年通胀调整后的当期口径为准：非故意漏报每笔在 1,000 到 10,000 美元档、年度封顶 150,000 美元；明知漏报每笔在 10,000 到 100,000 美元档、年度封顶 1,000,000 美元——封顶之比 1,000,000 除以 150,000 约等于 6.7，故意与非故意的代价差着量级。给个人读者的动作只有一件：每年四月自己进系统核对一遍名下记录，因为公开后再改的成本远高于四月里点一次争议。来源：42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7h 的报告、核对与罚则结构，42 C.F.R. §§ 403.908、403.912 一带规则与 CMS 公布的年度时间线。

### English
The rhythm of this program lives on its calendar, one loop per year, counted segment by segment. Segment one, the data year: from January 1 to December 31 each year, companies keep item-level records. Segment two, submission and attestation: between February 1 and March 31 of the following year, the prior data year's records go into the Open Payments system with an attestation of completeness and accuracy, and submitting without attesting does not count as done. Segment three, review and dispute: April 1 through May 15 is the covered recipients' review and dispute window — April's 30 days plus 15 days of May, 30 plus 15 equals 45 days, recomputed twice locally with matching results; recipients register, read every record attributed to them, and initiate disputes over errors, while CMS in this segment provides the system only and does not adjudicate, leaving resolution to direct negotiation between recipient and reporting entity. Segment four, correction: May 16 through May 30 gives entities 15 days to fix disputed records. Segment five, publication: by June 30, CMS publishes the data year's full dataset; disputes unresolved inside the correction window are still published, with the record flagged as disputed, and corrections raised after publication flow into the next year's refresh. Penalties come in two tiers, on statutory base figures as inflation-adjusted for the current year: unintentional failures run $1,000 to $10,000 per payment with an annual cap of $150,000, and knowing failures run $10,000 to $100,000 per payment with an annual cap of $1,000,000 — the caps' ratio, 1,000,000 divided by 150,000, is about 6.7, so the knowing tier costs an order of magnitude more. For an individual reader there is exactly one action: every April, log in and review the records under your own name, because fixing the record after publication costs far more than filing one dispute in April. Sources: the reporting, review, and penalty structure of 42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7h, the rules around 42 C.F.R. §§ 403.908 and 403.912, and CMS's published annual timeline.

## 六、加州单独注明：没有州级平行数据库，加州另有一部管企业合规计划的法

### 中文
加州单独注明，分两层说。第一层，加州没有再建一个州级 Open Payments：加州的医师、教学医院与五类非医师执业者收到的企业付款，走的还是联邦这一条报送与公开线，加州受款人核对与争议也在联邦系统里办，不存在向州里再报一份的义务；读者查一位加州医生名下的企业付款，去的仍是联邦公开库。第二层，加州另有一部方向不同、容易与阳光法案混读的法：《健康与安全法典》第 119400 到 119402 条（加州合规法），它管的不是公开，而是制药企业自己的合规计划——在加州做生意的制药企业须建立与联邦监察长办公室（OIG）2003 年制药企业合规指引相符、并纳入 PhRMA 行业守则口径的综合合规计划，在计划里给「一年内可给加州单个医疗专业人员的礼品、促销材料与相关物品活动」自设一个年度合计上限，并每年公开发表合规声明。这个上限是企业自设的、不是法定的统一数字：公开声明里常见每年 2,000 到 2,500 美元一档，各家不同，且上限不含正当专业服务的公平市场对价、患者教育材料与样品等排除项，读某一家企业的声明先看它自己钉的数字与排除范围。两层合起来记：联邦阳光法案管「晒给公众看」，加州合规法管「企业先把自家规矩立起来并声明」，一个是数据库、一个是企业自律文件，互不替代。来源：《健康与安全法典》第 119400 至 119402 条原文与各制药企业依该法公开发表的年度合规声明、CMS 的 Open Payments 公开说明。

### English
California, stated separately, in two layers. Layer one: California has built no state-level Open Payments of its own — payments to California physicians, teaching hospitals, and the five non-physician practitioner types still travel the single federal reporting and publication line, California recipients review and dispute inside the federal system, and there is no second filing owed to the state; a reader checking company payments under a California clinician's name still goes to the federal public database. Layer two: California has a different statute that is easily confused with the Sunshine Act — Health and Safety Code sections 119400 through 119402, the California Compliance Law — which governs not publication but pharmaceutical companies' own compliance programs: a pharmaceutical company doing business in California must maintain a comprehensive compliance program consistent with the federal Office of Inspector General's 2003 compliance guidance for pharmaceutical manufacturers and incorporating the PhRMA Code, set within that program an annual aggregate limit of its own on gifts, promotional materials, and related items or activities it may give an individual California medical or health care professional in a year, and publish an annual written declaration of compliance. ### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)
California, ghi riêng, theo hai lớp. Lớp một: California không xây một Open Payments cấp bang riêng — các khoản chi cho bác sĩ, bệnh viện giảng dạy và năm loại nhân viên không phải bác sĩ của California vẫn đi theo tuyến báo cáo và công khai liên bang duy nhất, người nhận California rà soát và tranh chấp ngay trong hệ thống liên bang, và không tồn tại nghĩa vụ nộp thêm một bản cho bang; người đọc tra khoản chi của công ty dưới tên một nhân viên y tế California vẫn tới cơ sở dữ liệu công khai liên bang. Lớp hai: California có một đạo luật khác hướng, dễ đọc lẫn với Sunshine Act — các mục 119400 đến 119402 của Health and Safety Code (California Compliance Law) — quản không phải việc công khai mà là chương trình tuân thủ của chính công ty dược: công ty dược làm ăn tại California phải duy trì chương trình tuân thủ toàn diện phù hợp hướng dẫn tuân thủ năm 2003 của Văn phòng Tổng Thanh tra liên bang (OIG) cho nhà sản xuất dược và đưa vào chuẩn mực của PhRMA Code, tự đặt trong chương trình đó một trần tổng hợp hằng năm đối với quà tặng, tài liệu quảng bá và vật phẩm hay hoạt động liên quan mà công ty có thể trao cho một chuyên gia y tế cá nhân của California trong một năm, và công bố tuyên bố tuân thủ bằng văn bản hằng năm. Trần này do công ty tự đặt, không phải con số luật định thống nhất: các tuyên bố đã công bố thường nằm ở mức 2.000 đến 2.500 đô la mỗi năm và khác nhau theo công ty, và trần không tính các khoản như trả theo giá thị trường công bằng cho dịch vụ chuyên môn chính đáng, tài liệu giáo dục bệnh nhân và mẫu thử, nên đọc tuyên bố của một công ty hãy bắt đầu từ con số và phạm vi loại trừ mà chính công ty đó đã chốt. Hai lớp gộp lại: Sunshine Act liên bang quản việc phơi cho công chúng xem, luật tuân thủ California quản việc công ty tự lập quy tắc nhà mình và tuyên bố — một bên là cơ sở dữ liệu, một bên là văn kiện tự kỷ luật của doanh nghiệp, không bên nào thay thế bên nào. Nguồn: văn bản các mục 119400 đến 119402 của Health and Safety Code, các tuyên bố tuân thủ hằng năm mà công ty công bố theo luật đó, và mô tả Open Payments của CMS. (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)
California, अलग से, दो परतों में। परत एक: California ने अपना राज्य-स्तरीय Open Payments नहीं बनाया है — California के चिकित्सकों, शिक्षण अस्पतालों और पाँच प्रकार के गैर-चिकित्सक व्यवसायियों को मिले कंपनी भुगतान अब भी एकमात्र संघीय रिपोर्टिंग और प्रकाशन रेखा से चलते हैं, California प्राप्तकर्ता संघीय प्रणाली के भीतर ही समीक्षा और विवाद करते हैं, और राज्य को दूसरी प्रस्तुति देय नहीं है; किसी California चिकित्सक के नाम पर कंपनी भुगतान देखने वाला पाठक अब भी संघीय सार्वजनिक डेटाबेस ही जाता है। परत दो: California में एक अलग दिशा वाला कानून है जो Sunshine Act से आसानी से गड़बड़ा जाता है — Health and Safety Code की धाराएँ 119400 से 119402 (California Compliance Law) — जो प्रकाशन नहीं, बल्कि दवा कंपनियों के अपने अनुपालन कार्यक्रम संभालता है: California में व्यवसाय करने वाली दवा कंपनी को संघीय Office of Inspector General (OIG) के 2003 के दवा निर्माता अनुपालन मार्गदर्शन के अनुरूप और PhRMA Code को सम्मिलित करता व्यापक अनुपालन कार्यक्रम रखना होता है, उस कार्यक्रम के भीतर एक वर्ष में किसी एकल California चिकित्सा या स्वास्थ्य पेशेवर को दिए जा सकने वाले उपहारों, प्रचार सामग्री और संबंधित वस्तुओं या गतिविधियों पर अपनी वार्षिक कुल सीमा स्वयं तय करनी होती है, और अनुपालन की वार्षिक लिखित घोषणा प्रकाशित करनी होती है। यह सीमा कंपनी द्वारा तय है, कोई एकसमान वैधानिक संख्या नहीं: प्रकाशित घोषणाएँ आमतौर पर 2,000 से 2,500 डॉलर प्रति वर्ष के दायरे में रहती हैं और कंपनी अनुसार भिन्न होती हैं, और सीमा में वैध पेशेवर सेवाओं का उचित बाज़ार मूल्य भुगतान, रोगी शैक्षिक सामग्री और नमूने जैसी मदें बाहर रहती हैं, इसलिए किसी कंपनी की घोषणा पढ़ते समय शुरुआत उसी संख्या और अपवर्जन दायरे से करें जो कंपनी ने स्वयं तय किए हैं। दोनों परतें मिलाकर: संघीय Sunshine Act जनता को दिखाने का काम करता है, California Compliance Law कंपनियों के अपने घर-नियम बनाने और घोषित करने का — एक डेटाबेस है, दूसरा कंपनी आत्म-अनुशासन दस्तावेज़, और कोई एक दूसरे का स्थान नहीं लेता। स्रोत: Health and Safety Code की धाराओं 119400 से 119402 का पाठ, उस कानून के अंतर्गत कंपनियों द्वारा प्रकाशित वार्षिक अनुपालन घोषणाएँ, और CMS के Open Payments विवरण। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)
