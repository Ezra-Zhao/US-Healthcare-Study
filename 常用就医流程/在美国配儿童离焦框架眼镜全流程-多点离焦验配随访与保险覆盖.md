## 一、先分清：普通单光镜只管看清，离焦镜多一个管进展的目的

### 中文

普通单光眼镜把中心视力矫正清楚就完成了任务，对近视进展本身不作干预。离焦框架眼镜在镜片上多做了一层设计：中央是正常矫正区，保证看远清楚；中央区之外布满许多微小的透镜单元（lenslets），让周边光线落在视网膜前方一层，形成所谓近视性离焦信号，目的从「看清」多出一条「让近视涨得慢一点」。以美国食品药品监督管理局（FDA）2025 年 9 月 25 日授权上市的依视路 Stellest 镜片为例，公开口径是：用于矫正伴或不伴散光的近视，并延缓 6 到 12 岁起始治疗儿童的近视进展——这是美国第一款获 FDA 上市授权、带延缓近视进展适应证的框架眼镜镜片，走的是 De Novo 上市前审查通道，此前在 2021 年 4 月 30 日拿到突破性医疗器械认定。它的设计按美国眼科学会（AAO）公开介绍是中央约 9 毫米直径的清晰区、周围环绕微透镜点阵。先把预期摆正：离焦镜的目标是把进展速度压下来，不是把已有度数涨回去，更不是治愈近视；孩子戴上它，度数仍可能继续涨，只是涨幅的目标比戴普通单光镜小。还有一条监管边界要先说清：同类多点离焦设计在美国以外的一些市场上市更早，美国境内以 FDA 授权状态与开方验配口径为准，来路不明的跨境镜片不要自行网购回来给孩子戴——处方、瞳距瞳高与镜架适配错一格，防控设计就落不到眼睛该落的位置上。

### English

Single-vision glasses finish their job once central vision is clear; they do not intervene in myopia progression itself. Defocus spectacle lenses add a design layer: a normal correction zone in the center for clear distance vision, surrounded by many tiny lenslets that place peripheral light in front of the retina, creating a myopic-defocus signal, so the goal becomes not only seeing clearly but also progressing more slowly. The example with a U.S. regulatory anchor is the Essilor Stellest lens, for which the FDA authorized marketing on September 25, 2025: to correct myopia, with or without astigmatism, and to slow progression in children aged 6 to 12 at the initiation of treatment — the first eyeglass lens authorized in the U.S. for slowing pediatric myopia progression, reviewed through the De Novo pathway after a Breakthrough Device designation on April 30, 2021. Per the American Academy of Ophthalmology's public summary, the design is a clear central zone about 9 mm in diameter surrounded by rings of lenslets. Set expectations correctly: the aim is a slower rate of progression, not reversal of existing myopia and not a cure; the prescription may still increase, just by less than the target with ordinary single-vision lenses. Regulatory boundary: similar lenslet designs reached some non-U.S. markets earlier, but inside the U.S. the FDA authorization status and local fitting rules govern — do not buy gray-market lenses online for a child, because prescription, pupillary distance, fitting height, and frame fit all have to land the defocus design where the eye needs it.

## 二、证据怎么读：两年数据是减速百分比，不是停涨承诺

### 中文

FDA 授权时评估的是一项临床研究的两年数据，两个数字要分开读。第一个是等效球镜度数的进展：与单光对照镜片相比，离焦组两年近视进展减少 71%。把这个百分比翻译成算例：若对照组两年进展记为 100 个单位，离焦组约为 100 减 71 等于 29 个单位（本地复算两遍一致）——剩下的那部分进展还在，只是少了。第二个是眼轴长度：眼球变长的速度减少 53%，即对照组记为 100 个单位时离焦组约 100 减 53 等于 47 个单位（本地复算两遍一致）。两个数字不一样很正常：度数和眼轴是两把尺，防控看的是长期把眼轴拉长的速度压下来，因为高度近视的眼底风险跟着眼轴走。读证据的三条纪律：一，71% 是组平均，不是对某个孩子的个人承诺，个体反应有分布；二，两年数据支持的是「延缓」这一定位，停戴后会怎样、更长年份怎样，以后续公开证据与验配者说明为准，不在本篇写死；三，授权年龄窗是起始治疗时 6 到 12 岁，窗外年龄用不用、怎么用，由眼科或验光医生按孩子具体情况评估，不自行套用。这组证据也解释了随访为什么必须量眼轴：只盯度数，眼镜处方换来换去会把进展信号搅浑，眼轴是更稳的那把尺。

### English

The FDA's authorization rested on two years of clinical-study data, and its two numbers must be read separately. First, spherical-equivalent progression: over two years the defocus group progressed 71 percent less than the single-vision control group. As arithmetic: if control progression is scored as 100 units, the defocus group is about 100 minus 71 equals 29 units (recomputed twice locally) — the remaining progression still happens, just less of it. Second, axial length: eye elongation slowed by 53 percent, i.e., about 100 minus 53 equals 47 units against the same control scoring. The two percentages differ because refraction and axial length are two different rulers; myopia control is about slowing axial elongation over the long run, because the retinal risks of high myopia track axial length. Three reading rules: 71 percent is a group average, not a personal promise for any one child; two-year data support the word "slow," and what happens after stopping or over longer horizons follows later public evidence and the fitter's guidance, not this article; and the authorized window is age 6 to 12 at initiation, with use outside that window assessed individually by the eye doctor. This is also why follow-up must measure axial length: refractions alone, shifting from prescription to prescription, blur the progression signal, while axial length is the steadier ruler.

### Español（初译待校）

La autorización de la FDA se apoyó en dos años de datos de un estudio clínico, y sus dos cifras se leen por separado. Primera, la progresión del equivalente esférico: en dos años el grupo de desenfoque progresó un 71 por ciento menos que el grupo control de monofocales. En aritmética: si la progresión control vale 100 unidades, el grupo de desenfoque queda en unas 100 menos 71 igual a 29 unidades (recalculado dos veces) — la progresión restante sigue ocurriendo, solo que en menor cantidad. Segunda, la longitud axial: el alargamiento del ojo se redujo un 53 por ciento, es decir, unas 100 menos 53 igual a 47 unidades frente al mismo control. Las dos cifras difieren porque graduación y longitud axial son dos reglas distintas; el control de la miopía consiste en frenar el alargamiento axial a largo plazo, porque los riesgos retinianos de la miopía alta siguen a la longitud axial. Tres reglas de lectura: el 71 por ciento es una media de grupo, no una promesa personal para ningún niño; los datos de dos años sostienen la palabra «ralentizar», y lo que ocurre tras suspender el uso o en horizontes más largos lo marcan la evidencia pública posterior y el criterio del adaptador, no este artículo; y la ventana autorizada es de 6 a 12 años al inicio, quedando el uso fuera de ella a evaluación individual del profesional. Por eso el seguimiento debe medir la longitud axial: la graduación sola, cambiando de prescripción en prescripción, enturbia la señal de progresión, mientras que la longitud axial es la regla más estable. ## 三、验配五步：查、量、选架、定位、试戴复核

### 中文

验配按五步走，逐项数得 5。第一步，全面眼科检查与屈光定底盘：孩子先做综合眼科检查排掉其他眼病，屈光检查在儿童常配合睫状肌麻痹验光这一类做法把真实度数测准（用药类别与做法由医生决定，本篇不写），近视按足矫原则定方——欠矫不会让近视涨得慢，这个老说法早被研究否定，处方写少了只会让孩子看不清。第二步，眼轴基线：定方同时量一次眼轴长度作防控基线，之后每次复诊同口径复测，进展快慢拿基线比。第三步，选架与尺寸：镜架要稳、能把镜片光学中心托在瞳孔正前方，孩子好动、鼻梁低，镜架下滑一格，中央清晰区就偏离瞳孔、离焦区也跟着错位，所以鼻托与镜腿的贴合比款式重要，抗冲击镜片材质与儿童普通配镜的安全口径相同。第四步，定位测量：单眼瞳距与瞳高逐眼量准，加工时把中央区对准瞳孔，取镜时当场戴上复核中心对位、看远清晰度与周边感受，孩子说看远清楚但总歪头，先查对位与镜架变形，不先怪镜片设计。第五步，佩戴与随访安排落纸：每天佩戴时长听验配者按研究口径与孩子情况说明——临床研究里是全天长时间佩戴才得到那组数据，断断续续戴，周末不戴，效果预期要相应打折，这一条要在定方时就跟家长说清，不要等复诊发现进展快了才回头查佩戴时长。试戴不适多在头几天：周边视野发糊、找焦点的感觉与单光镜不同，多属适应；持续头痛、看远反而不清、恶心眩晕不退，回验配处复查对位与处方，不自行停戴了事。

### English

Fitting runs in five counted steps. First, a comprehensive eye examination and a refraction baseline: other eye disease is screened out, children's refraction is often done with cycloplegic techniques in the category sense to measure the true prescription (agents and methods are the doctor's call and are not written here), and the prescription is set at full correction — undercorrection does not slow myopia, an old idea the research has rejected, and it only leaves the child seeing less clearly. Second, an axial-length baseline measured at fitting and remeasured the same way at every review, so progression is judged against the baseline. Third, frame selection and sizing: the frame must hold the lens optical center in front of the pupil; children are active and have low nose bridges, and a frame that slides down one notch shifts both the clear central zone and the defocus zone off target, so nose-pad and temple fit matter more than style, with impact-resistant lens material under the same safety rules as ordinary children's eyewear. Fourth, positioning measurements: monocular pupillary distances and fitting heights measured per eye, the central zone centered on the pupil at fabrication, and an in-person check at pickup for centration, distance clarity, and peripheral feel — if the child sees clearly but keeps tilting the head, check centration and frame deformation before blaming the lens design. Fifth, the wearing and follow-up plan in writing: daily wearing time follows the fitter's instruction based on the study conditions and the child — the trial data came from long daily wear, so intermittent wear, with weekends off, must come with discounted expectations, and this belongs in the fitting conversation, not discovered at a review after fast progression. Early discomfort is usually adaptation — peripheral blur and a different focusing feel; persistent headache, worse distance vision, or nausea that does not settle means a return visit to recheck centration and prescription rather than quietly abandoning the lenses.

Thử kính đi theo năm bước đếm được. Thứ nhất, khám mắt toàn diện và nền khúc xạ: loại trừ các bệnh mắt khác, khúc xạ ở trẻ thường kết hợp kỹ thuật liệt điều tiết ở mức nhóm phương pháp để đo đúng độ thật (nhóm thuốc và cách làm do bác sĩ quyết định, không viết ở đây), và đơn kính được chốt theo nguyên tắc chỉnh đủ — chỉnh thiếu không làm cận tiến triển chậm lại, quan niệm cũ đó đã bị nghiên cứu bác bỏ, và chỉ khiến trẻ nhìn kém rõ hơn. ### English

Myopia control is not finished at purchase; the review rhythm is set at fitting. The interval is set by the fitter according to age and progression speed, with roughly every six months a common clinical rhythm. Each review remeasures the two rulers — refraction and axial length — and checks three things: whether frame centration has drifted, how many hours the lenses are actually worn each day, and whether outdoor time and near-work habits have changed materially. If progression is faster than expected, work through four countable checks first: insufficient wearing time (the most common); frame deformation or slippage losing centration; a prescription that has changed materially while the old lenses are still being worn; and intrinsically faster factors such as a young starting age or highly myopic parents. Only after those four should the conversation move, on the remeasured data, to adding or switching control methods — for example to the orthokeratology line or other options assessed by the eye doctor — rather than on parental anxiety or a sales recommendation. Safety note: spectacle defocus lenses never touch the cornea, so they carry none of the infection risk of contact-lens-based control, their main risks being optical and fitting ones — lost centration means lost control effect, and wearing an expired prescription amounts to undercorrection, both caught by follow-up. 钱的账先把结构说清，再谈数字。检查一格：儿童综合眼科检查与屈光检查的费用口径与普通儿童配镜相同，医疗险与 vision plan 谁接、接多少，看主诉与计划条款，约诊时先说清这次带近视防控评估目的。第二格是镜片材料：离焦设计镜片比普通单光镜片贵出一截，vision plan 的材料津贴通常按普通镜片福利那一池算，盖不住的差额多为自付；少数计划开始把近视防控产品单列覆盖口径，变化很快，下单前让配镜机构按孩子本人的计划当场核价，把「计划付多少、自付多少、复诊复测包不包在验配费里」三笔写在报价单上，不听口头大概。第三格是镜架与加工：镜架走同一材料福利池的，要在镜架与镜片之间怎么分这池津贴，先算再选，别先挑贵镜架把津贴用光、回头发现防控镜片的差额全落自付。税优账户一格：为矫正视力所需的眼镜属 IRS Publication 502 口径下的合格医疗费用，离焦眼镜的自付部分一般可用 HSA 或 FSA 支付、票据留好，同一笔支出不得在保险与税优账户之间重复报销。加州单独注明：处方由验光师（OD）或眼科医生（MD）开具，处方释放与患者拿到处方的权利走联邦贸易委员会（FTC）《眼镜规则》（16 C.F.R. Part 456）的同一套底盘；注册配镜师按处方调配，不验光、不改方。最后一条消费纪律：防控效果看的是两年尺度的趋势，不存在「更贵的离焦设计必然更好」的货架逻辑，选哪款已授权设计、镜架贵不贵，都替代不了佩戴时长与半年复测这四件基本功（查、量、戴、复）——钱花在复诊与换镜的节奏上，比花在镜架品牌上更接近防控本身。

### English

Sort the structure before the numbers. Examination: a child's comprehensive eye examination and refraction are billed on the same footing as ordinary pediatric eyewear, and whether medical insurance or the vision plan takes them depends on the presenting purpose and plan terms — state at booking that this visit includes a myopia-control assessment. Materials: defocus-design lenses cost materially more than ordinary single-vision lenses; a vision plan's materials allowance usually comes out of the ordinary lens-benefit pool, leaving the difference out of pocket, while a minority of plans are beginning to carve out myopia-control coverage and the terms change quickly — before ordering, have the optical shop price the child's actual plan on the spot and put three figures on the quote: what the plan pays, what the family pays, and whether review remeasurements are inside the fitting fee. Frames and fabrication share the same materials pool, so decide how the allowance splits between frame and lenses before choosing, rather than spending the allowance on an expensive frame and finding the whole defocus difference out of pocket. Tax-advantaged accounts: eyeglasses needed to correct vision are a qualified medical expense under IRS Publication 502, so the out-of-pocket part can generally be paid from an HSA or FSA with receipts kept, and the same expense may not be reimbursed twice. California note: prescriptions are written by an optometrist (OD) or ophthalmologist (MD), prescription release and the patient's right to a copy follow the FTC Eyeglass Rule (16 C.F.R. Part 456), and registered dispensing opticians dispense to the prescription without refracting or altering it. Final consumer rule: control effect is read on a two-year trend, and there is no shelf logic that a pricier defocus design is automatically better — which authorized design and how expensive the frame is can never substitute for the four basics of wearing time and semiannual remeasurement; money spent on keeping the review-and-replacement rhythm does more for control than money spent on a frame brand.

### Español（初译待校）

Primero la estructura, luego las cifras. Examen: el examen ocular completo y la refracción del niño se facturan en el mismo pie que las gafas pediátricas ordinarias, y quién los asume — seguro médico o plan de visión — depende del motivo declarado y de las condiciones del plan; al reservar, diga que la visita incluye evaluación de control de miopía. Materiales: las lentes con diseño de desenfoque cuestan sensiblemente más que las monofocales ordinarias; la asignación de materiales del plan de visión suele salir del mismo fondo de lentes ordinarias, dejando la diferencia de bolsillo, mientras una minoría de planes empieza a separar la cobertura de control de miopía y las condiciones cambian rápido — antes de encargar, pida a la óptica que valore el plan concreto del niño en el acto y escriba tres cifras en el presupuesto: cuánto paga el plan, cuánto paga la familia y si las remediciones de revisión están dentro de la tarifa de adaptación. Montura y fabricación comparten el mismo fondo de materiales, así que reparta la asignación entre montura y lentes antes de elegir, en lugar de gastarla en una montura cara y descubrir después que toda la diferencia del desenfoque sale del bolsillo. Cuentas con ventaja fiscal: las gafas necesarias para corregir la visión son un gasto médico cualificado según la Publicación 502 del IRS, por lo que la parte de bolsillo puede pagarse en general con HSA o FSA guardando los recibos, y el mismo gasto no puede reembolsarse dos veces. Nota de California: prescriben el optometrista (OD) o el oftalmólogo (MD), la entrega de la prescripción y el derecho del paciente a su copia siguen la Regla de Gafas de la FTC (16 C.F.R. Parte 456), y los ópticos dispensadores registrados dispensan según la prescripción sin refractar ni alterarla. Regla final de consumo: el efecto de control se lee en una tendencia de dos años, y no existe la lógica de estantería de que un diseño de desenfoque más caro sea automáticamente mejor — qué diseño autorizado se elija y cuánto cueste la montura nunca sustituyen a lo básico del tiempo de uso y la remedición semestral; el dinero puesto en mantener el ritmo de revisiones y recambios hace más por el control que el dinero puesto en una marca de montura. ### हिन्दी（初译待校）

पहले संरचना साफ़ करें, फिर आँकड़े। जाँच का खाना: बच्चे की व्यापक नेत्र जाँच और अपवर्तन सामान्य बाल चश्मों के समान आधार पर बिल होते हैं, और चिकित्सा बीमा या दृष्टि योजना कौन कितना लेगा, यह आने के उद्देश्य और योजना की शर्तों पर निर्भर है — बुकिंग पर कह दें कि इस दौरे में निकट-दृष्टि नियंत्रण का मूल्यांकन शामिल है। सामग्री का खाना: डिफ़ोकस डिज़ाइन वाले लेंस साधारण एकल-दृष्टि लेंसों से काफी महंगे होते हैं; दृष्टि योजना का सामग्री भत्ता आमतौर पर उसी सामान्य लेंस-लाभ कोष से आता है और अंतर अधिकतर जेब से जाता है, जबकि कुछ योजनाएँ निकट-दृष्टि नियंत्रण उत्पादों की कवरेज अलग करने लगी हैं और शर्तें तेज़ी से बदलती हैं — ऑर्डर से पहले दुकान से बच्चे की वास्तविक योजना पर उसी समय मूल्य निकलवाएँ और कोट पर तीन आँकड़े लिखवाएँ: योजना कितना देती है, परिवार कितना देता है, और समीक्षा की पुनःमाप फ़िटिंग शुल्क में शामिल है या नहीं। फ़्रेम और निर्माण उसी सामग्री कोष को साझा करते हैं, इसलिए भत्ते का फ़्रेम और लेंस के बीच बँटवारा पहले तय करें, बजाय इसके कि महंगे फ़्रेम पर भत्ता ख़त्म करके डिफ़ोकस का पूरा अंतर जेब पर आ पड़े। कर-लाभ खातों का खाना: दृष्टि सुधार के लिए आवश्यक चश्मे IRS प्रकाशन 502 के अंतर्गत योग्य चिकित्सा व्यय हैं, इसलिए जेब वाला हिस्सा आम तौर पर HSA या FSA से रसीदें रखकर दिया जा सकता है, और एक ही खर्च दो बार वापस नहीं लिया जा सकता। कैलिफ़ोर्निया टिप्पणी: नुस्खा ऑप्टोमेट्रिस्ट (OD) या नेत्र रोग विशेषज्ञ (MD) लिखता है, नुस्खा सौंपना और रोगी का प्रति पाने का अधिकार FTC चश्मा नियम (16 C.F.R. Part 456) के उसी ढाँचे पर चलता है, और पंजीकृत वितरण ऑप्टिशियन नुस्खे के अनुसार ही देता है, अपवर्तन या बदलाव नहीं करता। अंतिम उपभोक्ता नियम: नियंत्रण का प्रभाव दो वर्ष की प्रवृत्ति पर पढ़ा जाता है, और यह अलमारी-तर्क नहीं चलता कि महंगा डिफ़ोकस डिज़ाइन अपने आप बेहतर होगा — कौन-सा अधिकृत डिज़ाइन चुना और फ़्रेम कितने का है, ये पहनने के समय और छमाही पुनःमाप की बुनियादी बातों की जगह कभी नहीं ले सकते; समीक्षा और बदलाव की लय बनाए रखने पर लगाया पैसा, फ़्रेम के ब्रांड पर लगाए पैसे से नियंत्रण के अधिक निकट है। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद, 初译待校)
