# 在美国给孩子拿药与安全给药全流程：体重剂量口径、量具与 OTC 年龄线及加州规则

这篇讲什么：
- 这是「拿药流程」②的一篇深挖，单讲给孩子拿药、量药、喂药这一条线：从儿科开方、药房领药，到回家存放、学校存药的完整链条。
- 先把全篇的红线说清：文中不写任何具体药物的剂量与频次。孩子吃多少、隔多久吃，以处方和药品说明书为准；拿不准就问开方医生或药师，不凭经验估。
- 三个最常见的家庭错误先拆掉：按年龄随手减半成人药、用厨房茶匙汤匙量药、退烧药与复方感冒药叠着吃——每一条都有对应的制度与安全原因。
- 来源口径：美国食品药品管理局（FDA）儿童用药与量具指引、美国儿科学会（AAP）咳嗽感冒药立场、联邦药师咨询义务（OBRA '90）与《毒物预防包装法》（PPPA），加州落点为《教育法典》第 49423 条的在校服药规则。

## 一、先认口径：儿童剂量按体重算，不是把成人药减半

### 中文
给孩子用药，第一条口径是剂量按体重计算，而不是按年龄拍脑袋、更不是把成人的量减半。原因不复杂：同样年龄的孩子体重可以差很多，而药物在体内的处理又与体重、发育阶段直接相关，儿科开方与药房核验都以孩子当下的实际体重为准。所以就诊时医生会先给孩子称重，药房也可能再问一次近期体重——这不是多余的流程，体重变了，原来的用量口径就要重新核。家长要做的配合有三件：报体重报实数，不报「大概」；孩子短期内明显长胖或变瘦，再拿药时主动提一句；家里几个孩子年龄相近，也不能互相套用对方的处方与用量，每个人的处方只对本人。至于具体吃多少，本篇不写、也无法写任何按体重换算的数字，那是处方与说明书的事，家长只负责把体重信息给准、把处方核对清楚。

### English
The first rule for medicating children is that doses are calculated by body weight — not guessed from age, and never by cutting an adult dose in half. Children of the same age can weigh very different amounts, and how the body handles a drug tracks weight and developmental stage, so pediatric prescribers and pharmacists work from the child's current actual weight. That is why the clinician weighs your child at the visit and the pharmacy may ask for a recent weight again; it is not redundant paperwork, because when weight changes, the dosing basis has to be rechecked. Parents help in three ways: report the real measured weight rather than a rough guess; mention it if the child has recently gained or lost noticeable weight before a refill; and never let siblings of similar age share each other's prescriptions or dosing — a prescription belongs to one child only. As for how much that is in concrete terms, this article states no weight-based conversion figures at all; the prescription and the product labeling govern, and the parent's job is to supply accurate weight information and verify the prescription carefully.

### Español (译稿)
La primera regla al medicar a un niño es que la dosis se calcula por peso corporal, no se adivina por la edad ni se obtiene reduciendo a la mitad la dosis de un adulto. Niños de la misma edad pueden pesar cantidades muy distintas, y el modo en que el cuerpo procesa un fármaco depende del peso y de la etapa de desarrollo, así que quien prescribe en pediatría y quien verifica en la farmacia trabajan con el peso real y actual del niño. Por eso el médico pesa al niño en la consulta y la farmacia puede volver a pedir un peso reciente; no es un trámite redundante, porque si el peso cambia hay que revisar la base de cálculo. Los padres ayudan en tres cosas: dar el peso real medido y no una aproximación; avisar si el niño ha ganado o perdido peso de forma notable antes de una reposición; y no permitir que hermanos de edades parecidas compartan recetas ni cantidades: cada receta es solo para un niño. En cuanto a cuánto es eso en cifras concretas, este artículo no da ninguna conversión por peso; mandan la receta y la etiqueta del producto, y la tarea de los padres es aportar el peso correcto y verificar bien la receta.

## 二、量具只认药房随附的那把：标签以 mL 计，厨房勺子不用

### 中文
液体药喂错，多半错在量具。FDA 反复提醒：不要用厨房里的茶匙、汤匙量药——家用勺子大小不一、没有刻度，一勺到底是多少全凭手感，量多量少都出问题。正确做法只有一套：用药房随附的口服注射器或带刻度的量杯、滴管，标签和处方都以毫升（mL）计量，就按刻度读数，先把气泡排掉、在与视线平齐处读数。随附量具丢了或坏了，不要随手抓厨房勺子顶上，回药房要一把新的，药房一般能补。还有一个细节常被忽略：一瓶药配一把量具，不同药的浓度口径不完全一样，量具不要跨药混用，喂完冲洗晾干、和对应的药放在一起。另外，说明书上若写的是 mL 就按 mL 量，见到以 teaspoon 等家用单位写的旧式说明，先问药师换算成 mL 再喂，不要自己在勺子上估。量具这件小事，是儿童液体药最常见的出错点，值得当成规矩立住。

### English
Most liquid-medication errors at home are measuring-device errors. The FDA warns repeatedly against using kitchen teaspoons or tablespoons: household spoons vary in size, carry no graduations, and a "spoonful" is whatever the hand pours — too much or too little follows easily. The correct setup is a single one: use the oral syringe, graduated dosing cup, or dropper that comes with the medicine; prescriptions and labels are written in milliliters (mL), so read the marking at eye level after expelling air bubbles. If the supplied device is lost or broken, do not substitute a kitchen spoon — go back to the pharmacy for a replacement, which pharmacies routinely provide. One more detail is often missed: one device goes with one medicine. Concentrations differ between products, so do not share measuring devices across medicines; rinse and air-dry after each use and store the device with its own bottle. And if any instruction is written in household units such as teaspoons, ask the pharmacist to convert it into mL before you dose rather than estimating on a spoon. This small discipline is where pediatric liquid dosing most often goes wrong, and it deserves to be a standing household rule.

### Español (译稿)
La mayoría de los errores con medicamentos líquidos en casa son errores del instrumento de medida. La FDA advierte una y otra vez que no se usen cucharillas ni cucharas de cocina: las cucharas domésticas varían de tamaño, no tienen graduación y «una cucharada» es lo que la mano vierte, así que sobrar o faltar es fácil. La forma correcta es una sola: usar la jeringa oral, el vasito graduado o el gotero que viene con el medicamento; las recetas y etiquetas vienen en mililitros (mL), así que se lee la marca a la altura de los ojos tras expulsar las burbujas. Si el instrumento suministrado se pierde o se rompe, no lo sustituya por una cuchara de cocina: vuelva a la farmacia a por uno nuevo, algo que las farmacias hacen de forma rutinaria. Otro detalle que suele pasarse por alto: un instrumento para cada medicamento. Las concentraciones difieren entre productos, así que no comparta instrumentos entre medicamentos; enjuague y seque al aire tras cada uso y guarde cada instrumento con su frasco. Y si alguna instrucción viene en unidades domésticas como cucharaditas, pida al farmacéutico que la convierta a mL antes de dosificar, en lugar de estimar con una cuchara. Esta pequeña disciplina es donde más a menudo falla la dosificación líquida pediátrica, y merece ser una regla fija del hogar.

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ, chờ hiệu đính)
Phần lớn sai sót khi cho trẻ uống thuốc dạng lỏng tại nhà là sai sót ở dụng cụ đong. FDA nhiều lần cảnh báo không dùng thìa cà phê hay thìa canh trong bếp: thìa gia dụng không đồng nhất kích cỡ, không có vạch chia, và "một thìa" là tùy tay rót — thừa hay thiếu đều dễ xảy ra. Cách đúng chỉ có một: dùng ống tiêm uống, cốc đong có vạch hoặc ống nhỏ giọt đi kèm thuốc; đơn thuốc và nhãn đều ghi bằng mililít (mL), nên hãy đuổi hết bọt khí rồi đọc vạch ở ngang tầm mắt. Nếu dụng cụ đi kèm bị mất hoặc hỏng, đừng thay bằng thìa bếp — hãy quay lại nhà thuốc xin cấp lại, việc này nhà thuốc vẫn thường làm. Một chi tiết nữa hay bị bỏ qua: mỗi thuốc dùng một dụng cụ riêng. Nồng độ giữa các sản phẩm khác nhau, nên đừng dùng chung dụng cụ đong cho nhiều thuốc; sau mỗi lần dùng hãy rửa, để khô tự nhiên và cất dụng cụ cùng chai thuốc của nó. Và nếu hướng dẫn nào ghi bằng đơn vị gia dụng như thìa cà phê, hãy nhờ dược sĩ đổi sang mL trước khi cho uống, thay vì tự ước lượng bằng thìa. ## 三、认浓度、认成分：退烧药与复方感冒药最怕叠加

## 四、OTC 咳嗽感冒药的年龄线：四岁以下不用，六岁以下先问医生

### 中文

### English
Over-the-counter cough and cold medicines carry the hardest age lines in pediatric medication, and the lines are drawn by the FDA and by manufacturer labeling. The FDA does not recommend these products for children younger than 2, because serious and potentially life-threatening side effects can occur; manufacturers voluntarily label the products "do not use in children under 4 years of age," so that small print on the shelf is an industry-wide age line, not decoration. For ages 4 to 6 the line stays tight: use only when a clinician specifically advises it. The American Academy of Pediatrics is more conservative than the label — in one sentence, it broadly advises against this class of combination cough and cold products in young children whenever they can be avoided. Three companion rules follow: never give a child medicine packaged and formulated for adults, since adult products are an overdose risk for children; homeopathic cough and cold products are not FDA-approved, and the FDA likewise urges against their use in young children; and because the common cold is usually viral and self-limited, these products do not shorten the illness — in the youngest children they offer risk without benefit. When a child is miserable with a cough or cold, the first route is the pediatric clinician, not the store shelf.

## 六、安全存放与误服：防护盖不是保险箱，中毒热线先存好

### 中文

### English

But a resistant cap is designed to slow a child down and buy adults time to notice — it is not a safe a child can never open. Given enough time, children do open them, and the line collapses when grandparents leave caps loose because they are hard to turn, or when pills are moved into unprotected travel organizers. Household rules sit in three places. First, keep all medicines up high or locked away, out of reach, and put them back immediately after use — never leave bottles on counters, nightstands, or in purses. Second, never call medicine "candy," and avoid making your own pills look like a snack in front of children; imitation is exactly the mechanism of many poisonings. Third, save the national Poison Control number, 1-800-222-1222, in your phone now: if a child swallows something they should not, or you suspect they did, call that number first and follow the guidance, and call 911 directly for abnormal breathing, seizures, or unresponsiveness. Expired and unneeded medicines leave the house through take-back channels — see the companion antibiotics article and the drug-disposal law article — rather than accumulating in a cabinet.

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ, chờ hiệu đính)
Bao bì chống trẻ em bắt nguồn từ Đạo luật Bao bì Phòng ngừa Ngộ độc (Poison Prevention Packaging Act, PPPA), do Ủy ban An toàn Sản phẩm Tiêu dùng (CPSC) thực thi, yêu cầu phần lớn thuốc kê đơn và một số thuốc không kê đơn phải dùng nắp chống trẻ em (child-resistant packaging). Nhưng nắp chống trẻ được thiết kế để làm trẻ mở chậm lại, giành thời gian cho người lớn phát hiện — nó không phải két sắt mà trẻ không bao giờ mở được. Có đủ thời gian, trẻ vẫn mở được, và hàng rào này sụp đổ khi ông bà để nắp lỏng vì khó vặn, hoặc khi thuốc được chia sang hộp thuốc du lịch không có chức năng bảo vệ. Quy tắc trong nhà nằm ở ba chỗ. Một, mọi thuốc đều cất cao hoặc khóa lại, ngoài tầm với của trẻ, và dùng xong cất lại ngay; không để chai thuốc bừa trên mặt bếp, tủ đầu giường hay trong túi xách. Hai, đừng bao giờ gọi thuốc là "kẹo", và đừng làm cho việc uống thuốc của mình trông như ăn vặt trước mặt trẻ — bắt chước chính là cơ chế của nhiều ca ngộ độc. Ba, lưu ngay số đường dây nóng Kiểm soát Chất độc toàn quốc 1-800-222-1222 vào điện thoại: nếu trẻ nuốt phải thứ không được nuốt, hoặc nghi ngờ trẻ đã nuốt, hãy gọi số này trước và làm theo hướng dẫn; còn nếu trẻ thở bất thường, co giật hay không tỉnh táo thì gọi thẳng 911. Thuốc hết hạn hoặc không dùng nữa hãy đưa ra khỏi nhà qua kênh thu hồi — xem bài kháng sinh cùng kho và bài luật về thu hồi thuốc kê đơn — thay vì để dồn trong tủ. (Bản dịch sơ bộ, chờ hiệu đính)

## 七、学校存药、给药记录与回诊红旗

### 中文
孩子白天在学校，疗程又不能断，在校服药就走学校的正式通道，不走「塞进书包自己吃」这条路。加州《教育法典》第 49423 条写得很具体：学生在校期间需要服用处方药的，可由学校护士或指定的学校人员协助，前提是学校同时收到两份书面材料——一份是开方医师的书面说明，写明药物名称、服用方法、用量与时间安排；一份是家长或监护人的书面说明，表明希望学校按医师说明协助孩子服药。这两份书面材料至少每年更新一次，药物、用量、频次或用药理由有变化时要更频繁地更新。实操上再加三条：药要放在原装带标签的容器里交给学校、由学校统一保管，不让孩子自己揣着到处走；剂量或时间有任何调整，必须回到医师书面说明这条线，不接受口头转达；放学后与周末的给药由家里接上，两边在同一张记录上衔接。在家这一侧，给药记录值得认真记：哪天几点、喂了哪种药、孩子吃下去没有、有没有吐掉或不适，退热时把测温结果与时间一并记下——回诊时这张记录就是医生判断疗效与安全最直接的依据，比「好像吃了几次」有用得多。最后是红旗，只写类别、不写处置：呼吸困难或喘鸣明显、精神萎靡叫不醒、脱水征象（哭时没眼泪、口干、尿量明显变少）、皮疹伴高热不退、症状服药后反而持续加重——出现这些就及时就医，危急征象直接拨 911，不在家继续观察等下一顿药。

### English
When a course of treatment runs through the school day, medication at school goes through the school's formal channel, not a pill tucked into a backpack. California Education Code § 49423 is specific: a pupil who must take prescribed medication during the regular school day may be assisted by the school nurse or other designated school personnel when the school has received two written statements — one from the prescribing physician detailing the name of the medication, the method, the amount, and the time schedules for taking it; and one from the parent or guardian asking the school to assist as the physician's statement directs. Both statements must be provided at least annually, and more often whenever the medication, amount, frequency, or reason changes. Practice adds three points: hand the medicine to the school in its original labeled container for school custody rather than letting the child carry it around; route any change in amount or timing back through the physician's written statement, never through word of mouth; and keep home dosing after school and on weekends on the same record as school dosing. At home, a dosing log is worth keeping properly — date and time, which medicine, whether the child actually swallowed it, any spitting up or discomfort, and for fever, the measured temperature with its time. At a follow-up visit that log is the clinician's most direct evidence of effect and safety, far more useful than "I think we gave it a few times." Finally, red flags, stated as categories only: breathing difficulty or obvious wheezing, lethargy or difficulty waking, signs of dehydration (no tears when crying, dry mouth, markedly fewer wet diapers or urinations), rash with persistent high fever, or symptoms that keep worsening despite medication. These mean prompt medical care, and emergency signs mean 911 — not waiting at home for the next dose.

Por último, las señales de alarma, solo como categorías: dificultad respiratoria o sibilancias evidentes, somnolencia o dificultad para despertar, signos de deshidratación (llanto sin lágrimas, boca seca, orina claramente reducida), erupción con fiebre alta persistente, o síntomas que siguen empeorando pese a la medicación. Gặp những dấu hiệu này thì đi khám kịp thời, còn dấu hiệu nguy cấp thì gọi thẳng 911, không ở nhà chờ tới liều thuốc tiếp theo. (Bản dịch sơ bộ, chờ hiệu đính)

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद, समीक्षा लंबित)
जब इलाज का क्रम स्कूल के दिन के बीच से गुज़रता है, तो स्कूल में दवा औपचारिक माध्यम से ही जाती है — बैग में रखी गोली से नहीं। California Education Code § 49423 स्पष्ट है: जिस छात्र को नियमित स्कूल-दिवस में प्रिस्क्रिप्शन दवा लेनी हो, उसे स्कूल नर्स या अन्य नामित स्कूल कर्मचारी सहायता दे सकते हैं, जब स्कूल को दो लिखित विवरण मिल चुके हों — एक प्रिस्क्राइबर चिकित्सक का, जिसमें दवा का नाम, लेने की विधि, मात्रा और समय-सारणी लिखी हो; और दूसरा माता-पिता या अभिभावक का, जिसमें यह इच्छा लिखी हो कि स्कूल चिकित्सक के विवरण के अनुसार सहायता करे। दोनों विवरण कम से कम हर वर्ष देने होते हैं, और दवा, मात्रा, आवृत्ति या कारण बदलने पर उससे अधिक बार। व्यवहार में तीन बातें और जुड़ती हैं: दवा मूल लेबल वाले डिब्बे में स्कूल को सौंपें ताकि वह स्कूल की अभिरक्षा में रहे, बच्चा उसे अपने साथ न घुमाए; मात्रा या समय का कोई भी बदलाव मौखिक संदेश से नहीं, चिकित्सक के लिखित विवरण के रास्ते से ही आए; और स्कूल के बाद व सप्ताहांत की घर की ख़ुराकें स्कूल वाली ख़ुराकों के साथ एक ही अभिलेख में जुड़ें। घर पर ख़ुराक-अभिलेख ठीक से रखना लाभदायक है — तारीख़ और समय, कौन-सी दवा, बच्चा सचमुच निगल पाया या नहीं, थूका या असहज तो नहीं हुआ, और बुख़ार हो तो मापा हुआ तापमान उसके समय के साथ। फ़ॉलो-अप मुलाक़ात में यही अभिलेख चिकित्सक के पास प्रभाव और सुरक्षा का सबसे प्रत्यक्ष प्रमाण होता है, "लगता है कुछ बार दी थी" से कहीं अधिक उपयोगी। अंत में, लाल झंडे — केवल श्रेणियों के रूप में: साँस लेने में कठिनाई या स्पष्ट घरघराहट, सुस्ती या जगाने में कठिनाई, निर्जलीकरण के संकेत (रोने पर आँसू न आना, मुँह सूखना, पेशाब स्पष्ट रूप से कम होना), लगातार तेज़ बुख़ार के साथ दाने, या दवा के बावजूद लगातार बिगड़ते लक्षण। इनका अर्थ है तुरंत चिकित्सकीय देखभाल, और आपात संकेतों का अर्थ है 911 — घर पर अगली ख़ुराक की प्रतीक्षा नहीं। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद, समीक्षा लंबित)
