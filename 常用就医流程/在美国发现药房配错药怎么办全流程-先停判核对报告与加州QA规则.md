# 在美国发现药房配错药怎么办全流程：先停判、核对、报告与加州 QA 规则

## 一、先定性：什么算配药错误，什么只是虚惊

### 中文
拿回家的药看着不对，先别慌，也别先吃。配药错误（medication error）说的是实际拿到的药与处方不一致：药名不对、规格不对、数量不对、用法说明与处方不一致，或是拿成了别人的药。加州法规里的定义抓的正是这一点——与处方或医嘱不符、且未经处方者授权的偏差才算错误；在交到患者手里之前就被拦下改掉的偏差，不算已发生的配药错误。最常见的虚惊要先分出去：换了生产厂商的仿制药，药片颜色、形状、刻印都可能变，但通用名与规格没变，那不是配错。判断标准从来不是长得像不像上次，而是瓶签与药片能不能与处方逐项对上。拿不准时按配错处理：先停、别吃、打电话问药房，把判断交给药师核对记录，而不是自己猜。

### English
When a filled medication looks wrong at home, do not panic and do not take it yet. A medication error means what was actually dispensed does not match the prescription: the wrong drug name, the wrong strength, the wrong quantity, directions that do not match the order, or someone else's medication entirely. California's regulatory definition turns on exactly this point — a variation from the prescription or drug order that the prescriber did not authorize is an error, while a variation caught and corrected before the drug is furnished to the patient is not a completed error. Rule out the most common false alarm first: a generic made by a different manufacturer may change color, shape, and imprint while the generic name and strength stay the same, and that is not an error. The test is never whether the tablets look like last time; it is whether the label and the tablets match the prescription item by item. When in doubt, treat it as an error: stop, do not take it, and call the pharmacy, so a pharmacist checks the record instead of the patient guessing.

### Español (初译待校)
Cuando un medicamento entregado se ve raro en casa, no entre en pánico y todavía no lo tome. Un error de dispensación (medication error) significa que lo entregado no coincide con la receta: nombre equivocado, concentración equivocada, cantidad equivocada, instrucciones que no coinciden con la orden, o el medicamento de otra persona. La definición reglamentaria de California gira sobre ese punto: una desviación de la receta o de la orden que el prescriptor no autorizó es un error, mientras que la desviación detectada y corregida antes de entregar el fármaco al paciente no es un error consumado. Descarte primero la falsa alarma más común: un genérico de otro fabricante puede cambiar de color, forma y grabado manteniendo el mismo nombre genérico y la misma concentración, y eso no es un error. La prueba nunca es si las tabletas se parecen a las de la vez anterior, sino si la etiqueta y las tabletas coinciden con la receta punto por punto. Ante la duda, trátelo como error: pare, no lo tome y llame a la farmacia, para que un farmacéutico revise el registro en lugar de que el paciente adivine. (初译待校)

집에 가져온 약이 이상해 보이면 당황하지 말고 아직 먹지 마십시오. 조제 오류(medication error)란 실제로 받은 약이 처방과 맞지 않는 경우입니다. 약 이름이 다르거나, 함량이 다르거나, 수량이 다르거나, 복용 안내가 처방과 다르거나, 아예 남의 약을 받은 경우입니다. 캘리포니아 규정상의 정의도 정확히 이 지점을 잡습니다. 처방자가 허가하지 않은 처방이나 의사 지시와의 어긋남이 오류이고, 환자에게 건네지기 전에 잡혀 고쳐진 어긋남은 이미 일어난 오류로 셈하지 않습니다. 가장 흔한 헛소동부터 가려내십시오. 제조사가 바뀐 제네릭은 색과 모양, 각인이 달라져도 일반명과 함량이 같으면 조제 오류가 아닙니다. 판단 기준은 지난번 약과 닮았는지가 아니라, 병 라벨과 알약이 처방과 항목마다 맞는가입니다. 헷갈리면 오류로 취급하십시오. 멈추고, 먹지 말고, 약국에 전화해 약사가 기록을 확인하게 하십시오. 환자 스스로 짐작할 일이 아닙니다. ### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)
Khi thuốc đã nhận về nhà trông không đúng, đừng hoảng và đừng uống vội. Lỗi cấp phát thuốc (medication error) nghĩa là thứ thực tế được giao không khớp với đơn thuốc: sai tên thuốc, sai hàm lượng, sai số lượng, hướng dẫn dùng không khớp với y lệnh, hoặc nhận nhầm thuốc của người khác. Định nghĩa trong quy định của California bám đúng điểm này: sự sai lệch so với đơn thuốc hay y lệnh mà người kê đơn không cho phép là lỗi, còn sai lệch bị chặn và sửa trước khi thuốc tới tay bệnh nhân thì không tính là lỗi đã xảy ra. Hãy loại trừ báo động giả phổ biến nhất trước: thuốc generic của hãng sản xuất khác có thể đổi màu, hình dạng và dấu khắc trong khi tên generic và hàm lượng giữ nguyên, và đó không phải lỗi. Chuẩn không bao giờ là viên thuốc có giống lần trước hay không — chuẩn đúng là nhãn lọ và viên thuốc có khớp với đơn thuốc từng mục hay không. Khi không chắc, hãy xử lý như lỗi: dừng lại, không uống, và gọi nhà thuốc để dược sĩ kiểm tra hồ sơ, thay vì bệnh nhân tự đoán. (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)

घर लाई गई दवा ग़लत लगे तो घबराएँ नहीं और अभी उसे न लें। Medication error का अर्थ है कि वास्तव में दी गई दवा prescription से मेल नहीं खाती: ग़लत दवा का नाम, ग़लत strength, ग़लत मात्रा, prescription से न मिलने वाले निर्देश, या किसी और की दवा। California की नियामक परिभाषा ठीक इसी बिंदु पर है — prescription या drug order से वह विचलन जो prescriber ने authorize नहीं किया, वही error है, जबकि patient को दवा दिए जाने से पहले पकड़ा और सुधारा गया विचलन पूर्ण error नहीं है। सबसे आम झूठा alarm पहले अलग करें: दूसरे manufacturer की generic दवा का रंग, आकार और imprint बदल सकता है, पर generic नाम और strength वही रहे तो यह error नहीं है। कसौटी यह कभी नहीं कि गोलियाँ पिछली बार जैसी दिखती हैं या नहीं; कसौटी यह है कि label और गोलियाँ prescription से मद-दर-मद मिलती हैं या नहीं। संदेह हो तो इसे error ही मानें: रुकें, न लें, और pharmacy को call करें, ताकि pharmacist record जाँचे, patient अनुमान न लगाए। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)

## 二、已经吃下去了怎么办：先看人，再看药

### 中文
如果发现配错时药已经吃下去了，顺序反过来：先看人，再看药。出现呼吸困难、意识改变、严重过敏样反应等急症信号，先打 911 或去急诊，不在药房柜台和电话队列里等。没有急症信号但已经服下错误药物的，尽快打美国中毒控制中心全国热线 1-800-222-1222，或直接联系药房与开方医生，把药名、规格、吃下的数量与时间说清，由专业人员判断要不要就医观察。不要做的三件事同样要记牢：不要自己催吐、不要拿别的药去对冲、不要因为暂时没感觉就当没事——有些后果出现得晚，专业判断要走在感觉前面。把药瓶、药片和收据都留好，它们是后面核对与报告的原始证据，不要整瓶倒掉或混进其他药瓶。

### English
If the error is discovered only after the medication has been taken, the order reverses: check the person first, then the drug. Emergency signs such as breathing difficulty, altered consciousness, or a severe allergic-type reaction mean calling 911 or going to the emergency department first, not waiting in a pharmacy line or a phone queue. If there are no emergency signs but the wrong medication was taken, call the national Poison Control line at 1-800-222-1222 promptly, or contact the pharmacy and the prescriber directly, stating the drug name, strength, amount taken, and time, so professionals can judge whether medical observation is needed. Three things not to do matter just as much: do not induce vomiting, do not take another drug to counteract it, and do not assume that feeling fine means nothing happened — some effects arrive late, and professional judgment must run ahead of how one feels. Keep the bottle, the tablets, and the receipt; they are the primary evidence for the checking and reporting that follow, so do not discard the bottle or mix the tablets into another container.

### Español (初译待校)
Si el error se descubre cuando el medicamento ya se tomó, el orden se invierte: primero la persona, después el fármaco. Señales de emergencia como dificultad para respirar, alteración de la conciencia o una reacción grave de tipo alérgico exigen llamar primero al 911 o ir a urgencias, no esperar en la fila de la farmacia ni en una cola telefónica. Si no hay señales de emergencia pero se tomó el medicamento equivocado, llame pronto a la línea nacional de Poison Control, 1-800-222-1222, o contacte directamente a la farmacia y al prescriptor, indicando nombre del fármaco, concentración, cantidad tomada y hora, para que los profesionales juzguen si hace falta observación médica. Tres cosas que no hacer importan igual: no provocarse el vómito, no tomar otro fármaco para contrarrestar, y no suponer que sentirse bien significa que no pasó nada, porque algunos efectos llegan tarde y el juicio profesional debe ir por delante de la sensación. Guarde el frasco, las tabletas y el recibo; son la prueba primaria para la revisión y el reporte que siguen, así que no tire el frasco ni mezcle las tabletas en otro envase. (初译待校)

## 三、还没吃时的核对动作：瓶签五项对处方

### 中文
还没吃下去的，核对动作在家里就能先做一遍，把结果带去药房能省很多来回。核对五项：一看患者姓名，是不是本人或家人的药，有没有拿错别人的袋子；二看药名，瓶签上的通用名与商品名与处方或出院带药清单是否一致；三看规格，同一药名下不同规格最容易混；四看数量与药片外观，数量对不对、同一瓶里有没有混进形状明显不同的药片；五看用法说明与有效期，标签上的服用说明与医生交代是否一致、药有没有过期。核对材料从哪来：处方副本、患者门户里的处方记录、出院带药清单、上一瓶的空瓶都可以作对照。五项里任何一项对不上，就整瓶停用、拍照留存、联系药房，不要只把可疑的几片挑出来、剩下的照吃。

### English
If nothing has been taken yet, the checking can start at home, and bringing the result to the pharmacy saves repeated trips. Check five items. One, the patient name: is this medication for oneself or one's family member, or is it someone else's bag. Two, the drug name: whether the generic and brand names on the label match the prescription or the discharge medication list. Three, the strength: different strengths under one drug name are the easiest to confuse. Four, quantity and tablet appearance: whether the count is right and whether any visibly different tablets are mixed into the same bottle. Five, the directions and the expiry date: whether the label directions match what the prescriber said and whether the medication is expired. Checking materials can come from the prescription copy, the prescription record in the patient portal, the discharge medication list, or the previous empty bottle. If any one of the five items fails to match, stop the whole bottle, photograph it for the record, and contact the pharmacy; do not pick out only the suspicious tablets and keep taking the rest.

### Español (初译待校)
Si todavía no se tomó nada, la revisión puede empezar en casa, y llevar el resultado a la farmacia ahorra viajes repetidos. Revise cinco puntos. Uno, el nombre del paciente: si el medicamento es propio o de un familiar, o es la bolsa de otra persona. Dos, el nombre del fármaco: si el nombre genérico y el de marca de la etiqueta coinciden con la receta o con la lista de medicamentos del alta. Tres, la concentración: las distintas concentraciones bajo un mismo nombre son lo más fácil de confundir. Cuatro, la cantidad y el aspecto de las tabletas: si el conteo es correcto y si hay tabletas visiblemente distintas mezcladas en el mismo frasco. Cinco, las instrucciones y la fecha de caducidad: si lo que dice la etiqueta coincide con lo indicado por el prescriptor y si el medicamento está vencido. Los materiales de contraste pueden ser la copia de la receta, el registro en el portal del paciente, la lista de medicamentos del alta o el frasco vacío anterior. Si cualquiera de los cinco puntos no coincide, suspenda todo el frasco, fotografíelo para el registro y contacte a la farmacia; no retire solo las tabletas sospechosas y siga tomando el resto. ## 四、回药房之后：药房要做什么，患者要拿到什么

### English
Taking the medication back to the pharmacy — or calling first and following its instructions — usually runs in four steps. First, pharmacist review: the pharmacy pulls the original prescription, the dispensing record, and inventory data, and compares label and tablets to locate where the error happened; the patient's part is to state the home-checking result clearly and hand over the bottle and receipt. Second, correction and continuity: the wrongly dispensed medication is taken back by the pharmacy and the correct one is dispensed anew; if the error delayed therapy, the pharmacy should coordinate with the prescriber rather than leaving the patient to carry messages between the two. Third, the record enters the pharmacy's quality assurance process (section six), and the patient may ask how the event is logged internally and how follow-up will come. Fourth, the money must be put right: the copay and the insurance claim for the erroneous fill should be reversed or refunded, and the patient should check the later statements so as not to pay for the error. The patient's own paper trail has four items: the date and time of discovery, the drug name and strength, who was contacted, and what was said — written down, these serve both the pharmacy's internal process and any board complaint later.

## 五、再往上走：投诉与报告的三个去处

### 中文

Second, the prescriber must be told in parallel: a wrong medication may already have affected treatment judgment, and the prescriber needs to know what the patient actually took and what was missed in order to adjust the plan — this step is not blame assignment but correction of the medical record. Third, voluntary safety reporting: the Food and Drug Administration's MedWatch receives reports of medication errors and other adverse events, and the Institute for Safe Medication Practices runs a national medication-error reporting program collecting reports from patients and professionals alike; such reports do not settle personal compensation — their function is to make recurring errors visible at system level. ## 六、加州单独注明：质量保证程序是药房的法定义务

### 中文
加州一节落在药房的制度义务上，法源是《加州法规汇编》第 16 编第 1711 条的质量保证程序（Quality Assurance Program）。要点四条。第一，每家药房必须建立或参加既定的质量保证程序，把配药错误记录下来、评估原因并定出应对，目的是改进服务质量、防错误重演。第二，调查有时钟：每一件被发现的配药错误都要进入质量保证审查，调查应在发现后尽快启动、最迟不超过 2 个工作日——教学算例：周一发现的错误，调查启动的最后期限是周三（周二算第 1 个工作日、周三算第 2 个），本地复算两遍一致。第三，审查记录要能在药房内即时调取，并至少保存 1 年。第四，患者沟通在制度里有位置：药师认定错误已经发生、且药物已被患者服用或错误造成临床上显著的治疗延误时，应尽快把错误事实与避免伤害的步骤告知患者或其代理人，并告知开方者。州执照局分工单独注明：加州药房与药师的发照监管机关是加州药房委员会，药房层面的错误处理先走药房质量保证程序，委员会一侧走投诉调查，两条线不互相替代。

### English
The California section lands on the pharmacy's institutional duties, sourced in the quality assurance program requirement at Title 16, California Code of Regulations, section 1711. Four points. First, every pharmacy must establish or participate in an established quality assurance program that documents medication errors, assesses their cause, and sets an appropriate response, with the purpose of improving service quality and preventing recurrence. Second, investigation carries a clock: every discovered medication error goes through quality assurance review, and the investigation must begin as soon as reasonably possible and no later than two business days after discovery — a teaching example: an error discovered on Monday must have its investigation started by Wednesday at the latest, Tuesday counting as the first business day and Wednesday as the second, recomputed locally twice with the same result. Third, the review record must be immediately retrievable in the pharmacy and kept for at least one year. Fourth, patient communication has a place in the system: where a pharmacist determines that an error occurred and the drug was taken by the patient or the error caused a clinically significant delay in therapy, the pharmacist should as soon as possible tell the patient or the patient's agent the fact of the error and the steps to avoid injury, and tell the prescriber as well. The state-board division is stated separately: the licensing regulator for California pharmacies and pharmacists is the California State Board of Pharmacy; error handling at pharmacy level runs first through the pharmacy's quality assurance program, while the Board's side runs through complaint investigation, and neither line substitutes for the other.

### Español (初译待校)
La sección de California recae en los deberes institucionales de la farmacia, con fuente en el programa de garantía de calidad del Título 16 del Código de Regulaciones de California, artículo 1711. Cuatro puntos. Primero, cada farmacia debe establecer o participar en un programa establecido de garantía de calidad que documente los errores de medicación, evalúe su causa y fije una respuesta adecuada, con el fin de mejorar la calidad del servicio y evitar que se repitan. Segundo, la investigación tiene reloj: todo error descubierto pasa por la revisión de garantía de calidad, y la investigación debe comenzar lo antes posible y a más tardar dos días laborables después del descubrimiento —ejemplo didáctico: un error descubierto el lunes debe tener su investigación iniciada el miércoles a más tardar, contando el martes como primer día laborable y el miércoles como segundo, recalculado localmente dos veces con el mismo resultado—. Tercero, el registro de la revisión debe poder recuperarse de inmediato en la farmacia y conservarse al menos un año. Cuarto, la comunicación con el paciente tiene lugar en el sistema: cuando un farmacéutico determina que ocurrió un error y el fármaco fue tomado por el paciente o el error causó un retraso clínicamente significativo de la terapia, debe comunicar cuanto antes al paciente o a su agente el hecho del error y los pasos para evitar el daño, y comunicarlo también al prescriptor. El reparto de la junta estatal se anota por separado: el regulador de licencias de farmacias y farmacéuticos de California es la Junta Estatal de Farmacia; el manejo del error en la farmacia corre primero por su programa de garantía de calidad, y el lado de la junta corre por la investigación de quejas, sin que una vía sustituya a la otra. ### 한국어 (초역 · 初译待校)

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)
California खंड pharmacy के संस्थागत कर्तव्यों पर है, जिसका स्रोत Title 16, California Code of Regulations, धारा 1711 का quality assurance program है। चार बिंदु। पहला, प्रत्येक pharmacy को एक स्थापित quality assurance program बनाना या उसमें भाग लेना होता है, जो medication errors को दर्ज करे, उनका कारण आँके और उपयुक्त प्रतिक्रिया तय करे, उद्देश्य सेवा गुणवत्ता सुधारना और पुनरावृत्ति रोकना है। दूसरा, जाँच पर घड़ी लगी है: खोजा गया प्रत्येक medication error quality assurance समीक्षा से गुज़रता है, और जाँच यथाशीघ्र शुरू होनी चाहिए, खोज के बाद दो business days से देर नहीं — शिक्षण उदाहरण: सोमवार को खोजे गए error की जाँच बुधवार तक शुरू हो जानी चाहिए, मंगलवार पहला business day और बुधवार दूसरा, स्थानीय रूप से दो बार पुनः गणना करके समान पाया गया। तीसरा, समीक्षा record pharmacy में तुरंत उपलब्ध होना चाहिए और कम से कम एक वर्ष रखा जाना चाहिए। चौथा, patient संवाद को व्यवस्था में स्थान है: जब pharmacist तय करे कि error हुआ है और दवा patient ने ले ली है या error से चिकित्सकीय रूप से महत्वपूर्ण therapy विलंब हुआ है, तो उसे यथाशीघ्र patient या उसके agent को error का तथ्य और हानि से बचने के कदम बताने चाहिए, और prescriber को भी बताना चाहिए। राज्य बोर्ड का विभाजन अलग से दिया गया है: California की pharmacies और pharmacists का लाइसेंस नियामक California State Board of Pharmacy है; pharmacy स्तर पर error निपटान पहले pharmacy के quality assurance program से चलता है, जबकि बोर्ड की ओर शिकायत जाँच चलती है, और कोई रेखा दूसरी का विकल्प नहीं है। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)
