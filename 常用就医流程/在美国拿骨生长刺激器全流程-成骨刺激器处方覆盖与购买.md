# 在美国拿骨生长刺激器全流程：成骨刺激器处方、覆盖门槛与购买

> 本文为个人学习笔记，不构成医疗建议。骨生长刺激器（成骨刺激器）是处方级耐用医疗设备（DME），须由治疗医师开具医嘱并留档影像证据后经合格供应商交付。与本仓《在美国拿医疗器械全流程：DME 处方、保险覆盖与租购》总览篇以指针分工：总览篇管 DME 通用规则，本篇专讲成骨刺激器这一品类的覆盖门槛与办理顺序。全文不写任何药物剂量。

## 一、先分清三类器械：电刺激分脊柱与非脊柱，超声是第三类

### 中文
骨生长刺激器不是一种机器，而是靠物理能量促进骨愈合的一类处方设备，联邦覆盖文件里按三类编码分开管理。第一类是非脊柱电刺激器（HCPCS 编码 E0747），贴在或绕在骨折部位外部使用，典型场景是长骨骨折迟迟不愈合。第二类是脊柱电刺激器（E0748），服务于脊柱融合手术之后融合失败或高风险融合的辅助场景，与第一类的部位和门槛完全分开，不能混着申请。第三类是超声刺激器（E0760），用低强度脉冲超声作用于骨折处，覆盖条件比电刺激更收紧：骨折部位与病因都有排除项。另有一类植入式电刺激器由手术环节处理，不走家用 DME 的订购路径，本篇不展开。把类别分对是第一步：类别错了，后面的影像证据与等待期都对不上，申请会在医学必要性审查处直接退回。监管侧，这类设备由美国食品药品管理局（FDA）按医疗器械上市路径管理，具体机型的适应证以其获批说明书为准；支付侧的覆盖门槛由 Medicare 国家覆盖决定（NCD 150.2，成骨刺激器）与 DME 地区承包商的本地覆盖决定（LCD L33796）共同框定，商业计划多参照这一框架再叠加自家政策。

### English
A bone growth stimulator is not one machine but a family of prescription devices that use physical energy to support bone healing, managed under three separate codes in federal coverage documents. The first is the non-spinal electrical stimulator (HCPCS E0747), worn outside the body at the fracture site, typically when a long-bone fracture is not healing. The second is the spinal electrical stimulator (E0748), serving failed spinal fusion or high-risk fusion support — a different site and a different threshold set from the first, and the two must never be filed as interchangeable. The third is the ultrasonic stimulator (E0760), applying low-intensity pulsed ultrasound to the fracture, with tighter coverage conditions than electrical stimulation: both the fracture site and its cause carry exclusions. Implantable electrical stimulators belong to the surgical episode rather than the home DME ordering path and are not covered here. Getting the category right is step one: with the wrong category, the imaging evidence and waiting periods that follow will not line up, and the claim comes straight back at medical-necessity review. On the regulatory side these devices reach the market through U.S. Food and Drug Administration device pathways, with each model's indications set by its cleared or approved labeling; on the payment side, coverage is framed by Medicare National Coverage Determination 150.2 (osteogenic stimulators) together with the DME contractor Local Coverage Determination L33796, and commercial plans commonly reference this framework under their own policies.

## 二、覆盖门槛：不愈合要靠两套间隔九十天的影像说话

### 中文
Medicare 口径下，非脊柱电刺激器（E0747）的覆盖要满足下列情形之一：其一，长骨骨折不愈合——影像证据显示骨折愈合已经停滞三个月或以上；其二，脊柱以外关节的融合失败，且距上一次手术至少已满九个月；其三，先天性假关节。长骨清单以 LCD 附录为准，包括锁骨、肱骨、桡骨、尺骨、股骨、胫骨、腓骨、掌骨与跖骨一带。不愈合的举证是硬环节：开始治疗之前要有至少两套 X 光片，前后间隔至少九十天，每套都要多角度投照，并附治疗医师的书面判读，写明两套片子之间没有临床意义上的愈合进展。把算例摆出来：第一套片子在 1 月 5 日拍、第二套在 4 月 10 日拍，间隔为 26 加 28 加 31 加 10 等于 95 天，超过九十天门槛 5 天，举证节奏才成立；若第二套只隔六十天拍，写得再像不愈合也不算数。脊柱电刺激器（E0748）的核心情形是脊柱融合失败且距上次手术至少九个月，或按 LCD 所列的高风险融合辅助情形逐项核对；算例：上次融合手术在去年 6 月、今年 3 月评估，6 月到次年 3 月正好 9 个月，刚好踩线，差一个月都不行。超声刺激器（E0760）要在不愈合举证成立的前提上再加两条：骨折不在颅骨与椎体、且不是肿瘤相关骨折；新鲜骨折与延迟愈合用超声刺激在 Medicare 口径下不算合理必要，这一条最容易被销售话术绕过去，要单独记住。

### English
Under Medicare rules, coverage for a non-spinal electrical stimulator (E0747) requires one of the following: first, nonunion of a long-bone fracture — imaging evidence that healing has ceased for three or more months; second, failed fusion of a joint other than the spine, with at least nine months elapsed since the last surgery; or third, congenital pseudarthrosis. The long-bone list follows the LCD appendix and includes the clavicle, humerus, radius, ulna, femur, tibia, fibula, metacarpals, and metatarsals. Proving nonunion is the hard step: before treatment starts there must be at least two sets of radiographs, taken at least ninety days apart, each with multiple views of the fracture site, plus a written interpretation by the treating practitioner stating there has been no clinically significant healing between the two sets. Work the example: first set on January 5 and second on April 10 gives 26 plus 28 plus 31 plus 10 equals 95 days — five days past the ninety-day threshold, so the evidence rhythm holds; if the second set were taken only sixty days later, no wording could make it count. For the spinal electrical stimulator (E0748), the core situation is failed spinal fusion at least nine months after the last surgery, or a high-risk fusion-support situation checked item by item against the LCD; example: surgery last June and assessment this March is exactly nine months — right on the line, and one month short fails. The ultrasonic stimulator (E0760) adds two further conditions on top of proven nonunion: the fracture is not of the skull or vertebrae, and it is not tumor-related; under Medicare rules, ultrasonic stimulation for a fresh fracture or delayed union is not considered reasonable and necessary — the point sales talk most often tries to talk around, and worth remembering on its own.

### Español (译稿)
En las reglas de Medicare, la cobertura del estimulador eléctrico no espinal (E0747) exige una de estas situaciones: primero, falta de consolidación de una fractura de hueso largo, con evidencia radiográfica de que la consolidación lleva detenida tres meses o más; segundo, fusión fallida de una articulación distinta de la columna, con al menos nueve meses desde la última cirugía; o tercero, seudoartrosis congénita. La lista de huesos largos sigue el apéndice de la LCD e incluye clavícula, húmero, radio, cúbito, fémur, tibia, peroné, metacarpianos y metatarsianos. Probar la falta de consolidación es el paso duro: antes de empezar el tratamiento deben existir al menos dos series de radiografías, separadas por al menos noventa días, cada una con varias proyecciones del sitio de fractura, más la interpretación escrita del profesional tratante indicando que entre ambas series no hubo consolidación clínicamente significativa. Haga el ejemplo: primera serie el 5 de enero y segunda el 10 de abril dan 26 más 28 más 31 más 10 igual a 95 días, cinco por encima del umbral de noventa, y el ritmo probatorio se sostiene; si la segunda serie se tomara solo sesenta días después, ninguna redacción lo haría contar. Para el estimulador eléctrico espinal (E0748), la situación central es la fusión espinal fallida al menos nueve meses después de la última cirugía, o una situación de apoyo a fusión de alto riesgo verificada punto por punto contra la LCD; ejemplo: cirugía en junio pasado y evaluación en marzo dan exactamente nueve meses, justo en la línea, y un mes menos no alcanza. El estimulador ultrasónico (E0760) añade dos condiciones sobre la falta de consolidación probada: la fractura no es de cráneo ni de vértebras, y no está relacionada con un tumor; en las reglas de Medicare, la estimulación ultrasónica para una fractura reciente o una consolidación retardada no se considera razonable ni necesaria, el punto que el discurso de ventas más intenta rodear.

## 三、办理顺序五步与费用口径：先证据、后医嘱、再供应商

### 中文
办理顺序不要倒。第一步，骨科随访先把愈合状态钉死：按覆盖门槛的节奏凑齐两套间隔达标的影像与书面判读，病历里写清骨折部位、受伤或手术日期、固定方式与此前处理。第二步，治疗医师开具医嘱（处方级文件），写明器械类别与编码方向、骨折部位与使用场景；医嘱在证据之前开、或类别写错，都是白跑。第三步，把医嘱与影像包交给在 Medicare 登记、满足 DMEPOS 供应商标准（42 C.F.R. § 424.57）的合格供应商，由供应商完成事先授权或预先判定（商业计划与 Medicare Advantage 多有这一步，Original Medicare 以事后审查为主、文件留档要求不减）。第四步，交付与培训：当面或按供应商流程确认佩戴位置、每日使用时长按机型说明书与医嘱执行（本篇不写统一时长数字，各机型口径不同）、充电与耗材更换、出现皮肤破损或疼痛加重时的停用与回诊路径。第五步，随访复查影像与记录留存：有没有长骨痂要靠复查片子说话，使用记录与病历一起留档，申诉或审查时这套文件就是全部依据。费用口径按 Part B 的 DME 通则理解：先过年度自付额，之后 Medicare 通常按核准金额的百分之八十支付、病人付百分之二十；示意算例：核准金额 1,000 美元且自付额已满足，1,000 乘以百分之八十等于 800 美元由 Medicare 付，余下 200 美元由病人或补充保险承担——这只是结构示意，成骨刺激器的实际核准价、是购买还是租赁分类、以及要不要竞标价，一律以交付当期的供应商报价与计划文件为准。加州单独一句：Medi-Cal 按 DHCS 的 DME 提供者手册与事先授权（TAR）口径处理，商业计划看各自 Evidence of Coverage，本篇的联邦框架不能直接套到个人保单上。

### English
Do not run the order backwards. Step one: orthopedic follow-up pins down the healing state first — assemble the two properly spaced imaging sets and written interpretation the coverage threshold requires, with the record stating the fracture site, the injury or surgery date, the fixation method, and prior treatment. Step two: the treating practitioner issues the order, naming the device category and code direction, the fracture site, and the use setting; an order written before the evidence exists, or with the wrong category, wastes the whole run. Step three: deliver the order and imaging packet to a qualified supplier enrolled in Medicare and meeting the DMEPOS supplier standards (42 C.F.R. § 424.57), which completes any prior authorization or advance determination (common with commercial plans and Medicare Advantage; Original Medicare leans on later review, with no reduction in documentation duties). Step four: delivery and training — confirm placement in person or per the supplier's process, follow the daily use time in the model instructions and the order (this note states no single duration figure, because models differ), charging and supply replacement, and the stop-and-return path if skin breaks down or pain worsens. Step five: follow-up imaging and record retention — whether callus is forming is a question the follow-up films answer, and the use log stays with the chart, because that packet is the entire basis in any appeal or audit. Read the cost through the Part B DME general rule: after the annual deductible, Medicare usually pays eighty percent of the approved amount and the patient twenty percent; illustration only — on an approved amount of 1,000 dollars with the deductible already met, 1,000 times eighty percent equals 800 dollars paid by Medicare and the remaining 200 dollars falls to the patient or supplemental coverage. This is structure, not a quote: the actual approved price for an osteogenic stimulator, whether it is classed as purchase or rental, and any competitive-bidding price are set by the supplier quote and plan documents in force at delivery. One California line: Medi-Cal follows the DHCS DME provider manual and treatment authorization (TAR) process, and commercial plans follow their own Evidence of Coverage — the federal frame in this note does not transfer directly onto an individual policy.

Bước một: tái khám chỉnh hình chốt trạng thái liền xương trước — tập hợp hai bộ phim đúng khoảng cách cùng bản đọc bằng văn bản mà ngưỡng chi trả yêu cầu, hồ sơ ghi rõ vị trí gãy, ngày chấn thương hoặc phẫu thuật, cách cố định và xử trí trước đó. Bước hai: người điều trị ra y lệnh, ghi rõ loại thiết bị và hướng mã, vị trí gãy và bối cảnh sử dụng; y lệnh viết trước khi có bằng chứng, hoặc viết sai loại, đều làm hỏng cả quá trình. Bước ba: giao y lệnh và bộ hồ sơ hình ảnh cho nhà cung cấp đủ điều kiện, đã đăng ký với Medicare và đáp ứng tiêu chuẩn nhà cung cấp DMEPOS (42 C.F.R. § 424.57), để họ hoàn tất phê duyệt trước hoặc xác định trước khi áp dụng (thường gặp ở chương trình thương mại và Medicare Advantage; Medicare nguyên bản dựa vào hậu kiểm nhưng nghĩa vụ hồ sơ không giảm). Bước bốn: giao hàng và hướng dẫn — xác nhận vị trí đeo, tuân theo thời gian dùng hằng ngày trong hướng dẫn của mẫu máy và y lệnh (bài này không nêu một con số thời lượng chung vì các mẫu khác nhau), sạc và thay vật tư, cùng đường dừng dùng và quay lại khám nếu da tổn thương hoặc đau tăng. Bước năm: chụp phim theo dõi và lưu hồ sơ — có mô sẹo xương hay không là câu hỏi do phim theo dõi trả lời, và nhật ký sử dụng lưu cùng hồ sơ bệnh án, vì bộ hồ sơ đó là toàn bộ căn cứ trong mọi khiếu nại hay kiểm tra. Đọc chi phí theo quy tắc chung của thiết bị bền thuộc Part B: sau khoản khấu trừ hằng năm, Medicare thường trả tám mươi phần trăm số tiền được duyệt và bệnh nhân trả hai mươi phần trăm; chỉ là minh họa — với số tiền được duyệt 1.000 đô la và khoản khấu trừ đã đủ, 1.000 nhân tám mươi phần trăm bằng 800 đô la do Medicare trả và 200 đô la còn lại thuộc về bệnh nhân hoặc bảo hiểm bổ sung. Đây là cấu trúc, không phải báo giá: giá duyệt thực tế của máy kích thích tạo xương, việc nó được xếp là mua hay thuê, và bất kỳ giá đấu thầu cạnh tranh nào đều theo báo giá của nhà cung cấp và tài liệu chương trình tại thời điểm giao hàng. Một dòng cho California: Medi-Cal theo sổ tay nhà cung cấp thiết bị bền của DHCS và quy trình phê duyệt trước (TAR), còn các chương trình thương mại theo Chứng cứ Chi trả của riêng họ — khung liên bang trong bài này không chuyển thẳng sang một hợp đồng cá nhân. (Bản dịch sơ bộ, 初译待校)

## 四、防坑清单：销售先行、部位不对与随访断档

### 中文
这一品类最常见的坑有四个。第一，销售先行：接到主动推销电话、对方承诺 Medicare 全额买单并要走了保险号码，却没有骨科医师的影像证据与医嘱——正规顺序永远是先证据后医嘱再供应商，来路不明的整套免单承诺是 DMEPOS 欺诈的经典开场，个人信息先收好。第二，部位与病因不对版：超声刺激器用在颅骨、椎体或肿瘤相关骨折上不在覆盖内；把电刺激的门槛套到超声上、或把新鲜骨折包装成不愈合申请，都会在审查处翻车，也耽误真正的处理窗口。第三，把刺激器当成手术替代品：不愈合的处理谱系里还有翻修固定、植骨与生物学辅助等外科路径，刺激器是其中一档非侵入选项，适不适合由骨科结合骨折间隙、固定稳定性与感染与否来判，感染性不愈合要先处理感染，顺序不能反。第四，随访断档：机器拿到手却不复查影像、不留使用记录，几个月后既说不清效果、也补不齐申诉文件；开始用之前就把复查日期排进日历。红旗信号单列：骨折部位疼痛突然加重、肢体肿胀发红发热、新出现麻木或无力、固定物松动外露、伤口渗液或发热——出现任何一件先停用并联系骨科或急诊评估，不要自行加用或继续硬撑。

### English
Four pitfalls recur with this device family. First, sales first: an unsolicited call promising that Medicare will pay in full and asking for the insurance number, with no orthopedic imaging evidence and no order behind it — the proper order is always evidence, then order, then supplier, and a full-payment promise from an unknown seller is the classic opening of DMEPOS fraud; keep personal information back. Second, wrong site or wrong cause: ultrasonic stimulation for skull, vertebral, or tumor-related fractures sits outside coverage; applying the electrical thresholds to the ultrasonic device, or dressing a fresh fracture up as a nonunion claim, fails at review and burns the real treatment window. Third, treating the stimulator as a substitute for surgery: the nonunion spectrum also includes revision fixation, bone grafting, and biologic adjuncts on the surgical side, and the stimulator is one noninvasive option within it — suitability is an orthopedic judgment weighing fracture gap, fixation stability, and infection, and an infected nonunion needs its infection handled first; the order cannot be reversed. Fourth, follow-up gaps: a device in hand with no follow-up imaging and no use log leaves both effect and appeal undocumented months later; put the recheck date on the calendar before starting. Red flags, listed separately: sudden worsening pain at the fracture site, swelling with redness and warmth, new numbness or weakness, loosened or exposed hardware, wound drainage, or fever — stop using the device and contact orthopedics or emergency care for evaluation rather than pushing on.

### हिन्दी (अनुवाद, 初译待校)
इस उपकरण परिवार के साथ चार जाल दोहराए जाते हैं। पहला, बिक्री पहले: कोई अनचाही कॉल जो वादा करे कि Medicare पूरा भुगतान करेगा और बीमा नंबर माँगे, जबकि पीछे न अस्थि-रोग इमेजिंग साक्ष्य हो न आदेश — सही क्रम हमेशा साक्ष्य, फिर आदेश, फिर आपूर्तिकर्ता है, और किसी अज्ञात विक्रेता का पूर्ण-भुगतान वादा टिकाऊ उपकरण धोखाधड़ी की क्लासिक शुरुआत है; अपनी निजी जानकारी संभालकर रखें। दूसरा, गलत स्थल या गलत कारण: खोपड़ी, कशेरुका या ट्यूमर-संबंधी फ्रैक्चर के लिए अल्ट्रासोनिक उत्तेजन कवरेज से बाहर है; विद्युत सीमाएँ अल्ट्रासोनिक उपकरण पर लगाना, या ताज़ा फ्रैक्चर को न-जुड़ने के दावे के रूप में पेश करना, समीक्षा में असफल होता है और असली उपचार खिड़की भी जला देता है। तीसरा, उत्तेजक को शल्य का विकल्प मानना: न-जुड़ने के स्पेक्ट्रम में स्थिरीकरण संशोधन, अस्थि-रोपण और जैविक सहायक भी शल्य पक्ष में हैं, और उत्तेजक उसी के भीतर एक गैर-आक्रामक विकल्प है; उपयुक्तता अस्थि-रोग का निर्णय है जो फ्रैक्चर अंतर, स्थिरीकरण स्थिरता और संक्रमण को तौलता है, और संक्रमित न-जुड़ने में पहले संक्रमण संभालना होता है — क्रम उल्टा नहीं हो सकता। चौथा, अनुवर्ती अंतर: उपकरण हाथ में हो पर अनुवर्ती इमेजिंग और उपयोग लॉग न हो तो महीनों बाद न प्रभाव का न अपील का दस्तावेज़ बचता है; शुरू करने से पहले पुनःजाँच की तिथि कैलेंडर में डालें। लाल झंडे, अलग से: फ्रैक्चर स्थल पर अचानक बढ़ा दर्द, लाली और गर्माहट के साथ सूजन, नई सुन्नता या कमज़ोरी, ढीला या खुला स्थिरीकरण सामग्री, घाव से रिसाव, या बुखार — उपयोग रोकें और मूल्यांकन के लिए अस्थि-रोग या आपात देखभाल से संपर्क करें, ज़बरदस्ती जारी न रखें। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद, 初译待校)
