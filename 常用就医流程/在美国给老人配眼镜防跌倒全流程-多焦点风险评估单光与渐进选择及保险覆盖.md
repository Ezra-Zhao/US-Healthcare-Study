# 在美国给老人配眼镜防跌倒全流程：多焦点风险评估、单光与渐进选择及保险覆盖

这篇讲什么：
- 配镜流程的防跌倒专格，不重复讲普通渐进镜怎么量瞳高：专讲老年人戴多焦点／渐进镜时，户外与楼梯场景的跌倒风险怎么评估、一副镜还是两副镜怎么分工、保险那本账怎么算。
- 先把证据摆正：多焦点镜片下半部分的近用区会模糊脚下的台阶与路缘，这不是感觉问题，有前瞻队列与随机试验两层证据；但随机试验同时说清了——分工配镜只对常出门活动的人有益，几乎不出门的人反而可能受害，不能一刀切。
- 再把选择做成框架：按户外活动量分人，按场景分镜，最后落到 vision plan、Medicare 与加州 Medi-Cal 三本账。

## 一、风险证据：下半视野模糊脚下，两层证据、两个方向

### 中文
机制先行：双光、三光与渐进这类多焦点镜片，下半部分是看近的度数。低头看脚下时，视线正好穿过这块近用区，台阶边缘、路缘与地面障碍会被模糊，边缘对比敏感度与深度知觉随之下降——下楼梯时最要紧的那一眼，恰好是最糊的一眼。第一层证据是前瞻队列（证据等级 B）：Lord 等 2002 年发表于《美国老年医学会杂志》（Journal of the American Geriatrics Society）的队列研究随访社区老人一年，发现常戴多焦点眼镜者跌倒的可能性是非多焦点佩戴者的两倍以上（调整后比值比约 2.29），且跌倒更多发生在绊倒、户外与上下楼梯的场景。第二层证据是随机对照试验（证据等级 A）：Haran、Lord 等 2010 年发表于《英国医学杂志》（BMJ）的 VISIBLE 试验，把常戴多焦点的老人随机分组，干预组另配一副单光远用镜、建议走路与户外活动时戴——结果全体跌倒差异不显著，但预先设定的亚组里，经常参加户外活动的人跌倒显著减少；而户外活动很少的人，户外跌倒反而增加。两层证据合起来的读法只有一条：风险真实存在，但解法是「按活动量分人分工」，不是「老人一律摘掉渐进镜」。本节数字均注明出处、只作定性框架的支撑，具体到个人先做第二节的评估。本篇为个人学习笔记，不构成医疗建议；全篇不写任何药物剂量与频次。

### English
Mechanism first: in bifocals, trifocals and progressives, the lower part of the lens carries the near power. Looking down at your feet, your line of sight passes exactly through that near zone, so step edges, curbs and ground obstacles blur, and edge-contrast sensitivity and depth perception fall with them — the glance that matters most on stairs is precisely the blurriest one. The first evidence layer is a prospective cohort (evidence grade B): Lord and colleagues, in a 2002 Journal of the American Geriatrics Society cohort followed for a year, found regular multifocal wearers more than twice as likely to fall as non-multifocal wearers (adjusted odds ratio about 2.29), with falls concentrated in trips, outdoor settings, and going up or down stairs. The second layer is a randomized trial (evidence grade A): the VISIBLE trial by Haran, Lord and colleagues (BMJ, 2010) randomized regular multifocal wearers, giving the intervention group an additional pair of single-vision distance glasses with advice to wear them for walking and outdoor activities — overall falls did not differ significantly, but in the pre-planned subgroup of people who regularly took part in outdoor activities, falls fell significantly, while among people with little outdoor activity, outdoor falls actually increased. Read together, the two layers say one thing: the risk is real, but the remedy is dividing the work by activity level and scene, not taking progressives away from every older adult. Figures here are cited to their sources and support a qualitative framework only; individual decisions start with the assessment in the next section. Personal study notes; not medical advice; no drug doses or frequencies appear anywhere in this article.

### Español (初译待校)
Primero el mecanismo: en bifocales, trifocales y progresivas, la parte inferior de la lente lleva la potencia de cerca. Al mirar hacia los pies, la línea de visión pasa justo por esa zona de cerca, así que los bordes de escalón, los bordillos y los obstáculos del suelo se borran, y con ellos caen la sensibilidad al contraste de bordes y la percepción de profundidad: la mirada que más importa en las escaleras es justo la más borrosa. La primera capa de evidencia es una cohorte prospectiva (grado de evidencia B): Lord y cols., en una cohorte de 2002 en el Journal of the American Geriatrics Society seguida un año, hallaron que quienes usaban multifocales de forma regular tenían más del doble de probabilidad de caer que los no usuarios (odds ratio ajustada de unas 2,29), con caídas concentradas en tropiezos, exteriores y subidas o bajadas de escaleras. La segunda capa es un ensayo aleatorizado (grado A): el ensayo VISIBLE de Haran, Lord y cols. (BMJ, 2010) aleatorizó a usuarios habituales de multifocales y dio al grupo de intervención unas gafas monofocales de lejos adicionales, con la recomendación de usarlas al caminar y en actividades exteriores — el total de caídas no difirió de forma significativa, pero en el subgrupo predefinido de personas con actividad exterior regular las caídas bajaron significativamente, mientras que en quienes tenían poca actividad exterior las caídas fuera de casa aumentaron. Leídas juntas, las dos capas dicen una sola cosa: el riesgo es real, pero el remedio es repartir el trabajo según nivel de actividad y escena, no quitar las progresivas a todos los mayores. Las cifras aquí citan su fuente y solo sostienen un marco cualitativo; la decisión individual empieza con la evaluación de la sección siguiente. Notas personales de estudio; no es asesoría médica; sin dosis ni frecuencias de medicamentos.

## 二、验光评估：先盘活动分工，再谈镜片

### 中文
给老人配镜，评估要多问三组问题。第一组是眼睛本身：完整屈光检查（球镜、散光与轴位）、下加光 ADD、双眼视功能，以及白内障、黄斑病变等会影响对比敏感度的眼病排查——视力表上读得出，不等于楼梯上看得清，对比敏感度与视野的问题要单独留意。第二组是活动分工盘点，这是本篇与普通配镜篇分工的地方：每天出门走路几次、走多远；家里与常去场所的楼梯有多少；开车还开不开；室内时间主要花在阅读、屏幕还是手工活；过去一年跌倒过没有、在哪里跌的、当时的眼镜是哪一副。第三组是身体与环境：下肢力量与平衡、用药里有没有影响平衡的类别（只问类别、不在本篇展开）、居家照明与扶手的大致情形。评估的出口是一个分工决定，而不是一张处方就完事：常出门的人走两副分工（第三节），几乎不出门的人以一副为主、把注意事项讲透。处方释放与拿处方比价的权利走联邦《眼镜规则》（16 C.F.R. Part 456）的老口径，本篇不重复。

### English
Fitting an older adult means asking three extra sets of questions. The first is the eyes themselves: a complete refraction (sphere, cylinder and axis), the near addition (ADD), binocular vision, and screening for eye diseases such as cataract and macular degeneration that erode contrast sensitivity — reading the chart is not the same as seeing a staircase, so contrast sensitivity and visual field problems need their own attention. The second set is the activity inventory, where this article parts company with a general fitting guide: how often and how far the person walks outdoors each day; how many stairs at home and in regular places; whether they still drive; what indoor time is mostly spent on — reading, screens, or handwork; and whether they fell in the past year, where, and wearing which glasses. The third set is body and environment: lower-limb strength and balance, whether any medication categories affect balance (categories only; this article does not expand on them), and the general state of home lighting and handrails. The output of the assessment is a division-of-labor decision, not just a prescription: regular outdoor walkers go to the two-pair split in the next section, while people who rarely go out stay mainly with one pair and get the cautions explained in full. Prescription release and the right to shop around follow the federal Eyeglass Rule (16 C.F.R. Part 456) and are not repeated here.

El segundo bloque es el inventario de actividades, donde este artículo se separa de una guía general de adaptación: cuántas veces y qué distancia camina la persona fuera cada día; cuántas escaleras hay en casa y en los lugares habituales; si sigue conduciendo; en qué pasa el tiempo interior — lectura, pantallas o manualidades —; y si se cayó en el último año, dónde y con qué gafas. El tercer bloque es cuerpo y entorno: fuerza y equilibrio de las piernas, si alguna categoría de medicamentos afecta el equilibrio (solo categorías; este artículo no las desarrolla) y el estado general de la iluminación y los pasamanos en casa. ### English
The framework has three tiers, matched to the activity inventory. Tier one, people who walk outdoors regularly: a two-pair split — one pair of single-vision distance glasses owns outdoor walking, stairs, and unfamiliar places; one pair of progressives (or bifocals) owns indoor reading, screens, and daily wear. The VISIBLE benefit appeared exactly in this tier, provided the "which pair for which scene" counselling lands and swapping before going out becomes a habit. Tier two, people who rarely go out: mainly one pair, and do not push a second pair — in the trial this tier's extra outdoor pair was associated with more outdoor falls, plausibly because infrequent outings make every use an unfamiliar one; here the work is teaching progressive-stair cautions in full: on stairs, tilt the head slightly so the gaze goes through the distance zone, use the handrail, and do not carry loads up and down stairs while wearing progressives. Tier three, in between: start with single-vision distance as a low-cost pilot, and revisit only if it wears well and outings really are frequent — with the same counselling during the pilot. Three lens-and-frame details: first, the peripheral aberration zones of progressives produce a swim sensation when scanning, so during adaptation avoid taking new glasses straight onto long staircases; second, the lower rim and lower lens are exactly the region used to look at the ground, and a frame that is too shallow squeezes the near and transition zones tighter, so leave enough vertical depth when choosing a frame (consistent with the fitting-height guidance in the progressives article); third, changeover discipline — use a new pair first on flat, familiar ground for a few days, add stairs after adaptation, and settle once what happens to the old pair (kept as backup or retired), so several pairs of different powers are never mixed at random.

## 四、防跌倒综合与保险覆盖：眼镜只是一环，账有三本

### 中文
眼镜之外只带一句：配镜解决的是「看清脚下」这一环，居家照明不足、楼梯无扶手、地面杂物与不合脚的鞋是另外几环，要与配镜同一次谈话里点到、另行处置，本篇不展开。账有三本。第一本，商业视力保险（vision plan）：典型结构是镜片与镜框津贴加选项自付，津贴通常按「一副」计——两副分工时，先把津贴用在贵的那副（多为渐进镜）上，单光远用那副多半走自付或折扣价，下单前让门店把两副的逐项估价打出来。第二本，联邦 Medicare：一般口径是不覆盖常规眼镜与常规验光（《社会保障法》第 1862(a)(7) 条的排除项），唯一的常规例外是白内障手术植入人工晶体之后——Part B 按假体装置口径（第 1861(s)(8) 条与 42 C.F.R. Part 410 一带）为每次这类手术后覆盖一副带标准镜框的眼镜，达到 Part B 免赔额后通常按核准额的 80% 与 20% 分担，升级镜片与第二副的钱不在这本账里；Medicare Advantage 计划常另带视力津贴，按各自计划文件走。第三本，加州 Medi-Cal：成人眼镜福利的历史上有过收缩与恢复，现行覆盖范围、频率与是否需要事先授权，以加州医疗保健服务部（DHCS）当期政策与提供者手册为准，办理前先核当期口径，不拿往年经验下单。教学算例（明标为示例价、本地复算两遍一致）：两副分工，渐进镜用掉 vision plan 津贴后自付 260 美元，单光远用镜现金价 90 美元，合计自付 260 加 90 等于 350 美元；术后标准镜示例，核准额 100 美元、免赔已满时 Medicare 付 80%、个人付 100 乘以 20% 等于 20 美元。HSA／FSA 可支付合格配镜费用（IRS Publication 502 把眼镜列为合格医疗费用），留好处方与收据。

### English
One sentence beyond the glasses: fitting solves the "seeing the ground clearly" link; poor home lighting, stairs without handrails, clutter and ill-fitting shoes are other links, to be named in the same conversation and handled separately — this article does not expand on them. The money sits in three ledgers. First, a commercial vision plan: the typical structure is a lens-and-frame allowance plus option copays, and the allowance usually counts per pair — in a two-pair split, spend the allowance on the expensive pair (usually the progressives) and expect the single-vision distance pair to run on self-pay or a discount price; have the store print an itemized estimate for both pairs before ordering. Second, federal Medicare: as a general rule it does not cover routine eyeglasses or routine eye exams (the exclusion at section 1862(a)(7) of the Social Security Act), and the one routine exception is after cataract surgery with an intraocular lens implant — Part B covers one pair of eyeglasses with standard frames after each such surgery under the prosthetic-device heading (section 1861(s)(8) and 42 C.F.R. Part 410 area), typically split 80/20 on the approved amount after the Part B deductible; upgraded lenses and a second pair are not in this ledger, and Medicare Advantage plans often carry their own vision allowance under their own plan documents. Third, California Medi-Cal: the adult eyeglasses benefit has contracted and been restored over the years, so take the current covered scope, frequency, and any prior-authorization requirement from the Department of Health Care Services' (DHCS) current policy and provider manual — check the current rules before ordering rather than relying on past years' experience. Teaching example (labelled sample prices, recomputed twice and consistent): in a two-pair split, the progressives cost $260 out of pocket after the vision plan allowance, the single-vision distance pair costs $90 at a cash price, for a total of 260 plus 90 equals $350; in a post-surgery standard-glasses example with an approved amount of $100 and the deductible met, Medicare pays 80% and the person pays 100 times 20% equals $20. An HSA or FSA can pay for qualifying eyewear (IRS Publication 502 lists eyeglasses as a qualifying medical expense); keep the prescription and receipts.

### Español (初译待校)
Una sola frase más allá de las gafas: la adaptación resuelve el eslabón de «ver el suelo con claridad»; la iluminación pobre en casa, las escaleras sin pasamanos, el desorden y el calzado inadecuado son otros eslabones, que deben nombrarse en la misma conversación y tratarse por separado — este artículo no los desarrolla. El dinero está en tres libros. Primero, el plan comercial de visión (vision plan): la estructura típica es una asignación para lentes y montura más copagos por opciones, y la asignación suele contar por par — en un reparto de dos pares, gaste la asignación en el par caro (normalmente las progresivas) y cuente con que el par monofocal de lejos irá a pago propio o a precio con descuento; pida a la óptica el presupuesto detallado de ambos pares antes de encargar. Segundo, el Medicare federal: como regla general no cubre gafas rutinarias ni exámenes rutinarios de la vista (la exclusión del artículo 1862(a)(7) de la Ley de Seguridad Social), y la única excepción rutinaria es tras una cirugía de cataratas con implante de lente intraocular — la Parte B cubre un par de gafas con montura estándar tras cada cirugía de ese tipo bajo el epígrafe de dispositivo protésico (artículo 1861(s)(8) y el entorno de 42 C.F.R. Part 410), normalmente con reparto 80/20 sobre el monto aprobado una vez alcanzado el deducible de la Parte B; las lentes mejoradas y un segundo par no están en este libro, y los planes Medicare Advantage suelen llevar su propia asignación de visión según sus documentos. Tercero, Medi-Cal de California: la prestación de gafas para adultos se ha contraído y restaurado a lo largo de los años, así que tome el alcance vigente, la frecuencia y cualquier requisito de autorización previa de la política y el manual de proveedores vigentes del Departamento de Servicios de Salud (DHCS) — compruebe las reglas actuales antes de encargar, no se fíe de la experiencia de años pasados. Ejemplo didáctico (precios de muestra señalados, recalculado dos veces y consistente): en un reparto de dos pares, las progresivas cuestan 260 dólares de bolsillo tras la asignación del plan, el par monofocal de lejos cuesta 90 dólares a precio en efectivo, total 260 más 90 igual a 350 dólares; en un ejemplo de gafas estándar posquirúrgicas con monto aprobado de 100 dólares y deducible ya cubierto, Medicare paga el 80 % y la persona paga 100 por 20 % igual a 20 dólares. Una HSA o FSA puede pagar el gasto de gafas calificadas (la Publicación 502 del IRS incluye las gafas como gasto médico calificado); guarde la receta y los recibos.

### 한국어 (初译待校)
안경 너머는 한 문장만 보탭니다. 맞춤은 '발밑을 또렷이 보는' 고리를 해결할 뿐, 집 조명 부족, 손잡이 없는 계단, 바닥 잡동사니, 맞지 않는 신발은 다른 고리이므로 같은 대화에서 짚고 따로 처리해야 하며, 이 글에서는 펼치지 않습니다. 돈은 세 장부에 있습니다. 첫째, 상업 비전 플랜(vision plan)입니다. 전형적 구조는 렌즈와 테 보조금에 옵션 본인부담금을 얹는 방식이고, 보조금은 보통 '한 벌' 단위로 셉니다. 두 벌 분담에서는 보조금을 비싼 벌(대개 누진)에 먼저 쓰고, 단초점 원용 벌은 자비나 할인가로 갈 것을 예상하십시오. 주문 전에 두 벌 모두의 항목별 견적을 뽑아 달라고 하십시오. 둘째, 연방 Medicare입니다. 일반 원칙으로 일상 안경과 일상 검안은 커버하지 않으며(《사회보장법》 제1862(a)(7)조의 제외 항목), 유일한 일상적 예외는 백내장 수술로 인공수정체를 넣은 뒤입니다. Part B가 가공 장치(prosthetic device) 기준(제1861(s)(8)조와 42 C.F.R. Part 410 일대)으로 그런 수술 뒤마다 표준 테 안경 한 벌을 커버하고, Part B 공제액을 채운 뒤에는 보통 승인액의 80%와 20%로 나눕니다. 업그레이드 렌즈와 두 번째 벌은 이 장부에 없고, Medicare Advantage 플랜은 대개 자체 비전 보조금을 자체 약관으로 둡니다. 셋째, 캘리포니아 Medi-Cal입니다. 성인 안경 혜택은 지난 세월 줄었다 늘었다 했으므로, 현행 커버 범위, 빈도, 사전 승인 필요 여부는 캘리포니아 보건서비스부(DHCS)의 현행 정책과 제공자 안내서로 확인하십시오. 지난 경험으로 주문하지 말고 현행 기준부터 확인하십시오. 교수용 예시(예시 가격임을 밝혔고, 두 번 다시 계산해 일치합니다): 두 벌 분담에서 누진은 비전 플랜 보조금을 쓴 뒤 본인부담 260달러, 단초점 원용은 현금가 90달러로, 합계는 260 더하기 90으로 350달러입니다. 수술 후 표준 안경 예시에서는 승인액 100달러에 공제액을 이미 채웠다면 Medicare가 80%를 내고 본인은 100 곱하기 20%로 20달러를 냅니다. HSA/FSA로 적격 안경 비용을 낼 수 있으며(IRS Publication 502가 안경을 적격 의료비로 올립니다), 처방전과 영수증을 보관하십시오.

### Tiếng Việt (初译待校)
Chỉ một câu ngoài chiếc kính: việc lắp kính giải quyết mắt xích «nhìn rõ mặt đất»; chiếu sáng kém trong nhà, cầu thang không tay vịn, đồ đạc bừa bộn và giày không vừa là những mắt xích khác, cần được nêu trong cùng cuộc trò chuyện và xử lý riêng — bài này không mở rộng chúng. Tiền nằm trong ba sổ. Thứ nhất, bảo hiểm thị lực thương mại (vision plan): cấu trúc điển hình là trợ cấp tròng và gọng cộng khoản đồng trả cho các tùy chọn, và trợ cấp thường tính theo «một cặp» — trong phương án hai cặp, hãy dùng trợ cấp cho cặp đắt (thường là đa tiêu cự) và dự kiến cặp đơn tiêu nhìn xa sẽ đi theo đường tự trả hoặc giá chiết khấu; yêu cầu cửa hàng in bảng ước tính từng mục cho cả hai cặp trước khi đặt. Thứ hai, Medicare liên bang: theo quy tắc chung, nó không bao trả kính mắt thường quy hay khám mắt thường quy (điều khoản loại trừ tại mục 1862(a)(7) của Đạo luật An sinh Xã hội), và ngoại lệ thường quy duy nhất là sau phẫu thuật đục thủy tinh thể có cấy thủy tinh thể nhân tạo — Part B bao trả một cặp kính với gọng tiêu chuẩn sau mỗi ca phẫu thuật như vậy dưới tiêu đề thiết bị giả (mục 1861(s)(8) và khu vực 42 C.F.R. Part 410), thường chia 80/20 trên số tiền được duyệt sau khi đã đạt khấu trừ Part B; tròng nâng cấp và cặp thứ hai không nằm trong sổ này, và các chương trình Medicare Advantage thường có trợ cấp thị lực riêng theo tài liệu chương trình của chúng. Thứ ba, Medi-Cal của California: phúc lợi kính mắt cho người lớn đã từng co lại và được khôi phục qua các năm, vì vậy hãy lấy phạm vi bao trả hiện hành, tần suất và mọi yêu cầu phê duyệt trước từ chính sách và sổ tay nhà cung cấp hiện hành của Sở Dịch vụ Chăm sóc Sức khỏe (DHCS) — kiểm tra quy định hiện hành trước khi đặt, đừng dựa vào kinh nghiệm những năm trước. Ví dụ giảng dạy (giá mẫu đã ghi rõ, tính lại hai lần và nhất quán): trong phương án hai cặp, kính đa tiêu tốn 260 đô la tự trả sau trợ cấp của vision plan, cặp đơn tiêu nhìn xa tốn 90 đô la theo giá tiền mặt, tổng cộng 260 cộng 90 bằng 350 đô la; trong ví dụ kính tiêu chuẩn sau phẫu thuật với số tiền được duyệt 100 đô la và khấu trừ đã đạt, Medicare trả 80% và người bệnh trả 100 nhân 20% bằng 20 đô la. HSA hoặc FSA có thể trả cho chi phí kính đủ điều kiện (Ấn phẩm 502 của IRS liệt kê kính mắt là chi phí y tế đủ điều kiện); hãy giữ đơn và biên lai.

### हिन्दी (初译待校)
चश्मे से परे केवल एक वाक्य: fitting 'ज़मीन को स्पष्ट देखने' वाली कड़ी को हल करती है; घर की कम रोशनी, बिना handrail की सीढ़ियाँ, अव्यवस्था और नाप के न जूते अन्य कड़ियाँ हैं, जिन्हें उसी बातचीत में नाम लेकर अलग से संभालना है — यह लेख उन्हें नहीं खोलता. पैसा तीन खातों में है. पहला, वाणिज्यिक vision plan: सामान्य संरचना लेंस और frame भत्ते के साथ विकल्पों पर copay है, और भत्ता आमतौर पर 'एक जोड़ी' के हिसाब से गिना जाता है — दो-जोड़ी विभाजन में भत्ता महँगी जोड़ी (आमतौर पर progressives) पर खर्च करें और single-vision distance जोड़ी के self-pay या छूट-मूल्य पर जाने की अपेक्षा रखें; ऑर्डर से पहले दुकान से दोनों जोड़ियों का मदवार अनुमान छपवाएँ. दूसरा, संघीय Medicare: सामान्य नियम के रूप में यह नियमित चश्मे या नियमित नेत्र-परीक्षण को cover नहीं करता (Social Security Act की धारा 1862(a)(7) का अपवर्जन), और एकमात्र नियमित अपवाद cataract surgery में intraocular lens implant के बाद है — Part B prosthetic device शीर्षक (धारा 1861(s)(8) और 42 C.F.R. Part 410 के आसपास) के तहत ऐसी हर surgery के बाद standard frame वाला एक चश्मा cover करता है, आमतौर पर Part B deductible पूरे होने के बाद अनुमोदित राशि पर 80/20 बँटवारे के साथ; upgraded लेंस और दूसरी जोड़ी इस खाते में नहीं हैं, और Medicare Advantage योजनाएँ अक्सर अपने योजना-दस्तावेज़ों के अनुसार अपना vision भत्ता रखती हैं. तीसरा, California Medi-Cal: वयस्क चश्मा लाभ वर्षों में सिकुड़ा और बहाल हुआ है, इसलिए वर्तमान covered दायरा, आवृत्ति और किसी पूर्व-अनुमति की आवश्यकता California के स्वास्थ्य देखभाल सेवा विभाग (DHCS) की वर्तमान नीति और provider manual से लें — पिछले वर्षों के अनुभव के भरोसे ऑर्डर न करें, पहले वर्तमान नियम जाँचें. शिक्षण उदाहरण (नमूना मूल्य स्पष्ट लिखे हैं, दो बार पुनः गणना करके सुसंगत): दो-जोड़ी विभाजन में progressives vision plan भत्ते के बाद जेब से 260 dollar, single-vision distance जोड़ी नकद मूल्य पर 90 dollar, कुल 260 जमा 90 बराबर 350 dollar; शल्य-चिकित्सा के बाद के standard चश्मे के उदाहरण में, अनुमोदित राशि 100 dollar और deductible पूरे होने पर, Medicare 80% देता है और व्यक्ति 100 गुणा 20% बराबर 20 dollar देता है. HSA या FSA योग्य चश्मा-खर्च का भुगतान कर सकता है (IRS Publication 502 चश्मे को योग्य चिकित्सा व्यय में रखता है); prescription और रसीदें रखें.
