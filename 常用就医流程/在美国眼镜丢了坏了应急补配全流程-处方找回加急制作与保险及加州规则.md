## 一、先定性：应急补配是找回处方再制作，不是重新看病

### 中文
眼镜突然没了，先分三种情形，逐项数得 3：整副丢失（落在外地、被盗、掉进水里）；镜架或镜片损坏（镜腿断、镜片裂、螺丝脱落到无法佩戴）；人在旅途或临近重要场合（考试、长途驾驶、演出）而手边无镜。三种情形的共同点是眼睛本身没有新症状，缺的只是矫正工具，所以应急补配的主线是两步——先把仍然有效的验光处方找回来，再找最快的制作通道按原处方重做。先划一条界：若同时出现眼痛、眼红、视力突然下降、外伤撞击等新症状，那已经不是补配问题，要先走眼科急诊线（见本仓看眼科篇），补镜排在诊疗之后。另一条界是备用镜：家里若有度数接近的旧镜，先拿出来顶上，应急等级立刻降一档——找处方与制作都可以按正常节奏走，不必为加急多付钱。来源：美国验光协会（AOA）与联邦贸易委员会（FTC）眼镜规则的公开口径。

### English
When glasses suddenly disappear, first sort the situation into three kinds, counted one by one as 3: the whole pair is lost (left behind on a trip, stolen, dropped into water); the frame or lenses are damaged (a temple arm snapped, a lens cracked, screws gone beyond wearing); or the person is traveling or facing an important occasion (an exam, a long drive, a performance) with no glasses at hand. All three share one feature — the eyes themselves have no new symptoms; what is missing is only the correction tool — so the emergency replacement main line has two steps: first recover the still-valid refraction prescription, then find the fastest production channel to remake the glasses to the original prescription. Draw one boundary first: if there are new symptoms at the same time — eye pain, redness, sudden vision loss, an impact injury — this is no longer a replacement problem; go to the eye-care urgent line first (see this repository's eye-care article), and remaking comes after care. The other boundary is a spare pair: if an old pair with a close prescription is at home, put it on first and the urgency drops a level at once — finding the prescription and production can both follow the normal timetable, with no surcharge paid for speed. Sources: the American Optometric Association (AOA) and the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Eyeglass Rule public materials.

### Español (译稿)
Cuando las gafas desaparecen de golpe, primero ordene la situación en tres clases, contadas una a una como 3: se perdió el par entero (olvidado en un viaje, robado, caído al agua); el armazón o los cristales están dañados (una patilla rota, un cristal agrietado, tornillos perdidos hasta no poder usarlas); o la persona está de viaje o ante una ocasión importante (un examen, un viaje largo en coche, una actuación) sin gafas a mano. Las tres comparten un rasgo: los ojos en sí no tienen síntomas nuevos, lo que falta es solo la herramienta de corrección, así que la línea principal de la reposición urgente tiene dos pasos: primero recuperar la prescripción de refracción aún vigente y después buscar el canal de producción más rápido para rehacer las gafas con la prescripción original. Trace antes un límite: si al mismo tiempo hay síntomas nuevos — dolor ocular, enrojecimiento, pérdida súbita de visión, un golpe — ya no es un problema de reposición; toca primero la línea urgente de atención ocular (véase el artículo de atención ocular de este repositorio), y la reposición va después de la atención. El otro límite es el par de repuesto: si en casa hay unas gafas viejas de graduación cercana, póngaselas primero y la urgencia baja un nivel al instante — la búsqueda de la prescripción y la producción pueden seguir el ritmo normal, sin pagar recargo por rapidez. Fuentes: materiales públicos de la American Optometric Association (AOA) y de la regla de gafas de la Federal Trade Commission (FTC). (初译待校)

## 二、处方找回四路：从原配镜处到旧订单，先核有效期

### 中文
处方找回有四路，逐项数得 4：一路回原验光处与原配镜店调档——验光诊所与配镜店都留有处方与配镜记录，打电话报姓名与生日即可请对方复述或发送处方全文，包括球镜、柱镜、轴位、双眼瞳距（PD），配渐进镜的还要原镜的瞳高与 ADD；二路翻旧订单——线上配镜账户的历史订单、电子邮件订单确认、支付记录里通常整表存着处方参数，截图留存即可直接复用；三路查 vision plan 与诊所患者门户——近年验光记录与处方单常同步在门户里，下载件与纸质处方同等可用；四路问同行家人代取——本人在外地时可授权家人到原店代为领取记录副本。四路都拿到处方后，先核一件事：处方上印的到期日。FTC 眼镜规则要求验光后处方自动交到患者手里，处方有效期由开方者按临床判断与州法写明；到期未过的处方可以直接拿去制作，过了期的一般要重新验光才能配——先核日期再找制作通道，免得跑到柜台才被退回。加州口径：验光师（OD）与眼科医师开方、配镜环节由注册光学机构（RDO）与注册配镜师（SLD）承接（Business and Professions Code 第 2550 条起的注册框架），补配同样走这套分工，店家核验处方与到期日是标准动作。来源：FTC 眼镜规则与加州 Medical Board、加州验光委员会公开口径。

### English
There are four routes to recovering the prescription, counted one by one as 4: first, pull the record from the original refraction office and the original dispensing shop — both keep the prescription and the dispensing record, and a phone call with name and date of birth gets the full prescription read out or sent, including sphere, cylinder, axis and binocular pupillary distance (PD), plus the original fitting height and ADD for progressive wearers; second, dig through old orders — online eyewear account order history, email order confirmations and payment records usually store the full parameter table, and a saved screenshot can be reused directly; third, check the vision plan and the clinic patient portal — recent refraction records and prescription forms often sync into the portal, and a downloaded copy works the same as paper; fourth, have a family member collect it — when away from home, authorize family to pick up a copy of the record at the original shop. Once any route delivers the prescription, check one thing first: the expiration date printed on it. The FTC Eyeglass Rule requires the prescription to be handed to the patient automatically after the exam, and its validity period is set by the prescriber from clinical judgment and state law; an unexpired prescription can go straight to production, while an expired one generally needs a new refraction first — check the date before hunting for a production channel, so the counter does not turn you away. California framework: optometrists (OD) and ophthalmologists prescribe, while dispensing runs through registered dispensing opticians (RDO) and spectacle lens dispensers (SLD) under the registration framework beginning at Business and Professions Code section 2550, and replacement orders follow the same division of labor, with the shop verifying the prescription and its expiration as a standard step. Sources: the FTC Eyeglass Rule and California Medical Board and Board of Optometry public materials.

## 三、加急制作三档：单光当日、渐进等工、临时过渡有边界

### 中文

### English
Production speed falls into three tiers by lens type, counted one by one as 3: the first tier is same-day pickup — single-vision stock lenses covering most common prescriptions, with in-store labs at large chains and mall shops the fastest, some finishing in about an hour, provided the prescription sits within stock-lens range; the second tier takes several days — progressive multifocals, high astigmatism, high-index thin lenses, prism and other custom lenses must go to a laboratory, and no amount of urgency makes them same-day, so pin down the pickup date when ordering and leave a contact for the ready notice; the third tier is online rush — online retailers' expedited production plus fast shipping fits people who do not need the pair today but want it within days, with PD and fitting-height data ready before ordering (see the general eyewear article for how those numbers are obtained). Interim options have clear boundaries: over-the-counter reading glasses can only cover near reading, cannot replace a distance prescription and must not be worn for driving; a spare old pair with a close prescription can cover all scenes; contact lenses must never be borrowed from someone else, and only count as an interim route when one's own contact prescription is valid and spare lenses or a trial channel are actually available. Sources: AOA public patient-education materials and the FTC Contact Lens Rule public materials.

## 四、钱怎么算：vision plan 多不赔丢坏，先算示例账再下单

### 中文
钱这一节先说清一个常见误会：多数 vision plan 的常规福利是周期性的检查与配镜额度（例如每个福利周期一副），眼镜丢失或意外损坏通常不在常规赔付里，不要指望报计划全额重配；能指望的是另外三件——部分计划或会员条款附带的镜片防损、保修加购项，镜架品牌方对材料与工艺缺陷的保修（只管缺陷、不管坐压踩坏），以及部分门店对自家售出眼镜的折价重配政策，三件都要在下单前问清、落到订单备注里。再算一笔示例账把加急的代价摆明：常规制作单光一副自付 59 美元，加急当日取自付 89 美元，加急差价 89 减 59 等于 30 美元；若再加购防损保修 25 美元，合计 89 加 25 等于 114 美元（本地复算两遍一致）。结论只有一句：有备用镜顶着就走常规档省下 30 美元，没有备用镜且要用镜，加急差价买的是时间、不是质量，按场合重要性决定。加州一侧另记：眼镜属普通消费品交易，价格与保修条款以门店书面政策为准，签单前把「保修管什么、不管什么、重配折价多少」三件问到纸面，口头承诺不算数。来源：主要 vision plan 公开福利口径与加州消费者事务部（DCA）公开消费提示。

## 五、安全线与防复发：没镜不开车，处方与瞳距从今天起存档

### 中文
安全线三条，逐项数得 3：第一，没有合适矫正就不要开车——看不清路牌与仪表时先停驾，改乘公共交通、网约车或请家人接送，加州 DMV 的视力筛查以矫正后视力是否达线为准，戴着不合适的旧镜硬开，风险与责任都在自己身上；第二，儿童与老人无镜期间防跌防撞——楼梯、浴室与夜间起身先有人扶或开灯慢行，孩子无镜上学先向学校说明、安排前排座位；第三，镜片碎裂伤过眼或镜架断茬划伤皮肤的，先处理伤口与眼表，再谈补配。防复发只做三件小事，逐项数得 3：把处方全文与 PD 拍照存进手机与云端各一份，下次丢镜时第二、三节整段跳过；长期保留一副度数接近的备用镜，放在家里固定抽屉而不是随身包里；每次新配眼镜时顺手把旧镜降级为备用镜，形成轮换。做到这三件，下一次丢镜就从「应急」降级为「普通重配」。来源：加州 DMV 视力标准公开口径与 AOA 公开患者教育口径。

### English
There are three safety lines, counted one by one as 3: first, do not drive without proper correction — when road signs and the dashboard are not clear, stop driving and switch to public transit, a rideshare or a family pickup; the California DMV vision screening is judged on corrected vision meeting its line, and forcing a drive in a wrong old pair puts the risk and the responsibility on the driver; second, prevent falls and collisions for children and older adults while uncorrected — stairs, bathrooms and getting up at night call for a helping hand or lights on and slow movement first, and a child going to school without glasses should have the school told and a front-row seat arranged; third, if a shattered lens has injured the eye or a broken frame edge has cut the skin, treat the wound and the eye surface first and talk replacement afterward. Prevention is just three small habits, counted one by one as 3: photograph the full prescription and PD and keep a copy on the phone and one in the cloud, so next time sections two and three are skipped entirely; keep one spare pair with a close prescription long-term, in a fixed drawer at home rather than in a carried bag; and at every new fitting, demote the old pair to spare status, forming a rotation. With these three done, the next lost pair is downgraded from an emergency to an ordinary remake. Sources: the California DMV public vision-standard materials and AOA public patient-education materials.

Nguồn: tài liệu công khai về tiêu chuẩn thị lực của DMV California và tài liệu giáo dục bệnh nhân của AOA. (bản dịch, 初译待校)

### हिन्दी (अनुवाद, 初译待校)
सुरक्षा-पंक्तियाँ तीन हैं, एक-एक करके गिनें तो 3: पहली, उचित सुधार के बिना गाड़ी न चलाएँ — जब सड़क-संकेत और डैशबोर्ड स्पष्ट न दिखें, तो गाड़ी रोककर सार्वजनिक परिवहन, राइडशेयर या परिवार की पिकअप पर आ जाएँ; California DMV की दृष्टि-जाँच इस पर होती है कि सुधरी हुई दृष्टि उसकी रेखा तक पहुँचती है या नहीं, और ग़लत पुराने जोड़े के साथ ज़बरदस्ती चलाने से जोखिम और ज़िम्मेदारी चालक पर ही आती है; दूसरी, बिना सुधार के समय बच्चों और बुज़ुर्गों को गिरने व टकराने से बचाएँ — सीढ़ियों, स्नानघर और रात में उठने पर पहले सहारा दें या रोशनी जलाकर धीरे चलें, और बिना चश्मे स्कूल जाने वाले बच्चे के लिए स्कूल को बताकर आगे की सीट व्यवस्थित करें; तीसरी, यदि टूटा लेंस आँख को चोट पहुँचाए या टूटा फ़्रेम-किनारा त्वचा काट दे, तो पहले घाव और नेत्र-सतह का उपचार करें, प्रतिस्थापन बाद में। पुनरावृत्ति-रोकथाम केवल तीन छोटी आदतें हैं, एक-एक करके गिनें तो 3: पूरे नुस्खे और PD की तस्वीर लेकर एक प्रति फ़ोन में और एक cloud में रखें, ताकि अगली बार खंड दो और तीन पूरी तरह छूट जाएँ; निकट नुस्खे वाला एक अतिरिक्त जोड़ा लंबे समय तक घर की तय दराज़ में रखें, साथ ले जाने वाले बैग में नहीं; और हर नई फ़िटिंग पर पुराने जोड़े को अतिरिक्त दर्जे पर उतारकर एक चक्र बनाएँ। ये तीनों हो जाएँ तो अगली हानि «आपात» से घटकर «सामान्य पुनर्निर्माण» रह जाएगी। स्रोत: California DMV की सार्वजनिक दृष्टि-मानक सामग्री और AOA की सार्वजनिक रोगी-शिक्षा सामग्री। (अनुवाद, 初译待校)
