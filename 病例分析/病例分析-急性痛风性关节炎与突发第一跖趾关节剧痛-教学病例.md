# 病例分析：急性痛风性关节炎与突发第一跖趾关节剧痛——虚构教学病例

> 虚构教学病例，不对应真实患者。本文为个人学习笔记，不构成医疗建议。如出现单个关节突发剧痛伴红肿发热、不能承重、高热寒战或全身不适，请立即就医，急症拨打 911。全文不写任何药物剂量、给药频次或具体处方用量，只写药物类别与处理原则。

## 一、病例呈现：半夜痛醒的第一跖趾关节

### 中文
李先生（化名），56 岁，男性，虚构病例。凌晨被右脚大脚趾根部（第一跖趾关节）的剧痛痛醒：关节又红又肿、发烫，床单碰到都痛，脚根本不敢落地承重。这种足部第一跖趾关节的急性发作有个专门的名字，叫足痛风 (podagra)，是痛风最经典的起病位置。两年前他有过一次很像的发作，当时几天后自己慢慢好了，没有认真查过。这次就诊测体温轻度升高，关节局部红肿热痛明显，没有外伤史。背景因素要一条条记清：有高血压，长期服用噻嗪类利尿剂；有高尿酸血症史；有慢性肾脏病 3 期；体重偏高；平日常饮啤酒。这些都不是确诊本身，却是判断痛风可能性与后面选药时绕不开的共病底盘。本节先立一条口径：单个关节半夜突发剧痛、红肿发热、不能承重，又伴体温升高时，不能先在家等它自己好——化脓性关节炎同样可以长这样，而它拖不得（见第三节）。当天急诊或急症评估、尽快做关节评估，才是安全顺序；出现高热寒战、全身毒性表现时直接拨打 911 或去急诊。

### English
Mr. Li (pseudonym), 56, is a fictional patient. He was woken in the early hours by severe pain at the base of his right great toe (the first metatarsophalangeal joint): the joint was red, swollen, and hot, hurt even under a bedsheet, and could not bear weight. An acute attack at this joint has its own name — podagra — and is the most classic presentation of gout. Two years earlier he had a very similar episode that settled on its own over several days and was never properly worked up. At this visit his temperature was mildly raised, the joint was markedly red, swollen, hot, and tender, and there was no history of trauma. The background must be recorded item by item: hypertension treated long-term with a thiazide diuretic; a history of hyperuricemia; stage 3 chronic kidney disease; excess body weight; and regular beer drinking. None of these is the diagnosis itself, but together they set the prior probability of gout and form the comorbidity base that later drug-class choices cannot ignore. One rule is set here: when a single joint erupts overnight with severe pain, redness, heat, and inability to bear weight, plus raised temperature, the patient should not simply wait at home for it to pass — septic arthritis can look exactly like this and cannot wait (see section three). Same-day emergency or urgent evaluation, with prompt joint assessment, is the safe order; with high fever, rigors, or systemic toxicity, call 911 or go to the emergency department directly.

## 二、确诊关键：关节液结晶与两个化验陷阱

### 中文
痛风的临床画像再像，也只是画像；能把诊断钉住的是关节液。只要条件允许、没有禁忌，急性单关节炎就应做关节穿刺，关节液同时送三件事：细胞计数、革兰染色与培养、偏振光显微镜下查结晶。痛风的结晶是单钠尿酸盐 (monosodium urate) 结晶，在偏振光下呈针状、负性双折射——形状与光学性质两头对上，才是痛风的硬证据。两个陷阱必须写清。陷阱一：发作期血尿酸可以正常。急性炎症期血清尿酸会往下掉，相当一部分患者发作当时测出的血尿酸并不升高，所以不能拿一次正常的血尿酸排除痛风；血尿酸更适合在发作缓解后复查，用作长期管理的基线。陷阱二：查到尿酸盐结晶，也不能排除合并感染。痛风与化脓性关节炎可以同时发生在同一个关节里，结晶阳性不是免查培养的理由——关节液培养与革兰染色该送还是要送，发热与全身表现越重，这一步越不能省。除关节液外，炎症指标、肾功能与共病评估帮助判断严重度与后续选药，但都不替代关节液这一步。

### English
However classic the clinical picture, it remains a picture; what pins the diagnosis down is synovial fluid. Whenever feasible and not contraindicated, an acute monoarthritis should be aspirated, and the fluid sent for three things at once: cell count, Gram stain and culture, and crystal examination under polarized light. The gout crystal is monosodium urate, needle-shaped under polarized light with negative birefringence — shape and optical behaviour together are the hard evidence for gout. Two traps must be stated plainly. Trap one: serum urate can be normal during a flare. Serum urate falls during acute inflammation, and in a substantial share of patients the level measured mid-flare is not elevated, so a single normal urate cannot exclude gout; serum urate is better rechecked after the flare settles, as the baseline for long-term management. Trap two: finding urate crystals does not exclude coexisting infection. Gout and septic arthritis can occur in the same joint at the same time, and a positive crystal result is no reason to skip culture — synovial fluid culture and Gram stain should still be sent, and the more prominent the fever and systemic features, the less this step may be omitted. Beyond the fluid, inflammatory markers, kidney function, and comorbidity assessment help judge severity and later drug-class choices, but none of them replaces aspiration.

### Español (译稿)
Por clásica que sea la imagen clínica, no deja de ser una imagen; lo que fija el diagnóstico es el líquido sinovial. Siempre que sea factible y no haya contraindicación, una monoartritis aguda debe aspirarse, y el líquido debe enviarse para tres cosas a la vez: recuento celular, tinción de Gram y cultivo, y búsqueda de cristales con luz polarizada. El cristal de la gota es el urato monosódico, en forma de aguja bajo luz polarizada y con birrefringencia negativa: forma y comportamiento óptico juntos son la prueba dura de la gota. Hay que dejar claras dos trampas. Trampa uno: el urato sérico puede ser normal durante el brote. El urato sérico baja durante la inflamación aguda y, en una parte sustancial de los pacientes, el valor medido en pleno brote no está elevado, así que un urato normal aislado no puede descartar la gota; el urato sérico es mejor repetirlo cuando el brote cede, como línea base del manejo a largo plazo. Trampa dos: encontrar cristales de urato no descarta una infección coexistente. La gota y la artritis séptica pueden darse a la vez en la misma articulación, y un resultado positivo de cristales no es motivo para omitir el cultivo: el cultivo del líquido sinovial y la tinción de Gram deben enviarse igualmente, y cuanto más marcadas sean la fiebre y las manifestaciones sistémicas, menos puede ahorrarse este paso. Más allá del líquido, los marcadores inflamatorios, la función renal y la valoración de comorbilidades ayudan a juzgar la gravedad y las elecciones posteriores de clases de fármacos, pero ninguno sustituye la aspiración.

Bẫy hai: tìm thấy tinh thể urat cũng không loại trừ được nhiễm khuẩn đi kèm. Gút và viêm khớp nhiễm khuẩn có thể cùng xảy ra trong cùng một khớp, và kết quả tinh thể dương tính không phải lý do để bỏ qua cấy dịch — cấy dịch khớp và nhuộm Gram vẫn phải gửi, và sốt cùng biểu hiện toàn thân càng rõ thì bước này càng không được bỏ. Ngoài dịch khớp, các chỉ số viêm, chức năng thận và đánh giá bệnh đi kèm giúp phán đoán mức nặng và chọn nhóm thuốc về sau, nhưng không thứ nào thay thế được chọc hút khớp. (bản dịch sơ bộ·待校)

## 三、鉴别诊断与红旗：先排最危险的化脓性关节炎

### 中文
鉴别按危险程度排，不按像不像排。第一位是化脓性关节炎（感染性关节炎）：表现为发热、关节红肿热痛、不能承重，再加上全身毒性——寒战、精神状态变差、血压不稳——或高危背景，如糖尿病、免疫抑制、人工关节、近期关节注射或皮肤感染入口。纪律只有一句：有疑问就先穿刺送培养，把感染的证据链先立住；处理顺序参本仓急性化脓性关节炎篇的原则——病情稳定者先抽液再谈抗感染，已出现脓毒症表现者先取血培养并立即治疗、穿刺随后跟上，绝不为了等检查把治疗拖住。化脓性关节炎拖延的代价是关节软骨在短时间内被破坏，所以它排在痛风前面排除。第二位是蜂窝织炎：炎症主要在皮肤与皮下，红肿范围更贴皮肤表面，关节被动活动受限的程度通常不如关节内病变那样全面，但两者重叠时单靠外观分不清，仍回到关节液说话。第三位是假性痛风，即焦磷酸钙沉积病 (CPPD)：结晶是另一副模样——菱形结晶、正性双折射，常累及膝等较大关节，急性发作同样红肿热痛，只有结晶形态与光学性质能把它和痛风分开。第四位是骨折与外伤：有明确外伤、扭伤或过度使用史时先想它，影像能快速分流。红旗汇总：高热寒战、全身毒性、疼痛与肿胀快速加重、关节周围皮肤破溃或水疱、免疫抑制或人工关节背景——出现任何一条，都按急症处理，立即就医、急症拨打 911，不在门诊慢排。

### English
The differential is ordered by danger, not by resemblance. First is septic (infectious) arthritis: fever, a red, hot, swollen, exquisitely tender joint that cannot bear weight, plus systemic toxicity — rigors, altered mental state, unstable blood pressure — or a high-risk background such as diabetes, immunosuppression, a prosthetic joint, a recent joint injection, or a skin portal of infection. The discipline is one sentence: when in doubt, aspirate first and send cultures, and establish the infection evidence chain before anything else. Sequencing follows the principles in this repository's septic arthritis article — in a stable patient, aspirate before antimicrobials; in a patient already showing sepsis, take blood cultures and treat immediately, with aspiration following promptly, never letting workup delay treatment. The cost of delay in septic arthritis is cartilage destroyed within a short window, which is why it is excluded ahead of gout. Second is cellulitis: inflammation sits mainly in skin and subcutaneous tissue, redness tracks the skin surface, and passive joint motion is usually less globally restricted than with an intra-articular process — but when the two overlap, appearance alone cannot separate them and synovial fluid must speak. Third is pseudogout, calcium pyrophosphate deposition disease (CPPD): its crystals look different — rhomboid crystals with positive birefringence — it often involves larger joints such as the knee, and its acute attack is just as red, hot, and painful; only crystal shape and optical behaviour separate it from gout. Fourth is fracture and trauma: with a clear history of injury, sprain, or overuse, think of it first, and imaging triages quickly. Red-flag summary: high fever with rigors, systemic toxicity, rapidly worsening pain and swelling, broken skin or blistering around the joint, or an immunosuppressed or prosthetic-joint background — any one of these means emergency handling: seek care immediately, call 911 for emergencies, and do not queue slowly in clinic.

### Español (译稿)
El diagnóstico diferencial se ordena por peligro, no por parecido. La primera es la artritis séptica (infecciosa): fiebre, articulación roja, caliente, hinchada y muy dolorosa que no puede apoyar, más toxicidad sistémica —escalofríos, alteración del estado mental, presión arterial inestable— o un contexto de alto riesgo como diabetes, inmunosupresión, prótesis articular, inyección articular reciente o una puerta de entrada cutánea. La disciplina cabe en una frase: ante la duda, aspirar primero y enviar cultivos, dejando establecida la cadena de evidencia de la infección antes que nada. El orden sigue los principios del artículo de artritis séptica de este repositorio: en el paciente estable, aspirar antes de los antimicrobianos; en quien ya muestra sepsis, hemocultivos y tratamiento inmediato, con la aspiración justo después, sin que el estudio retrase nunca el tratamiento. El coste del retraso en la artritis séptica es un cartílago destruido en poco tiempo, y por eso se descarta antes que la gota. La segunda es la celulitis: la inflamación está sobre todo en la piel y el tejido subcutáneo, el enrojecimiento sigue la superficie cutánea y la limitación del movimiento pasivo suele ser menos global que en un proceso intraarticular; pero cuando ambas se solapan, el aspecto por sí solo no las separa y debe hablar el líquido sinovial. La tercera es la seudogota, enfermedad por depósito de pirofosfato cálcico (CPPD): sus cristales tienen otro aspecto —cristales romboidales con birrefringencia positiva—, afecta a menudo a articulaciones grandes como la rodilla y su ataque agudo es igual de rojo, caliente y doloroso; solo la forma del cristal y su comportamiento óptico la separan de la gota. La cuarta es la fractura y el traumatismo: con antecedente claro de golpe, esguince o sobreuso, piense primero en ello, y la imagen clasifica rápido. Resumen de banderas rojas: fiebre alta con escalofríos, toxicidad sistémica, empeoramiento rápido del dolor y la hinchazón, piel rota o ampollas alrededor de la articulación, o contexto de inmunosupresión o prótesis articular: cualquiera de ellas impone manejo de urgencia, atención inmediata, llamada al 911 en emergencias, sin esperar turno tranquilo en consulta.

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)
Chẩn đoán phân biệt được xếp theo mức nguy hiểm, không theo mức giống nhau. Thứ nhất là viêm khớp nhiễm khuẩn: sốt, khớp đỏ, nóng, sưng và rất đau, không chịu được lực, cộng với nhiễm độc toàn thân — rét run, thay đổi trạng thái tinh thần, huyết áp không ổn định — hoặc nền nguy cơ cao như đái tháo đường, suy giảm miễn dịch, khớp nhân tạo, tiêm khớp gần đây, hay cửa ngõ nhiễm trùng qua da. Kỷ luật gói trong một câu: khi còn nghi ngờ, hãy chọc hút trước và gửi cấy, dựng chuỗi bằng chứng nhiễm khuẩn trước mọi thứ khác. Thứ tự xử trí theo nguyên tắc trong bài viêm khớp nhiễm khuẩn của kho này — ở bệnh nhân ổn định, chọc hút trước khi dùng kháng khuẩn; ở bệnh nhân đã có biểu hiện nhiễm khuẩn huyết, lấy cấy máu và điều trị ngay lập tức, chọc hút theo sau kịp thời, tuyệt đối không để xét nghiệm làm chậm điều trị. Cái giá của chậm trễ trong viêm khớp nhiễm khuẩn là sụn khớp bị phá hủy trong thời gian ngắn, nên nó phải được loại trừ trước gút. Thứ hai là viêm mô tế bào: viêm chủ yếu ở da và mô dưới da, vùng đỏ lan theo bề mặt da, và hạn chế vận động thụ động của khớp thường không toàn diện như quá trình trong khớp; nhưng khi hai bệnh chồng lấp, chỉ nhìn bề ngoài không tách được, vẫn phải để dịch khớp lên tiếng. Thứ tư là gãy xương và chấn thương: khi có tiền sử rõ ràng về va đập, bong gân hoặc quá tải, hãy nghĩ đến nó trước, và hình ảnh học sẽ phân luồng nhanh. ## 四、处理原则与长期管理：发作期只谈类别，长期盯住尿酸目标

### 中文
发作期处理只写原则，不写剂量与疗程，具体方案由接诊医生结合化验与共病决定。纲是抗炎镇痛：尽快控制炎症与疼痛，同时保护肾脏与胃肠。三条药物类别路线按共病取舍。其一，非甾体抗炎药 (NSAID) 类：抗炎镇痛的主力类别之一，但本例有慢性肾脏病 3 期与高血压，肾功能与胃肠风险都要先掂量，不合适就不走这条。其二，秋水仙碱类：痛风发作的传统类别，取舍时同样要看肾功能与药物相互作用。其三，糖皮质激素类：前两条路因肾功能、胃肠风险或合并症走不通时常用的类别，可经口服或关节内等途径由医生决定。非药物措施并行：发作关节休息、冰敷、抬高，少负重。还有一条 ACR 2020 痛风指南的明确口径：发作期间不新启动降尿酸治疗，也不停用已经在用的降尿酸治疗——怕的是血尿酸忽上忽下反而延长或加重发作，启动时机等发作缓解后与医生另行商定。长期管理看四件事。一，降尿酸治疗指征：一年发作达到 2 次或以上、出现痛风石、影像学已有骨与关节损害等情形，应与医生认真讨论长期降尿酸治疗，是否启动由专科评估决定。二，目标值：长期治疗的血清尿酸目标为低于 6 mg/dL，这是化验目标值，靠定期复查盯住，不是靠感觉。三，可改的背景：与医生复核噻嗪类利尿剂是否可换（降压药不可自行停）、减重、限制饮酒尤其啤酒，并管理血压、肾脏与代谢共病。四，随访与转诊：发作频繁、出现痛风石、诊断不确定、尿酸迟迟达不到目标、或慢性肾脏病进展时，转风湿科进一步评估。

### English
Flare management is stated as principles only, with no doses and no course lengths; the concrete plan belongs to the treating clinician, weighed against labs and comorbidities. The headline is anti-inflammatory analgesia: control inflammation and pain promptly while protecting kidneys and gut. Three drug-class routes are chosen between by comorbidity. First, the NSAID class: a mainstay anti-inflammatory analgesic class, but this patient has stage 3 chronic kidney disease and hypertension, so kidney function and gastrointestinal risk must be weighed first, and if they argue against it, this route is not used. Second, the colchicine class: the traditional gout-flare class, with kidney function and drug interactions likewise part of the choice. Third, the glucocorticoid class: the route commonly used when the first two are blocked by kidney function, gastrointestinal risk, or comorbidity, given orally or intra-articularly as the clinician decides. Non-drug measures run in parallel: rest the flaring joint, apply ice, elevate it, and limit weight-bearing. One explicit position from the 2020 ACR gout guideline: during a flare, do not newly start urate-lowering therapy, and do not stop urate-lowering therapy already in use — swings in serum urate can prolong or worsen the flare, and the start decision is revisited with the clinician after the flare settles. Long-term management has four parts. One, indications for urate-lowering therapy: two or more flares in a year, tophi, or imaging evidence of bone and joint damage should trigger a serious discussion of long-term urate-lowering therapy, with the start decision made on specialist assessment. Two, the target: long-term serum urate below 6 mg/dL — a laboratory target, held by scheduled rechecks, not by feel. Three, modifiable background: review with the clinician whether the thiazide diuretic can be switched (blood-pressure medication must never be stopped on one's own), lose weight, limit alcohol — beer in particular — and manage blood pressure, kidney, and metabolic comorbidities. Four, follow-up and referral: refer to rheumatology when flares are frequent, tophi appear, the diagnosis stays uncertain, urate will not reach target, or chronic kidney disease progresses.

दीर्घकालिक गुर्दा रोग और उच्च रक्तचाप है, इसलिए गुर्दा-कार्य और जठरांत्र जोखिम पहले तौलने होंगे, और यदि वे इस राह के विरुद्ध हों तो यह राह नहीं ली जाती। दूसरी, colchicine वर्ग: गाउट के दौरे का पारंपरिक वर्ग, जिसके चुनाव में भी गुर्दा-कार्य और दवा-अंतःक्रियाएँ देखी जाती हैं। तीसरी, glucocorticoid वर्ग: वह राह जो अक्सर तब ली जाती है जब पहली दो राहें गुर्दा-कार्य, जठरांत्र जोखिम या सह-रोग से अवरुद्ध हों, मुँह से या जोड़ के भीतर, जैसा चिकित्सक तय करे। गैर-दवा उपाय साथ-साथ चलते हैं: दौरे वाले जोड़ को आराम देना, बर्फ़ लगाना, ऊपर उठाकर रखना और वज़न कम डालना। ACR 2020 गाउट दिशानिर्देश की एक स्पष्ट स्थिति भी दर्ज है: दौरे के दौरान यूरेट-घटाने वाला उपचार न तो नया शुरू करें और न ही पहले से चल रहा उपचार रोकें — सीरम यूरेट के उतार-चढ़ाव से दौरा लंबा या बदतर हो सकता है, और शुरू करने का निर्णय दौरे के शांत होने के बाद चिकित्सक के साथ फिर लिया जाता है। दीर्घकालिक प्रबंधन के चार भाग हैं। एक, यूरेट-घटाने वाले उपचार के संकेत: एक वर्ष में दो या अधिक दौरे, tophi का होना, या इमेजिंग में हड्डी व जोड़ की क्षति, इन पर दीर्घकालिक यूरेट-घटाने वाले उपचार पर गंभीर चर्चा होनी चाहिए, और शुरू करने का निर्णय विशेषज्ञ मूल्यांकन से होगा। दो, लक्ष्य: दीर्घकालिक सीरम यूरेट 6 mg/dL से नीचे — यह प्रयोगशाला लक्ष्य है, जो निर्धारित पुनर्जाँचों से बना रहता है, अंदाज़ से नहीं। तीन, बदली जा सकने वाली पृष्ठभूमि: चिकित्सक के साथ समीक्षा करें कि thiazide मूत्रवर्धक बदला जा सकता है या नहीं (रक्तचाप की दवा स्वयं कभी न रोकें), वज़न घटाएँ, शराब — विशेषकर बीयर — सीमित करें, और रक्तचाप तथा गुर्दा व चयापचय सह-रोगों का प्रबंधन करें। चार, अनुवर्ती और रेफरल: दौरे बार-बार हों, tophi दिखें, निदान अनिश्चित बना रहे, यूरेट लक्ष्य तक न पहुँचे, या दीर्घकालिक गुर्दा रोग बढ़े, तो रुमेटोलॉजी को भेजकर आगे का मूल्यांकन कराएँ। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद·待校)
