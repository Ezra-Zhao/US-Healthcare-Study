# 病例分析：丹毒与边界清楚的鲜红斑、发热——虚构教学病例

> 虚构教学病例，不对应真实患者。本文为个人学习笔记，不构成医疗建议。全文不写任何药物剂量、给药频次或具体处方用量，只写处理类别与原则。红旗信号见第四节，出现时立即就医，急症拨打 911。
> 选题说明：本轮病例分析经 ls/grep 实查，「丹毒」grep 全仓零命中、无本体专篇；仓内已有《病例分析-急性蜂窝织炎与坏死性筋膜炎鉴别-教学病例》讲的是深层感染与坏死性筋膜炎的鉴别，本篇专讲位置更表浅、边界清楚隆起的丹毒，两篇以指针分工，不重复选题。

## 一、病例呈现：小腿擦伤后三天，一片鲜红顶着烧往上爬

### 中文
刘先生（化名），63 岁，男性，虚构病例。他有多年下肢静脉功能不全，小腿常年发胀，脚趾缝常有脱皮发痒，自己一直没当回事。三天前搬东西时小腿前面被纸箱边擦破一小块皮，当时只简单冲了冲。今天来门诊：擦伤一侧的小腿出现一大片鲜红色斑块，摸上去发烫、略高出周围皮肤，边缘像用笔画过一样清楚，斑块还在往上蔓延；人发冷、发热，自测体温偏高，腿一走路就疼。医生在红斑最上缘用笔做了标记并写上时间，又问了趾缝脱皮和既往有没有犯过同样的毛病。先把观察算例对一遍：上午 9 点标记线在踝骨上方约 10 厘米处，下午 3 点复看时发热发红的边缘已经推到约 16 厘米处，6 小时推进 16 减 10 等于 6 厘米，平均约每小时 1 厘米——这种推进速度本身就是要尽快处置、不能在家观察的信号。体温换算顺带核一遍：39 摄氏度乘 9 除 5 加 32 等于 102.2 华氏度。来源框架：美国感染病学会（IDSA）皮肤软组织感染指南公开口径。

### English
Mr. Liu (pseudonym), 63, is a fictional patient with years of venous insufficiency in the legs, chronically heavy calves, and itchy scaling between the toes that he never took seriously. Three days ago the front of one shin was scraped by a cardboard box edge and only rinsed briefly. Today a large bright-red patch covers that shin: hot to the touch, slightly raised above the surrounding skin, with an edge as sharp as if drawn with a pen, and still spreading upward. He has chills and fever and the leg hurts when he walks. The clinician marks the upper edge of the redness with a pen, notes the time, and asks about the toe-web scaling and any prior similar episodes. Check the observation arithmetic: at 9 a.m. the mark sat about 10 cm above the ankle bone; at 3 p.m. the hot red edge had advanced to about 16 cm, an advance of 16 minus 10 equals 6 cm over 6 hours, roughly 1 cm per hour — a pace that itself argues for prompt treatment rather than watching at home. Temperature check: 39 degrees Celsius times 9 divided by 5 plus 32 equals 102.2 degrees Fahrenheit. Framework source: the Infectious Diseases Society of America (IDSA) skin and soft-tissue infection guideline, public materials.

## 二、诊断框架：丹毒蹲在皮肤浅层，边界和隆起是它的名片

### 中文
丹毒（erysipelas）是累及真皮浅层和浅表淋巴管的急性细菌性皮肤感染，病原以 A 组链球菌最常见。它的名片有三张：一是红斑略隆起、高出正常皮肤，二是边界清楚锐利、和正常皮肤分界分明，三是起病急、常先发热寒战再出疹。与它最容易混的是蜂窝织炎（cellulitis）：蜂窝织炎位置更深，累及真皮深层和皮下脂肪，红斑多是平的、边界模糊一片。二者处置大方向相近，但先分清层次有助于判断严重度和随访时看什么。再往深处必须排除的对手是坏死性筋膜炎：疼痛与体征不成比例地剧烈、出现紫斑水疱、血压往下掉，是外科急症，不在本篇的门诊框架里。其他对手还包括下肢深静脉血栓（多为肿胀疼痛、发红发热的程度轻得多）、接触性皮炎（痒为主、少有发热）和急性痛风（多在关节上）。入口同样要查清：趾缝真菌感染、皮肤小破口、静脉功能不全和淋巴水肿都是反复发作的温床，本例三样占了两样半。无并发症的典型丹毒以临床诊断为主，是否抽血、做培养和影像，按全身毒性程度和合并症由医生决定。侵袭性 A 组链球菌病在加州属于应报告情形，普通丹毒则以临床处置为主。

### English
Erysipelas is an acute bacterial skin infection of the upper dermis and superficial lymphatics, most often caused by group A Streptococcus. Its calling cards are three: a plaque that is raised above normal skin, a sharply demarcated border, and an abrupt onset in which fever and chills often precede the rash. Its closest look-alike is cellulitis, which sits deeper — deep dermis and subcutaneous fat — and is usually flat with a blurred, fading border. Management points the same general direction, but sorting out the layer helps judge severity and what to watch. The deeper diagnosis that must not be missed is necrotizing fasciitis: pain wildly out of proportion to the findings, purple blisters, and falling blood pressure mark a surgical emergency outside this outpatient frame. Other mimics include deep vein thrombosis (swelling and pain with far less redness and heat), contact dermatitis (itch-dominant, rarely febrile), and acute gout (centered on a joint). The entry point matters just as much: toe-web fungal infection, small skin breaks, venous insufficiency, and lymphedema all invite recurrence, and this patient has most of them. Typical uncomplicated erysipelas is a clinical diagnosis; whether to draw labs, cultures, or imaging follows the degree of systemic toxicity and comorbidity. Invasive group A streptococcal disease is reportable in California; ordinary erysipelas is managed clinically.

### Español (译稿)
La erisipela es una infección bacteriana aguda de la dermis superficial y los linfáticos superficiales, causada con mayor frecuencia por el estreptococo del grupo A. Sus tres señas son: una placa elevada sobre la piel normal, un borde nítido y bien delimitado, y un inicio brusco en el que la fiebre y los escalofríos suelen preceder a la erupción. Su imitación más cercana es la celulitis, que asienta más profundo — dermis profunda y grasa subcutánea — y suele ser plana, de borde difuso. El manejo general apunta en la misma dirección, pero distinguir la capa ayuda a juzgar la gravedad y qué vigilar. El diagnóstico profundo que no puede escaparse es la fascitis necrosante: dolor desproporcionado respecto a los hallazgos, ampollas violáceas y presión arterial en caída marcan una urgencia quirúrgica fuera de este marco ambulatorio. Otros imitadores son la trombosis venosa profunda (hinchazón y dolor con mucho menos enrojecimiento y calor), la dermatitis de contacto (predomina el picor, rara vez con fiebre) y la gota aguda (centrada en una articulación). La puerta de entrada importa igual: la infección por hongos entre los dedos, las pequeñas roturas de la piel, la insuficiencia venosa y el linfedema invitan a la recurrencia, y este paciente reúne la mayoría. La erisipela típica sin complicaciones es un diagnóstico clínico; análisis, cultivos o imágenes dependen de la toxicidad sistémica y las comorbilidades. La enfermedad invasiva por estreptococo del grupo A es de declaración obligatoria en California; la erisipela ordinaria se maneja clínicamente.

## 三、处置框架：分层用药类别、画线追踪、抬高患肢，再堵上入口

### 中文

## 四、红旗信号与随访：推进不停、起疱发紫、血压发飘，一样都不能等

Keep three things from the visit: a photo of the marked border, the time treatment started, and the entry-point checklist for toe webs and skin breaks. California adds no special rule here; the emergency path matches the national one — when unsure whether to watch or go in, phone the clinic or nurse line first, describe the pace of spread and the systemic symptoms, and let the professional end triage. Closing line: erysipelas rewards early action — its sharp border is a convenience it hands you; mark it, recheck it, close the entry point, and keep a superficial infection superficial instead of letting it travel deeper.
