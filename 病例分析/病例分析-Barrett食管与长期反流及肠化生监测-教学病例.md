# 病例分析：Barrett 食管与长期反流及肠化生监测（教学病例）

> 完全虚构的教学病例，不针对任何真实患者。个人学习笔记，不构成医疗建议。处置只写原则与类别，不写药物剂量与治疗参数。

## 中文主文

### 第一节 虚构病例与关键拼图

虚构病例：58 岁男性，胃食管反流症状十余年，间断自行服用抑酸药，近两年吞咽固体食物时有梗阻感。胃镜见食管下段柱状上皮延伸，活检示肠上皮化生、无异型增生，诊断 Barrett 食管。关键拼图有三：长期反流暴露史、内镜下柱状上皮范围记录、活检病理分层（肠化生与异型增生有无）。与单纯反流病病例的分工在于：本篇的决策点不是症状控制，而是化生确认后的监测间隔与异型增生升级路径。

### 第二节 处置原则与随访

原则分三层：一是持续抑酸与生活方式类别管理（体重、睡前进食、烟酒），控制反流暴露；二是按内镜与病理结果进入定期监测轨道，无异型增生者按指南间隔复查内镜并系统活检，发现低度或高度异型增生时转入内镜下治疗评估通道（如消融与黏膜切除类别），并由消化专科主导；三是报警症状教育——进行性吞咽困难、体重下降、黑便或呕血须及时就医。筛查层面，高危人群（长期反流合并多项风险因素）的内镜筛查价值按美国胃肠病学会（ACG）Barrett 食管指南口径与主治医生共同决策。

## English

### Section 1 Fictional case and key pieces

Fictional case: a 58-year-old man with more than a decade of reflux symptoms, intermittent self-medicated acid suppression, and two years of solid-food sticking. Endoscopy shows columnar epithelium extending above the gastroesophageal junction; biopsies confirm intestinal metaplasia without dysplasia: Barrett's esophagus. The decisive pieces are the long reflux history, the documented extent of columnar lining, and pathology stratification by intestinal metaplasia and dysplasia. Unlike a plain reflux case, the decisions here concern surveillance intervals and escalation if dysplasia appears.

### Section 2 Management principles

Management has three layers: sustained acid suppression and lifestyle measures to control reflux exposure; scheduled endoscopic surveillance with systematic biopsies when no dysplasia is present, with referral into endoscopic therapy evaluation (ablation or mucosal resection categories) if low- or high-grade dysplasia is found; and alarm-symptom education — progressive dysphagia, weight loss, melena, or hematemesis requires prompt care. Screening of high-risk patients follows the ACG Barrett's esophagus guideline framework shared with the treating clinician.

### Sección 1 Caso ficticio

Caso ficticio: varón de 58 años con más de una década de reflujo, automedicación intermitente y dos años de atasco con sólidos. La endoscopia muestra epitelio columnar ascendente y la biopsia confirma metaplasia intestinal sin displasia: esófago de Barrett. ### Sección 2 Principios

Tres capas: supresión ácida sostenida y medidas de estilo de vida; vigilancia endoscópica programada con biopsias sistemáticas si no hay displasia, y derivación a evaluación de terapia endoscópica si aparece displasia; y educación sobre síntomas de alarma —disfagia progresiva, pérdida de peso, melena o hematemesis— que exigen atención pronta. El cribado de alto riesgo sigue el marco de la guía ACG.

세 층위: 지속적인 위산 억제와 생활습관 관리, 이형성이 없으면 정기 내시경 감시와 체계적 생검, 이형성 발견 시 내시경 치료 평가로 연계, 그리고 진행성 연하곤란·체중 감소·흑변·토혈 같은 경고 증상 교육이다. 고위험군 선별은 ACG 바렛 식도 가이드라인 틀을 따른다.
