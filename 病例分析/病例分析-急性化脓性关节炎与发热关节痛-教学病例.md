# 病例分析：急性化脓性关节炎与发热关节痛（教学病例）

> 虚构教学病例，仅用于学习讨论，不构成医疗建议。全文只写诊断与处理原则，不涉及任何药物剂量；出现发热伴关节红肿热痛请及时就医，急症拨打 911。

## 一、病例呈现：膝盖一夜之间肿得不能落地（虚构病例）

### 中文
王女士，68 岁，有 2 型糖尿病史。两天前右膝开始隐隐作痛，昨夜疼痛骤然加重，膝盖明显肿胀、发红、发热，腿不能伸直、不能负重，今晨体温 38.9°C，自行服了退烧药仍来急诊。查体：右膝肿胀发热，关节活动因疼痛大幅受限，被动活动也痛；左膝与双手关节无红肿。实验室：外周血白细胞与 C 反应蛋白明显升高。问题来了：这是痛风发作、蜂窝织炎，还是关节腔里出了感染？教学要点先行：单个关节在数小时到两天内出现红、肿、热、痛加发热，第一位要排除的就是化脓性（细菌性）关节炎——因为它能在几天内不可逆地毁掉软骨，是骨科急症，不是「观察两天再说」的病。

### English
Ms. Wang, 68, has type 2 diabetes. Two days ago her right knee began to ache dully; overnight the pain escalated sharply, and the knee is now visibly swollen, red, and hot, cannot be straightened, and cannot bear weight. This morning her temperature is 38.9 °C, and she comes to the emergency department despite taking an antipyretic. Examination: the right knee is swollen and hot with severely pain-limited motion, passive movement also painful; the left knee and hands are not red or swollen. Labs: peripheral white cell count and C-reactive protein are markedly elevated. Is this gout, cellulitis, or an infection inside the joint space? The teaching point comes first: when a single joint becomes red, swollen, hot, and painful over hours to two days with fever, the first diagnosis to exclude is septic (bacterial) arthritis — it can irreversibly destroy cartilage within days and is an orthopedic emergency, not a "watch it for two days" condition.

## 二、定性靠关节穿刺：先抽液，再谈抗生素顺序

### 中文
确诊的枢纽是关节穿刺抽液 (arthrocentesis)：在无菌操作下抽出关节液送检——细胞计数与分类、革兰染色、细菌培养、偏光显微镜查晶体，并同时抽血培养与炎症指标。经典教学数字是关节液白细胞超过 50,000/µL 且中性粒细胞占绝对多数时高度提示感染，但这个数字不是开关：早期感染、部分免疫抑制患者或已用过抗生素的人，计数可以没那么高；痛风与假痛风的晶体性炎症同样可以冲得很高，反过来，查到晶体也不能排除合并感染。顺序原则要分两头记：病人情况稳定时，先穿刺、再开始经验性抗生素，这样培养阳性率最高；病人已经出现脓毒症表现或穿刺会显著延误时，先抽血培养、立即给抗感染治疗，穿刺随后跟上——救命优先于标本完美。关节腔内注射激素在疑似感染时绝对禁止，这条是红线。

### English
The diagnostic pivot is arthrocentesis: under sterile technique, joint fluid is aspirated for cell count and differential, Gram stain, culture, and polarized microscopy for crystals, alongside blood cultures and inflammatory markers. The classic teaching number is a synovial white cell count above 50,000/µL with a strong neutrophil predominance pointing toward infection — but the number is not a switch. Early infection, immunosuppression, or prior antibiotics can yield lower counts, crystal arthritis from gout or pseudogout can run just as high, and finding crystals does not exclude coexisting infection. Sequencing has two ends. In a stable patient, aspirate first and start empiric antibiotics after, which maximizes culture yield. In a patient already septic, or when aspiration would substantially delay treatment, draw blood cultures, start anti-infective therapy immediately, and aspirate as soon as feasible — saving life outranks specimen perfection. Injecting corticosteroid into a joint with suspected infection is absolutely prohibited; that is a red line.

### Español (译稿)
El eje diagnóstico es la artrocentesis: con técnica estéril se aspira líquido articular para recuento celular y fórmula, tinción de Gram, cultivo y microscopía de luz polarizada en busca de cristales, junto con hemocultivos y marcadores inflamatorios. La cifra clásica de enseñanza es un recuento de leucocitos en líquido sinovial superior a 50.000/µL con predominio claro de neutrófilos, orientativo de infección; pero la cifra no es un interruptor. La infección precoz, la inmunosupresión o los antibióticos previos pueden dar recuentos más bajos; la artritis por cristales de gota o pseudogota puede alcanzar cifras igual de altas, y hallar cristales no excluye una infección coexistente. El orden tiene dos extremos: en el paciente estable, primero aspirar y después iniciar el antibiótico empírico, para maximizar el rendimiento del cultivo; en el paciente ya séptico, o cuando aspirar retrasaría mucho el tratamiento, extraer hemocultivos, iniciar de inmediato el tratamiento antiinfeccioso y aspirar en cuanto sea posible: salvar la vida manda sobre la perfección de la muestra. Inyectar corticoides en una articulación con sospecha de infección está absolutamente prohibido: es una línea roja.

निदान की धुरी जोड़-आकर्षण (arthrocentesis) है: विसंक्रमित तकनीक से जोड़ द्रव निकालकर कोशिका गणना और विभेदन, Gram अभिरंजन, संवर्धन, और क्रिस्टलों के लिए ध्रुवीकृत सूक्ष्मदर्शन भेजा जाता है, साथ में रक्त संवर्धन और शोथ चिह्नक। पारंपरिक शिक्षण संख्या यह है कि श्लेष द्रव में श्वेत कोशिकाएँ 50,000/µL से अधिक और न्यूट्रोफ़िल की स्पष्ट प्रधानता संक्रमण की ओर इशारा करती है — किंतु यह संख्या कोई स्विच नहीं है। प्रारंभिक संक्रमण, प्रतिरक्षा दमन या पहले दिए गए एंटीबायोटिक से गणना कम आ सकती है; गाउट या स्यूडोगाउट की क्रिस्टल गठिया भी उतनी ही ऊँची जा सकती है, और क्रिस्टल मिल जाने से सह-विद्यमान संक्रमण बाहर नहीं होता। क्रम के दो सिरे हैं। स्थिर रोगी में, पहले आकर्षण करें और फिर आनुभविक एंटीबायोटिक शुरू करें, जिससे संवर्धन सफलता अधिकतम होती है। पहले से सेप्टिक रोगी में, या जब आकर्षण से उपचार में बड़ी देरी हो, रक्त संवर्धन लें, तुरंत संक्रमण-रोधी उपचार शुरू करें, और जितनी जल्दी संभव हो आकर्षण करें — जीवन बचाना नमूने की पूर्णता से ऊपर है। संक्रमण के संदेह वाले जोड़ में कॉर्टिकोस्टेरॉइड इंजेक्शन पूर्णतः निषिद्ध है; यह लाल रेखा है। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद·待校)

## 三、处理框架：引流加抗感染，骨科尽早介入

### 中文
化脓性关节炎的处理只有两个支柱，缺一不可：一是引流，二是抗感染。感染关节液是脓，留在腔里就持续侵蚀软骨并维持全身炎症，所以确诊或高度怀疑后要尽快请骨科评估引流方式——关节镜冲洗引流、关节切开引流，或在特定关节与情形下反复针吸引流，由骨科按关节部位、感染程度与病人情况决定；髋关节等深部关节与假体关节感染处理更急、路径更专门。抗感染一侧只记原则：先留好培养标本再尽早开始经验性静脉抗感染治疗，经验覆盖要考虑金黄色葡萄球菌 (含 MRSA 风险评估) 与链球菌等常见病原，再按培养与药敏结果收窄（降阶梯）；年轻、性活跃患者的播散性淋球菌感染是单独的鉴别与覆盖线。疗程以周计、静脉转口服的时机由感染科与骨科共同评估，不在笔记里写具体方案。假体关节（人工关节置换后）出现同样画面是另一条更复杂的路径，须假体感染专科团队介入。

### English
Management rests on two pillars, and neither is optional: drainage and anti-infective therapy. Infected joint fluid is pus; left in the cavity it keeps eroding cartilage and sustaining systemic inflammation, so once the diagnosis is made or strongly suspected, orthopedics should be consulted promptly to choose the drainage route — arthroscopic washout, open arthrotomy drainage, or, for selected joints and situations, repeated needle aspiration — decided by joint, severity, and patient factors. Deep joints such as the hip, and infected prosthetic joints, are more urgent and follow more specialized pathways. On the anti-infective side, keep only principles: secure cultures first, then start empiric intravenous therapy early; empiric coverage is built around common pathogens such as Staphylococcus aureus (with MRSA risk assessment) and streptococci, then narrowed by culture and susceptibility results (de-escalation). Disseminated gonococcal infection in a young, sexually active patient is a separate diagnostic and coverage track. Courses run in weeks; the intravenous-to-oral switch is judged jointly by infectious diseases and orthopedics, and no specific regimen belongs in a study note. The same presentation in a prosthetic joint follows a different, more complex pathway requiring a prosthetic-joint infection team.

## 四、鉴别与儿童注记：晶体、蜂窝织炎与 Kocher 标准

### 中文

### English
Line up the differential by "looks alike, is not alike." Gout and pseudogout start just as abruptly with the same red, hot, swollen joint, and crystals on polarized microscopy support crystal arthritis — but as above, crystals do not close the door on infection; the two can coexist, and with high fever and a very high white count, following the infection pathway is never wrong. Cellulitis puts the redness and heat in the skin and subcutaneous tissue around the joint, with relatively less pain on passive joint motion — when the border is blurry, treat as joint infection and let aspiration decide. Reactive arthritis and rheumatoid flares are usually polyarticular or set against a known history; a first hot monoarthritis still excludes infection first. A separate note for children: in a child with hip pain and fever, separate septic hip arthritis from transient synovitis — the classic Kocher criteria use four clinical predictors (non-weight-bearing, fever, elevated ESR, elevated white count) to estimate the probability of a septic hip, and a high estimate goes down the emergency imaging and surgical pathway, because the child's hip cannot wait: rising intracapsular pressure can compromise the femoral head's blood supply.

## 五、复盘：这个病例真正考的五步

### 中文
复盘五步。一，识别：单关节红肿热痛加发热，先想化脓性关节炎，糖尿病、类风湿、人工关节、静脉药瘾与近期关节穿刺或外伤都是危险因素。二，定位：关节穿刺是枢纽检查，关节液送细胞计数、革兰染色、培养与晶体，血培养同行。三，排序：稳定者先抽液后抗生素；已脓毒症者先抽血培养、立即治疗、穿刺随后。四，处置：引流与抗感染双支柱，骨科尽早介入，疑似感染关节绝不注激素。五，边界：本篇为虚构教学病例，只写原则、不写剂量；真实诊疗以接诊团队判断与当期指南为准，关节红肿热痛伴发热请及时就医、急症拨打 911。

### English
Five steps to review. (1) Recognize: a hot, swollen, painful single joint with fever means think septic arthritis first; diabetes, rheumatoid arthritis, a prosthetic joint, injection drug use, and recent joint injection or trauma are risk factors. (2) Localize: arthrocentesis is the pivotal test — synovial cell count, Gram stain, culture, and crystals, with blood cultures alongside. (3) Sequence: in the stable patient, aspirate before antibiotics; in the septic patient, blood cultures, immediate therapy, aspiration right after. (4) Treat: drainage plus anti-infective therapy as twin pillars, orthopedics involved early, and never inject steroid into a joint with suspected infection. (5) Boundaries: this is a fictional teaching case stating principles only, with no drug doses; real care follows the treating team and current guidelines — a red, hot, swollen, painful joint with fever warrants prompt care, and emergencies warrant 911.

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)
Năm bước ôn lại. (1) Nhận diện: một khớp đơn độc nóng, sưng, đau kèm sốt thì nghĩ viêm khớp nhiễm khuẩn trước; đái tháo đường, viêm khớp dạng thấp, khớp nhân tạo, tiêm chích ma túy, và mới chọc khớp hay chấn thương khớp là các yếu tố nguy cơ. (2) Định vị: chọc hút khớp là xét nghiệm trục — đếm tế bào dịch khớp, nhuộm Gram, nuôi cấy và tìm tinh thể, cùng cấy máu song song. (3) Thứ tự: bệnh nhân ổn định thì chọc hút trước kháng sinh; bệnh nhân nhiễm khuẩn huyết thì cấy máu, điều trị ngay, chọc hút liền sau. (4) Xử trí: dẫn lưu cộng điều trị chống nhiễm trùng là hai trụ cột song sinh, chỉnh hình tham gia sớm, và tuyệt đối không tiêm steroid vào khớp nghi nhiễm trùng. (5) Ranh giới: đây là ca giảng dạy hư cấu chỉ nêu nguyên tắc, không có liều thuốc; chăm sóc thực tế theo đội điều trị và hướng dẫn hiện hành — khớp đỏ, nóng, sưng, đau kèm sốt cần được khám kịp thời, và cấp cứu thì gọi 911. ### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)
पाँच चरणों की समीक्षा। (1) पहचानें: बुखार के साथ एक ही गर्म, सूजा, दर्दनाक जोड़ हो तो पहले पूयजनक गठिया सोचें; मधुमेह, रूमेटॉइड गठिया, कृत्रिम जोड़, इंजेक्शन दवा उपयोग, और हाल का जोड़ इंजेक्शन या आघात जोखिम कारक हैं। (2) स्थान तय करें: जोड़-आकर्षण केंद्रीय जाँच है — श्लेष कोशिका गणना, Gram अभिरंजन, संवर्धन और क्रिस्टल, साथ में रक्त संवर्धन। (3) क्रम: स्थिर रोगी में एंटीबायोटिक से पहले आकर्षण; सेप्टिक रोगी में रक्त संवर्धन, तत्काल उपचार, आकर्षण तुरंत बाद। (4) उपचार: निकासी और संक्रमण-रोधी उपचार जुड़वाँ स्तंभ, अस्थिरोग की शीघ्र भागीदारी, और संक्रमण-संदिग्ध जोड़ में स्टेरॉइड इंजेक्शन कभी नहीं। (5) सीमाएँ: यह काल्पनिक शिक्षण मामला केवल सिद्धांत कहता है, दवा की खुराक नहीं; वास्तविक देखभाल उपचार टीम और वर्तमान दिशानिर्देशों के अनुसार होती है — बुखार के साथ लाल, गर्म, सूजा, दर्दनाक जोड़ शीघ्र देखभाल माँगता है, और आपात में 911 बुलाएँ। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद·待校)
