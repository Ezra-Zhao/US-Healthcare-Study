# 病例分析：传染性单核细胞增多症与发热、咽痛及脾肿大——虚构教学病例

> 虚构教学病例，不对应真实患者。本文为个人学习笔记，不构成医疗建议。全文不写任何药物剂量、给药频次或具体处方用量，只写处理类别与原则。红旗信号见第四节，出现时立即就医，急症拨打 911。
> 选题说明：本轮病例分析经 ls/grep 实查，「传染性单核细胞增多症」「单核细胞增多」「EB 病毒」grep 全仓均零命中、无本体专篇；仓内已有病毒性脑膜炎、病毒性肝炎与单纯疱疹病毒性脑炎诸篇讲的是脑膜、肝脏与脑实质的病毒感染，本篇专讲 EB 病毒原发感染引起的单核细胞增多综合征这条线，两边以指针分工，不重复选题。

## 一、病例呈现：合住宿舍的大学生，咽痛烧了十天，人累得只想躺着

### 中文
林同学（化名），19 岁，女性，虚构病例，大学二年级，与三位室友合住宿舍。十天前开始咽痛、低热，自以为是普通感冒，撑着上课；咽痛一天天加重，扁桃体肿大、表面有白色渗出，两侧颈部摸到多个肿大的淋巴结、按着发胀，后颈那一串尤其明显。这一周她累得反常：睡满一夜仍起不来，下午要补觉才能撑住。五天前在校医室按链球菌咽炎的初步印象拿了抗菌药物类别中的一类，咽痛没有退，身上反而冒出一片弥漫的红色皮疹。今晨体温 38.9 摄氏度，到急诊就诊。查体：咽部明显充血、双侧扁桃体肿大带渗出，颈后淋巴结成串肿大，左上腹轻压不适、脾脏可触及边缘。先把时间线算例对一遍：她回忆约五周前与生病的朋友共用过水杯，5 乘 7 等于 35 天；EB 病毒感染后出现症状的潜伏期通常为 4 到 6 周，也就是 28 到 42 天，35 天正落在窗内，窗宽 42 减 28 等于 14 天。体温换算顺带核一遍：38.9 摄氏度乘 9 除 5 加 32 等于 102.02，约 102.0 华氏度。血常规提示淋巴细胞比例明显升高、可见异型淋巴细胞，肝功能有轻度异常。来源框架：美国疾病控制与预防中心（CDC）传染性单核细胞增多症公开临床口径。

### English
Ms. Lin (pseudonym), 19, is a fictional patient, a college sophomore sharing a dormitory room with three roommates. Ten days ago a sore throat and low-grade fever began; she assumed it was an ordinary cold and kept attending class. The sore throat worsened day by day, the tonsils enlarged with white exudate on the surface, and multiple swollen lymph nodes appeared on both sides of the neck, tender and full, with the posterior cervical chain especially prominent. This week her fatigue turned abnormal: a full night of sleep still left her unable to get up, and she needed an afternoon nap to get through the day. Five days ago, under a first impression of streptococcal pharyngitis at the campus clinic, she received one category of antibacterial drug; the sore throat did not settle, and instead a diffuse red rash spread over her body. This morning her temperature was 38.9 degrees Celsius and she came to the emergency department. Examination shows a markedly red pharynx, enlarged tonsils with exudate, chains of swollen posterior cervical nodes, and mild left-upper-abdominal discomfort with a palpable spleen edge. Check the timeline arithmetic: she recalls sharing a water cup with an ill friend about five weeks ago, and 5 times 7 equals 35 days; symptoms after EB virus infection usually appear at 4 to 6 weeks, that is 28 to 42 days, so 35 days falls inside the window, whose width is 42 minus 28 equals 14 days. Temperature check: 38.9 times 9 divided by 5 plus 32 equals 102.02, about 102.0 degrees Fahrenheit. Blood work shows a clearly raised lymphocyte share with atypical lymphocytes, and mildly abnormal liver tests. Framework source: the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) public clinical materials on infectious mononucleosis.

## 二、诊断框架：经典画像是发热、咽炎与淋巴结肿大，确诊靠血象与抗体两条线

### 中文
传染性单核细胞增多症是一组综合征，最常见的病原是 EB 病毒，经唾液近距离传播，所以在青少年与年轻成人、宿舍与集体生活环境里多见；巨细胞病毒、急性 HIV 感染、弓形虫与 HHV-6 等也能引起类似的单核细胞增多综合征，EB 病毒阴性时要想到这一排。经典画像是四件：发热、咽炎（常带扁桃体肿大与渗出）、淋巴结肿大（颈后链最有提示性，全身也可及）、压倒性的疲劳；再加上脾肿大这一件——临床上约半数患者可查到，50 除以 100 等于一半这个量级要记牢，因为它直接决定后面的活动限制。诊断靠两条线。第一条是血象线：淋巴细胞增多伴异型淋巴细胞，是免疫系统对受感染 B 细胞作出反应的样子。第二条是抗体线：嗜异性抗体快检（heterophile 抗体）上手快，但发病第一周假阴性可达约四分之一，起病头几天阴性不能排除本病；要钉死病原时用 EB 病毒特异性抗体组合判读——衣壳抗原 IgM 阳性提示急性期感染，核抗原抗体出现则提示感染已过急性期，判读组合由实验室与临床一起看。还要并行排掉一个必须分开的对手：链球菌咽炎——两者咽部表现重叠，而链球菌那条线需要抗菌治疗、EB 病毒这条线不需要，所以咽拭子或链球菌检测该做就做，不能凭渗出就二选一。肝功能轻度异常在本病里常见，多数随病程回落；黄疸明显、腹痛突出时要把肝炎线再单独评估。本例五天前那片皮疹也是一条线索：在单核细胞增多症背景下用过氨基青霉素类别后出现的弥漫皮疹相当常见，它提示回头重估诊断，但不等于从此贴上青霉素过敏的终身标签，是否真过敏留待以后由专科评估，不要在本轮病程里自行定性。

### English
Infectious mononucleosis is a syndrome whose commonest cause is EB virus, spread through close contact with saliva, which is why it clusters in adolescents and young adults and in dormitory-style living. Cytomegalovirus, acute HIV infection, toxoplasma, and HHV-6 can produce a similar mononucleosis syndrome, so keep that row in mind when EB virus testing is negative. The classic picture has four pieces: fever, pharyngitis (often with enlarged tonsils and exudate), lymph node enlargement (the posterior cervical chain is the most suggestive, though nodes can be generalized), and an overwhelming fatigue. Add a fifth piece: splenomegaly, found on examination in about half of patients — 50 divided by 100 equals one half — and that proportion matters because it drives the activity restrictions below. Diagnosis rests on two lines. The first is the blood-count line: lymphocytosis with atypical lymphocytes, the visible shape of the immune response to infected B cells. The second is the antibody line: the heterophile antibody rapid test is quick, but false negatives in the first week of illness can reach about one in four, so a negative result in the first days does not exclude the disease; to pin the pathogen down, use the EB virus specific antibody panel — capsid antigen IgM positivity points to acute infection, while nuclear antigen antibody appearance points past the acute phase, with the combination read by laboratory and clinician together. One rival must be separated in parallel: streptococcal pharyngitis. The throat findings overlap, but the streptococcal line needs antibacterial treatment and the EB virus line does not, so do the throat swab or streptococcal test rather than choosing by exudate alone. Mildly abnormal liver tests are common in this illness and usually settle as the course runs; when jaundice is marked or abdominal pain stands out, reassess the hepatitis line on its own. The rash five days ago is also a clue: a diffuse rash appearing after an aminopenicillin category drug in a mononucleosis setting is quite common. It should trigger a rethink of the diagnosis, but it does not by itself earn a lifelong penicillin-allergy label — true allergy is for specialist assessment later, not a label to fix during this illness.

### Español (译稿)
La mononucleosis infecciosa es un síndrome cuya causa más frecuente es el virus de Epstein-Barr, transmitido por contacto estrecho con saliva, por lo que abunda en adolescentes y adultos jóvenes y en ambientes de convivencia como residencias. El citomegalovirus, la infección aguda por VIH, el toxoplasma y el HHV-6 pueden producir un síndrome mononucleósico parecido, así que conviene recordar esa fila cuando el estudio de Epstein-Barr sale negativo. El cuadro clásico tiene cuatro piezas: fiebre, faringitis (a menudo con amígdalas agrandadas y exudado), ganglios inflamados (la cadena cervical posterior es la más sugestiva, aunque la afectación puede ser generalizada) y un cansancio abrumador. Se suma una quinta pieza: la esplenomegalia, detectable en la exploración en torno a la mitad de los pacientes — 50 entre 100 igual a la mitad — y esa proporción importa porque decide las restricciones de actividad de más abajo. El diagnóstico se apoya en dos líneas. La primera es la del hemograma: linfocitosis con linfocitos atípicos, la forma visible de la respuesta inmune frente a los linfocitos B infectados. La segunda es la de los anticuerpos: la prueba rápida de anticuerpos heterófilos es ágil, pero los falsos negativos en la primera semana pueden llegar a cerca de uno de cada cuatro, así que un negativo en los primeros días no excluye la enfermedad; para fijar el patógeno se usa el panel de anticuerpos específicos frente a Epstein-Barr — el IgM contra el antígeno de la cápside positivo apunta a infección aguda, y la aparición de anticuerpos contra el antígeno nuclear apunta a que la fase aguda ya pasó, con la combinación interpretada por laboratorio y clínica a la vez. Hay un rival que debe separarse en paralelo: la faringitis estreptocócica. Los hallazgos de garganta se solapan, pero la línea estreptocócica sí necesita tratamiento antibacteriano y la de Epstein-Barr no, así que corresponde hacer el hisopo faríngeo o la prueba de estreptococo en lugar de elegir solo por el exudado. Las pruebas hepáticas levemente alteradas son frecuentes en esta enfermedad y suelen normalizarse con el curso; si la ictericia es marcada o el dolor abdominal destaca, revalúe la línea de hepatitis por separado. La erupción de hace cinco días también es una pista: una erupción difusa tras un fármaco de la categoría de las aminopenicilinas en un contexto de mononucleosis es bastante frecuente. Debe hacer replantear el diagnóstico, pero no equivale a una etiqueta de alergia a la penicilina para toda la vida: la alergia verdadera se evalúa después con el especialista, no se fija durante este episodio.

## 三、处置框架：没有特效抗病毒这条路，主线是支持处理加护住脾脏

### 中文
处置先把三件没有的事说清：没有针对 EB 病毒的特效抗病毒常规路径；抗菌药物类别对病毒本身无效，只在合并链球菌等细菌感染被证实时才有位置；激素类别不是常规件，只在气道受压、严重血液学并发症等特定情形下由专科评估使用。主线是支持处理四件：一是休息与活动量按疲劳程度调节，急性期少熬夜、课业与训练先降档，多数人在 2 到 4 周内明显好转，2 与 4 的均值 3 周只是量级感、不是返校返赛的自动放行日，疲劳拖尾数周并不少见；二是退热与镇痛按对症类别处理，补液与进食跟上，咽痛重时以能吞下为先；三是护住脾脏——脾肿大期间避免对抗性运动、负重冲撞与腹部受压，脾破裂发生率低但后果重，回运动场的时间由复查触诊、必要时影像确认脾脏回缩后由医生放行，起病后头几周是风险最集中的时段，这段时间不参加对抗性运动是硬线；四是护住肝——病程内不饮酒，肝功能异常未回落前避免给肝脏添负担的自行用药，加新药前先问医生或药师。生活面还有两件小事：急性期唾液传播力在，不共用水杯餐具、咳嗽礼仪做到位，但本病不需要长期隔离，症状期过后按校医口径返校；室友与密切接触者一般不做预防性处理，出现同样的发热咽痛组合再就医评估。本例的次序是急诊评估与血象先行、嗜异性抗体阴性后以 EB 病毒特异性抗体组合定案、链球菌检测阴性后停掉抗菌类别、书面交代脾脏活动限制与复查时间，皮疹随停药与病程自行消退。

### English
Start management by naming three things that do not exist here. There is no routine specific antiviral path against EB virus. Antibacterial categories do nothing to the virus itself and earn a place only when a bacterial coinfection such as streptococcus is proven. Steroid categories are not routine either; they belong to specific situations such as airway compromise or severe hematologic complications, under specialist assessment. The main line is supportive care in four pieces. First, rest and activity follow the fatigue: cut late nights in the acute phase and scale back coursework and training; most people improve clearly within 2 to 4 weeks — the midpoint of 2 and 4, that is 3 weeks, is only a sense of scale, not an automatic clearance date — and a fatigue tail lasting several more weeks is not unusual. Second, fever and pain are handled with symptomatic categories, fluids and food are kept up, and swallowing comes first when the throat is very sore. Third, protect the spleen: while it is enlarged, avoid contact sports, heavy collisions, and abdominal pressure; splenic rupture is uncommon but severe, and return to sport is cleared by the clinician only after re-examination, with imaging when needed, confirms the spleen has shrunk — the first weeks after onset carry the most concentrated risk, and staying out of contact sports in that window is a hard line. Fourth, protect the liver: no alcohol during the illness, and avoid adding self-chosen medicines that burden the liver before liver tests settle; ask the clinician or pharmacist before adding anything new. Two small daily-life items follow. Saliva is infectious in the acute phase, so do not share cups or utensils and keep cough etiquette, but the illness needs no long isolation — return to campus follows the campus clinician's rule once the symptomatic phase passes. Roommates and close contacts generally receive no preventive treatment; they seek assessment if the same fever-plus-sore-throat combination appears. In this case the order was emergency assessment and blood count first, EB virus specific antibodies to settle the diagnosis after a negative heterophile test, the antibacterial category stopped once streptococcal testing came back negative, and written instructions on spleen activity limits and the follow-up date; the rash faded on its own as the drug stopped and the course ran.

### 中文
出现以下任何一条，不要等复诊，直接急诊或拨打 911：左上腹或左肩部突然疼痛、头晕发虚甚至晕厥——这是脾破裂的警报线；扁桃体肿大到张口困难、流口水、说话含糊或喘不上气——这是气道受压的警报线，儿童尤其要警惕；高热多日不退、人越来越蔫、黄疸加深或尿色明显变深；皮疹合并嘴唇口腔破溃、大片脱皮等重症皮肤信号。随访这一段要盯四件事：一是脾脏账——复查时确认脾脏回缩、活动限制逐级放开，对抗性运动的放行由医生当面决定，不按日历自己推算；二是疲劳账——按周看精力回升的斜率，疲劳拖过数月仍无好转的，要回门诊重估其他原因，不把所有不舒服都归给这一场病；三是肝功能账——复查肝功能回到基线再销账，中间不饮酒、不自行叠加保健品与新药；四是标签账——本轮那片皮疹的经过要完整记进病历：用了哪一类抗菌药物、起疹在第几天、停药后如何消退，日后谈青霉素类过敏时拿这份记录说话，不靠记忆。加州口径没有为本病单设报告或通道另规，就医与复诊与全国一致，学校返校按校医与校方病假口径走。结尾归拢一句：单核细胞增多症多半是自己好的病，真正要认真对待的只有两件——确诊别停在嗓子发炎这一层，以及脾脏没回缩之前别回对抗性运动场。

### English
With any of the following, skip the follow-up wait and go to the emergency department or call 911: sudden pain in the left upper abdomen or left shoulder, dizziness and faintness or outright fainting — the alarm line for splenic rupture; tonsils so enlarged that opening the mouth is hard, with drooling, muffled speech, or breathlessness — the alarm line for airway compromise, with children watched especially closely; fever that stays high for days with growing listlessness, deepening jaundice, or clearly darkened urine; and a rash combined with mouth sores or widespread skin peeling, the severe skin signals. Follow-up watches four accounts. First, the spleen account: confirm at re-examination that the spleen has shrunk and loosen activity limits stepwise — clearance for contact sports is the clinician's in-person decision, never a date calculated from a calendar. Second, the fatigue account: watch the slope of energy returning week by week; fatigue that drags for months without improvement goes back to clinic for other causes rather than blaming everything on this one illness. Third, the liver account: liver tests return to baseline before the account closes, with no alcohol and no self-added supplements or new medicines in between. Fourth, the label account: record this episode's rash completely in the chart — which antibacterial category was used, on which day the rash appeared, and how it faded after stopping — so any future discussion of penicillin-class allergy speaks from this record, not from memory. California sets no separate reporting rule or special pathway for this illness; care and follow-up match the national pattern, and return to school follows the campus clinician and school sick-leave rules. Closing line: mononucleosis usually heals on its own, and only two things truly deserve seriousness — do not let the diagnosis stop at a sore throat, and do not return to contact sports before the spleen has shrunk.

Cierre: la mononucleosis suele curarse sola, y solo dos cosas merecen verdadera seriedad — no dejar el diagnóstico detenido en una garganta inflamada, y no volver al deporte de contacto antes de que el bazo se haya reducido.

### 한국어 (초역, 初译待校)
다음 중 어느 하나라도 나타나면 재진을 기다리지 말고 응급실로 가거나 911에 전화하십시오. 왼쪽 윗배나 왼쪽 어깨에 갑자기 생긴 통증, 어지럽고 기운이 빠지거나 실신하는 경우는 비장 파열의 경보선입니다. 편도가 너무 커져서 입을 벌리기 힘들고 침을 흘리며 말이 어눌하거나 숨이 차는 경우는 기도가 눌리는 경보선으로, 어린이에게서 특히 조심해야 합니다. 며칠째 가라앉지 않는 고열에 점점 처지는 모습, 짙어지는 황달이나 뚜렷이 어두워진 소변 색도 마찬가지입니다. 발진에 입술과 입안의 궤양이나 넓게 벗겨지는 피부가 겹치는 경우도 중한 피부 신호입니다. 추적에서는 네 가지 장부를 봅니다. 첫째, 비장 장부입니다. 재진에서 비장이 줄었는지 확인하고 활동 제한을 단계적으로 풀며, 격돌 운동 복귀 허락은 의사가 직접 만나 내리는 결정이지 달력으로 스스로 계산하는 날짜가 아닙니다. 둘째, 피로 장부입니다. 주 단위로 기운이 돌아오는 기울기를 보고, 몇 달을 끌어도 나아지지 않는 피로는 이 병 탓으로만 돌리지 말고 외래에서 다른 원인을 다시 평가하십시오. 셋째, 간 장부입니다. 간 기능이 원래 수준으로 돌아온 뒤에야 장부를 닫고, 그 사이에는 술을 마시지 말고 건강식품과 새 약을 스스로 겹치지 마십시오. 넷째, 꼬리표 장부입니다. 이번 발진의 경과를 차트에 온전히 남기십시오. 어느 항균 범주를 썼는지, 며칠째에 발진이 돋았는지, 약을 중단한 뒤 어떻게 가라앉았는지를 적어 두면, 나중에 페니실린 계열 알레르기를 논할 때 기억이 아니라 이 기록이 말하게 됩니다. 캘리포니아는 이 병에 대해 별도의 신고 규정이나 전용 경로를 두지 않으며 진료와 추적은 전국과 같고, 학교 복귀는 학교 보건실과 학교의 병가 기준을 따릅니다. 마무리하면, 단핵구증은 대개 저절로 낫는 병이고 진지하게 챙길 일은 두 가지뿐입니다. 진단이 목이 부었다는 층위에서 멈추지 않게 하는 것과, 비장이 줄어들기 전에 격돌 운동장으로 돌아가지 않는 것입니다. (초역, 初译待校)

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch, 初译待校)
Khi xuất hiện bất kỳ dấu hiệu nào dưới đây, đừng chờ tái khám mà hãy vào cấp cứu hoặc gọi 911: đau đột ngột vùng bụng trên bên trái hoặc vai trái, chóng mặt xây xẩm hoặc ngất — đường báo động của vỡ lách; amidan to đến mức khó há miệng, chảy nước dãi, nói ngọng hoặc khó thở — đường báo động của chèn ép đường thở, đặc biệt phải cảnh giác ở trẻ em; sốt cao nhiều ngày không lui kèm ngày càng lờ đờ, vàng da đậm dần hoặc nước tiểu sẫm màu rõ; và ban đỏ kèm loét môi miệng hoặc bong da diện rộng, những tín hiệu da nghiêm trọng. Theo dõi gồm bốn sổ. Thứ nhất, sổ lách: khám lại để xác nhận lách đã thu nhỏ và nới lỏng giới hạn vận động từng bước — việc cho phép chơi thể thao đối kháng là quyết định trực tiếp của bác sĩ, không bao giờ là ngày tự tính theo lịch. Thứ hai, sổ mệt mỏi: xem độ dốc hồi phục năng lượng theo từng tuần; mệt mỏi kéo dài nhiều tháng không cải thiện thì quay lại phòng khám để đánh giá nguyên nhân khác, đừng đổ mọi khó chịu cho một đợt bệnh này. Thứ ba, sổ gan: men gan trở về mức nền mới đóng sổ, trong thời gian đó không uống rượu và không tự cộng thêm thực phẩm bổ sung hay thuốc mới. Thứ tư, sổ nhãn dị ứng: ghi đầy đủ đợt ban lần này vào hồ sơ — đã dùng nhóm kháng khuẩn nào, ban xuất hiện vào ngày thứ mấy, sau khi dừng thuốc thì mờ đi ra sao — để sau này khi bàn về dị ứng nhóm penicillin sẽ nói bằng hồ sơ này chứ không bằng trí nhớ. California không đặt quy định báo cáo riêng hay kênh riêng cho bệnh này; khám chữa và theo dõi khớp với mẫu chung toàn quốc, và việc quay lại trường theo quy định của y tế trường cùng chế độ nghỉ ốm của trường. Lời kết: bệnh tăng bạch cầu đơn nhân phần lớn tự khỏi, và chỉ có hai việc thật sự đáng nghiêm túc — đừng để chẩn đoán dừng ở mức viêm họng, và đừng quay lại thể thao đối kháng trước khi lách đã thu nhỏ. (bản dịch sơ bộ, 初译待校)

### हिन्दी (अनुवाद, 初译待校)
निम्न में से कोई भी लक्षण आए तो follow-up की प्रतीक्षा न करें; आपात विभाग जाएँ या 911 पर कॉल करें: बायीं ऊपरी पेट या बाएँ कंधे में अचानक दर्द, चक्कर व कमज़ोरी या बेहोशी — यह spleen rupture की alarm रेखा है; tonsils इतने बड़े कि मुँह खोलना कठिन हो, लार टपके, आवाज़ दबी हो या साँस फूले — यह airway दबाव की alarm रेखा है, बच्चों में विशेष सतर्कता रखें; कई दिनों तक न उतरने वाला तेज़ बुखार व बढ़ती सुस्ती, गहराता पीलिया या स्पष्ट गहरे रंग का मूत्र; और rash के साथ होंठों-मुँह के छाले या व्यापक त्वचा-छिलना, जो गंभीर त्वचा संकेत हैं। Follow-up चार खातों पर नज़र रखता है। पहला, spleen खाता: पुनः जाँच में spleen के सिकुड़ने की पुष्टि करें और गतिविधि-सीमाएँ चरणबद्ध ढंग से ढीली करें — contact sports की अनुमति चिकित्सक का आमने-सामने का निर्णय है, calendar से स्वयं निकाली तिथि कभी नहीं। दूसरा, थकान खाता: सप्ताह-दर-सप्ताह ऊर्जा लौटने की ढलान देखें; महीनों तक बिना सुधार खिंचती थकान को इस एक बीमारी पर न डालकर clinic में अन्य कारणों का पुनर्मूल्यांकन कराएँ। तीसरा, liver खाता: liver tests के baseline पर लौटने के बाद ही खाता बंद हो, बीच में शराब न पिएँ और स्वयं supplements या नई दवाएँ न जोड़ें। चौथा, label खाता: इस episode की rash को chart में पूरी तरह दर्ज करें — कौन-सी antibacterial श्रेणी उपयोग हुई, rash किस दिन आया, और दवा बंद होने पर वह कैसे मद्धम पड़ा — ताकि भविष्य में penicillin-वर्ग allergy की चर्चा स्मृति से नहीं, इसी record से हो। California इस बीमारी के लिए कोई अलग reporting नियम या विशेष मार्ग नहीं रखता; देखभाल व follow-up राष्ट्रीय पैटर्न से मेल खाते हैं, और विद्यालय वापसी campus clinician व विद्यालय के sick-leave नियमों से चलती है। समापन: mononucleosis आमतौर पर स्वयं ठीक हो जाता है, और गंभीरता से लेने योग्य केवल दो बातें हैं — निदान को गले की सूजन पर ही रुकने न दें, और spleen सिकुड़ने से पहले contact sports में न लौटें। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद, 初译待校)
