# 病例分析：水痘与全身水疱样皮疹及同班暴露（教学病例）

> 虚构教学病例，不对应真实患者。本文为个人学习笔记，不构成医疗建议。全文不写任何药物剂量、给药频次或具体处方用量，只写处理类别与原则。红旗信号见第四节，出现时立即就医，急症拨打 911。
> 选题说明：本轮病例分析经 ls/grep 实查，「水痘」作病例本体 grep 全仓无专篇、无同名文件，仅在带状疱疹篇的病原叙述与手足口病篇的鉴别段中顺带出现；仓内已有手足口病篇讲肠道病毒的口手足疹线、麻疹篇讲发疹性病毒的另一条线，本篇专讲水痘-带状疱疹病毒初次感染的全身水疱线与同班暴露处置，两边以指针分工，不重复选题。

## 一、病例呈现：同班同学先出疹，十五天后她全身冒出痒水疱

### 中文
小琪（化名），5 岁，女性，虚构病例，幼儿园大班。十五天前，同班一名同学确诊水痘，两人同桌、发病前一周内天天近距离一起玩。一天前她开始发热、乏力、没有胃口，今晨头皮、脸和躯干先冒出红斑，很快变成透亮的小水疱，痒得直抓，背上和四肢也陆续跟上，新疱和已开始发暗的旧疱同时在场，体温 38.8 摄氏度，到门诊就诊。先把时间线算例对一遍：暴露距发病 15 天；水痘潜伏期通常 10 到 21 天、平均 14 到 16 天，15 天正落在平均段里，窗宽 21 减 10 等于 11 天。体温换算顺带核一遍：38.8 乘 9 除 5 加 32 等于 101.84，约 101.8 华氏度。皮损粗数两百多个、躯干最密，落在未接种者典型量级（常为 250 到 500 个）的下缘附近——她从未接种过水痘疫苗，入学记录里这一项是空的，家长一直以为她小时候得过，后来核对才发现那次是湿疹。来源框架：美国疾病控制与预防中心（CDC）水痘公开临床口径。

### English
Xiaoqi (pseudonym), 5, female, is a fictional patient in the senior class of a kindergarten. Fifteen days ago a classmate was diagnosed with chickenpox; the two shared a desk and played together at close range every day in the week before that. Yesterday fever, tiredness and loss of appetite began; this morning red spots appeared first on the scalp, face and trunk and quickly turned into clear small blisters, intensely itchy, with more following on the back and limbs, fresh blisters alongside older ones already darkening, her temperature was 38.8 degrees Celsius, and she was brought to clinic. Check the timeline arithmetic: exposure to onset is 15 days; the incubation period of chickenpox is usually 10 to 21 days, averaging 14 to 16 days, so 15 days sits right inside the average band, and the window width is 21 minus 10 equals 11 days. Temperature check: 38.8 times 9 divided by 5 plus 32 equals 101.84, about 101.8 degrees Fahrenheit. The lesions number roughly two hundred or more, densest on the trunk, near the lower edge of the typical range in unvaccinated children (often 250 to 500 lesions) — she had never received the chickenpox vaccine, that line in her school record was blank, and her parents had believed she had had the disease as a toddler until checking showed that episode had been eczema. Framework source: the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) public clinical materials on chickenpox (varicella).

## 二、诊断框架：全身水疱加新旧并存先入围，需要确证时靠疱液核酸

### 中文
诊断以临床为主，三件同框先入围，逐项数得 3：前驱发热与乏力一两天先行、全身分布且躯干最密的水疱样皮疹、新旧多期皮损同时在场（红斑、水疱、开始结痂的旧疱并排出现）。皮损分批冒出是常态，新疱一般在头几天里一茬接一茬，所以「今天比昨天又多了」本身不等于出事。实验室不是每个典型病例的必经门：需要确证时（皮疹不典型、免疫受损、住院或聚集疫情核实），疱液或痂皮的核酸检测是最有用的确认手段；血清学对急性期定案帮助有限。接种过疫苗的人发病是另一副面孔，要单独记一笔：突破性水痘通常更轻，皮损常少于 50 个、可以只有斑丘疹而没有典型水疱、也不结痂，50 与未接种者下限 250 之间差 200 个，别拿典型病例的皮损量级去套接种后病例。鉴别四对手逐个过，逐项数得 4：手足口病（疹在手、足、口与臀部，没有躯干向心的密布水疱这一件）；脓疱疮（局部蜜色结痂、没有全身多期并存的节奏）；单纯疱疹（局限在一处成簇、反复在同一部位，不全身开花）；丘疹性荨麻疹等虫咬反应（以瘙痒性丘疹为主、没有透亮水疱与结痂的演变线）。来源：CDC 水痘临床与实验室公开口径。

### English
Diagnosis is mainly clinical, and three findings together put chickenpox on the list, counted one by one as 3: a prodrome of fever and tiredness for a day or two first; a blistering rash spread over the whole body and densest on the trunk; and lesions of several stages present at the same time — red spots, blisters and older lesions starting to crust, side by side. Lesions arriving in successive crops are the norm, with new blisters appearing over the first few days, so "more spots today than yesterday" by itself does not mean something has gone wrong. Laboratory testing is not a required gate for every typical case: when confirmation is needed — an atypical rash, an immunocompromised patient, a hospitalization or a cluster investigation — nucleic-acid testing of blister fluid or crusts is the most useful confirmatory tool, while serology helps little in settling an acute case. Disease in a vaccinated person wears a different face and gets its own note: breakthrough chickenpox is usually milder, often with fewer than 50 lesions, sometimes only flat and raised spots with no typical blisters and no crusting, and 50 sits 200 below the 250 lower edge of the unvaccinated range — do not measure a post-vaccine case with the lesion yardstick of a classic case. Work through four differentials, counted as 4: hand, foot and mouth disease (rash on hands, feet, mouth and buttocks, without the dense trunk-centred blistering element); impetigo (local honey-coloured crusts, without the whole-body multi-stage rhythm); herpes simplex (clustered in one limited site and recurring in the same place, not flowering over the whole body); and bite reactions such as papular urticaria (mainly itchy raised bumps, without the clear-blister-to-crust evolution line). Source: CDC public clinical and laboratory materials on chickenpox.

### Español (译稿)
El diagnóstico es sobre todo clínico y tres hallazgos juntos meten a la varicela en la lista, contados uno a uno como 3: un pródromo de fiebre y cansancio de uno o dos días primero; una erupción ampollosa repartida por todo el cuerpo y más densa en el tronco; y lesiones de varias etapas presentes a la vez: manchas rojas, ampollas y lesiones viejas que empiezan a formar costra, una junto a otra. Que las lesiones salgan en tandas sucesivas es lo normal, con ampollas nuevas durante los primeros días, así que «hoy hay más manchas que ayer» por sí solo no significa que algo vaya mal. El laboratorio no es una puerta obligada en cada caso típico: cuando hace falta confirmación — erupción atípica, paciente inmunodeprimido, hospitalización o investigación de un conglomerado — la prueba de ácidos nucleicos del líquido de la ampolla o de las costras es la herramienta confirmatoria más útil, mientras la serología ayuda poco a cerrar un caso agudo. La enfermedad en una persona vacunada tiene otra cara y merece nota propia: la varicela posvacunal suele ser más leve, a menudo con menos de 50 lesiones, a veces solo manchas planas y elevadas sin ampollas típicas y sin costras, y 50 queda 200 por debajo del borde inferior de 250 del rango sin vacuna: no mida un caso posvacunal con la vara de lesiones de un caso clásico. Recorra cuatro diagnósticos diferenciales, contados como 4: enfermedad de manos, pies y boca (erupción en manos, pies, boca y nalgas, sin el elemento de ampollas densas centradas en el tronco); impétigo (costras locales color miel, sin el ritmo corporal de varias etapas); herpes simple (agrupado en un sitio limitado y recurrente en el mismo lugar, sin florecer por todo el cuerpo); y reacciones a picaduras como la urticaria papular (sobre todo bultos elevados que pican, sin la línea de evolución de ampolla transparente a costra). Fuente: materiales públicos clínicos y de laboratorio de los CDC sobre la varicela. (初译待校)

## 三、处置框架：健康孩子以支持处理为主，隔离与接触者处置另记一本账

### 中文
处置分三条线。第一条是支持处理，只写类别与原则，逐项数得 4：退热类别（儿童与青少年在病毒感染期间避开阿司匹林类别，防瑞氏综合征这一条要单独交代）；止痒类别与温水浴、宽松衣物等物理安抚；剪短指甲、勤洗手，防抓破后继发细菌感染；补液与休息。健康儿童多数不常规用抗病毒类别；抗病毒这条线留给重症风险高的人——青少年与成人、免疫受损者、慢性皮肤或肺病患者——由接诊医生评估后尽早定，本篇不写任何数值。第二条是隔离账：出疹前 1 到 2 天起就有传染性，一直持续到全部水疱结痂为止，结痂多在出疹后约 5 天上下；按示例账数一遍，出疹前 2 天加出疹后 5 天等于 7 天，是传染期的大致总长。结痂前不到校、不去托育与公共场所，在家避开孕妇、新生儿与免疫受损者；住院场景按空气加接触防护走。突破性病例没有痂可等，口径改为 24 小时无新疹出现后才解除隔离。第三条是接触者账：先核每个密切接触者的免疫证据（接种记录或既往患病记录）；没有免疫证据的人，暴露后 3 到 5 天内接种水痘疫苗可以预防发病或减轻病情——本例 3 岁的妹妹未接种、今天是与姐姐密切接触的第 2 天，窗口 5 减 2 等于还剩 3 天，要在这几天内把接种办掉；不能接种又属重症高危的人（例如家中无免疫史的孕妇、免疫受损的家人），由医生与卫生部门评估暴露后 10 天内使用水痘带状疱疹免疫球蛋白这一路，本例按第 2 天起算，10 减 2 等于还剩 8 天；接种与免疫球蛋白都办完也不等于万事大吉，接触者从暴露后第 8 天到第 21 天这段窗内要留意发热与出疹，窗宽 21 减 8 等于 13 天。水痘在全国属应报告病种，聚集疫情由机构与学校按辖区卫生部门当期口径通报与排除管理。来源：CDC 水痘隔离与暴露后处置公开口径。

### English
Management runs on three lines. The first is supportive care, stated in categories and principles only, counted as 4: fever-relief categories (with one warning stated separately — children and teenagers should avoid the aspirin category during viral illness because of Reye syndrome); itch-relief categories plus physical comforts such as lukewarm baths and loose clothing; keeping nails short and hands washed, to guard against bacterial infection of scratched skin; and fluids and rest. Most healthy children do not routinely receive antiviral categories; the antiviral line is reserved for people at higher risk of severe disease — adolescents and adults, immunocompromised patients, and those with chronic skin or lung conditions — assessed and started early by the treating clinician, with no amounts stated anywhere in this note. The second line is the isolation ledger: a patient is contagious from 1 to 2 days before the rash appears until every blister has crusted, and crusting usually completes about 5 days after the rash starts; on an example count, 2 days before the rash plus 5 days after equals 7 days as the rough total length of the contagious period. Until crusting is complete there is no school, no childcare and no public places, and at home the patient stays away from pregnant people, newborns and immunocompromised people; in hospital the pathway is airborne plus contact precautions. Breakthrough cases have no crusts to wait for, so the rule changes to release from isolation only after 24 hours with no new lesions. The third line is the contact ledger: first check each close contact's evidence of immunity (vaccination records or a history of disease); a person without evidence of immunity who is vaccinated within 3 to 5 days after exposure may be protected from illness or have it softened — in this case the 3-year-old younger sister is unvaccinated and today is day 2 of close contact with her sister, so the window leaves 5 minus 2 equals 3 days, and the vaccination needs to happen inside those days; people who cannot be vaccinated and are at high risk of severe disease — for example a pregnant household member without immunity or an immunocompromised family member — are assessed by the clinician and health department for varicella-zoster immune globulin within 10 days after exposure, which counted from day 2 leaves 10 minus 2 equals 8 days; and even with vaccination or immune globulin done, contacts watch for fever and rash from day 8 through day 21 after exposure, a window whose width is 21 minus 8 equals 13 days. Chickenpox is a nationally notifiable condition, and clusters are reported and managed for exclusion by facilities and schools under the current practice of the local health department. Source: CDC public guidance on chickenpox isolation and post-exposure management.

Chưa đóng vảy hết thì không đến trường, không đến nhà trẻ và nơi công cộng, ở nhà tránh người mang thai, trẻ sơ sinh và người suy giảm miễn dịch; trong bệnh viện đi theo phòng ngừa đường khí cộng tiếp xúc. Ca đột phá không có vảy để chờ, nên quy tắc đổi thành chỉ thôi cách ly sau 24 giờ không có tổn thương mới. Tuyến thứ ba là sổ người tiếp xúc: trước hết kiểm tra bằng chứng miễn dịch của từng người tiếp xúc gần (hồ sơ tiêm chủng hoặc tiền sử đã mắc); người không có bằng chứng miễn dịch mà tiêm vắc xin trong 3 đến 5 ngày sau phơi nhiễm có thể được bảo vệ khỏi mắc bệnh hoặc mắc nhẹ hơn: trong ca này em gái 3 tuổi chưa tiêm và hôm nay là ngày 2 tiếp xúc gần với chị, nên cửa sổ còn 5 trừ 2 bằng 3 ngày, và việc tiêm phải xong trong những ngày đó; người không thể tiêm và thuộc nhóm nguy cơ bệnh nặng cao — ví dụ người nhà đang mang thai không có miễn dịch hoặc người nhà suy giảm miễn dịch — được bác sĩ và cơ quan y tế đánh giá dùng globulin miễn dịch varicella-zoster trong 10 ngày sau phơi nhiễm, tính từ ngày 2 thì còn 10 trừ 2 bằng 8 ngày; và ngay cả khi đã tiêm hoặc đã dùng globulin, người tiếp xúc vẫn theo dõi sốt và phát ban từ ngày 8 đến ngày 21 sau phơi nhiễm, một cửa sổ có độ rộng 21 trừ 8 bằng 13 ngày. Thủy đậu thuộc diện báo cáo toàn quốc, và các ổ dịch do cơ sở và trường học báo cáo cùng quản lý tạm dừng đến trường theo thực hành hiện hành của cơ quan y tế địa phương. Nguồn: hướng dẫn công khai của CDC về cách ly thủy đậu và xử trí sau phơi nhiễm. (bản dịch, 初译待校)

## 四、红旗信号与随访：肺、脑、皮肤三处信号，一样都不能等

### 中文
红旗四组先行，逐项数得 4：咳嗽加重、呼吸发急或嘴唇发青，提示肺炎一线，水痘肺炎在成人与免疫受损者身上更凶，当天急诊；意识发蒙、走路不稳、反复呕吐或抽搐，提示脑炎或小脑受累一线，直接急诊；皮损周围红肿热痛迅速扩大、流脓或孩子高热回升，提示抓破后的细菌继发感染一线，包括蜂窝织炎方向，同日就医；皮损变成血疱、大片出血或家里免疫受损的成员也开始出疹，按播散与重症方向走急诊。随访三本账：皮损账——新疱一般在头几天内停住，全部结痂多在出疹后约一周上下，痂脱落后留下的浅印与色素多在数月内慢慢变淡，持续扩散或结痂迟迟不全要回诊重估；疫苗账——本例的空缺在这次病后由医生按程序补齐并登记入册，同班的暴露管理以学校与卫生部门的当期通知为准，自家妹妹的暴露后接种按第三节的 3 天余量办，不拖；记录账——隔离起止、出疹与结痂日期、接触者处置结果都记进病历，学校要的回校说明照着结痂完成这一条开。收口：全身水疱加新旧并存先想到水痘，典型病例靠临床、需要确证靠疱液核酸，处置靠支持处理、隔离与接触者的暴露后时钟，肺、脑、皮肤的信号一律按各自的急症线走。来源：CDC 水痘并发症公开口径。

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch, 初译待校)
Bốn nhóm dấu hiệu đỏ đặt lên trước, đếm thành 4: ho nặng lên, thở nhanh hoặc môi tím chỉ về tuyến viêm phổi — viêm phổi do thủy đậu dữ dội hơn ở người lớn và người suy giảm miễn dịch — và cần cấp cứu trong ngày; tri giác lơ mơ, đi đứng loạng choạng, nôn lặp lại hoặc co giật chỉ về tuyến viêm não hoặc tổn thương tiểu não và nghĩa là đến khoa cấp cứu trực tiếp; vùng quanh tổn thương đỏ, sưng, nóng, đau lan nhanh, chảy mủ hoặc sốt cao trở lại chỉ về nhiễm khuẩn thứ phát trên da bị gãi, gồm hướng viêm mô tế bào, và cần khám trong ngày; và tổn thương biến thành mụn máu, chảy máu diện rộng, hoặc một thành viên suy giảm miễn dịch trong nhà cũng bắt đầu nổi ban thì đi theo đường lan tỏa và bệnh nặng ở khoa cấp cứu. चार लाल-झंडा समूह पहले हैं, गिनें तो 4: बढ़ती खाँसी, तेज़ साँस या नीले होंठ pneumonia की रेखा की ओर इशारा करते हैं — चिकनपॉक्स pneumonia वयस्कों व प्रतिरक्षा-दुर्बलों में अधिक उग्र होता है — और उसी दिन आपात देखभाल माँगते हैं; धुंधली चेतना, लड़खड़ाती चाल, बार-बार उल्टी या दौरे encephalitis या cerebellar रेखा की ओर इशारा करते हैं और अर्थ सीधे आपात विभाग है; घावों के आसपास तेज़ी से फैलती लालिमा, सूजन, गर्माहट व दर्द, मवाद रिसना या फिर चढ़ता बुखार खरोंची त्वचा के जीवाणु superinfection की ओर, cellulitis दिशा सहित, इशारा करता है और उसी दिन देखभाल माँगता है; और घावों का रक्त-छालों में बदलना, विस्तृत रक्तस्राव, या घर के प्रतिरक्षा-दुर्बल सदस्य का भी दाने निकालना, आपात विभाग में dissemination व गंभीर-रोग राह से संभाला जाता है। अनुवर्ती तीन खाते रखता है: घाव खाता — नए छाले आमतौर पर पहले कुछ दिनों में रुक जाते हैं, पूरी पपड़ी rash शुरू होने के लगभग एक सप्ताह में बन जाती है, और पपड़ी गिरने के बाद बचे उथले निशान व रंगद्रव्य अधिकतर महीनों में धीरे-धीरे हल्के होते हैं, जबकि फैलाव जारी रहना या पपड़ी कभी पूरी न होना दोबारा आकर पुनर्मूल्यांकन दिलाता है; टीका खाता — इस रोगी की खाली पंक्ति इस बीमारी के बाद चिकित्सक द्वारा तय कार्यक्रम से भरी जाती है और रिकॉर्ड में दर्ज होती है, कक्षा-संपर्क प्रबंधन विद्यालय व स्वास्थ्य विभाग की वर्तमान सूचना अनुसार चलता है, और छोटी बहन का संपर्क-पश्चात टीकाकरण तीसरे खंड में गिने गए शेष 3 दिनों के भीतर किया जाता है, टाला नहीं जाता; और अभिलेख खाता — अलगाव की शुरुआत व समाप्ति, rash व पपड़ी की तिथियाँ और संपर्कों के लिए किया गया काम सब chart में जाता है, और विद्यालय की वापसी-टिप्पणी केवल पूरी पपड़ी के एक तथ्य के आधार पर लिखी जाती है। समापन: पूरे शरीर के छाले, पुराने व नए पास-पास, चिकनपॉक्स को सबसे पहले मन में लाएँ, सामान्य मामले नैदानिक तस्वीर पर टिके हैं और पुष्टि, ज़रूरत पड़ने पर, छाले के तरल की nucleic-acid जाँच पर; प्रबंधन सहायक देखभाल, अलगाव व संपर्कों की संपर्क-पश्चात घड़ी पर टिका है, और फेफड़े, मस्तिष्क व त्वचा के संकेत अपनी-अपनी आपात राह चलते हैं। स्रोत: चिकनपॉक्स जटिलताओं पर CDC की सार्वजनिक सामग्री। (अनुवाद, 初译待校)
