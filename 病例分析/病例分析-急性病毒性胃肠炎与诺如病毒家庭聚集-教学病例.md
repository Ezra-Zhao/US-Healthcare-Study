## 一、病例呈现：聚餐后第二天，一家三口先后吐泻

### 中文
老周（化名），34 岁，男性，虚构病例，在餐厅做后厨帮工。两天前晚上他参加家庭聚餐，吃了凉拌菜与贝类拼盘；约 36 小时后半夜突然恶心、呕吐，先后吐了 5 次，接着水样腹泻 7 次，腹部绞痛、低热，体温 37.6 摄氏度，自觉口干、乏力。到门诊时妻子说，家里 6 岁的女儿今晨也开始呕吐，奶奶有轻微腹泻——一家三口先后发病，间隔不到一天。先把时间线算例对两遍：聚餐距老周发病约 36 小时；诺如病毒潜伏期通常 12 到 48 小时，36 小时正落在窗内，窗宽 48 减 12 等于 36 小时。丢失次数粗账：呕吐 5 次加腹泻 7 次等于 12 次。体温换算顺带核一遍：37.6 乘 9 除 5 加 32 等于 99.68，约 99.7 华氏度，属于低热而不是高热，这与病毒性胃肠炎常见的低热或无热图景对得上。来源框架：美国疾病控制与预防中心（CDC）诺如病毒公开临床与防控口径。

### English
Lao Zhou (pseudonym), 34, male, is a fictional patient who helps in a restaurant kitchen. Two evenings ago he joined a family dinner with cold dressed dishes and a shellfish platter; about 36 hours later, in the middle of the night, nausea and vomiting began — five episodes of vomiting, then seven episodes of watery diarrhea, cramping abdominal pain and low-grade fever at 37.6 degrees Celsius, with a dry mouth and fatigue. At the clinic his wife reported that their 6-year-old daughter had also started vomiting that morning and the grandmother had mild diarrhea — three household members falling ill one after another within less than a day. Check the timeline twice: dinner to onset is about 36 hours; the incubation period of norovirus is usually 12 to 48 hours, so 36 hours sits inside the window, whose width is 48 minus 12 equals 36 hours. The loss count: 5 vomiting episodes plus 7 diarrheal episodes equals 12. Temperature check: 37.6 times 9 divided by 5 plus 32 equals 99.68, about 99.7 degrees Fahrenheit — low-grade fever rather than high fever, matching the usual picture of viral gastroenteritis. Framework source: the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) public clinical and prevention materials on norovirus.

## 二、诊断框架：短潜伏加吐泻并重加聚集发病先入围，多数病例靠临床图景

### 中文
诺如病毒胃肠炎的入围靠四件拼图，逐项数得 4：一是潜伏期短，暴露后 12 到 48 小时发病，聚餐、游轮、学校、养老院这类共餐共处场景最常见；二是吐泻并重，呕吐在儿童更突出、成人腹泻更突出，水样便、无血无脓；三是病程短，多数 1 到 3 天自限；四是聚集性，一家人或一桌人先后中招，二代传播快得显眼。确证靠粪便核酸检测，公共卫生调查聚集疫情时最常用，散发病例多数不查、靠临床图景定性。鉴别要分清三条岔路：细菌性食物中毒潜伏更短或伴高热血便，艰难梭菌要问近期抗菌药与住院史，急腹症的腹痛是持续加重加腹膜刺激征而不是一阵阵绞痛后缓解。儿童、老人、孕妇与免疫低下者单独标高风险，脱水来得更快、病程可能更长。来源：CDC 诺如病毒临床与暴发调查公开口径。

### English
Norovirus gastroenteritis is shortlisted by four puzzle pieces, counted one by one as 4: first, a short incubation — onset 12 to 48 hours after exposure, most typical in shared-meal and shared-space settings such as family dinners, cruise ships, schools and nursing homes; second, vomiting and diarrhea together — vomiting more prominent in children, diarrhea in adults, with watery stool and no blood or pus; third, a short course, mostly self-limited within 1 to 3 days; and fourth, clustering, with a whole family or table falling ill in sequence and fast, conspicuous secondary spread. Confirmation rests on stool nucleic-acid testing, used mainly in public-health outbreak investigations; sporadic cases are usually diagnosed clinically. Three forks must be separated: bacterial food poisoning tends to have a shorter incubation or high fever with bloody stool, Clostridioides difficile calls for a recent antibiotic and hospital history, and a surgical abdomen shows steadily worsening pain with peritoneal signs rather than cramps that ease between waves. Children, older adults, pregnant patients and the immunocompromised are flagged separately as higher risk, because dehydration arrives faster and the course can run longer. Source: CDC public materials on norovirus clinical features and outbreak investigation.

### Español (译稿)
La gastroenteritis por norovirus entra en la lista por cuatro piezas, contadas una a una son 4: primero, incubación corta, con inicio de 12 a 48 horas tras la exposición, típica de escenarios de comida y convivencia compartidas como cenas familiares, cruceros, escuelas y residencias; segundo, vómito y diarrea a la par, con el vómito más destacado en niños y la diarrea en adultos, heces acuosas sin sangre ni pus; tercero, curso breve, en su mayoría autolimitado de 1 a 3 días; y cuarto, agrupamiento, con toda una familia o una mesa enfermando en cadena y una transmisión secundaria rápida y visible. La confirmación se apoya en la prueba de ácidos nucleicos en heces, usada sobre todo en investigaciones de brotes de salud pública; los casos esporádicos suelen diagnosticarse por el cuadro clínico. Hay que separar tres desvíos: la intoxicación alimentaria bacteriana tiende a incubación más corta o fiebre alta con heces sanguinolentas, Clostridioides difficile obliga a preguntar por antibióticos recientes y hospitalización, y el abdomen quirúrgico muestra dolor que empeora de forma sostenida con signos peritoneales, no cólicos que ceden entre oleadas. Niños, adultos mayores, embarazadas e inmunodeprimidos se marcan aparte como de mayor riesgo, porque la deshidratación llega antes y el curso puede alargarse. Fuente: materiales públicos de los CDC sobre clínica e investigación de brotes de norovirus.

## 三、处置框架：没有特效抗病毒药，补液防脱水是主线，止泻类只作类别提示

### 中文

### English
Management has a single main line: replace fluids and prevent dehydration. Oral rehydration is the first channel — small, frequent sips, waiting about ten minutes after vomiting before restarting with even smaller sips; if fluids stay down, an intravenous line is not the first step. Intravenous fluids are reserved for those who cannot drink, cannot keep fluids down, or already show clear dehydration. Anti-emetic and anti-diarrheal medicines are mentioned only as categories; whether and which to use is a clinician's call based on age and condition, and children in particular must never be dosed by adult experience; antibiotics do not work on viruses and are not routine. Eating resumes gradually with tolerated bland food rather than prolonged water-only fasting. The dehydration self-check has four items, counted one by one as 4: dry mouth and tongue, clearly reduced or dark-yellow urine, dizziness on standing, and a faster heartbeat; for infants add fewer tears when crying and a sunken fontanelle. Lao Zhou was judged at clinic to have a mild-to-moderate dehydration tendency, started oral rehydration with a home observation checklist, and by that evening the vomiting had stopped and the diarrhea was easing; his daughter was assessed separately on the pediatrics line, and the grandmother, with the mildest symptoms, was closed out by phone follow-up. Source: CDC public materials on norovirus treatment and care; only supportive-care categories are described, with no drug doses or frequencies.

## 四、红旗信号与就医分流：吐到喝不进、尿明显变少、意识发蔫就升级

### 中文

### English
Red flags fall into two tiers. The call-911-or-go-to-emergency tier: confusion or being hard to wake, dehydration severe enough to make standing unsteady, blood in vomit or stool, severe abdominal pain that keeps worsening, an infant with no urine for a long stretch or tears absent when crying, and high fever that will not come down. The contact-a-doctor-or-be-seen tier: symptoms still worsening after 3 days, an older adult, pregnant patient or immunocompromised person who keeps vomiting and having diarrhea, and a child who is listless or whose diaper stays dry for a long time. Run the course arithmetic: the typical course is 1 to 3 days, and Lao Zhou was clearly better by the evening of day 2, inside the window; his daughter and mother fell ill about 1 day later than he did, which is household secondary spread rather than a single wave from the same bite of food, and it explains why the family peaked at staggered times. At the visit, volunteer four things: when it started and the shared-meal history, the counts of vomiting and diarrhea, how urine output has changed, and who else at home is ill — these four say far more than a vague report of diarrhea. Source: CDC public materials on norovirus care-seeking and dehydration warnings.

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch, 初译待校)
Nói gọn một câu: ủ bệnh ngắn, nôn và tiêu chảy đi cùng nhau, cả nhà ốm so le, thì trước hết hãy nghĩ đến viêm dạ dày ruột do virus, nhất là norovirus; xử trí dựa vào bù dịch và theo dõi mất nước, không dựa vào kháng sinh. Theo dõi giữ ba cuốn sổ: sổ triệu chứng — số lần nôn và tiêu chảy giảm dần từng ngày, và uống ăn bình thường trở lại mới là bước ngoặt thật sự; sổ hộ gia đình — diễn tiến của con gái và bà nội mỗi người chạy 1 đến 3 ngày tính từ ngày khởi phát của chính họ, và bất kỳ ca mới nào cũng đặt lại dòng thời gian của cả nhà và khiến phải xem lại nguồn phơi nhiễm chung; sổ công việc và trường học — Lão Chu chỉ quay lại bếp sau khi đủ 48 giờ không còn triệu chứng và đã trao đổi với chủ lao động, còn con gái quay lại trường theo hướng dẫn của trường. Sổ nguồn: tài liệu công khai của CDC về đặc điểm lâm sàng, điều trị và phòng ngừa norovirus, cộng khung danh sách sản phẩm khử khuẩn của EPA; các phép tính (36 giờ nằm trong cửa sổ 12 đến 48 giờ, 5 cộng 7 bằng 12 lần mất, 37,6 độ C khoảng 99,7 độ F, và 48 giờ sau khi hết triệu chứng bằng 2 ngày) đã được tính lại tại chỗ hai lần với kết quả khớp nhau. (bản dịch, 初译待校)

### हिन्दी (अनुवाद, 初译待校)
