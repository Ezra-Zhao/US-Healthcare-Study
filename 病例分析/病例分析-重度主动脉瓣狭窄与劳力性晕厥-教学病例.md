# 病例分析：重度主动脉瓣狭窄与劳力性晕厥、胸痛及呼吸困难——虚构教学病例

> 虚构教学病例，不对应真实患者。本文为个人学习笔记，不构成医疗建议。全文不写任何药物剂量、给药频次或具体处方用量，只写处理类别与原则。红旗信号见第四节，出现时立即就医，急症拨打 911。

## 一、病例呈现：上坡时眼前发黑的老人

### 中文
周先生（化名），76 岁，男性，虚构病例。近半年走上坡路和爬楼时越来越喘，家人以为是年纪大了。两个月前出现活动时胸口发闷，停下来歇几分钟就缓解。两周前在超市推购物车时突然眼前发黑、短暂失去意识，醒来时已经坐在地上，旁人说前后不到一分钟，没有抽搐、没有大小便失禁，醒后很快清醒。这次就诊血压与心率平稳，听诊在心底部可闻及粗糙的收缩期喷射样杂音、向颈部传导，第二心音减弱。既往有高血压与血脂异常，长期在门诊随访；多年前医生就提过心脏有杂音，他没有认真复查。超声心动图回报：主动脉瓣叶明显钙化、开放受限，瓣口面积 0.8 平方厘米，跨瓣峰值流速每秒 4.6 米，平均跨瓣压差 52 毫米汞柱，左心室射血分数保留，左心室壁增厚。先把算例对一遍：重度界值是瓣口面积不大于 1.0 平方厘米，本例 1.0 减 0.8 等于 0.2，比界值还小 0.2；峰值流速界值是每秒 4.0 米，本例高出 0.6；平均压差界值是 40 毫米汞柱，本例高出 12。三项同向越线，不是单项勉强达标。来源框架：2020 年 ACC/AHA 心脏瓣膜病指南的分期与重度标准。

### English
Mr. Zhou (pseudonym), 76, is a fictional patient. Over six months he has become increasingly breathless walking uphill and climbing stairs, which his family attributed to age. Two months ago exertional chest tightness began, easing after a few minutes of rest. Two weeks ago, while pushing a cart in a supermarket, his vision went black and he briefly lost consciousness; bystanders said it lasted under a minute, with no convulsions or incontinence, and he recovered quickly. Today his blood pressure and heart rate are steady. Auscultation finds a harsh crescendo-decrescendo systolic murmur at the base radiating to the neck, with a soft second heart sound. He has hypertension and dyslipidemia followed in clinic; a murmur was mentioned years ago but never rechecked. Echocardiography shows heavily calcified aortic leaflets with restricted opening, valve area 0.8 cm², peak jet velocity 4.6 m/s, mean gradient 52 mmHg, preserved ejection fraction, and left ventricular hypertrophy. Check the arithmetic: the severe threshold for valve area is 1.0 cm² or less, and 1.0 minus 0.8 leaves this valve 0.2 cm² below the line; peak velocity exceeds its 4.0 m/s threshold by 0.6; the mean gradient exceeds its 40 mmHg threshold by 12. All three point the same way. Framework source: the staging and severity criteria of the 2020 ACC/AHA valvular heart disease guideline.

## 二、定级靠什么：三项指标同向，加上症状，分期才落到 D1

### 中文
主动脉瓣狭窄的严重程度不能只看一项数字。2020 年 ACC/AHA 指南的高梯度重度口径是三项一起看：峰值流速不低于每秒 4.0 米、平均压差不低于 40 毫米汞柱、瓣口面积通常不大于 1.0 平方厘米（或指数化瓣口面积不大于每平方米 0.6 平方厘米）。三项同向时定级最稳；若面积已很小而流速与压差却不高，要先想低流量低梯度这一摊——射血分数降低型与射血分数保留的矛盾型低流量是两回事，定级路径不同，不能硬套高梯度结论。分期再叠一层症状：A 期只有危险因素，B 期是进行性轻中度病变，C 期是无症状重度（再按左心室功能分 C1 与 C2），D 期是有症状重度；其中高梯度有症状者为 D1，低流量低梯度伴射血分数降低者为 D2，射血分数正常而每搏量指数偏低者为 D3。本例三项同向越线，又有劳力性呼吸困难、胸闷与晕厥这一组典型症状（逐项数得 3 件），落在 D1。症状这一层不是装饰：晕厥、心绞痛样胸痛与心衰样呼吸困难是有症状重度的经典三联，出现任何一件，都意味着病程已经从观察期走进需要认真谈干预的阶段。还要留一句反向提醒：有症状重度高梯度者不要做运动负荷试验，指南明确提示这类人运动试验有血流动力学崩溃的风险，定级靠病史与静息超声已经足够。

### English
Severity in aortic stenosis never rests on one number. Under the 2020 ACC/AHA guideline, high-gradient severe disease is read from three measures together: peak velocity at least 4.0 m/s, mean gradient at least 40 mmHg, and valve area typically 1.0 cm² or less (indexed area 0.6 cm²/m² or less). When all three agree, staging is firm. When the area is small but velocity and gradient are not high, think first of the low-flow, low-gradient family — the reduced-ejection-fraction form and the paradoxical low-flow form with preserved ejection fraction are different problems with different staging paths, and the high-gradient conclusion must not be forced onto them. Staging then adds symptoms: stage A is risk only, B is progressive mild-to-moderate disease, C is asymptomatic severe disease (C1 or C2 by left ventricular function), and D is symptomatic severe disease — D1 for high-gradient, D2 for low-flow low-gradient with reduced ejection fraction, and D3 for low-flow low-gradient with normal ejection fraction and low stroke volume index. This patient has all three measures across the line plus the classic symptom set — exertional dyspnea, chest tightness, and syncope, three items counted one by one — which places him in D1. Symptoms are not decoration: syncope, angina-like chest pain, and heart-failure-type breathlessness are the classic triad of symptomatic severe disease, and any one of them moves the case out of watchful waiting and into a serious intervention discussion. One warning in the other direction: do not perform exercise testing in symptomatic high-gradient severe disease — the guideline flags a risk of hemodynamic collapse, and history plus resting echocardiography already settle the staging.

### Español (译稿)
La gravedad de la estenosis aórtica nunca depende de una sola cifra. Según la guía ACC/AHA de 2020, la enfermedad grave de gradiente alto se lee con tres medidas juntas: velocidad máxima de al menos 4,0 m/s, gradiente medio de al menos 40 mmHg y área valvular por lo general de 1,0 cm² o menos (área indexada de 0,6 cm²/m² o menos). Cuando las tres coinciden, la estadificación es firme. Cuando el área es pequeña pero la velocidad y el gradiente no son altos, piense primero en la familia de bajo flujo y bajo gradiente: la forma con fracción de eyección reducida y la forma paradójica con fracción conservada son problemas distintos, con rutas de estadificación distintas. La estadificación añade después los síntomas: estadio A es solo riesgo, B es enfermedad progresiva leve a moderada, C es enfermedad grave asintomática (C1 o C2 según la función ventricular izquierda) y D es enfermedad grave sintomática: D1 para gradiente alto, D2 para bajo flujo y bajo gradiente con fracción reducida, y D3 para bajo flujo y bajo gradiente con fracción normal y volumen sistólico indexado bajo. Este paciente tiene las tres medidas por encima de la línea y el conjunto clásico de síntomas —disnea de esfuerzo, opresión torácica y síncope, tres elementos contados uno a uno—, lo que lo sitúa en D1. Los síntomas no son decoración: el síncope, el dolor torácico de tipo anginoso y la disnea de tipo insuficiencia cardíaca son la tríada clásica de la enfermedad grave sintomática, y cualquiera de ellos saca el caso de la espera vigilante. Una advertencia en sentido contrario: no haga prueba de esfuerzo en la enfermedad grave sintomática de gradiente alto; la guía señala riesgo de colapso hemodinámico, y la historia más el ecocardiograma en reposo ya resuelven la estadificación.

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch, 初译待校)
Mức độ nặng của hẹp van động mạch chủ không bao giờ dựa vào một con số. Theo hướng dẫn ACC/AHA 2020, bệnh nặng chênh áp cao được đọc từ ba chỉ số cùng lúc: vận tốc tối đa ít nhất 4,0 m/giây, chênh áp trung bình ít nhất 40 mmHg, và diện tích van thường 1,0 cm² trở xuống (diện tích theo diện tích bề mặt cơ thể 0,6 cm²/m² trở xuống). ## 四、处置原则（类别层）与红旗信号

### 中文
处置的主线只有一条：有症状的高梯度重度主动脉瓣狭窄（D1），瓣膜置换是能改变病程的干预，药物类别只能处理合并症与症状背景，代替不了把梗阻解除。置换走两条通道——外科主动脉瓣置换与经导管主动脉瓣置换——选哪条由心脏团队结合年龄、手术风险、瓣膜与血管解剖、合并症和病人偏好一起定，不在学习笔记里替具体病人拍板。术前评估通常还包括冠状动脉评估与合并病变排查。无症状重度者是另一套节奏：定期超声随访，出现运动耐量下降、极重度流速、利钠肽明显升高或流速进展很快等信号时提前谈干预；这一层与 D1 的区别要分清，不能把有症状者放进观察节奏里。生活层只留保守口径：有症状重度者在评估完成前避免竞技性与高强度运动，活动以不诱发症状为界，出现症状即停下就医，不靠硬撑测试自己的极限。**红旗信号（单列）：** 再次晕厥或先兆晕厥；静息时也出现的胸痛或呼吸困难；夜间憋醒、不能平躺；下肢水肿快速加重伴体重骤增；心悸持续不缓解；咳粉红色泡沫样痰；任何一件出现都不要等下一次门诊，直接急诊评估，急症拨打 911。

### English
Management has one main line: in symptomatic high-gradient severe aortic stenosis (stage D1), valve replacement is the intervention that changes the course of disease; drug classes can only manage comorbidities and symptom background and cannot substitute for relieving the obstruction. Replacement runs through two channels — surgical aortic valve replacement and transcatheter aortic valve replacement — and the choice belongs to the heart team, weighing age, surgical risk, valve and vascular anatomy, comorbidities, and patient preference together; a study note does not decide it for an individual patient. Pre-procedure assessment commonly includes coronary evaluation and a screen for associated lesions. Asymptomatic severe disease follows a different rhythm: scheduled echo surveillance, with an earlier intervention conversation when exercise tolerance falls, velocity reaches the very severe range, natriuretic peptide rises markedly, or velocity progresses rapidly; this layer must not be confused with D1, and a symptomatic patient must never be parked in the surveillance rhythm. For daily life keep a conservative line: until assessment is complete, a symptomatic patient avoids competitive and high-intensity exercise, keeps activity below the symptom threshold, and stops and seeks care when symptoms appear rather than testing personal limits. **Red flags (listed separately):** another faint or near-faint; chest pain or breathlessness at rest; waking at night gasping or inability to lie flat; rapidly worsening leg swelling with sudden weight gain; palpitations that do not settle; coughing pink frothy sputum. Any one of these means emergency evaluation now rather than waiting for the next clinic visit — in an emergency call 911.

El manejo tiene una línea principal: en la estenosis aórtica grave sintomática de gradiente alto (estadio D1), el reemplazo valvular es la intervención que cambia el curso de la enfermedad; las clases de fármacos solo pueden manejar comorbilidades y el fondo sintomático, y no sustituyen el alivio de la obstrucción. El reemplazo corre por dos vías —reemplazo quirúrgico y reemplazo transcatéter— y la elección corresponde al equipo cardiaco, sopesando edad, riesgo quirúrgico, anatomía valvular y vascular, comorbilidades y preferencia del paciente; una nota de estudio no decide por un paciente concreto. La evaluación previa suele incluir valoración coronaria y búsqueda de lesiones asociadas. La enfermedad grave asintomática sigue otro ritmo: vigilancia ecográfica programada, con conversación de intervención anticipada cuando cae la tolerancia al ejercicio, la velocidad entra en el rango muy grave, el péptido natriurético sube mucho o la velocidad progresa rápido; esta capa no debe confundirse con D1, y un paciente sintomático nunca debe quedar aparcado en el ritmo de vigilancia. En la vida diaria mantenga una línea conservadora: hasta completar la evaluación, evite el ejercicio competitivo e intenso, mantenga la actividad por debajo del umbral de síntomas y deténgase y busque atención si aparecen, sin probar los límites personales. **Señales de alarma (enumeradas aparte):** otro desmayo o casi desmayo; dolor torácico o disnea en reposo; despertar nocturno con ahogo o incapacidad de acostarse plano; hinchazón de piernas que empeora rápido con aumento brusco de peso; palpitaciones que no ceden; tos con esputo espumoso rosado. Cualquiera de ellas exige evaluación urgente inmediata, no esperar a la próxima consulta; en una emergencia llame al 911.

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch, 初译待校)
Xử trí có một trục chính: ở hẹp van động mạch chủ nặng có triệu chứng với chênh áp cao (giai đoạn D1), thay van là can thiệp làm thay đổi diễn tiến bệnh; các nhóm thuốc chỉ xử lý bệnh kèm theo và nền triệu chứng, không thay thế được việc giải phóng tắc nghẽn. Thay van đi theo hai đường — thay van phẫu thuật và thay van qua ống thông — và lựa chọn thuộc về nhóm tim mạch, cân nhắc cùng lúc tuổi, nguy cơ phẫu thuật, giải phẫu van và mạch máu, bệnh kèm theo và nguyện vọng của bệnh nhân; một ghi chú học tập không quyết định thay cho từng bệnh nhân cụ thể. Đánh giá trước thủ thuật thường gồm đánh giá mạch vành và rà soát tổn thương kèm theo. Bệnh nặng không triệu chứng đi theo nhịp khác: theo dõi siêu âm định kỳ, và bàn can thiệp sớm hơn khi dung nạp gắng sức giảm, vận tốc vào vùng rất nặng, peptide lợi niệu tăng rõ, hoặc vận tốc tiến triển nhanh; không được nhầm lớp này với D1, và bệnh nhân đã có triệu chứng không bao giờ được xếp vào nhịp theo dõi chờ đợi. Trong sinh hoạt hằng ngày giữ đường lối thận trọng: trước khi hoàn tất đánh giá, tránh vận động thi đấu và cường độ cao, giữ hoạt động dưới ngưỡng xuất hiện triệu chứng, và khi triệu chứng xuất hiện thì dừng lại và đi khám, không thử giới hạn của bản thân. **Dấu hiệu cờ đỏ (liệt kê riêng):** ngất lại hoặc gần ngất; đau ngực hoặc khó thở ngay cả khi nghỉ; thức giấc ban đêm vì nghẹt thở hoặc không nằm phẳng được; phù chân nặng lên nhanh kèm tăng cân đột ngột; hồi hộp không dứt; ho ra đờm bọt hồng. Bất kỳ dấu hiệu nào cũng cần đánh giá cấp cứu ngay, không chờ đến lần khám tiếp theo; trong tình huống cấp cứu hãy gọi 911. (Bản dịch sơ bộ, 初译待校)

### हिन्दी (अनुवाद, 初译待校)
प्रबंधन की एक मुख्य रेखा है: लक्षणयुक्त उच्च-दाबांतर गंभीर महाधमनी संकुचन (चरण D1) में वाल्व प्रतिस्थापन ही वह हस्तक्षेप है जो रोग की दिशा बदलता है; दवा-वर्ग केवल सह-रोगों और लक्षणों की पृष्ठभूमि संभाल सकते हैं और रुकावट हटाने का विकल्प नहीं बन सकते। प्रतिस्थापन दो मार्गों से होता है — शल्य महाधमनी वाल्व प्रतिस्थापन और ट्रांसकैथेटर प्रतिस्थापन — और चुनाव हृदय टीम का है, जो उम्र, शल्य जोखिम, वाल्व व वाहिका शरीर-रचना, सह-रोग और रोगी की पसंद को साथ तौलती है; अध्ययन नोट किसी व्यक्ति के लिए निर्णय नहीं करता। प्रक्रिया-पूर्व मूल्यांकन में आमतौर पर कोरोनरी मूल्यांकन और संबद्ध क्षतियों की जाँच शामिल होती है। बिना लक्षणों का गंभीर रोग अलग लय अपनाता है: निर्धारित इको निगरानी, और जब व्यायाम सहनशीलता घटे, वेग अत्यंत गंभीर दायरे में पहुँचे, नैट्रियूरेटिक पेप्टाइड स्पष्ट बढ़े या वेग तेज़ी से बढ़े तो हस्तक्षेप की बात पहले करना; इस परत को D1 से न मिलाएँ, और लक्षणयुक्त रोगी को निगरानी लय में कभी न छोड़ें। दैनिक जीवन में रूढ़िवादी रेखा रखें: मूल्यांकन पूरा होने तक प्रतिस्पर्धी और उच्च-तीव्रता व्यायाम से बचें, गतिविधि लक्षण-सीमा के नीचे रखें, और लक्षण आते ही रुककर देखभाल लें, अपनी सीमा की परीक्षा न लें। **लाल झंडे (अलग से सूचीबद्ध):** फिर बेहोशी या बेहोशी-जैसा एहसास; विश्राम में भी सीना दर्द या साँस फूलना; रात में हाँफते हुए जागना या सीधा लेट न पाना; अचानक वज़न बढ़ने के साथ पैरों की सूजन का तेज़ी से बिगड़ना; न थमने वाली धड़कन; गुलाबी झागदार बलगम वाली खाँसी। इनमें से कोई भी हो तो अगले क्लिनिक की प्रतीक्षा न करें, तुरंत आपात मूल्यांकन लें — आपात स्थिति में 911 पर कॉल करें। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद, 初译待校)
