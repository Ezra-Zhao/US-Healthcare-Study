# 病例分析：急性细菌性鼻窦炎与感冒后双峰加重（教学病例）

> 虚构教学病例，不对应真实患者。本文为个人学习笔记，不构成医疗建议。全文不写任何药物剂量、给药频次或具体处方用量，只写处理类别与原则。红旗信号见第四节，出现时立即就医，急症拨打 911。
> 选题说明：本轮病例分析经 ls/grep 实查，「急性鼻窦炎」grep 全仓零命中、无本体专篇；仓内 ANCA 血管炎篇与脑脓肿篇仅顺带提及鼻窦炎，本篇专讲普通感冒之后分岔出的细菌性鼻窦炎线，两边以指针分工，不重复选题。

## 一、病例呈现：感冒先好转，第八天又烧起来

### 中文
陈先生（化名），38 岁，男性，虚构病例，办公室职员，无已知药物过敏史。第 0 天起鼻塞、清涕、咽痛、低热，按普通感冒在家休息；第 5 天热退、鼻涕明显减少，自觉好了大半；第 8 天起鼻塞再加重，鼻涕转黄绿色且量多，左侧面颊胀痛、低头时更重，体温再升到 38.6 摄氏度，夜间因鼻塞咳嗽睡不好；第 12 天到诊所就诊。先把三笔算例对一遍：体温换算，38.6 乘 9 除 5 加 32 等于 101.48，约 101.5 华氏度；双峰间隔，第 8 天减第 5 天等于 3 天，即好转 3 天后再加重；总病程，第 12 天已越过 10 天这条线（本地复算两遍一致）。来源框架：美国感染病学会（IDSA）急性细菌性鼻窦炎指南与美国耳鼻咽喉头颈外科学会（AAO-HNS）成人鼻窦炎临床实践指南的公开口径。

### English
Mr. Chen (pseudonym), 38 years old, male, is a fictional patient who works in an office and has no known drug allergies. On day 0 he developed nasal congestion, clear discharge, a sore throat and a low fever, and rested at home treating it as a common cold; by day 5 the fever had resolved and the discharge had clearly decreased, and he felt mostly better; from day 8 the congestion worsened again, the discharge turned yellow-green and heavy, he had fullness and pain over the left cheek that worsened when bending down, the temperature rose again to 38.6 degrees Celsius, and he slept poorly because of congestion and cough; on day 12 he visited a clinic. Check three calculations: the temperature conversion, 38.6 times 9 divided by 5 plus 32 equals 101.48, about 101.5 degrees Fahrenheit; the double-sickening interval, day 8 minus day 5 equals 3 days of improvement before worsening again; and the total course, day 12, which has crossed the 10-day line (recounted twice, consistent). Framework sources: the Infectious Diseases Society of America (IDSA) guideline on acute bacterial rhinosinusitis and the American Academy of Otolaryngology–Head and Neck Surgery (AAO-HNS) clinical practice guideline on adult sinusitis.

## 二、诊断框架：先分病毒还是细菌，三种病程型定方向

### 中文
鼻窦炎先按时长分格：症状在 4 周以内为急性，12 周或更久为慢性，中间为亚急性，本例 12 天落在急性格。急性里面再分病毒性与细菌性——多数急性鼻窦炎是病毒性的，随感冒在一周左右自行好转；指向细菌性的病程型有三种，逐项数得 3：持续型，鼻涕、鼻塞、白天咳嗽等症状持续 10 天或更久、中间没有任何好转；重症型，起病即高热不低于 39 摄氏度，伴脓性鼻涕与面部疼痛，连续至少 3 天；加重型，先好转、再在病程内新发发热或鼻涕与头痛明显加重，就是常说的先好后坏、双峰加重。本例命中加重型，总病程又越过 10 天线，两条同向。两个常见误读先纠正：鼻涕发黄发绿本身分不出病毒还是细菌，单侧面颊痛也只是辅助线索，定方向靠的是病程型而不是鼻涕颜色。检查一侧：无并发症迹象的急性鼻窦炎不常规拍片或做 CT，影像主要留给怀疑并发症、反复发作或准备手术评估时；鼻内镜与病原学检查由专科按需要决定。来源：IDSA 指南与 AAO-HNS 指南公开口径。

### English
Rhinosinusitis is first sorted by duration: symptoms under 4 weeks are acute, 12 weeks or longer are chronic, and the span in between is subacute; this case, at 12 days, sits in the acute box. Within acute disease, the next split is viral versus bacterial — most acute rhinosinusitis is viral and settles on its own with the cold in about a week. Three course patterns point toward a bacterial process, counted one by one as 3: the persistent pattern, in which discharge, congestion and daytime cough last 10 days or more with no improvement at any point; the severe pattern, with onset of high fever of at least 39 degrees Celsius together with purulent discharge and facial pain for at least 3 consecutive days; and the worsening pattern, in which the patient first improves and then develops new fever or clearly worse discharge and headache within the same illness — the familiar get-better-then-worse, double-sickening course. This case matches the worsening pattern, and its total course has also crossed the 10-day line, so two signals point the same way. Correct two common misreadings first: yellow or green discharge by itself cannot separate viral from bacterial disease, and one-sided cheek pain is only a supporting clue; the direction is set by the course pattern, not by the color of the discharge. On testing: acute rhinosinusitis without signs of complications does not routinely need plain films or CT; imaging is reserved mainly for suspected complications, recurrent episodes or surgical evaluation, and nasal endoscopy and microbiological testing are decided by specialists as needed. Sources: the IDSA and AAO-HNS guideline materials.

### Español (译稿)
La rinosinusitis se ordena primero por duración: síntomas de menos de 4 semanas son agudos, de 12 semanas o más son crónicos, y el tramo intermedio es subagudo; este caso, con 12 días, cae en la casilla aguda. Dentro de lo agudo, la siguiente división es viral frente a bacteriana: la mayoría de las rinosinusitis agudas son virales y se resuelven solas con el resfriado en torno a una semana. Tres patrones de evolución apuntan a un proceso bacteriano, contados uno a uno como 3: el patrón persistente, con secreción, congestión y tos diurna que duran 10 días o más sin ninguna mejoría; el patrón grave, con inicio de fiebre alta de al menos 39 grados Celsius junto con secreción purulenta y dolor facial durante al menos 3 días seguidos; y el patrón de empeoramiento, en el que el paciente primero mejora y luego presenta fiebre nueva o secreción y cefalea claramente peores dentro de la misma enfermedad, el conocido «primero mejor, luego peor» o doble empeoramiento. Este caso coincide con el patrón de empeoramiento y su curso total también cruzó la línea de los 10 días, así que dos señales apuntan en la misma dirección. Corrija antes dos lecturas erróneas comunes: la secreción amarilla o verde por sí sola no distingue lo viral de lo bacteriano, y el dolor en una sola mejilla es solo una pista de apoyo; la dirección la marca el patrón de evolución, no el color de la secreción. En cuanto a las pruebas: la rinosinusitis aguda sin signos de complicaciones no necesita de rutina radiografías ni TC; la imagen se reserva sobre todo para sospecha de complicaciones, episodios recurrentes o evaluación quirúrgica, y la endoscopia nasal y los estudios microbiológicos los decide el especialista según la necesidad. Fuentes: materiales de las guías de la IDSA y la AAO-HNS. (初译待校)

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch, 初译待校)
Viêm mũi xoang trước hết được chia theo thời gian: triệu chứng dưới 4 tuần là cấp, từ 12 tuần trở lên là mạn, và khoảng giữa là bán cấp; ca này, 12 ngày, nằm trong ô cấp. Trong nhóm cấp, bước chia tiếp theo là do virus hay vi khuẩn — đa số viêm mũi xoang cấp là do virus và tự lui cùng cảm lạnh trong khoảng một tuần. Ba kiểu diễn tiến hướng tới quá trình vi khuẩn, đếm từng kiểu thành 3: kiểu kéo dài, trong đó chảy mũi, nghẹt mũi và ho ban ngày kéo dài 10 ngày trở lên mà không hề cải thiện; kiểu nặng, khởi phát với sốt cao ít nhất 39 độ C kèm chảy mũi mủ và đau mặt trong ít nhất 3 ngày liên tiếp; và kiểu xấu đi, trong đó bệnh nhân đầu tiên cải thiện rồi xuất hiện sốt mới hoặc chảy mũi và đau đầu rõ ràng nặng hơn trong cùng một đợt bệnh — diễn tiến quen thuộc «tốt lên rồi xấu đi», hay hai đỉnh. Ca này khớp với kiểu xấu đi, và tổng diễn tiến cũng đã vượt mốc 10 ngày, nên hai tín hiệu cùng chỉ một hướng. Sửa hai cách hiểu sai thường gặp trước: nước mũi vàng hay xanh tự nó không phân biệt được virus với vi khuẩn, và đau một bên má chỉ là manh mối hỗ trợ; hướng đi do kiểu diễn tiến quyết định, không phải do màu nước mũi. Về xét nghiệm: viêm mũi xoang cấp không có dấu hiệu biến chứng thì không cần chụp phim thường hay CT một cách thường quy; hình ảnh chủ yếu dành cho khi nghi biến chứng, tái phát nhiều lần hoặc đánh giá phẫu thuật, và nội soi mũi cùng xét nghiệm vi sinh do chuyên khoa quyết định theo nhu cầu. ## 三、处置框架：支持处理打底，抗菌只在细菌型成立时上场

### 中文
处置分两条线。第一条是支持处理，所有急性鼻窦炎都能用：生理盐水冲洗鼻腔、鼻用糖皮质激素类别喷鼻、退热镇痛类别按说明书与医嘱使用（本篇不写任何数值），多休息、补足水分；减充血类别只作短期辅助，长期连用反而会造成反跳性鼻塞，具体节奏听医嘱与药师交代。第二条是抗菌线，只在细菌型病程成立时讨论：医生结合病程型、严重程度与过敏史，在及时使用抗菌药物类别与先观察等待之间二选一——病情不重、无并发症迹象者，指南允许约定好复评时点先观察数日，无好转或再加重再启动抗菌；重症型与高危者不走观察线。无论走哪条，都把复评时点当场说清：用药后数日内没有好转、或任何时点再加重，都回诊所复评而不是自己换药加药；更不许拿家里剩下的旧抗生素自行拼疗程，那既可能不对症，也会把后续判断搅乱。来源：AAO-HNS 成人鼻窦炎指南与 IDSA 指南的公开处置口径。

### English
Management runs on two lines. The first is supportive care, usable in all acute rhinosinusitis: saline irrigation of the nose, intranasal corticosteroid-category sprays, and fever- and pain-relief categories used as directed on the label or by the clinician (this article states no amounts), plus rest and adequate fluids; decongestant categories are only a short-term aid, because prolonged use can cause rebound congestion, and the exact rhythm follows the clinician's and pharmacist's instructions. The second line is the antibacterial line, discussed only when a bacterial course pattern is established: weighing the pattern, severity and allergy history, the clinician chooses between starting an antibacterial category promptly and watchful waiting first — for patients who are not severely ill and show no signs of complications, the guidelines allow an agreed re-evaluation point with a few days of observation, starting the antibacterial only if there is no improvement or further worsening; the severe pattern and high-risk patients do not take the observation line. Whichever line is chosen, the re-evaluation point is stated on the spot: no improvement within a few days of treatment, or worsening at any point, means a return visit for reassessment rather than switching or adding medicines on one's own; and leftover antibiotics at home must never be assembled into a self-made course, which may miss the target entirely and muddy every later judgment. Sources: the AAO-HNS adult sinusitis guideline and the IDSA guideline on management.

## 四、红旗：眼眶与颅内两组信号，占任何一组当天急诊

### 中文
鼻窦紧挨眼眶与颅底，少数病例的感染会越界，红旗分两组，逐项数得 2。第一组是眼眶信号：眼周红肿、眼球突出或移位、看东西重影或视力下降、眼球转动时疼痛——提示感染向眼眶扩散的方向。第二组是颅内信号：剧烈头痛、高热不退、反复呕吐、脖子发硬、意识或精神状态改变——提示颅内并发症方向。两组占任何一组都不在家观察，当天走急诊或拨打 911。另有两条随访线不算急诊红旗、但要记进日历：一年内反复发作多次、或症状拖过 12 周转入慢性格，都要转耳鼻喉专科评估鼻腔结构、过敏与免疫背景，不按一次感冒的节奏反复拖。来源：IDSA 指南与 AAO-HNS 指南的并发症公开口径。

### English
The sinuses sit right next to the orbit and the skull base, and in a minority of cases the infection crosses that border; the red flags fall into two groups, counted one by one as 2. The first group is orbital: redness and swelling around the eye, protrusion or displacement of the eyeball, double vision or reduced vision, and pain when moving the eye — pointing toward spread into the orbit. The second group is intracranial: severe headache, persistent high fever, repeated vomiting, a stiff neck, and altered consciousness or mental state — pointing toward an intracranial complication. Any item from either group means no further watching at home: go to emergency care the same day or call 911. Two follow-up lines are not emergency red flags but belong on the calendar: repeated episodes within a year, or symptoms dragging past 12 weeks into the chronic box, both call for referral to an ear, nose and throat specialist to assess nasal structure, allergy and immune background, rather than being carried repeatedly on a one-cold timetable. Sources: the IDSA and AAO-HNS guideline materials on complications.

## 五、预防与随访：能做的是减少感冒与管好鼻腔背景

### 中文
预防没有专门疫苗这一格，能执行的是三件，逐项数得 3：勤洗手、感冒流行季少揉眼摸鼻，减少病毒性感冒的次数，细菌性发作的入口也跟着少；按 CDC 推荐口径接种流感疫苗类别，减少流感后继发鼻窦问题的机会；有过敏性鼻炎背景者把过敏管理做稳，戒烟并避开二手烟，鼻腔黏膜的日常状态稳了，发作频率通常跟着降。随访刻度只有一句：急性发作按第三节约定的复评时点回读症状——鼻塞、鼻涕、面痛、体温四件一起看，好了就收尾，没好或转慢性就升级到专科线，不让同一段病程在没有复评的情况下无限延长。来源：CDC 呼吸道病毒预防公开口径与 AAO-HNS 指南公开资料。

### English
Prevention has no dedicated vaccine box; what can actually be done is three items, counted one by one as 3: wash hands often and rub the eyes and nose less during cold season, so viral colds become fewer and the entry points for bacterial episodes shrink with them; receive the influenza vaccine category as recommended in the CDC framework, reducing the chance of sinus problems following influenza; and, for those with an allergic rhinitis background, keep allergy management steady, quit smoking and avoid secondhand smoke — when the everyday state of the nasal lining is stable, the episode frequency usually falls with it. The follow-up scale is a single sentence: after an acute episode, re-read the symptoms at the re-evaluation point agreed in section three — congestion, discharge, facial pain and temperature taken together; if resolved, close the episode, and if not resolved or turning chronic, escalate to the specialist line, so that one illness is never stretched indefinitely without a re-evaluation. Sources: the CDC public materials on respiratory-virus prevention and the AAO-HNS guideline materials.

### Español (译稿)
La prevención no tiene una casilla de vacuna específica; lo ejecutable son tres puntos, contados uno a uno como 3: lavarse las manos a menudo y frotarse menos los ojos y la nariz en la temporada de resfriados, para que los resfriados virales sean menos y con ellos se achiquen las puertas de entrada de los episodios bacterianos; recibir la categoría de vacuna antigripal según el marco recomendado por los CDC, reduciendo la probabilidad de problemas de senos tras la gripe; y, en quienes tienen fondo de rinitis alérgica, mantener estable el manejo de la alergia, dejar de fumar y evitar el humo ajeno: cuando el estado cotidiano de la mucosa nasal es estable, la frecuencia de episodios suele bajar con él. La escala de seguimiento es una sola frase: tras un episodio agudo, relea los síntomas en el punto de reevaluación acordado en la tercera sección —congestión, secreción, dolor facial y temperatura tomados en conjunto—; si se resolvió, se cierra el episodio, y si no se resolvió o se está volviendo crónico, se escala a la línea del especialista, para que una misma enfermedad nunca se estire indefinidamente sin una reevaluación. Fuentes: materiales públicos de los CDC sobre prevención de virus respiratorios y materiales de la guía de la AAO-HNS. (初译待校)
