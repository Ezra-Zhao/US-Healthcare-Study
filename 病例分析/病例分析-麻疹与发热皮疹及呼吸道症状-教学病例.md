# 病例分析：麻疹与发热、皮疹及呼吸道症状（教学病例）

> 虚构教学病例，不对应真实患者。本文为个人学习笔记，不构成医疗建议。全文不写任何药物剂量、给药频次或具体处方用量，只写处理类别与原则。红旗信号见第五节，出现时立即就医，急症拨打 911。
> 选题说明：本轮病例分析经 ls/grep 实查，「麻疹」作病例本体 grep 全仓无专篇（仅旅行医学篇在疫苗名中顺带出现），仓内已有流行性腮腺炎篇、手足口篇与川崎病篇讲发热皮疹线，本篇专讲麻疹的前驱三联、Koplik 斑与头面部向躯干蔓延的斑丘疹线，各篇以指针分工，不重复选题。

## 一、病例呈现：旅行归来十二天后发热，第四天出疹

### 中文
小航（化名），4 岁，男孩，虚构病例，未接种麻疹相关疫苗，随家人赴国外旅行两周后归来。归来后第 10 天开始发热，体温 38.9 摄氏度，伴咳嗽、流涕、眼睛发红流泪，家人先按普通感冒在家观察。发热第 2 天，口腔颊黏膜上出现针尖大小的白色小点，周围带红晕。发热第 4 天，耳后与发际先冒出红色斑丘疹，随后蔓延到面部、躯干与四肢，疹子局部融合成片，发热仍未退。先把三笔算例对一遍：体温换算，38.9 乘 9 除 5 加 32 等于 102.02，约 102.0 华氏度；时间线，暴露（旅行中接触）到发热 10 天、发热到出疹 4 天，两段相加 10 加 4 等于 14 天，正落在麻疹暴露到出疹平均约 14 天的口径上；传染期，出疹前 4 天至出疹后 4 天，4 加 1 加 4 等于 9 天（本地复算两遍一致）。来源框架：美国疾病控制与预防中心（CDC）麻疹临床公开口径。

### English
Xiaohang (pseudonym), 4 years old, male, is a fictional patient who had not received measles-containing vaccine and returned from a two-week family trip abroad. On day 10 after return he developed fever of 38.9 degrees Celsius with cough, runny nose and red, watery eyes, and the family first watched him at home as if it were a common cold. On day 2 of fever, tiny white spots with a red halo appeared on the buccal mucosa. On day 4 of fever, a red maculopapular rash started behind the ears and at the hairline, then spread to the face, trunk and limbs, merging in places, while the fever persisted. Check three calculations: the temperature conversion, 38.9 times 9 divided by 5 plus 32 equals 102.02, about 102.0 degrees Fahrenheit; the timeline — 10 days from exposure (contact during travel) to fever plus 4 days from fever to rash, 10 plus 4 equals 14 days, matching the average of about 14 days from exposure to rash onset for measles; and the infectious period — 4 days before through 4 days after rash onset, 4 plus 1 plus 4 equals 9 days (recounted twice, consistent). Framework source: the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) public clinical materials on measles.

## 二、诊断框架：前驱三联、Koplik 斑与出疹顺序三段对齐

### 中文
麻疹的诊断先靠临床时间线，三段对齐就高度提示。第一段是前驱期：发热加上三联——咳嗽（cough）、流涕（coryza）、结膜炎（conjunctivitis），即临床常说的三个 C，逐项数得 3，通常在出疹前 2 到 4 天出现；潜伏期口径为暴露到前驱症状平均约 10 到 12 天、症状通常在感染后 7 到 14 天出现，本例 10 天正落在窗内。第二段是 Koplik 斑：症状开始后约 2 到 3 天，颊黏膜上出现白色小点，是麻疹较具特征性的早期体征，窗口很短，出疹前后就消退，所以发热孩子张嘴看一眼口腔不能省。第三段是出疹期：首发症状后约 3 到 5 天出疹，红色斑丘疹从耳后、发际与面部开始，自上而下蔓延到颈、躯干、四肢，持续约 5 到 6 天后按出现的顺序消退。实验室一侧，疑似病例要尽早通知辖区卫生部门，并按公共卫生口径留取标本做核酸与抗体检测核实——麻疹在美国属应立即报告的病种，临床怀疑本身就是报告与隔离的触发点，不等化验回来才行动。来源：CDC 麻疹临床诊断与症状并发症公开口径。

### English
The diagnosis of measles rests first on the clinical timeline, and three aligned segments make it highly suggestive. The first segment is the prodrome: fever plus the triad of cough, coryza and conjunctivitis — the three Cs, counted one by one as 3 — usually appearing 2 to 4 days before the rash; the incubation period averages about 10 to 12 days from exposure to prodromal symptoms, with symptoms usually beginning 7 to 14 days after infection, and this case's 10 days sits inside that window. The second segment is Koplik spots: tiny white spots on the buccal mucosa appearing about 2 to 3 days after symptoms begin, a relatively characteristic early sign of measles whose window is short — they fade around rash onset, so looking inside the mouth of a febrile child should not be skipped. The third segment is the rash phase: about 3 to 5 days after the first symptoms, a red maculopapular rash begins behind the ears, at the hairline and on the face, spreads downward to the neck, trunk and limbs, lasts about 5 to 6 days, and fades in the order it appeared. On the laboratory side, a suspected case should be reported promptly to the local health department, with specimens collected for nucleic-acid and antibody testing under public-health guidance — measles is an immediately notifiable condition in the United States, and clinical suspicion itself is the trigger for reporting and isolation, not the returned lab result. Source: CDC public materials on the clinical diagnosis, signs and complications of measles.

### Español (译稿)
El diagnóstico del sarampión se apoya primero en la línea temporal clínica, y tres segmentos alineados lo hacen muy sugestivo. El primero es el pródromo: fiebre más la tríada de tos, coriza y conjuntivitis —las tres C, contadas una a una como 3—, que suele aparecer 2 a 4 días antes de la erupción; el período de incubación promedia unos 10 a 12 días desde la exposición hasta los síntomas prodrómicos, y los síntomas suelen comenzar 7 a 14 días después de la infección, y los 10 días de este caso caen dentro de esa ventana. El segundo segmento son las manchas de Koplik: puntitos blancos en la mucosa bucal que aparecen unos 2 a 3 días después del inicio de los síntomas, un signo temprano relativamente característico cuya ventana es breve —se desvanecen en torno a la erupción—, así que no debe omitirse mirar la boca del niño febril. El tercer segmento es la fase eruptiva: unos 3 a 5 días después de los primeros síntomas, una erupción maculopapular roja comienza detrás de las orejas, en la línea del cabello y en la cara, se extiende hacia abajo a cuello, tronco y extremidades, dura unos 5 a 6 días y se desvanece en el orden en que apareció. En el lado del laboratorio, un caso sospechoso debe notificarse pronto al departamento de salud local, con muestras para pruebas de ácidos nucleicos y anticuerpos según la orientación de salud pública: el sarampión es de notificación inmediata en Estados Unidos, y la propia sospecha clínica es el disparador de la notificación y el aislamiento, no el resultado del laboratorio. Fuente: materiales públicos de los CDC sobre diagnóstico clínico, signos y complicaciones del sarampión. (初译待校)

### 한국어 (초역, 初译待校)
홍역 진단은 먼저 임상 시간선에 기대며, 세 구간이 맞아떨어지면 강하게 시사됩니다. 첫 구간은 전구기로, 발열에 기침, 콧물(코감기), 결막염의 세 증상의 묶음, 즉 세 가지 C가 더해지며 하나씩 세어 3가지이고 보통 발진 2일에서 4일 전에 나타납니다. 잠복기는 노출에서 전구 증상까지 평균 약 10일에서 12일이고 증상은 보통 감염 후 7일에서 14일에 시작하는데, 본 증례의 10일이 그 창 안에 들어갑니다. 둘째 구간은 코플릭 반점으로, 증상 시작 약 2일에서 3일 뒤 볼 점막에 나타나는 흰 반점이며 홍역에 비교적 특징적인 초기 징후이지만 창이 짧아 발진 전후로 사라지므로 열이 있는 아이의 입안을 들여다보는 일을 빼먹어서는 안 됩니다. 셋째 구간은 발진기로, 첫 증상 후 약 3일에서 5일에 붉은 반점구진 발진이 귀 뒤, 이마선, 얼굴에서 시작해 목, 몸통, 팔다리로 아래쪽으로 퍼지고 약 5일에서 6일 지속한 뒤 나타난 순서대로 사라집니다. 검사 쪽에서는 의심 증례를 관할 보건당국에 신속히 알리고 공중보건 지침에 따라 핵산 검사와 항체 검사용 검체를 남겨야 합니다. 홍역은 미국에서 즉시 보고 대상 질환이며, 보고와 격리의 방아쇠는 검사 결과가 아니라 임상적 의심 그 자체입니다. 출처는 CDC의 홍역 임상 진단, 증상과 합병증에 대한 공개 자료입니다. ## 四、并发症分层与鉴别：常见、严重与四个鉴别对手

### 中文

### English
Complications are read in two tiers. The common tier: diarrhea and otitis media are the most common — otitis media occurs in about 1 of every 10 children with measles, that is, 1 divided by 10 equals 10 percent. The severe tier: pneumonia is the most common cause of measles death in young children, on the order of up to about 1 in 20 children, that is, 1 divided by 20 equals 5 percent; encephalitis occurs in about 1 of every 1,000 children, that is, 1 divided by 1,000 equals 0.1 percent, and can leave convulsions, deafness or intellectual disability; death occurs in about 1 to 3 of every 1,000, and there is also the very rare late subacute sclerosing panencephalitis (SSPE), generally developing about 7 to 10 years after measles. ### English
Most measles improves gradually over one to two weeks, and what needs watching is the complication end; the red flags fall into four groups, counted one by one as 4. The first group is respiratory: rapid or labored breathing, bluish lips, clearly low oxygen saturation and listlessness — pointing toward pneumonia. The second group is neurological: hard-to-wake sleepiness, convulsions, neck stiffness, severe headache and repeated vomiting — pointing toward encephalitis. The third group is dehydration plus ear and eye signals: clearly reduced urine output, no tears when crying and cracked dry lips; severe ear pain or pus drainage; marked eye pain, worsening light sensitivity or vision change. The fourth group is host signals: the patient is a young infant, is pregnant, or is immunocompromised, or fever returns after having settled once the rash appeared — these are not watched at home on a routine timetable. Any one of the four groups means no further watching at home — go to emergency care the same day or call 911. Two further course judgments: a cough that drags on too long after the rash fades, or new fever and new symptoms days after the fever settled, deserve a clinic re-evaluation for secondary infection rather than being carried on a self-limited timetable; and pregnant people and young infants in the same household, even without symptoms yet, should contact a clinician and the health department at once to check post-exposure management instead of waiting for symptoms. Source: CDC public materials on measles complications.

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch, 初译待校)

Bất kỳ nhóm nào trong bốn nhóm cũng có nghĩa là không tiếp tục theo dõi ở nhà — đi cấp cứu trong ngày hoặc gọi 911.
