## 二、资格链与考试：先内科、再专科培训、再上考场

### 中文

资格链三段。第一段，持有有效的内科 board 认证，衔接状态以 ABIM 当期政策为准。第二段，完成 ACGME 认可的传染病专科培训（fellowship），通常为两年，训练重心在住院与门诊感染会诊、微生物实验室轮转、医院感染防控、抗菌药物管理与免疫受损宿主感染，培训结束时须由项目主任确认其临床胜任力。第三段，通过传染病认证考试（Infectious Disease Certification Examination）：机考，考纲覆盖细菌、病毒、真菌与寄生虫感染，HIV 感染，移植与免疫受损宿主感染，感染防控与抗菌药物管理，旅行与热带感染等域。通过后发亚专科证书，之后按 ABIM 的持续认证（MOC）体系维持，具体可选路径以 ABIM 当期公布为准，证书状态以 board 查询系统为准。备考要点：传染病考的不是背下多少病原体名，而是从发热曲线、暴露史与宿主免疫状态出发排鉴别、选检查、定经验性处理的大方向，药物只考类别与选用逻辑，本篇同样只记类别、不写任何药物剂量与频次。

### English

The eligibility chain has three links. First, current board certification in internal medicine, with transitional status read from ABIM's current policy. Second, completion of an ACGME-accredited infectious disease fellowship, typically two years, centered on inpatient and outpatient infection consultation, microbiology laboratory rotations, hospital infection prevention, antimicrobial stewardship, and infections in immunocompromised hosts, with the program director attesting to clinical competence at the end of training. Third, passing the Infectious Disease Certification Examination, a computer-based test spanning bacterial, viral, fungal, and parasitic infections, HIV infection, transplant and immunocompromised-host infections, infection prevention and antimicrobial stewardship, and travel and tropical infections. The subspecialty certificate is then maintained through ABIM's Maintenance of Certification (MOC) program, with available pathways read from ABIM's current postings and status verifiable in the board's lookup system. Exam strategy: the test rarely asks candidates to recite pathogen names; it asks them to rank a differential from the fever pattern, exposure history, and host immune status, choose the workup, and set the broad direction of empiric management. Drugs are tested at the level of classes and selection logic, and this article likewise records classes only, with no drug doses or frequencies.

### Español

La cadena de elegibilidad tiene tres eslabones. Primero, certificación vigente en medicina interna, con el estado transitorio según la política actual de ABIM. Segundo, completar una especialización (fellowship) en enfermedades infecciosas acreditada por ACGME, normalmente de dos años, centrada en consulta de infecciones hospitalarias y ambulatorias, rotaciones en el laboratorio de microbiología, prevención de infecciones, gestión de antimicrobianos e infecciones en huéspedes inmunocomprometidos, con la confirmación de competencia clínica por el director del programa. Tercero, aprobar el examen de certificación en Enfermedades Infecciosas, por computadora, con temario de infecciones bacterianas, virales, fúngicas y parasitarias, infección por VIH, infecciones en trasplantados e inmunocomprometidos, prevención y gestión de antimicrobianos, e infecciones del viajero y tropicales. El certificado se mantiene mediante el programa de certificación continua (MOC) de ABIM. Clave de examen: no se pide recitar nombres de patógenos, sino ordenar el diagnóstico diferencial desde la fiebre, los antecedentes de exposición y el estado inmunitario del huésped.

## 三、加州衔接：执照归州、认证归 board

### 中文

加州衔接记三点。第一，行医执照与专科认证是两本账：医师执照由加州医学委员会（Medical Board of California）按《商业与职业法典》第 2000 条一带发放，整骨医师走加州整骨医学委员会与 AOA 系认证通道；ABIM 的传染病证书不替代执照，没有加州执照，持证也不能在加州行医。第二，加州没有另设「传染病执照」这一州证类别，州层能查的是执照状态与纪律记录；医师对外宣传 board 认证时受第 651 条一带广告真实性规则约束，不得把自愿取得的专科认证说成州政府背书或州发的资格。第三，实务落点：加州医院的抗菌药物管理有州法一侧的要求（《健康与安全法典》第 1288.85 条一带要求医院建立抗菌药物管理机制，本仓抗菌药物管理法规篇有专篇），传染病医师常在其中担任牵头或核心会诊角色，认证也常出现在医院感染防控、移植中心与肿瘤中心会诊岗位的聘任要求里，具体以各机构当期要求为准。来源：ABIM 官方认证说明、加州医学委员会执照口径与上述加州法条。

声明：本文为个人学习笔记，不构成医疗建议；具体事务请咨询有执照的专业人士。全篇不写任何药物剂量与频次。

### English

Three California notes. First, license and certificate are separate ledgers: physician licensure comes from the Medical Board of California under the Medical Practice Act (Bus. & Prof. Code section 2000 et seq.), osteopathic physicians run through the Osteopathic Medical Board of California and AOA-board channels, and an ABIM infectious disease certificate never substitutes for a license. Second, California has no separate state infectious disease license category; the state verifies licensure status and discipline, and a physician advertising board certification is bound by the truth-in-advertising rules around Bus. & Prof. Code section 651 — a voluntary specialty certificate must not be presented as a state endorsement or a state-issued credential. Third, in practice California hospitals carry a state-law antimicrobial stewardship duty (Health & Saf. Code section 1288.85 et seq., covered in this repository's stewardship law article), and infectious disease physicians commonly lead or anchor those programs and appear in hiring requirements for infection prevention, transplant, and oncology consultation roles — always check the institution's current posting. Sources: ABIM certification materials, Medical Board of California licensure guidance, and the California statutes cited. Disclaimer: Personal study notes only; not medical advice. This article states no drug doses or frequencies.

### Español

Tres notas de California. Primera, licencia y certificado son cuentas separadas: la licencia médica la emite el Medical Board of California bajo la Medical Practice Act (Bus. & Prof. Code § 2000 y ss.), los médicos osteópatas van por el Osteopathic Medical Board y las juntas de la AOA, y el certificado de enfermedades infecciosas de ABIM nunca sustituye la licencia. Segunda, California no tiene una categoría estatal de «licencia en enfermedades infecciosas», y quien anuncia una certificación está sujeto a las reglas de publicidad veraz en torno a la § 651: un certificado voluntario no puede presentarse como respaldo estatal. Tercera, los hospitales de California tienen un deber estatal de gestión de antimicrobianos (§ 1288.85 y ss. del Código de Salud y Seguridad), y los infectólogos suelen encabezar esos programas y figurar en los requisitos de contratación de prevención de infecciones, trasplantes y oncología. ### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ, chờ hiệu đính)

Ba điểm nối với California. Một, giấy phép và chứng chỉ là hai sổ riêng: giấy phép hành nghề do Hội đồng Y khoa California cấp theo Đạo luật Hành nghề Y (Bus. & Prof. Code § 2000 trở đi), bác sĩ nắn xương đi qua Hội đồng Y khoa Nắn xương California và kênh hội đồng AOA, và chứng chỉ bệnh truyền nhiễm của ABIM không bao giờ thay thế giấy phép. Hai, California không có hạng mục giấy phép bệnh truyền nhiễm riêng của bang, và người quảng cáo chứng chỉ hội đồng phải tuân thủ các quy tắc quảng cáo trung thực quanh § 651 — chứng chỉ chuyên khoa tự nguyện không được trình bày như sự bảo chứng của bang. Ba, bệnh viện California có nghĩa vụ theo luật bang về quản lý kháng sinh (§ 1288.85 trở đi của Bộ luật Sức khỏe và An toàn), và bác sĩ truyền nhiễm thường dẫn dắt các chương trình đó, đồng thời xuất hiện trong yêu cầu tuyển dụng về phòng ngừa nhiễm khuẩn, ghép tạng và hội chẩn ung thư. Nguồn: tài liệu chứng chỉ của ABIM, hướng dẫn cấp phép của Hội đồng Y khoa California và các điều luật California đã dẫn. Ghi chú: tài liệu học tập cá nhân, không phải tư vấn y tế; bài này không ghi liều lượng hay tần suất thuốc nào.

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद, समीक्षा लंबित)

कैलिफ़ोर्निया से जुड़ी तीन बातें। पहली, लाइसेंस और प्रमाणपत्र अलग खाते हैं: चिकित्सक लाइसेंस Medical Board of California चिकित्सा व्यवहार अधिनियम (Bus. & Prof. Code § 2000 आदि) के तहत देता है, अस्थि-चिकित्सक Osteopathic Medical Board of California और AOA बोर्ड मार्ग से जाते हैं, और ABIM का संक्रामक रोग प्रमाणपत्र लाइसेंस का विकल्प कभी नहीं है। दूसरी, कैलिफ़ोर्निया में अलग राज्य «संक्रामक रोग लाइसेंस» श्रेणी नहीं है, और बोर्ड प्रमाणन का विज्ञापन करने वाला § 651 के आसपास के सत्य-विज्ञापन नियमों से बँधा है — स्वैच्छिक विशेषज्ञता प्रमाणपत्र को राज्य का समर्थन या राज्य-जारी योग्यता नहीं बताया जा सकता। तीसरी, कैलिफ़ोर्निया के अस्पतालों पर राज्य कानून के तहत रोगाणुरोधी प्रबंधन का दायित्व है (Health & Saf. Code § 1288.85 आदि), और संक्रामक रोग चिकित्सक प्रायः उन कार्यक्रमों का नेतृत्व करते हैं तथा संक्रमण-रोकथाम, प्रत्यारोपण और कैंसर परामर्श पदों की भर्ती आवश्यकताओं में दिखते हैं। स्रोत: ABIM प्रमाणन सामग्री, Medical Board of California लाइसेंस मार्गदर्शन और ऊपर उद्धृत कैलिफ़ोर्निया कानून। टिप्पणी: व्यक्तिगत अध्ययन सामग्री, चिकित्सा सलाह नहीं; इस लेख में कोई दवा-खुराक या आवृत्ति नहीं दी गई है।
