# NPTE 备考计划与冲刺策略深挖：FSBPT 蓝图权重、题量节奏与加州衔接

这篇讲什么：
- 执照备考子轮转本轮回到 NPTE（国家物理治疗考试，FSBPT 主办）。本仓已有 NPTE 总篇、内容蓝图、报名资格、计分、复考与 PEAT 各篇，本篇不重复那些规则本身，专管一件事：把蓝图权重和题量换算成一份能执行的备考计划与考前冲刺排法。
- 计划里的时间分配是学习方法，不是官方要求；官方口径只有蓝图、题量、计分与资格规则，来源为 FSBPT 公开材料。加州报考资格与州执照局要求在第四节单独注明。
- 本篇不写任何药物剂量或治疗参数；病例口径只谈备考，不谈真实患者处置。

## 一、先定靶：复习时间按蓝图权重排，不平均分

### 中文

NPTE-PT 现行内容蓝图由 FSBPT 发布，2024 年 1 月起生效，依据是 FSBPT 的执业分析。计分题共 180 题，按九个身体系统给的是区间不是定额：肌肉骨骼系统 44–54 题最大，神经肌肉与神经系统 39–48 题第二，心血管与肺系统 22–27 题第三；其余依次是皮肤系统 8–11 题、系统交互 8–10 题、淋巴系统 4–7 题、代谢与内分泌系统 4–6 题、胃肠系统 3–6 题、泌尿生殖系统 2–5 题。系统之外还有非系统领域 21–29 题（器械设备、安全与防护、治疗性物理因子、职业责任、研究与循证实践等）。冲刺排法的第一原则就从这里来：肌肉骨骼与神经肌肉两家下限相加 83 题、上限相加 102 题，约占计分题一半，复习时间先压这两家，再压心血管与肺；小系统按区间上限保底覆盖，不整块放弃。每个系统内部还横切三类任务——检查、评估与鉴别诊断及预后基础、干预——所以每学一个系统，都要同时练怎么查、怎么判、怎么定干预方向，只背疾病知识拿不全这一块的分。

### English

The current NPTE-PT content outline is published by FSBPT, effective January 2024, and rests on FSBPT's practice analysis. The 180 scored items are distributed across nine body systems as ranges, not fixed counts: the musculoskeletal system is largest at 44–54 items, neuromuscular and nervous systems second at 39–48, and cardiovascular and pulmonary systems third at 22–27; the rest are integumentary 8–11, system interactions 8–10, lymphatic 4–7, metabolic and endocrine 4–6, gastrointestinal 3–6, and genitourinary 2–5. Beyond the systems, nonsystem areas total 21–29 items, covering equipment and devices, safety and protection, therapeutic modalities, professional responsibilities, and research and evidence-based practice. The first rule of a sprint plan follows directly: the top two systems sum to a floor of 83 and a ceiling of 102 items — roughly half the scored form — so study time goes there first, then to cardiovascular and pulmonary, while smaller systems are covered to their ceilings rather than skipped. Each system is also cut across three task types — examination, foundations for evaluation, differential diagnosis and prognosis, and interventions — so every system must be studied as how to examine, how to judge, and how to intervene, not as disease facts alone.

## 二、题量节奏账：225 题、5 小时，拆到每组 60 分钟

### 中文

正式考试的规格先钉死：NPTE-PT 共 225 题，其中 180 题计分、45 题为不计分的预测试题，混在卷中无法识别；分五组、每组 45 题，考试时间 5 小时，整个预约时长 5 小时 30 分钟，第二组之后有一次 15 分钟的预定休息。节奏账本地算两遍：5 小时是 300 分钟，300 除以 225 约等于每题 1.33 分钟；按组算是每组 60 分钟做 45 题。计分是量表分 200 到 800，全美统一以 600 为通过线，答错不倒扣，所以任何一题都不留空。冲刺期的节奏训练就照这个账走：平时练习按组计时，一组 45 题压进 60 分钟，做不完先标记跳过、回头再补，而不是在一道题上耗掉三道题的时间；情景题组围绕一个病例连问数题，先读问题再回情境里找信息，比通读情境再做题省时。最后一组的质量是耐力问题，不是知识问题——只有做过全程计时的整卷模拟，才知道自己第五组还剩多少判断力。

### English

Fix the real specifications first: the NPTE-PT has 225 items — 180 scored and 45 unscored pretest items that cannot be identified — in five sections of 45, with five hours of testing time, a five-hour-30-minute appointment, and one scheduled 15-minute break after the second section. The pacing arithmetic, checked twice: five hours is 300 minutes, and 300 divided by 225 is about 1.33 minutes per item; by section, that is 60 minutes for 45 items. Scoring is a scaled score from 200 to 800, with 600 as the uniform passing standard in every jurisdiction, and there is no penalty for a wrong answer, so no item should be left blank. Sprint-phase pacing drills follow this arithmetic: practice in timed sections of 45 items in 60 minutes, mark and skip rather than spending three items' time on one, and for scenario-based sets read the question before mining the case for information. Quality in the final section is an endurance issue, not a knowledge issue — only a full-length timed simulation shows how much judgment is left in section five.

## 三、最后四周排法：两套 PEAT 分开用，中间只补短板

### 中文

官方练习评估 PEAT 是 FSBPT 自己出售的练习工具，一次购买含两套完整计时练习卷，PT 版每套 225 题、5 小时，与正式考试同规格；当期价格 99 美元，访问期 90 天，从开始第一套卷才起算，光购买和下载材料不扣天数，开始第一套后不退款。冲刺排法建议这样摆：系统复习过一轮之后做第一套，用它的分域报告定位薄弱系统；中间两到三周只按报告补短板——薄弱且高权重的系统优先，薄弱但低权重的排后面；正式考试前一到两周做第二套，验补习效果并定考场节奏。两套挤在一周内背靠背做，第二套的估分会被熟悉度抬高，诊断价值大打折扣。最后一周不再开新题量：回看错题的解析与出处、把每组 60 分钟的节奏走顺、按考试日作息调整睡眠。还要留一条纪律：估分只是估计，临界段尤其不能把 PEAT 估分当通过保险，估分低也先看低在哪个系统、是知识缺口还是节奏崩盘，再决定要不要改期，不要当场推翻整个计划。

### English

The official PEAT is FSBPT's own practice tool: one purchase contains two full timed practice forms, each PT form carrying 225 items in five hours, matching the real examination. The current price is $99, with 90 days of access that starts only when the first form is begun — buying and downloading materials does not consume days — and the fee is non-refundable once the first form is started. A workable final-four-weeks layout: take the first form after one full pass of systematic review and use its domain report to locate weak systems; spend the middle two to three weeks only on those gaps, weak-and-heavily-weighted systems first; take the second form one to two weeks before the real examination to verify the repair work and lock in pacing. Taking both forms back-to-back within one week inflates the second estimate through familiarity and destroys most of its diagnostic value. In the final week, open no new volume: review rationales and references for missed items, smooth out the 60-minutes-per-section rhythm, and shift sleep to the examination-day schedule. One discipline throughout: an estimate is an estimate, especially near the passing line, and a low estimate calls first for locating whether the loss is a knowledge gap or a pacing collapse, not for tearing up the whole plan on the spot.

Una distribución viable para las últimas cuatro semanas: hacer el primer examen tras una pasada completa de repaso sistemático y usar su informe por áreas para localizar los sistemas débiles; dedicar las dos o tres semanas intermedias solo a esos huecos, primero los sistemas débiles y de mayor peso; hacer el segundo examen una o dos semanas antes del real para verificar la mejora y fijar el ritmo. Hacer ambos en la misma semana infla la segunda estimación por familiaridad y destruye su valor diagnóstico. En la última semana no se abre volumen nuevo: se repasan las explicaciones de los errores, se afina el ritmo de 60 minutos por sección y se ajusta el sueño al horario del examen. La estimación es solo una estimación, sobre todo cerca de la línea de aprobado. (Traducción inicial, pendiente de revisión.)

## 四、加州单独注明：报考资格与州执照局要求

### 中文

考试与发照是两层，务必分开。NPTE 是全国统一考试，由 FSBPT 管理、在 Prometric 考场考；真正发执照的是各州委员会，加州是加州物理治疗委员会（Physical Therapy Board of California, PTBC）。报考资格的全国层口径：考生须满足所报考法域的教育与资格要求，一般须完成物理治疗教育项目；FSBPT 的尝试上限为 12 个月内最多考 3 次、终身最多 6 次，两次成绩低于量表分 400 的考生不得再考（申诉通道另见本仓复考篇）。费用口径：FSBPT 考试注册费 PT 与 PTA 均为 485 美元，另加 1.6% 处理费，Prometric 考场费与州申请费另付，以报考当次官方页面为准。加州层单独注明：第一，教育要求在《加州商业与职业法典》第 2650 条一带，含临床实习要求，外国教育背景申请人另走第 2653 条一带的学历等值评估。第二，加州发照要过两门考试（第 2636 条一带）：NPTE 与加州法规评估模块 CAL-JAM——CAL-JAM 为在线模块，50 题、90 分钟、费用 50 美元，须在 96 小时内完成，通过线 80%，次数不限；它考加州物理治疗法律与规章，PEAT 与任何临床题库都替代不了。第三，申请走加州 BreEZe 系统向 PTBC 提交，应届毕业生可按 PTBC 流程在毕业前提前启动申请与考试安排（以 PTBC 当期流程图为准）。第四，冲刺排期时把 CAL-JAM 单独留出时间，不要挤占 NPTE 最后一周：两门考试内容零重叠，法规模块虽小，挂了同样发不了照。

### English

Examination and licensure are two separate layers. The NPTE is the national examination, administered by FSBPT and delivered at Prometric centers; the license itself is issued by each state board — in California, the Physical Therapy Board of California (PTBC). At the national level, candidates must meet the education and eligibility requirements of the jurisdiction through which they apply, generally completion of a physical therapy education program. FSBPT attempt limits are three attempts in a 12-month period and six in a lifetime, and candidates with two scores below a scaled score of 400 may not test again, subject to the appeals route covered in this repository's retake article. On fees, FSBPT's registration is $485 for both PT and PTA, plus a 1.6% processing fee, with Prometric and state application fees separate; confirm on the current registration page. California specifics, stated separately: first, education requirements sit around Business and Professions Code section 2650, including clinical education, and internationally educated applicants follow the equivalency evaluation route around section 2653. Second, California licensure requires two examinations (around section 2636): the NPTE and the California Law Assessment Module, CAL-JAM — an online module of 50 items in 90 minutes costing $50, to be completed within 96 hours, with an 80% passing standard and unlimited attempts; it tests California physical therapy law and regulations, and neither PEAT nor any clinical question bank substitutes for it. Third, applications go to PTBC through California's BreEZe system, and new graduates may start the application and examination sequence before graduation under PTBC's current process. Fourth, schedule CAL-JAM separately rather than inside the NPTE final week: the two examinations share no content, and failing the small law module blocks the license just the same.
