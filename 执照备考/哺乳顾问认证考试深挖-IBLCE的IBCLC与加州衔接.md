# 哺乳顾问认证考试深挖：IBLCE 的 IBCLC 与加州衔接

执照备考其他类顺延专篇。原定本轮的呼吸治疗题与临床实验室科学家题，经开工前实查均已在仓内成篇（《呼吸治疗执照考试深挖》《临床实验室科学家执照考试深挖》），按不重题规则顺延至本篇：哺乳顾问（泌乳顾问）是「其他」类里尚未单独成篇的一条完整认证线。这篇讲清谁发证、报考资格怎么攒、三条临床路径怎么分、考试怎么考、五年再认证怎么维持，以及落到加州时为什么没有州执照这一步。来源口径：认证管理机构 IBCLC Commission（考生材料现由该机构发布，旧文献多写作 IBLCE，即 International Board of Lactation Consultant Examiners）官网与当期《Candidate Information Guide》《Detailed Content Outline》《Recertification Guide》；关键数字核查日期为 2026-11-05，报考与再认证一律以官网当期文本为准。本篇不写任何药物剂量。

## 一、先定性：国际认证，不是州执照

### 中文
IBCLC 全称 International Board Certified Lactation Consultant，中文一般译作国际认证泌乳顾问。它是泌乳照护领域层级最高的临床凭据，但性质要先钉死：这是国际性行业认证，不是美国联邦的执照，也不是任何一个州的政府执照。发证体系只管三件事——定报考资格、办考试、管再认证；能不能在某个机构上岗，由雇主与岗位要求决定；能不能做诊断、开处方，由当事人另外持有的执照（如注册护士、医师、注册营养师执照）决定，IBCLC 本身不创造这些权限。IBCLC 的日常工作是临床层面的母乳喂养支持：评估含乳与喂养过程、做喂养计划、处理常见的疼痛、乳量与婴儿摄入问题，识别超出自己范围的情形并转诊给儿科、产科、乳腺外科或言语吞咽等团队。还要与培训更短的支持者凭据分清：例如 CLC 一类哺乳咨询员培训，定位是教育与基础支持，遇到复杂临床问题应转给 IBCLC 或医疗团队；两者不是同一层级的凭据，招聘与排班时不要混用。

### English
IBCLC stands for International Board Certified Lactation Consultant, the highest-level clinical credential in lactation care. Fix its nature first: it is an international professional certification, not a federal license and not a state government license anywhere in the United States. The certifying system does three things — it sets eligibility, runs the examination, and manages recertification. Whether a person may work in a given setting is decided by employers and job requirements; whether that person may diagnose or prescribe is decided by a separate license the person may also hold, such as a registered nurse, physician, or registered dietitian license. The IBCLC itself creates none of those powers. Day to day, an IBCLC provides clinical breastfeeding support: assessing latch and feeding, building feeding plans, addressing common pain, supply, and infant-intake problems, and recognizing what lies beyond scope and referring to pediatrics, obstetrics, breast surgery, or swallowing specialists. Keep shorter support credentials distinct: counselor-level training such as the CLC is aimed at education and basic support, with complex clinical problems referred onward to an IBCLC or the medical team. The two are not interchangeable in hiring or staffing.

### Español (初译待校)
IBCLC son las siglas de International Board Certified Lactation Consultant, la credencial clínica de mayor nivel en la atención de la lactancia. Fije primero su naturaleza: es una certificación profesional internacional, no una licencia federal ni una licencia estatal en Estados Unidos. El sistema certificador hace tres cosas: fija la elegibilidad, administra el examen y gestiona la recertificación. Si una persona puede trabajar en un entorno concreto lo deciden los empleadores y los requisitos del puesto; si puede diagnosticar o recetar lo decide otra licencia que la persona posea, como la de enfermería, medicina o dietética registrada. El IBCLC por sí solo no crea esas facultades. Su trabajo diario es el apoyo clínico a la lactancia: evaluar el agarre y la toma, construir planes de alimentación, atender problemas frecuentes de dolor, producción e ingesta del bebé, y reconocer lo que excede su ámbito para derivar a pediatría, obstetricia, cirugía mamaria o especialistas en deglución. Distíngalo de las credenciales de apoyo más breves: la formación de consejero, como el CLC, apunta a la educación y el apoyo básico, y los problemas clínicos complejos se derivan al IBCLC o al equipo médico. No son credenciales intercambiables al contratar o programar turnos.

## 二、报考资格：三块拼图加一条守则

### 中文
报考资格由三块拼图加一条守则组成，缺一块都进不了考场。第一块是健康科学背景：本人已是发证体系认可的医疗专业人员（如注册护士、医师、助产士、注册营养师等），视为已打好底子；不是这类背景的人，须按《Health Sciences Education Guide》完成十四门健康科学科目，科目清单与学分形态以当期指南为准。第二块是泌乳专项教育：合计九十五小时，其中九十小时为泌乳专项教育（自 2025 年考试申请起，其中两小时须专门讲授世卫组织《国际母乳代用品销售守则》，即 WHO Code）、另五小时为沟通技能教育，且全部须在提交考试申请之前五年内完成，过期要重修。第三块是泌乳专项临床经验，按下一节的三条路径之一攒满。守则是签署遵守《Code of Professional Conduct for IBCLCs》（IBCLC 职业行为守则），作弊、材料造假与重大违规可被拒绝发证或撤销认证。本节数字核查日期为 2026-11-05，一律以 IBCLC Commission 官网与当期《Candidate Information Guide》为准。

### English
Eligibility is three puzzle pieces plus one code, and every piece is required. First, a health-sciences background: candidates who are already recognised health professionals, such as registered nurses, physicians, midwives, or registered dietitians, are treated as having the foundation; everyone else must complete the fourteen health-science subjects set out in the Health Sciences Education Guide, with the subject list and course format following the current guide. Second, lactation-specific education totalling ninety-five hours: ninety hours of lactation-specific education — with, for examination applications from 2025 onward, two of those hours focused on the WHO International Code of Marketing of Breast-milk Substitutes — plus five hours of communication-skills education, all completed within the five years immediately before the examination application; older hours expire and must be refreshed. Third, lactation-specific clinical experience, earned through one of the three pathways in the next section. The code is attestation to the Code of Professional Conduct for IBCLCs; cheating, falsified documents, and serious misconduct can lead to denial or revocation. Figures in this section were checked on 2026-11-05 and follow the IBCLC Commission website and the current Candidate Information Guide.

응시 자격은 세 조각의 퍼즐에 행동 강령 하나를 더한 것으로, 하나라도 빠지면 시험장에 들어갈 수 없습니다. 첫째는 보건과학 배경입니다. 등록간호사, 의사, 조산사, 등록영양사 등 인증 체계가 인정하는 의료 전문가인 사람은 이미 기초를 갖춘 것으로 봅니다. 그런 배경이 없는 사람은 《Health Sciences Education Guide》가 정한 열네 개 보건과학 과목을 마쳐야 하며, 과목 목록과 이수 형태는 현행 안내서를 따릅니다. 둘째는 수유 전문 교육으로 합계 아흔다섯 시간입니다. 그중 아흔 시간은 수유 전문 교육이고, 2025년 시험 신청부터는 그중 두 시간이 세계보건기구의 《모유 대체품 판매에 관한 국제 강령》, 즉 WHO Code에 집중해야 하며, 여기에 의사소통 기술 교육 다섯 시간이 더해집니다. 모두 시험 신청 직전 오 년 안에 마쳐야 하고, 기간이 지난 시간은 다시 채워야 합니다. 셋째는 수유 전문 임상 경험으로, 다음 절의 세 경로 중 하나로 채웁니다. 강령은 《Code of Professional Conduct for IBCLCs》를 지키겠다는 서약이며, 부정행위, 서류 위조, 중대한 위반이 있으면 발급 거부나 자격 취소가 될 수 있습니다. 이 절의 숫자는 2026-11-05에 확인했고, IBCLC Commission 웹사이트와 현행 《Candidate Information Guide》를 기준으로 합니다.

## 三、三条路径：临床小时从哪来

### 中文
三块拼图里最花时间的是临床经验，发证体系为不同起点的人留了三条路径，小时数与督导强度互为交换。路径一面向已在岗位上的人：认可的医疗专业人员、或经认可的母乳喂养支持组织里的咨询者，在申请前五年内完成一千小时泌乳专项临床实践；这条路不要求直接督导，靠的是本人已有专业身份与岗位场景兜底。路径二面向在校生：进入经认证的泌乳学术项目（由 CAAHEP 或同等标准的认证体系把关），在项目结构内完成三百小时直接督导下的临床实践，教育与临床打包完成。路径三是导师制，面向既无医疗背景、又不在学术项目里的人：先把与现行 IBCLC 导师的督导计划提交核准，再在直接督导下完成五百小时临床实践——顺序不能倒，计划核准之前自行攒的小时数可能不被承认。三条路径最后汇到同一场考试、同一个通过标准，没有哪条路的证书更「正宗」；选路的要点是先认清自己的起点身份，再倒查小时数能不能在五年窗口内攒齐。

### English
Clinical experience is the slowest piece to earn, and the system offers three pathways for different starting points, trading total hours against intensity of supervision. Pathway 1 is for people already in role: recognised health professionals, or counsellors within a recognised breastfeeding support organisation, complete one thousand hours of lactation-specific clinical practice within the five years before application; this route requires no direct supervision, because the candidate's professional standing and practice setting carry the weight. Pathway 2 is for students: enter an accredited lactation academic programme, accredited through CAAHEP or a body with equivalent standards, and complete three hundred hours of directly supervised clinical practice inside the programme structure, with education and clinical work packaged together. Pathway 3 is mentorship, for candidates with neither a health-professional background nor an academic programme: submit a mentorship plan with a currently certified IBCLC for verification first, then complete five hundred hours of directly supervised practice — the order cannot be reversed, and hours gathered before the plan is verified may not count. All three pathways converge on the same examination and the same passing standard; no pathway's credential outranks another. Choose by identifying your true starting status, then work backwards to check whether the hours fit inside the five-year window.

## 四、考试结构与定线：175 题、两部分、图像题

### 中文

### English

Once Part One is completed and submitted, you cannot return to it, so pace Part One with checking time in reserve. Most Part Two questions are associated with an image, testing whether you can read clinical meaning out of pictures — breast and latch states, infant states, and feeding scenes. Content follows the current Detailed Content Outline in seven domains: development and nutrition; physiology and endocrinology; pathology; pharmacology and toxicology; psychology, sociology and anthropology; techniques; and clinical skills, cross-cut by the chronological periods of infancy. The passing standard is criterion-referenced, set against a predetermined competence standard, and no fixed percentage pass mark is published — treat percentage figures circulating online as unofficial. Examination windows run each year; application deadlines, window dates, and fees follow the current IBCLC Commission announcements, and technical details such as British English as the translation foundation follow the current Candidate Information Guide.

## 五、维持：五年再认证与两本账的提醒

## 六、加州衔接与相邻岗位边界（单独注明）

### 中文
