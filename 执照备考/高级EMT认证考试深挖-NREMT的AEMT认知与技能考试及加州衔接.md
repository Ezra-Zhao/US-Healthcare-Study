## 一、先定性：AEMT 卡在 EMT 与 Paramedic 之间

### 中文
美国院前急救人员的主线分四档：EMR、EMT、AEMT、Paramedic。AEMT（Advanced Emergency Medical Technician，高级急救医疗技师）是第三档，定位是有限的高级生命支持（limited advanced life support）：在 EMT 的基础生命支持之上，多出建立静脉通路、部分高级气道与有限药物类别这几样侵入性本事；但气管插管、心电判读主导的复苏决策、完整的药物箱，仍是 Paramedic 的地盘。先把两本账分清：NREMT（National Registry of Emergency Medical Technicians，国家急救医疗技师注册机构）管的是全国认证考试与认证本身，认证不等于执业许可——能不能上车出勤，由所在州（加州是县级地方 EMS 机构）发证与授权。来源：NREMT 官方 AEMT 考生手册与国家 EMS 执业范围模型（National EMS Scope of Practice Model，NHTSA）的公开口径。

### English
The main prehospital ladder in the United States has four rungs: EMR, EMT, AEMT, and Paramedic. The AEMT (Advanced Emergency Medical Technician) is the third rung, positioned as limited advanced life support: on top of the EMT's basic life support it adds invasive skills such as establishing intravenous access, some advanced airway work, and a limited set of medication categories, while endotracheal intubation, ECG-led resuscitation decisions, and the full medication box remain Paramedic territory. Keep two ledgers separate: the NREMT (National Registry of Emergency Medical Technicians) runs the national certification examination and the certification itself, but certification is not a practice license — the right to work on an ambulance is granted and authorized by the state (in California, by county-level local EMS agencies). Sources: the official NREMT AEMT Candidate Handbook and the National EMS Scope of Practice Model (NHTSA).

## 二、报考资格：手册明列三件

### 中文
按 NREMT 现行 AEMT 考生手册，申请认证要同时满足三件，逐项数得 3。其一，手里有一张现行有效的 EMT 级或以上凭据——NREMT 的 EMT 认证，或州发的 EMT 执照／证书，二者居其一。其二，完成一门州批准的 AEMT 课程，课程须达到或超过《国家 EMS 教育标准》（National EMS Education Standards）的 AEMT 口径，且结课时间在申请前两年以内，结课事实由课程负责人（Program Director）在 NREMT 网站上核验确认。其三，通过 NREMT 的认证考试，考试成绩自通过起 24 个月内有效。手册另有一条时间戳：自 2024 年 7 月 1 日起，考生还须满足推荐版 AEMT 学生最低胜任力矩阵（Recommended AEMT Student Minimum Competency Matrix，SMC）的要求——技能与病人接触的底线改在课程内攒齐，第四节细说。年龄一格手册未单列统一数字，多数州与课程以成年为门槛，报名时以当期手册与报考州的当期口径逐项对，不要凭旧帖下单。

### English
Under the current NREMT AEMT Candidate Handbook, applicants must meet three requirements at once, counted one by one. First, hold a current credential at the EMT level or higher — either NREMT EMT certification or a state-issued EMT license or certificate. Second, complete a state-approved AEMT course that meets or exceeds the AEMT level of the National EMS Education Standards, finished within the two years before applying, with completion verified by the Program Director on the NREMT website. Third, pass the NREMT certification examination, whose result stays valid for 24 months. The handbook adds a dated rule: from July 1, 2024, candidates must also meet the Recommended AEMT Student Minimum Competency Matrix (SMC), so the floor for skills and patient contacts is now built up inside the course, as Section 4 explains. The handbook sets no single national age figure; most states and programs use adulthood as the threshold, so check the current handbook and the testing state's current rules item by item rather than relying on old posts.

## 三、认知考试：一张卷，135 题，3 小时

### 中文
现行 AEMT 认证考试在 Pearson 授权考场上机完成，以多选题为主，另有多选题型变体（multiple-response）与技术增强题（technology-enhanced items），部分题目挂在情景题干下。题量算例当场对清：全卷 135 题，其中 35 题是不计分的预试题（pilot），135 减 35 等于 100 题真正计分；作答时间 3 小时，即 180 分钟，180 除以 135 约等于 1.33 分钟一题——这是全卷均速的示例账，不是每题限时，本地复算两遍一致。判分不看原始分：成绩落在 100 至 1500 的量表分区间，通过线为 950；没过的人能看到自己离线多远，过了的人不发分项细报。流程数字也要记牢：申请、缴费与课程负责人核验三件齐了才发 ATT（Authorization to Test，准考授权），ATT 自发放起 90 天内有效，过期要重新缴费申请；经 Pearson 改期或取消收 30 美元，未能提前至少一个工作日（24 小时）通知的按缺考处理、费用没收；成绩一般在考后 3 个工作日内贴到考生账户。重考口径：结果公布后即可再申请，但距上次考试至少等 15 天；总机会 6 次，连败 3 次后，第 4 次申请前须先完成补修教育（可用 AEMT 25 学分的 NCCP 国家胜任力部分，或州／CAPCE 批准的 AEMT 复训课抵）。费用一格：当期公开口径每次考试申请费 159 美元，两次考试的示例账为 159 乘以 2 等于 318 美元，课程学费与州证费另算、不并入，缴费前以 NREMT 官网当期页面复核。

### English
The current AEMT certification examination is taken on a computer at an authorized Pearson testing center. It is mainly multiple-choice, with multiple-response and technology-enhanced items, some of them attached to scenarios. The item count checks out plainly: 135 questions in total, of which 35 are unscored pilot items, so 135 minus 35 equals 100 scored questions; the time limit is 3 hours, that is 180 minutes, and 180 divided by 135 is about 1.33 minutes per question — an average-pace illustration, not a per-item limit (recomputed twice, consistent). Scoring is scaled, not raw: scores fall on a 100-to-1500 scale and the passing point is 950; candidates who fail can see how far they were from the line, while those who pass receive no detailed breakdown. Process numbers matter too: the ATT (Authorization to Test) is issued only when the application, the payment, and the Program Director's verification are all complete; an ATT is valid for 90 days, and an expired one means a new application and fee; rescheduling or cancelling through Pearson costs 30 dollars, and failing to notify at least one business day (24 hours) ahead counts as a no-show with the fee forfeited; results generally post to the candidate account within three business days. On retesting: a candidate may reapply once results post but must wait at least 15 days from the last attempt; there are six chances in total, and after three failures remedial education is required before the fourth attempt (the 25-credit AEMT National Competency Component of the NCCP model, or a state- or CAPCE-approved AEMT refresher). On fees, the current published figure is 159 dollars per examination application, so two attempts illustrate to 159 times 2 equals 318 dollars; course tuition and state fees are separate, and the NREMT site should be rechecked before paying.

Predominan las preguntas de opción múltiple, con variantes de respuesta múltiple y preguntas con tecnología mejorada, algunas vinculadas a escenarios. La cuenta de preguntas es clara: 135 en total, de las cuales 35 son preguntas piloto no puntuables, así que 135 menos 35 igual a 100 preguntas puntuables; el tiempo es de 3 horas, es decir, 180 minutos, y 180 entre 135 da unas 1,33 minutos por pregunta, solo un promedio ilustrativo y no un límite por pregunta (recalculado dos veces, consistente). La puntuación es escalada: los resultados caen en una escala de 100 a 1500 y el punto de aprobación es 950; quien no aprueba puede ver a qué distancia quedó, y quien aprueba no recibe desglose detallado. También importan los números del proceso: la ATT (Authorization to Test) solo se emite cuando están completos la solicitud, el pago y la verificación del director del programa; la ATT vale 90 días y, si vence, hay que solicitar y pagar de nuevo; reprogramar o cancelar vía Pearson cuesta 30 dólares, y no avisar con al menos un día laborable (24 horas) de antelación cuenta como ausencia con pérdida de la tasa; los resultados suelen publicarse en la cuenta del candidato en tres días laborables. Para repetir: se puede volver a solicitar cuando salga el resultado, pero hay que esperar al menos 15 días desde el último intento; hay seis oportunidades en total, y tras tres suspensos se exige formación de recuperación antes del cuarto intento (el componente nacional de competencia AEMT de 25 créditos del modelo NCCP, o un curso de actualización AEMT aprobado por el estado o por CAPCE). En tasas, la cifra publicada vigente es de 159 dólares por solicitud de examen, de modo que dos intentos ilustran 159 por 2 igual a 318 dólares; la matrícula del curso y las tasas estatales van aparte, y conviene reverificar en el sitio del NREMT antes de pagar. (初译待校)

## 四、技能这一关改了：独立技能考退出，胜任力回课程里验

### 中文
老资料里 AEMT 有一场单独的心理运动考试（psychomotor examination，站点式技能考），这条已经作废：自 2024 年 7 月 1 日起，NREMT 对 AEMT 与 Paramedic 不再设独立的国家级技能考，技能胜任力改由州批准课程按 SMC 矩阵在教学过程中逐项验证——静脉通路、高级气道、情景处置这些本事，要在课程的技能签核与病人接触记录里留下证据，而不是在某一天去站点闯关。还拿着 2024 年以前备考帖按站点清单练的人，练的方向就偏了。边界有两条要钉清：其一，NREMT 取消的是它自家的独立技能考，州在发本州证书时另设什么技能核验，仍归州说了算，报考前查州 EMS 办公室当期口径；其二，EMT 级的考试安排与 AEMT 不同轨，不要把 EMT 的技能考口径套到 AEMT 头上。

### English
Older materials describe a separate psychomotor examination for the AEMT — a station-based skills test — and that route is gone: from July 1, 2024, the NREMT no longer runs a stand-alone national skills examination for AEMT and Paramedic candidates. Skills competency is instead verified inside the state-approved program against the SMC matrix, so intravenous access, advanced airway work, and scenario management must leave evidence in course skills sign-offs and patient-contact records rather than at a one-day testing site. Anyone still drilling station checklists from pre-2024 posts is preparing in the wrong direction. Two boundaries need pinning: first, what the NREMT retired is its own separate skills exam; whatever skills verification a state adds for its own certificate is still the state's call, so check the state EMS office's current rules before applying. Second, the EMT-level examination arrangements run on a different track — do not project EMT skills-exam rules onto the AEMT.

## 五、执业范围要点：只记类别，不背剂量

### 中文
国家执业范围模型给 AEMT 的增量，大体是四组类别。第一组是通路：外周静脉通路与骨内通路（成人与儿童），以及静脉输注不含药的液体类别。第二组是气道：在 EMT 气道本事之上加喉上气道装置（supraglottic airway）这一级，并能对已插管病人做气管支气管吸引、用持续气道正压通气装置。第三组是有限药物类别：升糖处理类、过敏急救类、阿片过量逆转类、吸入性呼吸急救类、镇痛气体类等——本篇只写类别，一个具体剂量与频次都不写，现场给什么、给多少，永远以本地医疗控制协议为准。第四组是评估增量：更完整的病人评估与临床判断，情景题在考试里占的分量正出自这一组。四组之外要泼一盆冷水：国家模型只是模型，不经州采纳就没有法律效力；同一个 AEMT，换一个州、换一个医疗主任，能做的事可以差出一截，出勤前认本地协议，不认全国口号。

### English
What the national scope-of-practice model adds at the AEMT level falls into four groups. The first is access: peripheral intravenous access and intraosseous access (adult and pediatric), plus intravenous infusion of non-medicated fluids. The second is airway: on top of EMT airway skills, the supraglottic airway tier, suctioning of an already-intubated patient, and continuous positive airway pressure devices. The third is a limited set of medication categories: glucose-raising treatment, allergy emergency treatment, opioid-overdose reversal, inhaled respiratory emergency treatment, and analgesic gas categories — this article names categories only and gives no specific dose or frequency; what is given in the field, and how much, is always set by local medical-control protocols. The fourth is assessment: fuller patient assessment and clinical judgment, which is exactly where the examination's scenario items draw their weight. Beyond the four groups, a cold reminder: the national model is only a model and has no legal force until a state adopts it; the same AEMT can face a noticeably different scope in another state or under another medical director, so work to the local protocol, not the national slogan.

## 六、加州单独注明：可选层级，认证在 LEMSA

### 中文
加州一侧，AEMT 不是全州统一铺开的必设层级，而是可选层级：法源在《加州法规法典》第 22 篇第 9 分部第 3.2 章（Title 22, Division 9, Chapter 3.2），该章把 Advanced EMT 定义为在 EMT 之上受过有限高级生命支持（LALS）训练的人；是否在本辖区开设 LALS／AEMT 项目，由地方 EMS 机构（LEMSA）决定——条文用词是地方机构「可以」批准此类项目，不是「必须」。认证主体也在地方：批准与认证落在 LEMSA 及其医疗主任一侧，这与 Paramedic 由州 EMSA 发全州执照的路径不同，跨县出勤前先核目的地县认不认这一层、要不要另办地方手续。执业范围的成文口径在同章第 100076.02 条：除 EMT 的全部范围外，可按 LEMSA 批准的政策与程序做喉上气道通气、对已插管病人吸引、建外周静脉通路与盐水锁、经静脉给予条文列明的有限类别（如葡萄糖溶液类）等；另有第 100076.03 条的地方可选范围，但那一格只留给 1994 年 1 月 1 日前已存在的 EMT-II 项目的存量人员，新入行者不要往那里对号入座。现实层面写保守一句：加州多数县的 911 主力配置仍以 EMT 与 Paramedic 两档为主，设 AEMT 岗位的县是少数，选课前先看目标县到底有没有这一层的岗位与认证通道，再交学费。来源：加州 EMSA 公布的第 22 篇第 9 分部第 3.2 章条文与 LEMSA 公开政策口径；条号与费用报名前以 EMSA 与目标县 LEMSA 当期页面复核。

### English

Its legal home is Title 22, Division 9, Chapter 3.2 of the California Code of Regulations, which defines the Advanced EMT as an EMT with additional training in limited advanced life support (LALS). Whether an LALS/AEMT program exists in a jurisdiction is decided by the local EMS agency (LEMSA) — the regulation says a LEMSA may approve such a program, not that it must. Approval and certification also sit locally, with the LEMSA and its medical director, unlike the Paramedic path where the state EMSA issues a statewide license; before working across county lines, check whether the destination county recognizes this tier and whether it requires separate local steps. The written scope is in section 100076.02 of the same chapter: beyond the full EMT scope, and under LEMSA-approved policies, an Advanced EMT may perform supraglottic airway ventilation, suction an intubated patient, establish peripheral intravenous lines and saline locks, and give intravenously the limited categories the section lists, such as glucose solutions. A conservative practical note: in most California counties the main 911 staffing still runs on the EMT and Paramedic tiers, and counties with AEMT positions are the minority, so before paying tuition, confirm that the target county actually has positions and a certification channel at this tier. Sources: the Chapter 3.2 text published by the California EMSA and public LEMSA policy materials; recheck section numbers and fees on current EMSA and county LEMSA pages before applying.

Phạm vi tùy chọn địa phương tại mục 100076.03 là một ô riêng, dành cho nhân lực kế thừa từ các chương trình EMT-II tồn tại trước ngày 1 tháng 1 năm 1994 — người mới vào nghề không nên tự đọc mình vào ô đó. Một ghi chú thực tế thận trọng: ở đa số quận của California, biên chế chủ lực của 911 vẫn chạy trên hai bậc EMT và Paramedic, và các quận có vị trí AEMT là thiểu số, nên trước khi đóng học phí hãy xác nhận quận mục tiêu thực sự có vị trí và kênh chứng nhận ở bậc này. Nguồn: văn bản Chapter 3.2 do EMSA California công bố và tài liệu chính sách công khai của các LEMSA; hãy kiểm tra lại số mục và lệ phí trên trang hiện hành của EMSA và LEMSA quận trước khi nộp đơn. (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)
California, अलग से: AEMT राज्य भर में समान रूप से लागू अनिवार्य स्तर नहीं, बल्कि वैकल्पिक स्तर है। इसका कानूनी आधार California Code of Regulations के Title 22, Division 9, Chapter 3.2 में है, जो Advanced EMT को EMT के ऊपर सीमित उन्नत जीवन-समर्थन (LALS) का अतिरिक्त प्रशिक्षण पाए व्यक्ति के रूप में परिभाषित करता है। किसी क्षेत्र में LALS/AEMT कार्यक्रम होगा या नहीं, यह स्थानीय EMS एजेंसी (LEMSA) तय करती है — नियम कहता है कि LEMSA ऐसा कार्यक्रम अनुमोदित कर सकती है, यह नहीं कि उसे करना ही होगा। अनुमोदन और प्रमाणन भी स्थानीय स्तर पर, LEMSA और उसके चिकित्सा निदेशक के पास रहते हैं, जो Paramedic मार्ग से भिन्न है जहाँ राज्य EMSA राज्यव्यापी लाइसेंस देती है; काउंटी सीमा पार काम करने से पहले जाँचें कि गंतव्य काउंटी इस स्तर को मानती है या नहीं और वहाँ अलग स्थानीय चरण चाहिए या नहीं। लिखित क्षेत्र उसी अध्याय की धारा 100076.02 में है: पूर्ण EMT क्षेत्र के अतिरिक्त, LEMSA-अनुमोदित नीतियों के अंतर्गत Advanced EMT supraglottic airway से वेंटिलेशन, अंतर्निवेशित रोगी का चूषण, परिधीय अंतःशिरा लाइनें और saline lock स्थापित करना, तथा धारा में सूचीबद्ध सीमित श्रेणियों, जैसे glucose विलयनों, का अंतःशिरा प्रशासन कर सकता है। धारा 100076.03 का स्थानीय वैकल्पिक क्षेत्र अलग खाना है, जो 1 जनवरी 1994 से पहले मौजूद EMT-II कार्यक्रमों के पुराने कर्मियों के लिए आरक्षित है — नए प्रवेशकों को स्वयं को उसमें नहीं पढ़ना चाहिए। एक सतर्क व्यावहारिक टिप्पणी: California की अधिकांश काउंटियों में 911 की मुख्य तैनाती अब भी EMT और Paramedic स्तरों पर चलती है, और AEMT पदों वाली काउंटियाँ अल्पसंख्या में हैं, इसलिए शुल्क देने से पहले पुष्टि करें कि लक्ष्य काउंटी में इस स्तर पर पद और प्रमाणन मार्ग वास्तव में मौजूद हैं। स्रोत: California EMSA द्वारा प्रकाशित Chapter 3.2 का पाठ और LEMSA की सार्वजनिक नीति सामग्री; आवेदन से पहले धारा संख्याएँ और शुल्क EMSA तथा संबंधित काउंटी LEMSA के वर्तमान पृष्ठों पर फिर जाँचें। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)
