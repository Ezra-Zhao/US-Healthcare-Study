# Pre-NAPLEX 官方预考深挖：NABP 退休真题、量表分与加州衔接

## 一、先定性：预考是体检表，不是入场券

### 中文
Pre-NAPLEX 是各州药房委员会全国协会（National Association of Boards of Pharmacy，NABP）官方出品的 NAPLEX 练习考试，是市面上唯一使用已退休真题的官方预考：题目直接取自以往正式考试用过的题库，题型与界面都照正式考的样子做。它在发照链条里的位置要先钉死：它只是备考工具，不属于报考资格审查，不计入任何发照条件，考得好坏不报送任何州药房委员会，也不改变考生参加正式 NAPLEX 的资格。把它当一次带分数的体检最合适——量的是现在离正式考的通过线还有多远，不是提前发一张通行证。第三方题库可以练手感，但题目来源与难度标定都不是官方口径，读分时不能与官方预考同等看待。

### English
The Pre-NAPLEX is the official NAPLEX practice examination produced by the National Association of Boards of Pharmacy (NABP), and it is the only official practice test built from retired real examination questions: its items come directly from previously administered examinations, in the formats and interface candidates will meet on test day. Its place in the licensure chain must be fixed first: it is a study tool only. It forms no part of eligibility review, counts toward no licensure requirement, is not reported to any state board of pharmacy, and neither improves nor harms a candidate's eligibility for the real NAPLEX. Treat it as a scored check-up — it measures how far current performance sits from the real passing standard; it issues no early pass. Third-party question banks can build stamina, but their items and difficulty calibration are not official, so their scores cannot be read on the same scale as the official practice examination.

## 二、规格账：100 题、140 分钟、90 美元、一年至多两次

### English

The practice examination has 100 questions with a 140-minute limit, which works out to 140 divided by 100, that is 1.4 minutes per question. The real NAPLEX has 225 questions in six hours, that is 360 minutes, or 360 divided by 225, equal to 1.6 minutes per question — the practice test is actually tighter per item, so pacing trained on it does not leave a candidate short. The fee is $90 per attempt, with at most two attempts per year; using both costs 90 times 2, equal to $180, recomputed locally twice with the same result. There is exactly one purchase channel: sign in to the NABP e-Profile account and buy through the Practice Exams tile. After payment, the candidate has seven days to complete that attempt, and an unused attempt does not roll over. The practice test is taken online without test-center proctoring, unlike the real examination at a Pearson VUE center; it trains items and pacing, not test-day procedure.

## 三、分数怎么读：量表分立刻出，但它不是正式考的通过与否

### 中文
预考交卷后立刻出分，形式是总分量表分（scaled score），并经电子邮件送一份可打印下载的成绩报告。读法有三条边界。第一，正式 NAPLEX 现在只报通过与否、不给量表分，预考给量表分是为了让考生定位，不代表正式考也会给分，更不能把某个预考分数当成官方公布的通过线——NABP 没有公布预考量表分与通过的一一对应线。第二，量表分的用途是纵向比较与找短板：与自己的上一次比、看内容大纲哪一域拖后腿，再把剩余复习时间压到弱项上。第三，单次分数有波动，一次考得好不等于稳过、一次考砸也不等于没戏；两次机会的正确用法是一早一晚——备考中段用一次摸底、正式考前用第二次验收，而不是连着两天把两次都烧掉。

### English
The practice examination is scored as soon as it is submitted, and the result is a total scaled score, with a score report also sent by email for printing or download. Three boundaries govern how to read it. First, the real NAPLEX now reports pass or fail only and gives no scaled score; the practice scaled score exists to locate the candidate, and no particular practice score may be treated as a published passing line — NABP publishes no one-to-one mapping from a practice scaled score to passing. Second, the score's proper uses are longitudinal and diagnostic: compare it with one's own earlier attempt, see which content-outline domain is lagging, and push the remaining study time into the weak areas. Third, a single score fluctuates; one good attempt does not guarantee passing and one poor attempt does not settle failure. The two annual attempts are best spent one early and one late — one midway through preparation as a baseline, the second shortly before the real examination as a final check — rather than burning both on consecutive days.

Tres límites rigen su lectura. Primero, el NAPLEX real ahora solo comunica aprobado o suspenso y no da puntuación en escala; la de la práctica existe para ubicar al candidato, y ninguna puntuación concreta de práctica puede tratarse como línea oficial de aprobado, porque la NABP no publica correspondencia alguna entre esa puntuación y el aprobado. Segundo, sus usos correctos son longitudinales y diagnósticos: compararla con el intento anterior propio, ver qué dominio del esquema de contenidos va rezagado y empujar el tiempo restante hacia los puntos débiles. Tercero, una sola puntuación fluctúa; un buen intento no garantiza aprobar y uno malo no sentencia el suspenso. Los dos intentos anuales rinden más uno temprano y uno tardío —uno a mitad de la preparación como base y el segundo poco antes del examen real como verificación final— en lugar de quemar ambos en días consecutivos. ## 五、使用顺序：先本体、再预考、后正式考

### 中文
把预考放进整条备考链，顺序是四步。第一步，先按现行内容大纲把本体知识过完，预考不是用来代替学习的，什么都没学就先烧一次机会，量出来的只是没学过这一件事。第二步，备考中段做第一次预考：全程计时、不暂停、不查资料，尽量还原正式考的作答纪律，出分后按内容域列出弱项清单。第三步，针对弱项回炉复习，再用正式考的剩余时间倒排：预考每题 1.4 分钟比正式考的 1.6 分钟紧，配速以预考口径练、到正式考自然有余量。第四步，正式考前做第二次预考作验收，重点看量表分相对第一次的移动与弱项是否收拢；两次分数与复习动作都记下来，复考或跨州再战时这份记录就是起点。纪律一条：预考成绩只进自己的备考档案，不进任何申请材料，也没有任何机构会向考生索取。

### English
Placed in the whole preparation chain, the practice examination follows four steps. First, work through the body of knowledge under the current content outline; the practice test is no substitute for learning, and burning an attempt before studying anything measures only that nothing has been studied. Second, take the first attempt midway through preparation: fully timed, no pausing, no looking anything up, reproducing test-day discipline as closely as possible, then list the weak domains from the result. Third, return to study against those weak areas and plan backward from the real examination date: at 1.4 minutes per item the practice test is tighter than the real 1.6, so pacing trained to the practice standard leaves margin on the real day. Fourth, take the second attempt shortly before the real examination as the final check, watching how the scaled score moved against the first attempt and whether the weak domains closed. Keep both scores and the study actions taken; if a retake or another state ever becomes necessary, that record is the starting point. One rule: a practice score belongs only in one's own preparation file — it goes into no application, and no body will ever ask a candidate for it.
