## 一、先定性：便利调整进入方式，不动胜任标准

### 中文
NREMT 的便利规矩写在其政策第 62.02 号《考试便利》（Accommodations for Exams）里，根基是《美国残疾人法》（Americans with Disabilities Act, ADA）：注册局以不歧视的方式举办认证考试，对有文件证明的残疾考生，在书面（认知）与实操两部分都提供合理且适当的便利。定性先钉住：便利调整的是进入考试的方式，不是考试要测的东西——政策审查准绳第 3 条写明，所求便利必须合理、与文件证明的残疾对应，且不得从根本上改变考试评估院前急救基本职能的能力；通过标准不因便利而动。范围要看清：政策适用于各级认证考试的认知考试，以及 AEMT 与 Paramedic 两级的心理运动／表现考试（psychomotor/performance examinations）；问卷表上可申请的级别逐项数得 4 级——EMR、EMT、AEMT、Paramedic。EMT 这一级的技能验证在许多州由州批准的项目与州流程把关（见本仓 EMT 报名篇），所以 EMT 考生实际打交道最多的，是认知考试这一侧的便利。

### English
The NREMT's accommodation rules live in its Policy 62.02, Accommodations for Exams, grounded in the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA): the Registry administers its certification examinations without discriminating against otherwise qualified candidates with disabilities and offers reasonable and appropriate accommodations for both the written and the practical components to persons with documented disabilities. The framing matters: an accommodation changes how a candidate accesses the examination, not what the examination measures — guideline 3 of the policy requires that a requested accommodation be reasonable and appropriate for the documented disability and not fundamentally alter the examination's ability to assess the essential functions of prehospital care, and the passing standard does not move. The scope is precise: the policy covers the cognitive examinations at all certification levels and the psychomotor/performance examinations for AEMT and Paramedic; the questionnaire lists four levels — EMR, EMT, AEMT, and Paramedic. Because EMT-level skills verification in many states runs through state-approved programs and state processes (see this repository's EMT registration article), the accommodation most EMT candidates actually use sits on the cognitive side.

### Español (译稿 · 初译待校)
Las reglas de adaptaciones del NREMT están en su Política 62.02, Accommodations for Exams, con base en la Ley de Estadounidenses con Discapacidades (ADA): el Registro administra sus exámenes de certificación sin discriminar a los candidatos con discapacidad que reúnen los requisitos y ofrece adaptaciones razonables y apropiadas, tanto en el componente escrito como en el práctico, a las personas con discapacidades documentadas. La idea central: la adaptación cambia cómo se accede al examen, no lo que el examen mide — la directriz 3 de la política exige que la adaptación solicitada sea razonable y apropiada para la discapacidad documentada y que no altere fundamentalmente la capacidad del examen para evaluar las funciones esenciales de la atención prehospitalaria, y el estándar de aprobación no se mueve. El alcance es preciso: la política cubre los exámenes cognitivos de todos los niveles de certificación y los exámenes psicomotores o de desempeño de AEMT y Paramedic; el cuestionario enumera cuatro niveles: EMR, EMT, AEMT y Paramedic. Como la verificación de habilidades del nivel EMT en muchos estados pasa por programas aprobados por el estado y procesos estatales (véase el artículo de inscripción EMT de este repositorio), la adaptación que más usan los candidatos EMT está en el lado cognitivo.

### 한국어 (초역 · 初译待校)
NREMT의 편의 규칙은 정책 62.02호 《시험 편의》(Accommodations for Exams)에 있으며 근거는 미국 장애인법(ADA)입니다. 등록국은 자격을 갖춘 장애인 응시자를 차별하지 않는 방식으로 인증 시험을 시행하고, 문서로 입증된 장애가 있는 사람에게 필기와 실기 양쪽 모두에서 합리적이고 적절한 편의를 제공합니다. 핵심은 편의가 시험에 접근하는 방식을 바꾸는 것이지 시험이 재는 내용을 바꾸는 게 아니라는 점입니다. 정책 심사 기준 제3항은 요청 편의가 문서화된 장애에 합리적이고 적절해야 하며, 시험이 병원 전 응급처치의 필수 기능을 평가하는 능력을 근본적으로 바꾸어서는 안 된다고 정하고, 합격 기준은 움직이지 않습니다. 적용 범위는 정확합니다. 모든 인증 등급의 인지 시험과 AEMT·Paramedic 두 등급의 심리운동/수행 시험이 대상이고, 신청서에는 EMR, EMT, AEMT, Paramedic 네 등급이 나열되어 있습니다. EMT 등급의 기술 검증은 많은 주에서 주 승인 프로그램과 주 절차가 맡으므로(본 저장소의 EMT 신청 글 참조), EMT 응시자가 실제로 가장 많이 쓰는 편의는 인지 시험 쪽입니다.

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)
Quy tắc về hỗ trợ thi của NREMT nằm trong Chính sách 62.02, Accommodations for Exams, dựa trên Đạo luật Người khuyết tật Hoa Kỳ (ADA): cơ quan đăng ký tổ chức các kỳ thi chứng nhận mà không phân biệt đối xử với thí sinh khuyết tật đủ điều kiện, và cung cấp hỗ trợ hợp lý, phù hợp cho cả phần thi viết lẫn phần thực hành đối với người có khuyết tật được chứng minh bằng hồ sơ. Điểm cốt lõi: hỗ trợ thay đổi cách tiếp cận kỳ thi, không thay đổi thứ mà kỳ thi đo — tiêu chí 3 của chính sách yêu cầu hỗ trợ được xin phải hợp lý và phù hợp với khuyết tật đã có hồ sơ, và không làm thay đổi căn bản khả năng của kỳ thi trong việc đánh giá các chức năng thiết yếu của chăm sóc tiền viện, và chuẩn đậu không dịch chuyển. Phạm vi rất rõ: chính sách áp dụng cho các kỳ thi nhận thức ở mọi cấp chứng nhận và các kỳ thi tâm vận động/thực hành của AEMT và Paramedic; bảng câu hỏi liệt kê bốn cấp — EMR, EMT, AEMT, Paramedic. Vì việc kiểm chứng kỹ năng cấp EMT ở nhiều bang chạy qua các chương trình được bang phê duyệt và quy trình của bang (xem bài đăng ký EMT của kho này), hỗ trợ mà thí sinh EMT thực tế dùng nhiều nhất nằm ở phía thi nhận thức.

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)
NREMT के सुविधा नियम उसकी नीति 62.02, Accommodations for Exams, में हैं, जिनका आधार Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) है: रजिस्ट्री अपने प्रमाणन परीक्षण इस तरह आयोजित करती है कि अन्यथा योग्य दिव्यांग अभ्यर्थियों से भेदभाव न हो, और दस्तावेज़ित दिव्यांगता वाले व्यक्तियों को लिखित और व्यावहारिक दोनों घटकों में उचित और उपयुक्त सुविधाएँ देती है। मूल बात यह है कि सुविधा परीक्षा तक पहुँचने का तरीका बदलती है, परीक्षा जो मापती है वह नहीं — नीति का मापदंड 3 कहता है कि माँगी गई सुविधा दस्तावेज़ित दिव्यांगता के लिए उचित और उपयुक्त हो और परीक्षा की अस्पताल-पूर्व देखभाल के आवश्यक कार्यों को मापने की क्षमता को मूलभूत रूप से न बदले, और उत्तीर्ण मानक नहीं हिलता। दायरा स्पष्ट है: नीति सभी प्रमाणन स्तरों की संज्ञानात्मक परीक्षाओं और AEMT व Paramedic की मनो-गतिक/प्रदर्शन परीक्षाओं पर लागू होती है; प्रश्नावली में चार स्तर गिने जाते हैं — EMR, EMT, AEMT, Paramedic। चूँकि EMT स्तर का कौशल सत्यापन कई राज्यों में राज्य-अनुमोदित कार्यक्रमों और राज्य प्रक्रियाओं से चलता है (इस संग्रह का EMT पंजीकरण लेख देखें), EMT अभ्यर्थी जो सुविधा सबसे अधिक उपयोग करते हैं वह संज्ञानात्मक पक्ष की है।

## 二、时机：至少提前 45 天，先批后约、先批后付

### 中文
政策的时间线只有一条硬线：请求最迟须在希望的考试日期之前至少 45 天送达注册局（原文：不少于 45 天），且注册局明说越早越好，好留出解决文件问题的时间。算一笔日历账：想在 2026 年 12 月 27 日考试，45 天前就是 2026 年 11 月 12 日——11 月 12 日加 45 天等于 12 月 27 日（本地复算两遍一致）。再叠官方《便利申请清单》（Accommodations Check List，2021 年 7 月 22 日修订）的处理口径：文件不齐时审查可能要 30 天或更久；45 减 30 等于 15，政策给的提前量里真正属于考生自己的缓冲只有约两周。动作次序有两条铁律：第一，考试费在便利审查结果出来之前不要付（清单第 2 步）；第二，批准信没到手之前不要预约考试——先约了，便利落不了地、只能改期（清单第 8 步）。结果以电子邮件附信通知，信里写明下一步怎么做。

### English
The policy's timeline has one hard line: a request must reach the Registry no less than forty-five (45) days before the desired test date, and the Registry urges candidates to submit as early as possible so documentation issues can be resolved. A calendar check: for a December 27, 2026 test date, 45 days earlier is November 12, 2026 — November 12 plus 45 days equals December 27 (checked locally twice). Layer on the official Accommodations Check List (revised July 22, 2021): if the documentation is incomplete, review may take 30 days or longer; 45 minus 30 equals 15, so only about two weeks of the lead time is truly the candidate's own buffer. Two sequencing rules follow: do not pay the exam fee until after the accommodations decision arrives (Check List step 2), and do not schedule the exam until the approval letter is in hand — schedule first and the accommodations will not be in place and the exam must be rescheduled (step 8). The decision comes by email with a letter spelling out the next steps.

### Español (译稿 · 初译待校)
El calendario de la política tiene una sola línea dura: la solicitud debe llegar al Registro no menos de cuarenta y cinco (45) días antes de la fecha deseada del examen, y el Registro insta a presentarla lo antes posible para resolver problemas de documentación. Una cuenta de calendario: para un examen el 27 de diciembre de 2026, 45 días antes es el 12 de noviembre de 2026 — 12 de noviembre más 45 días son 27 de diciembre (verificado localmente dos veces). Súmese el criterio de la lista oficial de verificación de adaptaciones (revisada el 22 de julio de 2021): si la documentación está incompleta, la revisión puede tomar 30 días o más; 45 menos 30 son 15, así que solo unas dos semanas del plazo son margen real del candidato. Dos reglas de orden: no pague la tarifa del examen hasta recibir la decisión sobre la adaptación (paso 2) y no reserve el examen hasta tener la carta de aprobación — si reserva antes, la adaptación no estará vigente y habrá que reprogramar (paso 8). La decisión llega por correo electrónico con una carta que detalla los pasos siguientes.

## 三、文件链：问卷加四类支持文件，专业信要写满三件

### 中文
申请是一条文件链，清单第 2 至 6 步逐项走：先在 NREMT 账户里新建申请并选级别（EMR／EMT／AEMT／Paramedic）；再打印《便利问卷》（Accommodations Questionnaire），填写、签名并注明日期；备齐支持文件后，全套发到 accommodations@nremt.org。支持文件分四类（清单第 5 步 a 至 d）：（a）有适当资质的专业人员（如心理学家或医生）的信，须用专业信笺、注明日期并签名，内容三件逐项数得 3——残疾诊断、具体残疾症状、建议的便利；（b）心理评估、签名的综合评估报告及附带的标准分数；（c）以往获批便利的证据；（d）考生本人的个人陈述，讲清残疾及其对日常生活与教育功能的影响。清单另提醒：某些请求可能还要补更多文件——第二节里那约两周的缓冲就是为这种来回留的。问卷还要考生列出从小学、中学、大学到 EMS 培训期间拿过的课堂与考试便利：既往便利史是审查的参照系，不是走过场。

### English
The application is a document chain, steps 2 through 6 of the Check List: create an application in your NREMT account and select the level (EMR, EMT, AEMT, or Paramedic); print, complete, sign, and date the Accommodations Questionnaire; assemble the supporting documentation; and email the complete package to accommodations@nremt.org. The supporting documentation falls into four categories (Check List step 5, items a–d): (a) a letter from an appropriately credentialed professional such as a psychologist or physician, on professional letterhead, dated and signed, containing three items — the diagnosis of the disability, the specific disability symptoms, and recommendations for accommodations; (b) a psychological evaluation, a signed comprehensive assessment report, and accompanying standard scores; (c) evidence of previously approved accommodations; and (d) a personal statement describing the disability and its impact on daily life and educational functioning. ## 四、审查六条准绳：逐案审、走互动、文件保密

### 中文

### English
The policy lists six review guidelines; four carry the substance. First, an individual must present adequate documentation demonstrating that the condition substantially limits one or more major life activities. Second, only individuals who, with or without reasonable accommodations, meet the eligibility requirements for certification at the requested level are eligible for accommodations — an accommodation never waives an eligibility gate. Third, the accommodation must be reasonable and appropriate for the documented disability and must not fundamentally alter the examination's ability to assess the essential functions of prehospital care (Section I). Fourth, the professionals who conduct assessments, render diagnoses, and recommend accommodations must be qualified to do so. The remaining two govern process: because each candidate's circumstances are unique, the Registry reviews case by case and engages in an interactive process with the candidate to identify reasonable accommodations; and all documentation is kept in confidence, disclosed to Registry staff and consultants only to the extent necessary to evaluate the request, and released to no third party without the candidate's written permission. An unfavorable outcome is not a verbal brush-off either: the candidate handbook's certification policies state that when the Registry makes an adverse decision — expressly including an unfavorable ruling on an accommodation request — the Executive Director sends the individual a statement of the decision by certified mail, return receipt requested, with further recourse under the handbook's current appeals policy.

## 五、两类考试两条程序：认知与心理运动各走各的

### 中文
政策列出的关联程序逐项数得 2 件：第 62.02.01 号管认知考试便利的处理，第 62.02.02 号管心理运动考试便利的处理——两类考试各走各的流程，认知考试的批准不要默认平移到技能一侧。认知考试在 Pearson 考场进行（见本仓 EMT 报名篇与 Paramedic 复考篇），考场只执行批准记录里写明的安排，现场临时加项没有通道，这条纪律与本仓其他考试的便利篇相同。心理运动一侧要多留个心眼：AEMT 与 Paramedic 自 2024 年 7 月 1 日起已无独立的国家级技能考，胜任力改由课程按矩阵验证（见本仓 AEMT 篇），但政策文本仍把心理运动／表现考试写在适用范围内；凡州或项目层面仍设有技能考核场合的，便利要向实际主办那一侧提前申请，NREMT 的认知批准函覆盖不到别人的考场。还有一条问卷上的特别约定：若获批的便利包含考试延时，考生须承诺从开考到考完，不以任何方式与其他考生交流考试内容——延时批的是作答时间，不是交流时间。

### English
The policy's related procedures number two: Procedure 62.02.01 covers processing accommodation requests for the cognitive examination, and Procedure 62.02.02 covers the psychomotor examination — each examination type runs its own process, and a cognitive approval should never be assumed to carry over to a skills setting. The cognitive examination is delivered at Pearson test centers (see this repository's EMT registration and Paramedic retake articles); the center delivers only the arrangements written in the approval record, and there is no channel for adding arrangements on site — the same discipline as in this repository's other accommodations articles. The psychomotor side needs extra care: since July 1, 2024, AEMT and Paramedic no longer have a standalone national skills examination, with competency verified through the program's matrix instead (see the AEMT article), yet the policy text still lists psychomotor/performance examinations within its scope; wherever a state- or program-level skills assessment still takes place, the accommodation request goes to whichever body actually runs that assessment, and an NREMT cognitive approval letter does not reach into someone else's examination room. One questionnaire term deserves note: if an approved accommodation includes extended time, the candidate agrees that, from the moment the examination begins until it is completed, they will not communicate in any way with other candidates about the examination's content — extended time buys answering time, not talking time.

## 六、加州单独注明：NREMT 批的只是 NREMT 那一关

### 中文
加州报考资格与州执照局要求单独注明如下。加州这一侧管发证的是州紧急医疗服务局（Emergency Medical Services Authority, EMSA）与县级地方 EMS 机构（LEMSA）：EMT 由地方一侧发证、Paramedic 由 EMSA 发照（衔接细节见本仓 EMT 与 Paramedic 总篇），NREMT 认证只是入场券之一，它的便利批准同样只管 NREMT 那一关。分工记三句：第一，NREMT 认知考试的便利只向 NREMT 申请——问卷加文件发 accommodations@nremt.org、至少提前 45 天，EMSA 与 LEMSA 不代收、不代批。第二，课程内的技能验证与临床实习需要调整时，找的是教育项目及其临床机构，按 ADA 与机构政策另行协商；NREMT 的批准函不自动延伸进教室与实习现场。第三，持证上岗之后的工作场所合理便利是雇主义务那一本账，与考试便利互不替代。加州的 18 岁线、两年续证等发证口径见本仓 EMT 报名篇与 NREMT 再认证篇，本篇不重复。最后提醒一句：政策第 62.02 号现行公开文本的版本号为 01、生效日期仍标注为待定（TBD），细则以 nremt.org 当期页面为准，付费与约考之前再回读一次原文。

### English
California requirements are noted separately, as required. On the state side, certification and licensure run through the Emergency Medical Services Authority (EMSA) and county local EMS agencies (LEMSA): EMTs are certified at the local level and Paramedics are licensed by EMSA (see this repository's EMT and Paramedic overview for the linkage), and an NREMT certification is only one of the entry tickets — its accommodations approval likewise governs only the NREMT gate. Three divisions of labor are worth memorizing. First, accommodations for the NREMT cognitive examination are requested from the NREMT alone — questionnaire plus documentation to accommodations@nremt.org, at least 45 days ahead; EMSA and the LEMSAs neither collect nor approve them. Second, adjustments inside a course's skills verification or clinical rotations are negotiated with the education program and its clinical sites under the ADA and institutional policy; an NREMT approval letter does not automatically extend into classrooms and clinical placements. Third, workplace reasonable accommodations after certification belong to the employer-obligation ledger and are no substitute for examination accommodations, nor the reverse. California's age-18 line, two-year certification cycle, and related issuance rules are covered in this repository's EMT registration and NREMT recertification articles and are not repeated here. One last caution: the current public text of Policy 62.02 is version 01 with its effective date still marked TBD, so treat the live pages at nremt.org as controlling and re-read the source before paying or scheduling.

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)
California की आवश्यकताएँ अलग से दर्ज हैं। राज्य पक्ष में प्रमाणन और लाइसेंस आपातकालीन चिकित्सा सेवा प्राधिकरण (EMSA) और काउंटी स्थानीय EMS एजेंसियों (LEMSA) से चलता है: EMT स्थानीय स्तर पर प्रमाणित होते हैं और Paramedic को EMSA लाइसेंस देता है (जुड़ाव के लिए इस संग्रह का EMT व Paramedic परिचय देखें), और NREMT प्रमाणन प्रवेश टिकटों में से केवल एक है — उसका सुविधा अनुमोदन भी केवल NREMT द्वार को ही संचालित करता है। श्रम-विभाजन की तीन बातें याद रखने योग्य हैं। पहली: NREMT संज्ञानात्मक परीक्षा की सुविधा केवल NREMT से माँगी जाती है — प्रश्नावली और दस्तावेज़ accommodations@nremt.org पर, कम से कम 45 दिन पहले; EMSA और LEMSA न इन्हें लेते हैं न स्वीकृत करते हैं। दूसरी: पाठ्यक्रम के कौशल सत्यापन या नैदानिक चक्रों के भीतर के समायोजन शिक्षा कार्यक्रम और उसके नैदानिक स्थलों के साथ ADA और संस्थागत नीति के तहत तय होते हैं; NREMT का अनुमोदन पत्र कक्षाओं और नैदानिक नियुक्तियों में स्वतः नहीं फैलता। तीसरी: प्रमाणन के बाद कार्यस्थल की उचित सुविधा नियोक्ता-दायित्व वाले खाते की बात है और परीक्षा सुविधा का विकल्प नहीं है, न ही इसका उलटा। California की 18 वर्ष की रेखा, दो वर्ष का प्रमाणन चक्र और संबंधित जारी-नियम इस संग्रह के EMT पंजीकरण और NREMT पुनःप्रमाणन लेखों में हैं और यहाँ दोहराए नहीं गए। अंतिम सावधानी: नीति 62.02 का वर्तमान सार्वजनिक पाठ संस्करण 01 है और उसकी प्रभावी तिथि अब भी TBD अंकित है, इसलिए nremt.org के जीवंत पृष्ठों को ही मानक मानें और भुगतान या बुकिंग से पहले स्रोत फिर से पढ़ लें।

### 参考依据（中文）
- NREMT 政策第 62.02 号《考试便利》（Accommodations for Exams，现行公开文本，版本 01）：适用范围（认知考试与 AEMT／Paramedic 心理运动／表现考试）、至少提前 45 天送达、审查准绳六条（实质限制主要生活活动、资格不豁免、不得根本改变、专业人员资质、逐案审查与互动过程、文件保密）、关联程序第 62.02.01 号（认知）与第 62.02.02 号（心理运动）。
- NREMT《便利申请清单》（Accommodations Check List，2021 年 7 月 22 日修订）：先建申请后交问卷、审查结果前不付考试费、支持文件四类（专业信三件／心理评估与标准分数／既往便利证据／个人陈述）、全套发 accommodations@nremt.org、文件不齐审查可达 30 天或更久、批准信到手前不约考。
- NREMT《便利问卷》（Accommodations Questionnaire）：可申请级别四级（EMR／EMT／AEMT／Paramedic）、既往课堂与考试便利史、延时获批者的考场保密承诺。
- NREMT 各级考生手册认证政策节（以 Paramedic 考生手册公开文本回核）：不利裁定（含便利请求被拒）由执行主任以需回执挂号信送达的口径，以及手册对第 62.02 号政策页面的指引。
- 加州衔接：EMSA 与县 LEMSA 的发证分工见本仓《EMT 与 Paramedic 执照考试深挖》总篇；EMT 年龄与续证口径见本仓 EMT 报名篇与 NREMT 再认证篇。细则以 NREMT 与 EMSA 当期原文为准。
