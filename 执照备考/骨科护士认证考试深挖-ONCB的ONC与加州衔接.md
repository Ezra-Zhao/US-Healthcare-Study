# 骨科护士认证考试深挖：ONCB 的 ONC、双轴蓝图与加州衔接

> 选题说明：执照备考子轮转「其他」格。原定 NBCOT 职业治疗师 OTR 与注册营养师 RD 两题，经 ls 与 grep 实查均已有专篇，故按查重口径自选同格未写题——骨科护士认证（ONCB 的 ONC），全仓实查无专篇。事实以 ONCB 考生手册与考试说明当期口径为准，易变项报考时回 ONCB 官网复核。全篇不写任何药物剂量。

## 一、这本证是什么：自愿性专科认证，不是州执照

### 中文
ONC 是 Orthopaedic Nurse Certified 的缩写，由骨科护士认证委员会（ONCB）颁发，面向照护肌肉骨骼疾病患者的注册护士（RN），覆盖关节置换、骨折、骨质疏松、运动损伤与退行性脊柱病等场景。性质先分清：ONC 是全国性、自愿性的专科能力认证，不是任何一州的执照，也不扩大 RN 的法定执业范围；能不能执业仍由所在州的 RN 执照决定，认证只是在执照之上证明专科经验与知识。ONCB 另有面向执业护士（nurse practitioner）的 ONP-C，资格与考试规格不同，不要混报。

### English
ONC stands for Orthopaedic Nurse Certified, issued by the Orthopaedic Nurses Certification Board (ONCB) for registered nurses caring for patients with musculoskeletal conditions such as joint replacement, fractures, osteoporosis, sports injuries, and degenerative spine disease. Its nature comes first: the ONC is a national, voluntary specialty certification. It is not a license in any state and does not expand an RN's statutory scope of practice; the right to practice still rests on the state RN license, and the certification evidences specialty experience and knowledge on top of it. ONCB also offers the ONP-C for nurse practitioners, with different eligibility and examination specifications — do not confuse the two tracks.

### Español (初译待校)

Primero, su naturaleza: el ONC es una certificación nacional y voluntaria de especialidad. No es una licencia en ningún estado ni amplía el ámbito legal del RN; el derecho a ejercer sigue dependiendo de la licencia estatal de RN, y la certificación solo acredita experiencia y conocimiento de especialidad sobre ella. ONCB ofrece también el ONP-C para enfermeros practicantes (nurse practitioner), con requisitos y examen distintos; no hay que confundir las dos vías. (初译待校)

## 二、报考资格与考试规格：两年 RN、一千小时、一百五十题

### 中文
资格三件，递交申请时须全部满足：持有现行、完整、无限制的美国 RN 执照（境外教育者须经认可机构做学历等值评估）；以 RN 身份执业满两个完整年；近三年内至少一千小时以 RN 身份从事骨科护理。不要求护理学士学位（BSN），专科小时才是硬门槛，且必须落在近三年窗口内。考试由 Meazure Learning 承办，共一百五十道单选题，其中一百三十五道计分、十五道为不计分试测题，限时三小时；通过线按 Angoff 方法定标，ONCB 考试说明给出的通过原始分为答对九十七题，个别版本可小幅浮动。报名费四百二十五美元，相邻两次报考间隔九十天，均以当期手册为准。

### English
Eligibility has three parts, all met when the application is filed: a current, full, unencumbered United States RN license (nurses educated abroad need an equivalency evaluation from an approved agency); two full years of practice as an RN; and at least 1,000 hours of RN practice in orthopaedic nursing within the past three years. No Bachelor of Science in Nursing is required; the specialty hours are the real gate, and they must fall inside the three-year window. The examination, delivered by Meazure Learning, has 150 multiple-choice questions — 135 scored and 15 unscored pretest items — in three hours. The passing point is set by the Angoff method; ONCB's examination description gives a passing raw score of 97 correct answers, with slight variation possible between forms. The fee is 425 US dollars, and attempts are separated by a 90-day wait, per the current handbook.

No se exige el Bachelor of Science in Nursing; la verdadera puerta son las horas de especialidad, dentro de la ventana de tres años. El punto de aprobación se fija con el método Angoff; la descripción de ONCB da una puntuación bruta de aprobación de noventa y siete aciertos, con leve variación posible entre versiones. La tarifa es de cuatrocientos veinticinco dólares y entre intentos hay noventa días de espera, según el manual vigente. (初译待校)

## 三、蓝图怎么读、加州怎么衔接：双轴命题与五年复证

### 中文
ONC 蓝图是双轴结构。疾病轴分八类，一百三十五道计分题的分布为：退行性疾病五十一题、骨科创伤二十六题、运动损伤十五题、代谢性骨病十二题、炎症性疾病十一题、先天与儿科七题、肌肉骨骼肿瘤七题、神经肌肉六题，退行性疾病一类占近四成，复习时间应按此权重分配。任务轴分六类，以区间给出：疼痛三十五至四十五题、并发症三十一至四十一题居前，自我照护二十二至三十二题、活动十五至二十五题居中，营养与心理社会各三至九题。每道题同时属于一个疾病类与一个任务类，要按交叉格读。复证周期五年：周期内再攒一千小时骨科相关实务并完成七十五个继续教育学时（骨科临床至少五十学时），或到期前重考，以当期复证手册为准。加州衔接：ONC 不是加州执照，加州不单设骨科护士执照类别；执业权来自加州 RN 执照，由注册护士委员会（BRN）依《护理执业法》（《商业与职业法典》第 2700 条起）核发监管。ONC 在加州是聘用与专科岗位分配时的能力证明，不改变 RN 执业范围。

来源：ONCB 官网 ONC 考试说明与考生手册；Meazure Learning 报考说明；加州《商业与职业法典》第 2700 条起与 BRN 执照口径。

### English
The ONC blueprint runs on two axes. The condition axis has eight categories, and the 135 scored questions distribute as follows: degenerative disorders 51, orthopaedic trauma 26, sports injuries 15, metabolic bone disorders 12, inflammatory disorders 11, congenital and pediatric disorders 7, musculoskeletal tumors 7, neuromuscular disorders 6. Degenerative disorders alone are nearly forty percent, so study time should follow that weight. The task axis has six categories in ranges: pain 35 to 45 and complications 31 to 41 lead; self-care 22 to 32 and activity 15 to 25 sit in the middle; nutrition and psychosocial care carry 3 to 9 each. Every question belongs to one condition and one task at once — read it as a cross-grid. Certification runs in five-year cycles: another 1,000 hours of orthopaedic-related practice plus 75 contact hours of continuing education (at least 50 in clinical orthopaedics), or retake the examination, per the current recertification handbook. For California: the ONC is not a California license, and California has no separate orthopaedic-nurse category. Practice authority comes from the California RN license, issued by the Board of Registered Nursing (BRN) under the Nursing Practice Act (Business and Professions Code section 2700 et seq.). In California the ONC evidences specialty competence in hiring and assignments; it does not change RN scope.

Sources: ONCB official ONC examination description and candidate handbook; Meazure Learning registration materials; California Business and Professions Code section 2700 et seq. and BRN licensure materials.

### Español (初译待校)
El plan del ONC funciona en dos ejes. El eje de afecciones tiene ocho categorías y las ciento treinta y cinco preguntas puntuables se distribuyen así: trastornos degenerativos cincuenta y una, traumatología ortopédica veintiséis, lesiones deportivas quince, enfermedades óseas metabólicas doce, trastornos inflamatorios once, congénitos y pediátricos siete, tumores musculoesqueléticos siete y neuromusculares seis. Solo los degenerativos suman cerca del cuarenta por ciento; el estudio debe seguir ese peso. El eje de tareas tiene seis categorías en rangos: dolor de treinta y cinco a cuarenta y cinco y complicaciones de treinta y una a cuarenta y una van primero; autocuidado de veintidós a treinta y dos y actividad de quince a veinticinco, en el medio; nutrición y atención psicosocial, de tres a nueve cada una. Cada pregunta pertenece a la vez a una afección y a una tarea: se lee como cuadrícula cruzada. La certificación corre en ciclos de cinco años: otras mil horas de práctica relacionada y setenta y cinco horas de contacto de educación continua (al menos cincuenta en ortopedia clínica), o repetir el examen, según el manual de recertificación vigente. Para California: el ONC no es una licencia californiana y California no tiene categoría separada de enfermería ortopédica. La autoridad viene de la licencia de RN de California, emitida por la Board of Registered Nursing (BRN) bajo la Nursing Practice Act (Business and Professions Code, sección 2700 y siguientes). En California el ONC acredita competencia de especialidad en contratación y asignaciones; no cambia el ámbito del RN. (初译待校)

(初译待校)

### Tiếng Việt (初译待校)
Khung đề ONC chạy trên hai trục. Trục bệnh lý có tám nhóm, và 135 câu tính điểm phân bổ như sau: bệnh thoái hóa 51 câu, chấn thương chỉnh hình 26, chấn thương thể thao 15, bệnh xương chuyển hóa 12, bệnh viêm 11, bẩm sinh và nhi khoa 7, u cơ xương 7, thần kinh cơ 6. Riêng nhóm thoái hóa đã gần bốn mươi phần trăm, nên thời gian ôn tập cần theo trọng số đó. Trục nhiệm vụ có sáu nhóm theo khoảng: đau 35 đến 45 và biến chứng 31 đến 41 dẫn đầu; tự chăm sóc 22 đến 32 và vận động 15 đến 25 ở giữa; dinh dưỡng và tâm lý xã hội mỗi nhóm 3 đến 9. Mỗi câu đồng thời thuộc một nhóm bệnh lý và một nhóm nhiệm vụ — hãy đọc như lưới chéo. Chu kỳ chứng chỉ là năm năm: thêm 1.000 giờ thực hành liên quan chỉnh hình và 75 giờ giáo dục liên tục (ít nhất 50 giờ chỉnh hình lâm sàng), hoặc thi lại, theo sổ tay tái chứng nhận hiện hành. Về California: ONC không phải giấy phép California, và bang này không có hạng riêng cho điều dưỡng chỉnh hình. Quyền hành nghề đến từ giấy phép RN California, do Board of Registered Nursing (BRN) cấp theo Nursing Practice Act (Business and Professions Code điều 2700 trở đi). Tại California, ONC là bằng chứng năng lực chuyên khoa khi tuyển dụng và phân công; nó không thay đổi phạm vi hành nghề RN. (初译待校)

### हिन्दी (初译待校)
ONC की रूपरेखा दो अक्षों पर चलती है। रोग-अक्ष में आठ श्रेणियाँ हैं और 135 अंक-योग्य प्रश्न इस प्रकार बँटे हैं: अपक्षयी विकार 51, अस्थि-रोग अभिघात 26, खेल चोटें 15, चयापचयी अस्थि रोग 12, शोथजन्य विकार 11, जन्मजात और बाल विकार 7, मांसपेशी-कंकाल अर्बुद 7, तंत्रिका-पेशी विकार 6। अकेले अपक्षयी विकार लगभग चालीस प्रतिशत हैं, इसलिए अध्ययन उसी भार के अनुसार हो। कार्य-अक्ष में छह श्रेणियाँ सीमाओं में हैं: दर्द 35 से 45 और जटिलताएँ 31 से 41 सबसे आगे; आत्म-देखभाल 22 से 32 और गतिविधि 15 से 25 बीच में; पोषण और मनोसामाजिक प्रत्येक 3 से 9। हर प्रश्न एक साथ एक रोग और एक कार्य से जुड़ा है — इसे क्रॉस-ग्रिड की तरह पढ़ें। प्रमाणन चक्र पाँच वर्ष का है: फिर से 1,000 घंटे अस्थि-रोग संबंधी अभ्यास और 75 संपर्क-घंटे सतत शिक्षा (कम से कम 50 नैदानिक अस्थि-रोग में), या पुनः परीक्षा, वर्तमान पुनःप्रमाणन पुस्तिका के अनुसार। कैलिफ़ोर्निया के लिए: ONC कैलिफ़ोर्निया लाइसेंस नहीं है, और वहाँ अस्थि-रोग नर्स की अलग श्रेणी नहीं है। अभ्यास का अधिकार कैलिफ़ोर्निया RN लाइसेंस से आता है, जिसे Board of Registered Nursing (BRN) Nursing Practice Act (Business and Professions Code धारा 2700 और आगे) के तहत जारी करता है। कैलिफ़ोर्निया में ONC भर्ती और नियुक्ति में विशेषज्ञ योग्यता का प्रमाण है; यह RN का कार्यक्षेत्र नहीं बदलता। (初译待校)
