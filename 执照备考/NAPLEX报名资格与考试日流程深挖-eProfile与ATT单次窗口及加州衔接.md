# NAPLEX 报名资格与考试日流程深挖：e-Profile、一年资格期与 ATT 单次窗口及加州衔接

这篇讲什么：
- 执照备考 NAPLEX 分站的报名与考试日细分专篇。选题经 ls/grep 实查：NAPLEX 本体（考什么、225 题与 6 小时）、复考与成绩报告、成绩转移与执照转移、CPJE 加州药法、药师续照、BPS 专科认证、外国毕业生 FPGEC 等均已有专篇；以「报名资格」「ATT」「考试日」实查，本体只用一段带过报名路径，没有专篇把资格确认、购考、ATT 窗口、预约改期与考试日入场逐段钉清——本篇补这一段：资格由报考州决定、购考在 NABP 的 e-Profile、ATT 只够一次预约、资格期一般一年且临近届满买不了考试，三本账分开讲。
- 来源口径：以各州药房委员会全国协会（National Association of Boards of Pharmacy，NABP）当期《Candidate Application Bulletin》与 NABP、Pearson VUE 公开口径为准；加州以加州药房委员会（California State Board of Pharmacy）执照申请公开口径为准。金额与期限会变，报考时以当期公告为准。全篇不写任何药物剂量与频次。

## 一、先分清三本账：资格、购考与预约不是一回事

### 中文
NAPLEX 报名最容易乱的地方，是把三件互相独立的事当成一件。第一本账是资格（eligibility）：能不能考，由申请执照的那一州药房委员会决定，NABP 本身不发执照，也不替所有州做资格决定。第二本账是购考：资格确认后，考生在 NABP 的 e-Profile 里提交考试申请并购买考试，钱付给 NABP。第三本账是预约与施考：购考后由 Pearson VUE 发出考试授权（Authorization to Test，ATT），考生拿 ATT 去 Pearson 考试中心预约并完成机考。顺序不能倒：没有资格就买不了考试，没有 ATT 就约不了考位；反过来，买了考试也不等于拿到执照，通过之后还要满足报考州的其余发照条件，法规考试与实习时数各有专篇。

### English
The easiest way to get confused about NAPLEX registration is to treat three independent things as one. Ledger one is eligibility: whether you may test is decided by the board of pharmacy of the state where you seek licensure; NABP itself issues no licenses and does not decide eligibility for every state. Ledger two is purchase: once eligibility is confirmed, the candidate files the examination application and buys the exam in NABP's e-Profile, paying NABP. Ledger three is scheduling and delivery: after purchase, Pearson VUE issues the Authorization to Test (ATT), and the candidate uses the ATT to book and sit the computer-based exam at a Pearson testing center. The order cannot be reversed — no eligibility, no purchase; no ATT, no seat — and buying the exam is not a license: the remaining licensure conditions of the testing state, its law examination and internship hours, are covered in their own articles.

### Español (初译待校)
La forma más fácil de confundirse con la inscripción al NAPLEX es tratar tres cosas independientes como una sola. El primer libro es la elegibilidad: si puede examinarse lo decide la junta de farmacia del estado donde busca la licencia; la NABP no emite licencias ni decide la elegibilidad de todos los estados. El segundo libro es la compra: confirmada la elegibilidad, el candidato presenta la solicitud y compra el examen en el e-Profile de la NABP, pagando a la NABP. El tercer libro es la reserva y la realización: tras la compra, Pearson VUE emite la autorización para examinarse (ATT), con la que el candidato reserva y rinde el examen por computadora en un centro Pearson. El orden no se puede invertir —sin elegibilidad no hay compra, sin ATT no hay asiento— y comprar el examen no es obtener la licencia: las demás condiciones del estado, su examen de legislación y las horas de prácticas, tienen sus propios artículos. (初译待校)

## 二、资格怎么来：申请费、代核名单与成绩单先行

### 中文
按 NABP 当期公告口径，提交 NAPLEX 考试申请时须付申请费 100 美元，该笔不可退。资格由谁确认分两路。一路是 NABP 代为确认资格的法域，公告名单逐项数得 14 个：阿拉斯加、亚利桑那、科罗拉多、哥伦比亚特区、肯塔基、路易斯安那、缅因、密歇根、内布拉斯加、北卡罗来纳、俄勒冈、罗得岛、犹他、威斯康星；选这些法域的考生另付 85 美元不可退的处理费，该费覆盖同一法域的 NAPLEX 与 MPJE 两门，有效期自初次申请起一年。另一路是不在名单上的州，由州委员会自行确认资格，考生先向州里拿到资格，再回 e-Profile 购考，加州走的就是这一路，第六节细说。还有一道先行件：2020 年及以后毕业的考生，须先由母校药学院把官方成绩单直接寄送 NABP，成绩单没到位就买不了考试。时间上，考生最早可在预计毕业日期前 6 个月提交申请，但资格确认与购考仍要等成绩单与州里审查走完。

### English
Under the current NABP bulletin, filing the NAPLEX application carries a nonrefundable application fee of $100. Who confirms eligibility runs on two tracks. On one track, NABP confirms eligibility for a published list of jurisdictions — count them: Alaska, Arizona, Colorado, the District of Columbia, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maine, Michigan, Nebraska, North Carolina, Oregon, Rhode Island, Utah, and Wisconsin, fourteen in all; candidates who select one of these pay an additional nonrefundable processing fee of $85, which covers both the NAPLEX and the MPJE for that one jurisdiction and stays valid for one year from the initial application. On the other track, states not on the list confirm eligibility themselves: the candidate gets the board's confirmation first and only then buys the exam in e-Profile — California runs on this track, detailed in section six. One item must move first: candidates who graduated in or after 2020 must have their college of pharmacy send the official transcript directly to NABP, and the exam cannot be purchased until it arrives. Candidates may apply as early as six months before the expected graduation date, but confirmation and purchase still wait on the transcript and the board's review.

## 三、购考与 ATT：考试费、一年资格期与单次窗口

### 中文
购考一侧的数字先算清。考试费为 520 美元，加上不可退的申请费 100 美元，合计 620 美元，100 加 520 等于 620，本地复算两遍一致。若在资格期届满前取消或撤回考试，按公告口径可退回考试费部分，申请费不退，等于最多退回 620 减 100 等于 520 美元。购考完成后，ATT 由 Pearson VUE 发到考生 e-Profile 所用的电子邮箱，内含考生姓名、考试资格日期与预约说明；ATT 只对一次考试预约有效，用掉或过期都不自动顺延。资格期一般为一年，自资格确认起算；两个截止要盯紧：资格期只剩 10 个工作日或更少时，已经不能再购买考试；资格期内没有完成预约与考试的，全部费用作废，须从申请流程重新走并重新缴费。一年等于 12 个月，最早提前申请的 6 个月正好是它的一半，12 除以 2 等于 6；但提前申请不等于资格期提前起跑，起算点以资格确认日为准。

### English
Clear the purchase numbers first. The examination fee is $520; with the nonrefundable $100 application fee the total is $620 — one hundred plus five hundred twenty equals six hundred twenty, recomputed locally twice with the same result. A candidate who cancels or withdraws before the eligibility period expires may get the examination fee back under the bulletin, while the application fee stays nonrefundable — at most six hundred twenty minus one hundred, that is $520, comes back. After purchase, Pearson VUE emails the ATT to the address used for e-Profile; it carries the candidate's name, the eligibility dates, and scheduling instructions, and it is valid for one testing appointment only — used or expired, it does not roll over. The eligibility period is generally one year from confirmation, with two deadlines to watch: with ten or fewer business days left in the period, the exam can no longer be purchased at all; and a candidate who never schedules and tests inside the period forfeits all fees and starts the application over, fees included. One year is twelve months, and the earliest application lead of six months is exactly half of it — twelve divided by two equals six — but applying early does not start the eligibility clock early; the clock starts on the confirmation date.

## 六、加州单独注明：资格由州药房委员会自核（州执照局要求）
