# 物理治疗师执照续照与继续胜任力深挖：加州两年 30 小时、PTBC 规则与 FSBPT 衔接

这篇讲什么：
- 执照备考 NPTE 分站的续照专篇。子轮转此前已走 NPTE 本体、复考与成绩报告、成绩转移与执照转移各篇，入场段基本齐了，唯独考过之后每两年靠什么把执照续下去、加州说的「继续胜任力」到底由哪几块拼成，仓内以「物理治疗续照」「继续胜任力」grep 仅在成绩转移篇顺带一句、无本体专篇——本篇补这一段：全国层没有统一学时表、FSBPT 只给模型与工具不发照、加州把三十小时与必修构成写进法规汇编，三层分开讲。
- 来源口径：加州以加州物理治疗委员会（Physical Therapy Board of California，PTBC）继续胜任力官方页、《商业与职业法典》第 2636 条一带与《加州法规汇编》第 16 卷第 1399.90 至 1399.99 条（继续胜任力专章）为准；全国层以州物理治疗委员会联合会（Federation of State Boards of Physical Therapy，FSBPT）公开口径为准。费用与表格版本变动快，一律回 PTBC 当期口径核，本篇不写死。全篇不写任何药物剂量与频次。

## 一、先定性：考试管入场，续照管在场，三本账别混

### 中文
物理治疗师的资格是一条时间线：NPTE 管入场，成绩转移与执照转移管搬家，续照管在场——拿到执照之后每两年凭什么继续合法执业。加州续照的核心工具不叫继续教育，而叫继续胜任力（continuing competency）：委员会不要求重考，而是要求持照人在每个周期内完成规定学时的胜任力活动，并能在审计时拿出证明。邻近三本账不要混进来：物理治疗助理 PTA 在加州与 PT 同归 PTBC、同一套继续胜任力要求，但 PTA 的入场考试是 NPTE-PTA，续照相同不等于入场相同；专科认证（如 ABPTS 各专科）有各自的维持周期，压在州执照之上，不替代州续照；物理治疗执照协定（PT Compact）给的是跨州执业特权，成员州里也要先有本州有效执照，特权不是续照的替代品。

### English
A physical therapist credential runs on a timeline: the NPTE governs entry, score and license transfer govern moving, and renewal governs staying — the basis for lawfully continuing to practice every two years. California's renewal instrument is called continuing competency rather than continuing education: the board does not re-examine; it requires a set number of competency hours inside each cycle, with proof available on audit. Three neighboring ledgers stay separate: California PTAs sit under the same board (PTBC) and the same continuing competency requirement, but their entry exam is the NPTE-PTA, so shared renewal does not mean shared entry; specialty certification such as the ABPTS specialties runs on its own maintenance cycle on top of the state license and never replaces state renewal; and the PT Compact grants a cross-state practice privilege that presupposes a valid home-state license — a privilege is not a renewal substitute.

### Español (初译待校)
La credencial de fisioterapeuta corre sobre una línea de tiempo: el NPTE gobierna la entrada, la transferencia de puntaje y de licencia gobierna la mudanza, y la renovación gobierna la permanencia. El instrumento californiano se llama competencia continua (continuing competency) y no educación continua: la junta no vuelve a examinar, exige un número de horas de actividad de competencia dentro de cada ciclo, con prueba disponible en auditoría. Tres libros vecinos quedan separados: los asistentes PTA de California están bajo la misma junta (PTBC) y el mismo requisito de competencia continua, pero su examen de entrada es el NPTE-PTA; la certificación de especialidad, como las de ABPTS, corre con su propio ciclo sobre la licencia estatal y nunca sustituye su renovación; y el PT Compact otorga un privilegio interestatal que presupone una licencia estatal válida, no un sustituto de la renovación. (初译待校)

Chứng chỉ vật lý trị liệu chạy trên một dòng thời gian: NPTE quản cổng vào, chuyển điểm và chuyển giấy phép quản việc dời bang, và gia hạn quản việc ở lại. ## 二、全国层：没有全国统一学时表，FSBPT 给模型不发照

### 中文
美国没有一张全国统一的物理治疗续照学时表。各州物理治疗委员会各自定周期、学时与指定内容，续照永远向发照州办理。FSBPT 的位置要摆正：它主办 NPTE、运营成绩转移与执照核验一类跨州工具，也发布继续胜任力的模型框架，供各州设计自家制度时参照；但模型不是法规，FSBPT 本身不发照、不续照，某个州采没采用模型里的某一条，要回该州自己的法规汇编核。法理（jurisprudence）类要求也是各州各表：有的州在续照时要求法理评估或考试，有的州只在入场时考，加州的法理考试属于入场段，续照段的法律内容是靠下面要讲的伦理、法律与法规必修学时来维持。这一层的结论只有一句：续照规则没有联邦版，只有发照州版，FSBPT 是工具箱与模型库，不是发照机关。

### English
There is no single national table of physical therapy renewal hours in the United States. Each state board sets its own cycle, hours, and mandated topics, and renewal is always done with the issuing state. FSBPT's place needs stating plainly: it administers the NPTE, runs cross-state tools such as score transfer and licensure verification, and publishes a continuing-competency model framework that states may draw on when designing their own systems — but a model is not a regulation, FSBPT neither issues nor renews licenses, and whether a given state adopted any given model element is a question for that state's own regulations. Jurisprudence requirements likewise vary by state: some states attach a jurisprudence assessment or exam to renewal, others test law only at entry; California's law examination sits at entry, and at renewal its legal content is maintained through the mandated ethics, laws, and regulations hours covered below. The federal layer, in short, has no renewal table at all — only issuing-state tables exist, and FSBPT is a toolbox and model library, not a licensing authority.

### Español (初译待校)
En Estados Unidos no existe una tabla nacional única de horas de renovación en fisioterapia. Cada junta estatal fija su propio ciclo, sus horas y sus temas obligatorios, y la renovación se hace siempre ante el estado emisor. El lugar de la FSBPT debe quedar claro: administra el NPTE, opera herramientas interestatales como la transferencia de puntaje y la verificación de licencias, y publica un marco modelo de competencia continua que los estados pueden consultar, pero un modelo no es una norma: la FSBPT no emite ni renueva licencias, y si un estado adoptó o no un elemento del modelo se comprueba en la normativa de ese estado. Los requisitos de jurisprudencia también varían: unos estados los atan a la renovación y otros solo a la entrada; en California el examen de leyes es de entrada, y en la renovación el contenido legal se mantiene con las horas obligatorias de ética, leyes y reglamentos. No hay tabla federal de renovación; solo hay tablas del estado emisor. (初译待校)

FSBPT는 NPTE를 주관하고 점수 이전과 면허 확인 같은 주간 도구를 운영하며, 각 주가 자기 제도를 설계할 때 참고할 수 있는 계속 역량 모델 틀을 내놓습니다. 그러나 모델은 법규가 아니고, FSBPT 자체는 면허를 발급하거나 갱신하지 않습니다. 어떤 주가 모델의 어느 항목을 채택했는지는 그 주의 규정집으로 확인해야 합니다. 법리(jurisprudence) 요구도 주마다 다릅니다. 갱신 때 법리 평가나 시험을 요구하는 주도 있고 입구에서만 보는 주도 있습니다. 캘리포니아의 법리 시험은 입구 단계에 있고, 갱신 단계의 법률 내용은 아래에서 말할 윤리·법률·규정 필수 시간으로 유지됩니다. 결론은 하나입니다. 갱신 규칙에 연방판은 없고 발급한 주의 판만 있습니다. ## 三、加州单独注明：两年 30 小时与必修构成（州执照局 PTBC 要求）

### 中文
加州这一层的发照机关是物理治疗委员会（Physical Therapy Board of California，PTBC）。法源为《商业与职业法典》第 2636 条一带（执照与续照）与《加州法规汇编》第 16 卷第 1399.90 至 1399.99 条（继续胜任力专章，其中第 1399.93 条管主题构成与限制、第 1399.94 条管达成路径）。续照周期为两年，PT 与 PTA 同表。学时账本身：在每个续照周期内完成三十个小时的继续胜任力活动，学时须在本周期内挣得，不能拿上个周期的余量充数。三十小时的构成是写死的、逐项数得三块：基本生命支持（basic life support）四小时，须达到或超过美国心脏协会医疗人员基本生命支持课程的水平、且必须含实操（hands-on）成分，该课程一律按四小时计、不按实际课时折算；伦理、法律与法规两小时，可单项也可组合；其余二十四小时在与物理治疗执业相关的主题内自选，走第 1399.94 条的传统或替代路径。算例两笔，本地复算两遍一致：四加二加二十四等于三十，必修六小时、自选二十四小时；三十除以二等于年均十五小时，这只是排课节奏、不是法定年度下限，规则看的是周期末总数与构成，不是每年是否达标。

### English
In California the issuing authority is the Physical Therapy Board of California (PTBC). The legal anchors are Business and Professions Code section 2636 and its neighbors (licensure and renewal) and Title 16, California Code of Regulations, sections 1399.90 through 1399.99, the continuing-competency article — with section 1399.93 setting subject matter and limits and section 1399.94 setting the authorized pathways. The cycle is two years, and PTs and PTAs share one table. The hours ledger: complete thirty hours of continuing competency activity inside each renewal cycle, earned within that cycle, with no carry-over from the last. The composition of the thirty is fixed, in three blocks: four hours of basic life support, at a level comparable to or more advanced than the American Heart Association's Basic Life Support for Health Care Providers course, with a hands-on component required, and credited at four hours regardless of the course's actual duration; two hours of ethics, laws, and regulations, singly or in combination; and twenty-four remaining hours in topics relevant to physical therapy practice, earned through the traditional or alternate pathways of section 1399.94. Two worked figures, each recomputed twice locally with matching results: four plus two plus twenty-four equals thirty — six mandated, twenty-four elective; and thirty divided by two equals fifteen hours a year on average, a pacing figure only, since the rule reads the cycle-end total and its composition, not a yearly floor.

### Español (初译待校)
En California la autoridad emisora es la Physical Therapy Board of California (PTBC). Los anclajes legales son el artículo 2636 y su entorno del Código de Negocios y Profesiones y el Título 16 del Código de Reglamentos de California, artículos 1399.90 a 1399.99, el capítulo de competencia continua: el 1399.93 fija la composición temática y el 1399.94 las vías autorizadas. El ciclo es de dos años y PT y PTA comparten tabla. El libro de horas: completar treinta horas de actividad de competencia continua dentro de cada ciclo, ganadas en ese ciclo y sin traslado del anterior. La composición es fija, en tres bloques: cuatro horas de soporte vital básico, de nivel comparable o superior al curso de la American Heart Association para personal sanitario, con componente práctico obligatorio, acreditadas siempre como cuatro horas sin importar la duración real; dos horas de ética, leyes y reglamentos, por separado o combinadas; y veinticuatro horas restantes en temas pertinentes al ejercicio, por las vías tradicional o alternativa del artículo 1399.94. Dos cálculos verificados dos veces en local: cuatro más dos más veinticuatro son treinta; y treinta entre dos son quince horas al año de promedio, solo un ritmo de planificación, porque la norma mira el total y la composición al cierre del ciclo, no un mínimo anual. ## 四、首次续照半额：及时交费十五小时，逾期回到三十小时

### 中文

### English

Two worked figures, recomputed locally with matching results: four plus two plus nine equals fifteen, the exact composition of the half cycle; and thirty divided by two equals fifteen, so the half rate is exactly half the normal cycle. Separately, section 1399.99 and its neighbors allow a licensee to apply for an exemption from the continuing competency requirements on stated grounds; an exemption counts only once the board grants it, and the eligible grounds and paperwork follow PTBC's current published rules.

## 五、达成路径与留证：传统与替代两条路，记录留五年

### 中文

### English
Not all thirty hours are earned sitting in courses. Section 1399.94 opens two routes: a traditional pathway of approved coursework, and an alternate pathway that converts real professional activity into hours — publishing a peer-reviewed article, developing or first-presenting an approved course, serving as a subject-matter expert in a board or FSBPT examination process, or serving on a board-appointed task force, among others. Alternate-pathway items carry their own hour caps (many sit around a sixteen-hour ceiling), and passing the California law examination can also convert into a small number of hours; the exact items, caps, and crediting mechanics follow the current text of section 1399.94 and PTBC's checklist, and should never be quoted from memory. On the proof side, licensees keep their own continuing competency records for five years and produce them when the board audits; an audit credits completion certificates and activity records, not a folder of platform screenshots. Five years spans two and a half cycles, so last cycle's records stay in the drawer long after renewal posts. Fabricated hours or missing records, once found, become a correction-and-discipline problem first — and at the serious end, a threat to the license itself.
